PREFACE TO THE REVISED EDITION

RSE 第 1 卷 · 早期论文与译序 · p. xxiii–xxxii · 40 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

James Strachey's monumental achievement in editing Freud's complete psychological works in a uniform English translation was widely acclaimed when the Standard Edition first appeared in 1953–66. But even then, some critical voices were heard (e.g. Brandt, 1961). By the early 1980s, the rumblings had increased to the level of outrage (e.g. Bettelheim, 1983). In response, the Institute of Psychoanalysis in London—the intellectual custodian of the Standard Edition and its co-publisher—called a symposium on the topic in 1989. Shortly thereafter they appointed a committee to formulate a practical plan.¹ The Institute also called for papers on the topic to be submitted to the International Review of Psycho-Analysis and a special number was devoted to the controversy. The decision to produce a Revised Standard Edition was formally announced in the special number (Yorke, 1991).

詹姆斯·斯特雷奇以统一的英译编纂弗洛伊德心理学著作全集，这一丰功伟绩在《标准版》于 1953–66 年间问世之际广受赞誉。但即便在当时，也已能听到一些批评的声音（如布兰特[Brandt]，1961）。到 1980 年代初，这些怨声已升级至愤慨的程度（如贝特尔海姆[Bettelheim]，1983）。作为回应，伦敦的精神分析研究所——这一《标准版》的学术监护者与联合出版者——于 1989 年就此召开了一次专题研讨会。此后不久，他们任命了一个委员会来拟定切实的方案。¹ 该研究所还征召就此主题的论文，提交给《国际精神分析评论》[International Review of Psycho-Analysis]，并辟出一个专号讨论这一争议。制作《修订标准版》[Revised Standard Edition] 的决定，便在那个专号中正式宣告（约克[Yorke]，1991）。

That the project has come to fruition only now, three decades later, is due mainly to the fact that the planned revision gradually expanded as the project progressed. In the process, editorial responsibility passed from the late Albert Dickson to me (in 1995), and the very extensive editing of the SE that Dickson had undertaken on the basis of Strachey's marked-up copies and notes (and those of Angela Richards, the editor of the 24th volume, published in 1974) was incorporated into the present work.

这一项目直到三十年后的今天才得以实现，主要原因是：随着项目的推进，原定的修订范围逐渐扩大。在此过程中，编辑职责从已故的阿尔伯特·迪克森[Albert Dickson] 移交于我（1995 年）；而迪克森依据斯特雷奇作了批注的副本与笔记（以及 1974 年出版的第 24 卷编者安杰拉·理查兹的笔记）对《标准版》[SE] 所作的大量编辑，均被纳入本版。

The editorial policies that underpin this revised edition were not formulated by me alone. They were the collective responsibility of the committee just mentioned, and successive Boards of the Institute. The scholarly work, too, was not my sole responsibility; I relied heavily on the work of others. This applies above all to Ilse Grubrich-Simitis—who is James Strachey's true successor, and not only in Germany. Apart from allowing me to draw freely upon her many authoritative studies of Freud's writings, she also assisted me in almost every aspect of this edition, including formulation of some aspects of the editorial policy, although quite significant policy decisions were also made despite her better judgment. I have likewise relied heavily on Riccardo Steiner's research (especially his definitive series of publications on the history of the Standard Edition) and on his valuable personal support. The same applies to the painstaking bibliographical research of the late Ingeborg Meyer-Palmedo and the late Gerhard Fichtner and (in more recent years) Albrecht Hirschmüller. The diverse ways in which these scholars have contributed to this edition far exceed the number of times their names are actually cited in it. The list of other colleagues to whom I owe similar scholarly debts, in varying degrees (starting with the names of K. R. Eissler and Patrick Mahony), is too long and ultimately too indeterminate to specify.

支撑这一修订版的各种编辑方针，并非由我一人拟定。它们是方才提到的那个委员会以及研究所历届理事会的集体责任。学术工作同样并非我一人之责；我在很大程度上倚仗他人之作。这尤其适用于伊尔泽·格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯[Ilse Grubrich-Simitis]——她是詹姆斯·斯特雷奇的真正继承者，且不限于在德国。除了允许我自由取用她对弗洛伊德著作的众多权威研究之外，她还几乎在本版的方方面面协助我，包括参与拟定编辑方针的某些内容——尽管在若干相当重大的方针决策上，即便她有更佳判断，仍一仍旧贯。我同样极其倚重里卡多·施泰纳[Riccardo Steiner] 的研究（尤其是他关于《标准版》历史的那一套权威性系列论著）及其宝贵的个人支持。已故的英格博格·迈尔-帕尔梅多[Ingeborg Meyer-Palmedo] 与已故的格哈德·菲希特纳[Gerhard Fichtner]，以及（近年来）阿尔布雷希特·希尔施米勒[Albrecht Hirschmüller] 的细致书目研究，亦同此例。这些学者对本版贡献之多，远超过其姓名在书中实际被引用的次数。我以不同程度感激的其他学界同人（从 K. R. 艾斯勒与帕特里克·马奥尼[Patrick Mahony] 二位的名字起算）名单太长，终究又太不确定，难以一一具列。

Nature and Scope of the Revision

The single most important principle upon which this revision is based is the decision to retain Strachey's SE translations—that is, to revise the translations rather than replace them. The justification for this decision is outlined in the Editor's Introduction to the 'Notes on the Translation of Some Technical Terms', now moved from Volume 1 to Volume 24. The reasoning behind the decision to retain Strachey's translations revolved mainly around questions of convention, but also the special problems of psychoanalytical terminology, and the limitations of translation itself.

本次修订所依据的唯一最重要的原则，是决定保留斯特雷奇《标准版》的译文——亦即修订这些译文，而非取代它们。这一决定的理由，概述于《若干技术术语翻译注》[Notes on the Translation of Some Technical Terms] 的编者导言之中，该注现已从第 1 卷移至第 24 卷。决定保留斯特雷奇译文的思路，主要围绕惯例问题，但也涉及精神分析术语的特殊问题，以及翻译本身固有的局限。

Strachey mentioned in his General Preface above that he translated Freud as though he were an 'English man of science'. No blame can attach to the first part of this characterization, of Freud as an English man. When translating one must either use the conventions of the language being translated from or of that being translated into. Neither solution is better and it is impossible to employ them both; one simply has to make a choice. When translating the French equivalent of the Standard Edition, the Oeuvres complètes, Jean Laplanche opted for the former solution; James Strachey opted for the latter. In doing so, he made the choice that Freud himself always made when translating and when reviewing the translations of others (including translations of his own work). Having made this choice, certain implications followed naturally. Least important among these are the purely linguistic ones. It is, for example, simply bad English to render Überich as 'I over myself' or Nachträglichkeit as 'afterwardsness', as some commentators have seriously recommended. We should never forget the outstanding literary merits of Freud's writing, which are widely acknowledged; the masterly quality of his prose strains the abilities of any translator.

斯特雷奇在上文《总序》中提到，他翻译弗洛伊德时，仿佛弗洛伊德是一位"英国科学人士"。这一描述的前半部分——将弗洛伊德视为一位英国人士——无可指责。翻译时，译者要么采用源语言的惯例，要么采用译入语言的惯例。两种方案都没有高下之分，且不可能兼用二者，只能择其一。在翻译《标准版》的法语对应本《全集》[Oeuvres complètes] 时，让·拉普朗什[Jean Laplanche] 选择了前者；詹姆斯·斯特雷奇则选择了后者。如此一来，他所做的选择，正是弗洛伊德本人在翻译时以及审阅他人译文（包括其本人著作的译文）时一贯作出的选择。作出这一选择之后，某些含义便自然随之而来。其中最不重要的是纯语言层面的含义。例如，把 Überich 译作"I over myself"，或把 Nachträglichkeit 译作"afterwardsness"（如某些评论者所郑重建议的那样），不过是拙劣的英语罢了。我们绝不应忘记弗洛伊德文笔那广受公认的卓越文学价值；其散文大师般的品质，足以考验任何译者的能力。

More pertinent are the conventions of English scientific writing and translation. This raises the spectre of the second part of Strachey's characterization, of Freud as a man of science. The most widely expressed criticism of Strachey's translation is that he 'falsely scientized' Freud; that he transformed his everyday descriptive language derived from ordinary German into an abstract technical language derived from ancient Greek and Latin. The first problem with this criticism is that Freud himself regularly described psychoanalysis as a natural science. It is therefore difficult to sustain the argument that it was Strachey who scientized Freud. Why, then, did Freud use everyday descriptive language? The answer is surely that, unlike English scientific writing, where it is common to use neologisms derived from classical antiquity, in German scientific writing it is conventional to use everyday descriptive words for the equivalent technical terms. This applies especially in anatomy, which is the science that Freud was most familiar with. It would therefore be entirely appropriate to translate the term angelehnte Fasern (which Freud coined in 1878 to describe a particular type of nerve fibre) as 'anaclitic' fibres; but it would be quite odd for an English anatomist to write about 'leaning-on' fibres, as is sometimes suggested for the psychoanalytic term Anlehnung. To translate like this would break conventions of English scientific writing, while simultaneously obscuring the link between Freud's later, psychoanalytic and earlier, neuroscientific work.

更为贴切的，是英语科学写作与翻译的惯例。这就引出了斯特雷奇描述的后半部分——把弗洛伊德视为一位科学人士——的幽灵。对斯特雷奇译文最为普遍的批评，是说他"不实地使弗洛伊德科学化"了，即把弗洛伊德源自日常德语的平实描述性语言，转变成了源自古希腊语与拉丁语的抽象技术性语言。这一批评首先遇到的问题在于：弗洛伊德本人就经常把精神分析描述为一门自然科学。因此，要论证是斯特雷奇使弗洛伊德科学化了，实难成立。那么，弗洛伊德为何使用日常的描述性语言？答案无疑是：与英语科学写作中惯用源自古典古代的新造词不同，在德语科学写作中，惯常以日常描述性词语来对应技术术语。这在解剖学中尤为如此，而解剖学正是弗洛伊德最为熟悉的科学。因此，把 angelehnte Fasern 一词（弗洛伊德 1878 年为描述某类特定神经纤维所造）译作"依附的"[anaclitic] 纤维，是完全恰当的；但若一位英国解剖学家写作"leaning-on"纤维（如有时有人就精神分析术语 Anlehnung 所建议的那样），则会相当古怪。这样翻译，既会破坏英语科学写作的惯例，同时又遮蔽了弗洛伊德后期精神分析工作与其早期神经科学工作之间的联系。

It is of course true that there is also much that separates Freud's earlier and later work; psychoanalysis is not only natural science. This introduces the fact that Strachey was constrained not only by existing conventions in English scientific writing but also by those of the other intellectual traditions upon which Freud drew. The term das Ich, for example, Strachey's translation of which as 'the ego' was decried by many detractors, has a very long history in German philosophy. By the time that Strachey plumped for 'the ego', it was already well established as the standard English equivalent of das Ich. To break with that tradition would have been most peculiar, and again obscured important links. In light of this, it is not surprising that all of Strachey's predecessors in translating Freud used the same neologism.

当然，弗洛伊德早期与后期著作之间也有许多相隔之处；精神分析并不仅仅是自然科学。这就带出一个事实：斯特雷奇不仅受制于英语科学写作中既有的惯例，也受制于弗洛伊德所汲取的其他思想传统的惯例。例如，das Ich 一词（斯特雷奇将其译作"the ego"，此举为众多诋毁者所非难）在德国哲学中有着悠久的历史。待到斯特雷奇择定"the ego"之时，它早已确立为 das Ich 公认的标准英语对应词。若与那一传统决裂，将极为怪异，并会再度遮蔽重要的联系。有鉴于此，斯特雷奇之前所有翻译弗洛伊德的人，都使用同一个新造词，也就不足为奇了。

This introduces the most important constraint upon Strachey: the existence of established conventions in English psychoanalytic translation. The ignoring of this tradition, so amply researched and documented by Riccardo Steiner, and so clearly acknowledged by Strachey himself, makes the widespread criticisms of his technical vocabulary not only unfair but also ill-informed. Almost all the technical terms for which Strachey has been condemned were, in fact, coined by others, some when he was still a schoolboy, long before the publication of the Standard Edition.

这就引出了对斯特雷奇最为重要的制约：英语精神分析翻译中既有惯例之存在。对这一传统的忽视——里卡多·施泰纳对此作了充分的研究与文献梳理，斯特雷奇本人也明确承认——使得针对其专门术语的种种普遍批评不仅不公，而且有失偏颇、论据不足。事实上，斯特雷奇因之而受谴责的几乎所有技术术语，都是由他人所造；其中一些，早在他还是个学童、远在《标准版》出版之前，便已造出。

This tradition, including 'official' German-English vocabularies published by the Glossary Committee of the Institute of Psychoanalysis, culminated in the very idea of a standard edition. A standardized technical vocabulary necessarily has special meaning in a discipline like psychoanalysis, the object of study of which is—by definition—invisible. The very act of psychoanalysis is an act of translation. Freud was, as he wrote, therefore obliged to invent a 'figurative language' peculiar to depth psychology: 'We could not otherwise describe the processes in question at all, and indeed we could not have become aware of them' (Freud, 1920g; RSE, *18, 57). Herein lies the tension between discovery and invention in psychoanalysis. Freud was in this sense his own first translator. And we, his psychoanalytic successors, translators all, are thereafter required to 'cathect' his figurative language with new observations. That is what a uniform psychoanalytical vocabulary is for: to ensure that we all know when we are talking about the same thing and when we are talking about different things; so that we can focus our attention on the things that we agree or disagree about, rather than the words*. In a field where the things themselves are so hard to grasp, it is difficult to see what is to be gained by introducing a gaggle of synonymous words.

这一传统，包括由精神分析研究所术语委员会印行的"官方"德英词汇表，在标准版这一构想本身中达到了顶点。标准化的技术词汇，在像精神分析这样一门其研究对象——按定义——不可见的学科中，必然具有特殊的意义。精神分析这一行为本身，就是一种翻译行为。因此，正如弗洛伊德所写，他不得不发明一种深蕴心理学所特有的"比喻性语言"："否则我们根本无法描述所讨论的过程，也确实无从觉察到它们"（弗洛伊德，1920g；RSE 第 18 卷，第 57 页）。精神分析中"发现"与"发明"之间的张力，即在于此。在这个意义上，弗洛伊德是他自己的第一位译者。而我们，他的精神分析后继者，全都是译者，此后便须以新的观察来"投注"[cathect] 他的比喻性语言。这正是统一的精神分析词汇表的意义所在：确保我们都清楚何时我们在谈论同一事物、何时在谈论不同事物；从而使我们把注意力集中于我们或赞同或分歧的事物，而非词语。在一个事物本身如此难以把握的领域里，很难看出引入一堆同义词究竟能有何所得。

These are the reasons why we have chosen to retain—and revise—Strachey's translations. This does not imply that they are perfect translations; there are no such things. In the words of a famous English psychoanalyst, these are merely 'good enough' translations. But they are also the culmination of decades of effort, not only by Strachey but by two whole generations of analysts, working closely with Freud himself to forge a generally agreed-upon English equivalent of the language he invented to describe a newly discovered but ultimately 'unknowable' part of nature. To abandon this vocabulary now would be foolhardy.

以上这些，便是我们何以选择保留——并修订——斯特雷奇译文的原因。这并不意味着它们是完美的译文；世上并无完美的译文。用一位著名英国精神分析家的话说，这些不过是"够好的"[good enough] 译文。但它们同时也是数十年努力的结晶——这努力不仅来自斯特雷奇，也来自整整两代分析家；他们与弗洛伊德本人密切合作，为他为描述自然中一个虽新近发现、终究"不可知"的部分而发明的语言，锻造出一种大体公认的英语对应语。如今若抛弃这套词汇，则是不智的。

There is little sense in replacing Strachey's authoritative and stylistically excellent (though imperfect) translations with others which, at best, will be imperfect in new and different ways. The preferable approach must be to revise Strachey's translations, and supplement them with new editorial annotations aimed at compensating for the imperfections which are inherent in all translation.

以另一些充其量只是以新的、不同的方式不完美的译文，来取代斯特雷奇那些权威而文笔卓越（尽管并不完美）的译文，并无多少道理。可取的做法，必然是修订斯特雷奇的译文，并以新的编者注释加以补充，以弥补一切翻译所固有的不完美之处。

The first and most important such annotations take the form of lengthy commentaries on the rendition of individual technical terms that have proved controversial. (These commentaries were compiled with the close assistance of Riccardo Steiner.) At the first appearance of such technical terms in each Freud text the original German word is given in square brackets and a superscript upper-case letter ᵀ indicates that the term is discussed in the 24th volume. Because the terms in the text are sometimes derivatives of the technical terms as they appear in Volume 24, the root word is given in the margin.¹ There (in RSE, *24, 55–99) the commentaries appear in alphabetical order, under the heading 'Notes on the Translation of Some Technical Terms'. The general plan of these commentaries is to acquaint the reader with the range of criticisms of Strachey's choices, the history behind those choices, the alternative English translations that have been proposed, and the arguments and counterarguments that have been mounted (not infrequently by Strachey himself). Little attempt is made in these commentaries to persuade the reader in one direction or another; the intention is mainly to inform. Although the commentaries focus on the published secondary literature, they also draw upon Strachey's unpublished notes and correspondences, mainly with Anna Freud and K. R. Eissler (both of whom wrote to him regularly about the SE*), but also with Jones and Alix Strachey, and also upon an unpublished report by Ilse Grubrich-Simitis prepared specifically for this edition.²

这类注释中最重要的一类，采取了对若干经证明存在争议的个别术语之译法作长篇评注的形式。（这些评注是在里卡多·施泰纳的密切协助下编纂的。）在每篇弗洛伊德文本中此类术语首次出现处，原文德语词置于方括号中，并以上标大写字母 ᵀ 表示该术语在第 24 卷中有所讨论。由于文本中的这些术语有时是第 24 卷中所示术语的派生形式，故在页边注出词根。¹ 在那里（RSE 第 24 卷，第 55–99 页），诸评注按字母顺序排列，冠以"若干技术术语翻译注"之标题。这些评注的总的构想，是让读者了解对斯特雷奇诸选择的种种批评、这些选择背后的历史、已提出的各种替代英译，以及所提出的各种论点与反驳（这些反驳常出自斯特雷奇本人）。这些评注几乎无意将读者引向某一方向；其宗旨主要在于提供信息。虽然评注着眼于已发表的二手文献，但也取材于斯特雷奇未刊的笔记与通信——主要是与安娜·弗洛伊德和 K. R. 艾斯勒的通信（二人常就《标准版》写信给他），但也有与琼斯和阿利克斯·斯特雷奇的通信；此外还取材于伊尔泽·格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯专为本版撰写的一份未刊报告。²

A second set of annotations, which are similarly identified by superscript references in the text to the editorial notes in Volume 24, concern controversial translations of particular words or phrases as opposed to general technical terms occurring throughout Freud's writings. Instances of this type commonly concern titles of works and noteworthy sentences (e.g. 'where id was, there ego shall be') whose translation has attracted comment. These annotations are on the whole considerably shorter than those concerning the technical terms, and they are correspondingly limited in scope. Most of them simply record the published criticisms and the proposed alternatives. They are distinguished from the former category of annotations by the use in the Freud texts of a superscript lower-case ᵗ instead of the upper-case ᵀ used for the more general technical terms. This second set of annotations appears on pp. 105–33 of Volume 24, in chronological order, after the 'Notes on the Translation of Some Technical Terms', under the heading 'Annotations to Individual Translations'.

第二类注释，同样通过文本中指向第 24 卷编者注的上标参照来标示，涉及的是对各别词语或短语（而非贯穿弗洛伊德著作的一般技术术语）的争议性译法。这类情形通常涉及作品的标题以及引人注目的句子（如"本我所在之处，自我应运而生"[where id was, there ego shall be]），其译法引起了议论。这类注释总体上比关于技术术语的注释短得多，其范围也相应有限。其中大多数只是记录已发表的批评与所提议的替代方案。它们与前一类注释的区别，在于弗洛伊德文本中使用上标小写字母 ᵗ，而非用于更一般技术术语的大写字母 ᵀ。这第二类注释见于第 24 卷第 105–33 页，按编年顺序排列，位于《若干技术术语翻译注》之后，冠以"个别译法注解"[Annotations to Individual Translations] 之标题。

In a few places, usually where Strachey himself had done so, further commentaries (of both the above types) are provided in the editorial introductions and footnotes scattered throughout the twenty-three volumes. These are indexed in Volume 24 (pp. 601–6) under the heading 'Notes on Terms and their Uses'. Hopefully this mixture of methods strikes an adequate balance and informs the interested reader without detracting excessively from the original Freud texts.

在少数地方（通常是斯特雷奇本人已如此处理之处），进一步的评注（上述两类皆有）置于散布于二十三卷中的编者导言与脚注里。这些评注在第 24 卷（第 601–6 页）以"术语及其用法注"[Notes on Terms and their Uses] 之标题编入索引。但愿这种多种方法并用之举能取得适当的平衡，既为有兴趣的读者提供信息，又不至于过多地减损弗洛伊德原文。

The annotations discussed so far concern controversial translations as opposed to erroneous ones. In those few places where Strachey's translations are unequivocally incorrect (e.g. where he misread a word or omitted a clause), or where the typographer slipped, my approach has been simply to correct the error. Except in the cases that were recorded already in the 'Addenda and Corrigenda' in SE, *24*, I have identified all the significant instances of this type in additional editorial footnotes.

迄今为止所讨论的注释，涉及的是争议性译法，而非错误的译法。在少数几处斯特雷奇的译文显然有误者（例如他误读了一个词或漏掉了一个分句），或排字者出了差错者，我的做法就是简单地纠正错误。除那些已记录于《标准版》第 24 卷《补遗与勘误》中的情形之外，我已在额外的编者脚注中辨识出此类的所有重大实例。

Needless to say, a sharp boundary between the controversial and the erroneous is not easily drawn. I have endeavoured to err on the side of caution, and treated as controversial some translations that others might consider frankly wrong. A particularly difficult borderline case was the translation of the German word 'Trieb', which Strachey translated as 'instinct' but I have translated as 'drive' (as is now conventional in Anglo-American psychoanalysis). The reasoning behind my decision to treat Strachey's translation of Trieb as an error is spelt out in the relevant technical note (RSE, *24*, 8–8). The fact that this instance is both corrected in the text and discussed in the end notes recognizes that it is by no means an unequivocal error.

毋庸赘言，争议与非争议、错误与正确之间的分明界线并不易划定。我力求宁可失之审慎，把某些他人或许直认有误的译法，也作为争议性译法来处理。一个格外困难的临界个例，是德语词 Trieb 的译法——斯特雷奇译作"instinct"，而我译作"drive"（此译名如今在英美精神分析中已属惯例）。我之所以将斯特雷奇对 Trieb 的译法视为错误，其理由已在相关技术注中详述（RSE 第 24 卷，第 8 页及以下）。这一例既在正文中予以更正，又在尾注中加以讨论，正表明它绝非一个明确无误的错误。

One further general change has been made in the revised translations. I have suppressed the use of hyphens, especially in such antiquated instances as 'psycho-analysis' and 'to-day'. Strachey would certainly have approved of this decision (see p. xix above). A list of the most common examples of this type is provided in the 'Note on the Revised Translation', which appears near the front of each volume (e.g. p. xxxiii below).

修订译文中还作了一项进一步的普遍改动：我已取消连字符的使用，尤其是在"psycho-analysis"与"to-day"这类陈旧写法中。斯特雷奇想必会赞同这一决定（见上文第 xix 页）。这类最常见例子的清单，见于《修订译文说明》[Note on the Revised Translation]，该文在各卷卷首附近（例如下文第 xxxiii 页）。

The fact that Strachey's translations have been retained in this revised edition does not mean that it includes no new translations. It has been necessary to include a surprisingly large amount of new Freud material. Fifty-six items not included in the Standard Edition appear in this revision, in their appropriate chronological positions (for a complete listing, see the 'List of New Freud Material', RSE, *24*, 19). Most of these items are very minor reviews, forewords, open letters and the like, but some of them are of considerable value. Foremost among these is the draft of one of Freud's famous missing metapsychological papers, 'Overview of the Transference Neuroses' (1985a [1915]), which now appears in Volume 14.¹

本修订版保留了斯特雷奇的译文，但这并不意味着它不含任何新译文。它有必要收入数量惊人的弗洛伊德新材料的译文。有五十六项《标准版》未收的篇目出现在本次修订中，各依其适当的编年位置排列（完整清单见《弗洛伊德新材料清单》[List of New Freud Material]，RSE 第 24 卷，第 19 页）。其中多数是极次要的书评、前言、公开信之类，但有若干颇具价值。其中最重要的，是弗洛伊德一篇著名的佚失元心理学论文的草稿——《移情神经症概述》[Overview of the Transference Neuroses]（1985a［1915］），现已收入第 14 卷。¹

Also of great interest—in no particular order—are the Four Documents in the Case of 'Nina R.' (1978a [1891–94]), the 'Mechanism of Obsessional Ideas and Phobias' (1895b), the 'Characteristics of My Own Dreams (Typical Dreams)' (1985f [after 1922]), the opening passages of 'Some Points of Agreement Between the Mental Lives of Savages and Neurotics', which formed the first essay in Totem and Taboo (1912), the Review of Löwenfeld's Psychical Compulsive Phenomena (1904f), the Addendum to 'Psychical (or Mental) Treatment' (c. 1918/19), one of the Two Letters to Einstein (1949d [1929]), the Contributions to Thomas Woodrow Wilson (c. 1931–32), the Letter on Homosexuality (1951a [1935]) and a censored passage from Freud's 1927 postscript to The Question of Lay Analysis (1926e). In preparing the translations of this new material, I have endeavoured to retain stylistic and terminological consistency with Strachey's translations, as revised, even with regard to the tendency (denied in his preface above) to sometimes 'improve' on Freud's original texts.

同样引人入胜的——不分先后——有"尼娜·R."案的四份文件[Four Documents in the Case of 'Nina R.']（1978a［1891–94］）、《强迫观念与恐惧症的机制》[Mechanism of Obsessional Ideas and Phobias]（1895b）、《我本人梦的特点（典型梦）》[Characteristics of My Own Dreams (Typical Dreams)]（1985f［1922 之后］）、《野蛮人与神经症患者心理生活之间若干契合点》[Some Points of Agreement Between the Mental Lives of Savages and Neurotics] 的开篇段落（该文构成《图腾与禁忌》的首篇论文，1912）、《评勒文费尔德〈心理强迫现象〉》[Review of Löwenfeld's Psychical Compulsive Phenomena]（1904f）、《"心理的（或精神的）治疗"补遗》[Addendum to 'Psychical (or Mental) Treatment']（约 1918/19）、《致爱因斯坦的两封信》之一（1949d［1929］）、《托马斯·伍德罗·威尔逊》[Thomas Woodrow Wilson] 撰稿（约 1931–32）、《论同性恋》书信[Letter on Homosexuality]（1951a［1935］），以及弗洛伊德 1927 年为《业余分析问题》[The Question of Lay Analysis]（1926e）所写后记中一段被删节的文字。在准备这些新材料的译文时，我力求与经修订的斯特雷奇译文在文体与术语上保持一致——即便对于那种（他上文序言中所否认的）有时"改进"弗洛伊德原文的倾向，亦复如此。

However, at the time of writing, I am some way towards completing the companion standard edition of The Complete Neuroscientific Works of Sigmund Freud (NSW, in four volumes), to complement the RSE. This will finally make available to the English-speaking world Freud's complete works. Or rather, his complete published works; for the scope of this edition, like the previous one, also does not extend to Freud's very voluminous correspondences and the like which were not published during his lifetime. As with the SE, only a few letters and unpublished manuscripts dealing with matters of particular importance found their way into this edition. Since the publication of the SE, however, many of Freud's most significant correspondences have become available in print, in good scholarly editions. These are listed in the revised 'Freud Bibliography' in Volume 24.

然而，在撰写本文时，我在完成《西格蒙德·弗洛伊德神经科学著作全集》[The Complete Neuroscientific Works of Sigmund Freud]（NSW，四卷）这一配套标准版的道路上已颇有进展——它将补足 RSE。这将最终使英语世界得以见到弗洛伊德的全部著作。或者更确切地说，是其全部已发表著作；因为本版的范围，一如前版，同样不扩及弗洛伊德卷帙浩繁的通信等在生前未刊之物。与《标准版》一样，只有少数涉及特别重要问题的书信与未刊手稿得以进入本版。然而，自《标准版》出版以来，弗洛伊德许多最重要的通信已在精良的学术版本中以印刷形式问世。这些均列于第 24 卷经修订的《弗洛伊德书目》[Freud Bibliography] 中。

The 'Freud Bibliography' in this edition is, in fact, very heavily revised and expanded. It may seem absurd to note that Freud's list of publications has continued to grow, by hundreds of titles, through the decades since his death. But continue to grow it has—mainly due to the ongoing publication of his letters, but also because of occasional discoveries of previously unknown works, which in the case of Freud's pre-analytic publications resulted in the addition of more than three hundred newly discovered titles. This finally brings our official English bibliography of Freud's works into line with the hitherto far more authoritative German one (Meyer-Palmedo & Fichtner, 1999). The revised 'Freud Bibliography' also rectifies, on the basis of new findings, the chronological order of some of the items (resulting in physical relocations in this edition). In this regard, see the 'List of Relocated Freud Material in the Revised Edition' in Volume 24 (p. 23).

本版中的《弗洛伊德书目》实际上经过了大力的修订与扩充。指出弗洛伊德的著述清单在他逝世后的数十年间持续增长、新增数以百计的篇目，或许显得荒诞。但它确实一直在增长——主要由于他的书信持续刊行，也由于时常有此前未知作品的发现；就弗洛伊德的前分析时期出版物而言，这导致新增了三百余篇新发现的篇目。这终于使我们官方的弗洛伊德著作英文书目，与迄今远更具权威性的德文书目（迈尔-帕尔梅多与菲希特纳，1999）趋于一致。经修订的《弗洛伊德书目》还依据新的发现，厘正了某些篇目的编年次序（从而在本版中造成了一些实际的位移）。关于这一点，参见第 24 卷（第 23 页）的《修订版中弗洛伊德材料位移清单》[List of Relocated Freud Material in the Revised Edition]。

The lists of German editions and English translations that preceded Strachey's SE versions, mainly at the start of the Editor's Introductions and Notes, have also been updated for this edition. However, Strachey's policy of including only the major scholarly editions has been continued; mere reprintings are not systematically recorded.

斯特雷奇《标准版》各篇译文之前所列的德文版本与英译清单（主要位于编者导言与题注的开头），也已为本版更新。不过，斯特雷奇只收录主要学术版本的做法得以延续；单纯的重印本不予系统记录。

The Editor's Introductions and Notes just mentioned (and the footnotes, too) have also been thoroughly updated, as have the editorial Appendices. A great amount of Freud scholarship has accumulated in the decades since Strachey's death. In revising this important aspect of the editorial apparatus, I have leant especially heavily on Ilse Grubrich-Simitis's studies of the surviving Freud manuscripts (Grubrich-Simitis, 1993; trans., 1996) and on her other research and editorial work on Freud's writings. Her research has provided invaluable glimpses into the dynamics of Freud's actual process of writing. The process seems to have generally progressed through three stages: from rough notes, to manuscript drafts, to fair copy ready for the printers. Grubrich-Simitis's discovery of alternate, earlier versions of some important papers, such as Beyond the Pleasure Principle and Moses and Monotheism, has also been highly revealing. I have of course made abundant use of the work of other scholars, too, but I have assiduously resisted revisions based on supposition rather than fact. I have similarly tried not to burden the reader with my own opinions, although it was essential to opine on the merits of Strachey's translations, and I have also added some remarks on neuroscientific aspects of the 'Project for a Scientific Psychology' (1950a [1895]) and The Interpretation of Dreams (1900a). I decided to make more substantial editorial revisions in these two instances for particular reasons. In the case of the 'Project', I understood some neuroscientific aspects of this iconic text (which is highly obscure in places) differently from my predecessor, who lacked specialist knowledge of the subject. In the case of The Interpretation of Dreams, substantial advances in neuroscientific knowledge which trench on the fundamental hypotheses of the book began to emerge almost immediately after the relevant volumes of the SE appeared in print. It seems likely that Strachey himself would have mentioned such paradigm-shifting developments had he been aware of them; I therefore make no apology for doing so, notwithstanding my personal involvement in the scientific developments at issue.

方才提到的编者导言与题注（以及脚注），也经过了彻底的更新，编辑性附录亦然。斯特雷奇逝世后的数十年间，积累了大量弗洛伊德学术成果。在修订编辑体例的这一重要方面时，我尤其倚重于伊尔泽·格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯对现存弗洛伊德手稿的研究（格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯，1993；英译，1996），以及她在弗洛伊德著作上的其他研究与编辑工作。她的研究为窥探弗洛伊德写作过程的实际动态，提供了极为珍贵的视角。这一过程似大体历经三个阶段：从粗略的笔记，到手稿草样，再到可供付印的誊清稿。格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯对若干重要论文（如《超越快乐原则》[Beyond the Pleasure Principle] 与《摩西与一神教》[Moses and Monotheism]）更早的异文之发现，也极具启发性。我当然也大量利用了其他学者的成果，但我孜孜以求地抵制以推测而非事实为依据的修订。同样，我力求不以己见加重读者负担，尽管对斯特雷奇译文的优劣作出评价是必要的；此外，我还就《科学心理学大纲》[Project for a Scientific Psychology]（1950a［1895］）与《梦的解析》[The Interpretation of Dreams]（1900a）的神经科学层面增添了一些评述。我决定在这两处作出较为实质性的编辑修订，各有其特殊理由。就《大纲》而言，我对这部标志性文本（某些地方极为晦涩）的某些神经科学层面的理解，与前人不同——他缺乏该课题的专门知识。就《梦的解析》而言，几乎就在《标准版》相关各卷付印之后不久，神经科学知识便开始出现重大进展，而这些进展与该书的基本假设息息相关。倘若斯特雷奇本人知晓这些，他很可能会提及这类范式转换式的发展；因此，我虽在有关的科学进展中亲身参与，却并不为此致歉。

A particularly difficult policy decision concerned the question as to whether (and, if so, how) all these additions, revisions and corrections should be identified. They are subtle and pervasive. Repeatedly interrupting the editorial Introductions, Notes and footnotes to distinguish my contributions from Strachey's (by means of curly brackets or the like) would be excessively distracting for the reader and highly impracticable. This applies especially to the revised translations of the Freud texts, which have their own integrity; if the innumerable little changes I made on almost every page were footnoted, the notes would routinely occupy more than half the page. Therefore, my initial intention was only to describe the general policies I followed, and not to identify my interventions and contributions individually. I reasoned that readers who were interested in such matters could always compare the SE and RSE versions manually. Moreover, I was aware that hypertext functionality could be incorporated in the electronic edition of the RSE, which would enable readers to explore the differences between the two editions at the click of a button. After long deliberations, however, including the canvasing of opinion among representative samples of readers, the policy we have decided upon is to lightly underscore everything that distinguishes this revised edition from the previous one, in the print format too.

一项格外艰难的方针决策，关乎这样一个问题：所有这些增补、修订与更正，是否（以及若是，如何）应加以标示。它们既细微又无处不在。若一再打断编者导言、题注与脚注，以（花括号之类的方式）把本人的贡献与斯特雷奇的区别开来，将使读者极感分心，且极不切实际。这尤其适用于弗洛伊德文本的修订译文——它们自有其完整性；倘若我几乎每一页所作的无数细微改动都加脚注，注释将惯常占据半页以上。因此，我最初的本意，只是描述我所遵循的一般方针，而不逐一标示我的介入与贡献。我当时的想法是，对此类事宜有兴趣的读者，尽可自行比对《标准版》与 RSE 两个版本。此外，我也意识到，超文本功能可纳入 RSE 的电子版，使读者得以轻点按钮即可探究两版之间的差异。然而，经过长时间的斟酌——包括征询具代表性的读者样本的意见——我们所决定的方针是：在印刷版中，也以轻度下划线标出一切使本修订版有别于前版之处。

Everything here means everything other than the modernized spellings of words that recur extremely frequently (e.g. 'psycho-analysis'), the added marginalia (e.g. the German root words, which obviously did not appear in the SE), and purely technical changes like pagination in cross-references, fonts and point-sizes.¹ This editorial decision—which was not made lightly—has several disadvantages, such as the resultant repetitive underscoring of the word 'drive' and its cognates, and the highlighting of what many will consider trivial differences between the SE and RSE. However, distinguishing between the significant and the trivial is, unfortunately, both context-dependent and subjective. For example, in some places Strachey translated 'treibende Kraft' as 'driving force' and in other places as 'motive force'. Since 'motives' have ideational content but 'drives' do not, my decision to change 'motive' to 'driving' is significant in some contexts but not in others. An assessment in each case is probably best left to the reader. The exact policy I have followed here is detailed in the 'Note on the Revised Translation', which appears at the front of each volume. The overriding intention behind this policy was to be as transparent as possible, which required an approach that is (1) simple and (2) consistently applied. I apologize in advance to readers for the distraction, and I reiterate that all the alternative policies that we considered had considerable drawbacks of their own. I have, however, asked the publishers to drop the underscoring in future printings of the RSE, once it has become established as the canonical edition and interest recedes in the matter of how it differs from the old one.

此处的一切，指的是除以下之外的一切：极为频繁出现的词语的现代化拼写（如"psycho-analysis"）、增添的页边注（如德语词根，这些显然未见于《标准版》），以及诸如交叉参照中页码、字体与字号等纯技术性改动。¹ 这一编辑决定——并非轻率作出——有若干缺点，例如由此导致"drive"一词及其同源词的重复下划线，以及凸显出许多人会视为《标准版》与 RSE 之间琐屑的差异。然而，区分重要与琐屑，不幸既取决于语境，又主观。例如，斯特雷奇在某些地方把 'treibende Kraft' 译作"driving force"，在另一些地方则译作"motive force"。由于"motive（动机）"含观念性内容，而"drive（驱力）"则不含，我把"motive"改为"driving"的决定，在某些语境中意义重大，在另一些语境中则不然。每一处究竟如何，或许最好留给读者自行判断。我在这一点上所遵循的确切方针，详载于各卷卷首的《修订译文说明》。这一方针背后的首要意图，是尽可能做到透明，而这要求一种（1）简单、（2）一贯应用的做法。我预先为这种分心向读者致歉，并重申我们所考虑过的所有替代方针，本身也都有相当大的缺点。不过，我已请出版者在未来的印次中取消下划线——RSE 一旦确立为经典版本、人们对其与旧版有何不同的兴趣消退，届时即可取消。

Returning, now, to the distinction between supposition and fact, a case in point is that I have elected to retain references to the original (1950a [1887–1902]) edition of the Freud-Fliess letters, rather than update them with references to the subsequent (1985c [1887–1904]) unabridged version. Although the later version of Freud's side of this highly important correspondence is more complete, both the editorial apparatus and the translation are certainly not improvements on the earlier one (especially as revised by Strachey for the SE). Because Freud's previously unpublished letters to Fliess did not require inclusion in the RSE, and to avoid any implicit endorsement of Jeffrey Masson's tendentious editorial apparatus in the unabridged edition, I have continued to link the RSE references to the first edition. (It should be said that the German unabridged edition, prepared by Michael Schröter (see Freud, 1985c), exceeds the English one; Masson's editorial apparatus is toned down somewhat and Ernst Kris's valuable editorial apparatus is retained from the first edition (Freud, 1950a).)

现在回到假设与事实之别。一个恰切的例子是：我选择保留对弗洛伊德-弗利斯书信原版（1950a［1887–1902］）的参照，而不将其更新为对后来全本（1985c［1887–1904］）的参照。尽管这一极其重要的通信中弗洛伊德一方的后来版本更为完整，但其编辑体例与译文，都绝谈不上胜于较早的版本（尤其是经斯特雷奇为《标准版》修订后的版本）。由于弗洛伊德此前未刊的致弗利斯书信无须收入 RSE，且为避免对杰弗里·马森[Jeffrey Masson] 在全本中带有倾向性的编辑体例作任何默许，我仍将 RSE 的参照与初版相联。（应当说明，由米夏埃尔·施勒特[Michael Schröter] 编订的德文全本［见弗洛伊德，1985c］优于英文全本；马森的编辑体例有所淡化，而恩斯特·克里斯极有价值的编辑体例则从初版［弗洛伊德，1950a］保留了下来。）

Similar considerations led to an editorial decision to continue Strachey's policy of not revealing the identities of Freud's patients. At the risk of appearing priggish, it seemed important in an authorized edition of Freud's works to uphold the absolute ethical principle of confidentiality. To do otherwise would be to convey to present and future analysands that a limit to this principle is the degree of historical interest that might attach someday to their analyst, or to themselves. Psychoanalysis under such conditions becomes impossible. Therefore, although I have made use of the research findings of scholars who did publish the identities of Freud's patients, which cannot be undone, I have not reproduced their names here. I hope that this decision will be understood.

类似的考虑，促成了另一项编辑决定：延续斯特雷奇不透露弗洛伊德病人身份的做法。纵有显得迂执之虞，在一部经授权的弗洛伊德著作版本中，维护保密这一绝对伦理原则，仍显得十分重要。若不然，便等于向现在和未来的被分析者传达：这一原则有一个限度，即他们的分析师或他们本人有朝一日可能具有的历史意义之大小。在这样的条件下，精神分析将无从进行。因此，尽管我利用了那些确曾发表弗洛伊德病人身份的学者的研究成果（既已发表，便无法挽回），我在此并未复述他们的姓名。希望这一决定能得到理解。

I hope that readers will understand also that no attempt has been made to improve the language Freud used to denote, for example, gay men and women as 'inverts', some non-European people as 'primitive' and 'savage', and the like. Although Freud was more enlightened than his contemporaries in particular respects, in others he was no less prejudiced than most bourgeois Europeans of his time, on matters such as race, class and gender. We acknowledge that both this language and its underlying belief systems are offensive, but the translator's task is to represent accurately what Freud wrote.

我也希望读者理解：本书并未试图去"改进"弗洛伊德用来指称某些人群的语言，例如把男同性恋者与女同性恋者称为"性倒错者"[inverts]，把某些非欧民族称为"原始的"[primitive] 与"野蛮的"[savage]，等等。尽管弗洛伊德在某些方面比同时代人更为开明，但在另一些方面，在种族、阶级与性别等议题上，他的偏见并不亚于当时大多数资产阶级欧洲人。我们承认，这类语言及其背后的信念体系是冒犯性的，但译者的任务，是如实呈现弗洛伊德所写的内容。

The last major difference between the RSE and the SE is the fact that the indexing has been thoroughly revised. This applies especially to the 'Index of Subjects' and 'Index of Names' in the 24th volume, and to the indexes in the individual volumes, which received much criticism over the years. Most of the more specialized indexes and listings in SE, *24, which were sandwiched between the old Bibliography and Author Index and the original General Subject Index, now seem superfluous. These have either been deleted or incorporated into the new 'Index of Subjects'. Readers who wish to make use of the other SE* indexes and listings can of course still readily do so. The 'Addenda and Corrigenda' in the old 24th volume, as already mentioned, have also been incorporated into the revised texts themselves. These changes made space in that volume for the lengthy annotations to the revised translations and the greatly expanded 'Freud Bibliography'. I have also expanded the 'Index of Editorial Annotations', which serves as a sort of super-index.

RSE 与 SE 之间最后一项重大差异，是索引经过了彻底修订。这尤其适用于第 24 卷中的"主题索引"[Index of Subjects] 与"人名索引"[Index of Names]，以及各卷的索引——后者多年来饱受批评。SE 第 24 卷中大多数较为专门的索引与列表，原本夹在旧书目与作者索引和原通用主题索引之间，如今似已多余。它们或已删除，或被并入新的"主题索引"。希望利用 SE 其他索引与列表的读者，当然仍可方便地使用。旧第 24 卷中的《补遗与勘误》，如前所述，也已并入经修订的文本本身。这些改动为第 24 卷腾出了空间，用以容纳针对修订译文的长篇注释，以及大为扩充的《弗洛伊德书目》。我还扩充了《编者注释索引》[Index of Editorial Annotations]，它起着某种超级索引的作用。

With all of these changes (including to the page format), it goes without saying that the pagination of the RSE does not match that of the SE. However, for ease of cross-referencing, not only to the original SE indexes but also—most importantly—to the vast secondary literature, the old pagination is recorded in square brackets in the margins of this edition.

有了这一切改动（包括页面版式的改动），毋庸赘言，RSE 的页码与 SE 的页码并不一致。然而，为了便于交叉参照——不仅是对原 SE 各索引的参照，而且（最重要的）是对浩繁二手文献的参照——本版在页边以方括号记录了旧页码。

From all of the above, it will be appreciated that the RSE is nothing more than its title implies: a Revised version of James Strachey's Standard Edition. I have accordingly applied Strachey's general editorial policies wherever possible; but it is important to acknowledge the limitations of this approach. No attempt has been made, for example, to systematically cross-reference the innumerable comments regarding his published works that Freud made in his vast correspondences. This revised edition aims only to update the existing SE, and it is accordingly an edition of equivalent scholarly level. It is not the ultimate historical-critical edition that Freud's work certainly deserves, and it makes no claims to being such (cf. Grubrich-Simitis, 1993, Part III; trans., 1996). Like the original SE, which Strachey described as 'an amateur production . . . the work of a few individuals usually engaged in other occupations', the RSE has been more a labour of love than a properly resourced academic (or even commercial) venture. I fear, as a result, that it may give rise to as many new questions as it has answered old ones. For this, despite the huge contributions made by the several scholars already mentioned, I, as the General Editor of this revision, must bear full responsibility.

从以上所述，当可理解：RSE 不过是其标题所示而已——詹姆斯·斯特雷奇《标准版》的一个修订本。我因而尽可能沿用了斯特雷奇的一般编辑方针；但承认这一做法的局限，实为重要。例如，本书并未试图系统地为弗洛伊德在其浩繁通信中关于自己已发表作品的无数评论建立交叉参照。本修订版只求更新现有的 SE，因而是一部学术水准与之相当的版本。它并不是弗洛伊德著作理所应得的那种终极的历史校勘版，也无意自称为此（参格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯，1993，第三部分；英译，1996）。一如原《标准版》——斯特雷奇曾称之为"一项业余之作……出自几个通常另有本职的人之手"——RSE 与其说是一项资源充足之学术（乃至商业）事业，不如说是一份出于热爱的劳作。我担心，其结果或许会引出与它解答的旧问题同样多的新问题。为此，尽管上述诸位学者作出了巨大贡献，作为本次修订的总编辑，我仍须负全部责任。

Acknowledgements

It is unnecessary to reiterate the acknowledgement of my enormous debt to the scholars already mentioned, but I do want to repeat that the special few named at the outset are not the only ones to whom I am indebted. This applies particularly to the many bits of helpful information and advice that I received over the years, frequently unsolicited, from students of Freud, and especially from those engaged in tasks similar to my own—such as from the editors of the French and Dutch equivalents of this edition. With them I share the precious insight that Freud's genius reveals itself to the editor and translator in ways that cannot easily be appreciated by others.

无须重申我对上述诸位学者所负的巨大人情，但我确愿再次强调：开篇所举的少数几位，并非我唯一受惠之人。这尤其适用于多年来我从弗洛伊德研究者那里所获得的许多有益的信息与建议——它们常是不请自来，尤其来自从事与我类似工作者，如本版法语本与荷兰语本的编者。我与他们共享一种珍贵的洞见：弗洛伊德的天才，是以他人不易领会的方式，向编者和译者显现自身的。

The publishers of Freud's collected works in German—S. Fischer Verlag and Fischer Taschenbuch Verlag—have been most gracious in permitting us to use extensive editorial material from the Gesammelte Werke Nachtragsband, Studienausgabe, Freud-Bibliographie and other, individual paperback editions, as well as invaluable material from Ilse Grubrich-Simitis's Zurück zu Freuds Texten. This continues a valuable tradition of collaboration between Freud's authorized German and English publishers that has now extended over five decades.

弗洛伊德德文著作集的出版者——S. 费舍尔出版社与菲舍尔袖珍书出版社[Fischer Taschenbuch Verlag]——极为慷慨地允许我们使用《全集·补编卷》[Gesammelte Werke Nachtragsband]、《研究版》[Studienausgabe]、《弗洛伊德书目》[Freud-Bibliographie] 及其他单行袖珍本中的大量编辑材料，以及伊尔泽·格鲁布里希-西米蒂斯《回归弗洛伊德文本》[Zurück zu Freuds Texten] 中的珍贵材料。这延续了弗洛伊德经授权的德英出版者之间一项宝贵的合作传统，该传统至今已逾五十年。

In addition to my colleagues at the Institute of Psychoanalysis already mentioned, I must acknowledge the support (and sometimes the 'unreasonable faith') of successive Boards and Publications Committees of the Institute—and in particular of its General Managers during the period of my work, Nick Hall, Jon Levett and Caroline Langley. It must be said that some institutional actions were less reasonable than others, so I would particularly like to thank the Board which saw the project through to its proper end under the Presidency of Rosine Perelberg. It was during her tenure that Rowman & Littlefield were selected to produce this high-quality edition, stewarded by the commendable Oliver Gadsby. Also richly deserving of acknowledgement is Elizabeth Coates Thummel, who chaired the ad hoc committee that brought this project to its happy conclusion. Henry Brown, an expert in copyright matters, now retired, also deserves special mention: he guided us through many tricky legal waters. (I have said that the main reason for the delay in completing this revision was the gradual expansion of the project, but that was not the only reason.) The final push to complete the project, when all else had failed, succeeded only because the Institute enlisted the support of a remarkably able copy editor, Mary Tobin. This acknowledgement is not merely conventional. Our debt to her is huge; and she is surely the best copy editor I have ever had the pleasure of working with. People not involved in this line of work cannot imagine the effort it entails. The Institute also engaged an indexing assistant, Natasha Jooste, and a supplementary copy editor with knowledge of the German language, Gyuri Fodor. Not least, but last as always, I must thank my assistants—both personal and research—especially the indomitable Paula Barkay and Eleni Pantelis, but also some others, once again too numerous and amorphously delimited to simply list.

除了已提到的我在精神分析研究所的同人之外，我必须致谢研究所历届理事会与出版委员会的支持（以及有时是"不近情理的信念"）——尤其要感谢在我工作期间历任总经理尼克·霍尔[Nick Hall]、乔恩·莱维特[Jon Levett] 与卡罗琳·兰利[Caroline Langley]。应当说，某些机构行为并不都同样合理，所以我尤愿感谢在罗辛·佩雷尔伯格[Rosine Perelberg] 担任主席期间使本项目得以善始善终的那一届理事会。正是在她任内，罗曼与利特菲尔德出版社[Rowman & Littlefield] 被选定来制作这部高质量的版本，由值得称道的奥利弗·加兹比[Oliver Gadsby] 主持其事。同样极应致谢的是伊丽莎白·科茨·图梅尔[Elizabeth Coates Thummel]，她主持了使本项目圆满收官的特设委员会。已退休的版权问题专家亨利·布朗[Henry Brown] 也值得特别提及：他引领我们闯过了许多棘手的法律险滩。（我已说过，完成本次修订之所以拖延，主要是因为项目范围逐渐扩大，但那并非唯一原因。）在万事俱废之际，完成本项目的最后一搏之所以成功，全因研究所争取到一位极为得力的文字编辑玛丽·托宾[Mary Tobin] 的助力。这一致谢并非客套。我们对她亏欠甚巨；她无疑是我有幸共事过的最出色的文字编辑。未涉此道者，无法想象这需要付出多大的努力。研究所还聘请了索引助理娜塔莎·朱斯特[Natasha Jooste]，以及一位通晓德语的辅助文字编辑久里·福多尔[Gyuri Fodor]。最后但一如既往并非最不重要的，我必须感谢我的助手们——无论是私人事务还是研究工作——尤其是那位不屈不挠的葆拉·巴尔凯[Paula Barkay] 与埃莱妮·潘特利斯[Eleni Pantelis]，此外还有一些其他人，同样人数众多、界限模糊，难以简单一一列举。

MARK SOLMS
Franschhoek, 2024

马克·索尔姆斯[Mark Solms]
弗朗斯胡克，2024 年
