GENERAL PREFACE

RSE 第 1 卷 · 早期论文与译序 · p. xv–xxii · 42 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

The Scope of the Standard Edition

The ground covered by this edition is shown by its title—The Complete Psychological Works of Sigmund Freud; but it is right that I should begin by indicating its contents more explicitly. My aim has been to include in it the whole of Freud's published psychological writings—that is, both the psychoanalytic and the pre-psychoanalytic. It does not include Freud's numerous publications on the physical sciences during the first fifteen years or so of his productive activity.² I have been fairly liberal in drawing the line here, for I have found a place for two or three works produced by Freud immediately after his return from Paris in 1886. These, dealing chiefly with hysteria, were written under the influence of Charcot, with scarcely a reference to mental processes; but they provide a real bridge between Freud's neurological and psychological writings.

本版所涵盖的范围，已由其标题所示——《西格蒙德·弗洛伊德心理学著作全集》[The Complete Psychological Works of Sigmund Freud]；但我理应先更明确地指明其内容。我的目标，是将弗洛伊德已发表的全部心理学著作收入其中——亦即既包括精神分析的、也包括前精神分析的著作。它不包括弗洛伊德在其创作生涯最初约十五年间关于物理科学的众多发表。² 我在划定这条界线时相当宽松，因为已为弗洛伊德 1886 年自巴黎归来后随即写就的两三篇作品留出了位置。这些作品主要论及癔症[hysteria]，写于沙可[Charcot] 的影响之下，几乎未涉及心理过程；但它们构成了弗洛伊德神经学著作与心理学著作之间的一座真正的桥梁。

The Standard Edition does not include Freud's correspondence. This is of enormous extent and only relatively small selections from it have been published hitherto. Apart from 'Open Letters' and a few others printed with Freud's assent during his lifetime, my main exception to this general rule is in the case of his correspondence with Wilhelm Fliess during the early part of his career. This is of such vital importance to an understanding of Freud's views (and not only of his early ones) that much of it could not possibly be rejected. The first volume of the edition accordingly contains the 'Project' of 1895 and the series of 'Drafts' sent by Freud to Fliess between 1892 and 1897, as well as such portions of the letters themselves as are of definite scientific interest.

《标准版》[Standard Edition] 不包括弗洛伊德的通信。其数量极其庞大，迄今只有相对很少的选段得以发表。除了"公开信"[Open Letters] 以及少数几篇经弗洛伊德生前同意付印者之外，我对这一通则的主要例外，是他早年与威廉·弗利斯[Wilhelm Fliess] 的通信。这批通信对于理解弗洛伊德的观点（而且不只是他早期的观点）具有如此重大的意义，以致其中很大一部分绝不可能被舍弃。本版第 1 卷因而收入了 1895 年的《大纲》[Project]，以及 1892 至 1897 年间弗洛伊德寄给弗利斯的一系列"草稿"[Drafts]，此外还收入了书信本身中那些确具科学意义的部分。

Nor, again, does the Standard Edition contain any reports or abstracts, published in contemporary periodicals, of the many lectures and papers given by Freud in early days at meetings of various medical societies in Vienna. The only exceptions here are the rare cases in which the report was made or revised by Freud himself.³

同样，《标准版》也不收录当时期刊上所发表的、关于弗洛伊德早年于维也纳各医学学会会议上所作众多演讲与论文的报道或摘要。此处唯一的例外，是那些由弗洛伊德本人撰写或修订报道的罕见情形。³

The Plan of the Edition

The first problem for an editor faced by a total of some two million words was to decide how best to present them to his readers. Was the material to be arranged on a classificatory or a chronological basis? The first German collected edition (the Gesammelte Schriften, issued during Freud's life) attempted a division according to subject matter; the more recent Gesammelte Werke aimed at being strictly chronological. Neither plan was satisfactory. Freud's writings would not fit comfortably into categories, and strict chronology meant interrupting close sequences of his ideas. Here, therefore, a compromise was adopted. The arrangement is in the main chronological, but I have disregarded the rule in certain cases—where, for instance, Freud wrote an addendum many years after the original work (as with the Autobiographical Study in Volume 20) or where he himself grouped together a set of papers of various dates (as with the papers on technique in Volume 12). In general, however, each volume contains all the works belonging to a specified span of years. The contents of each volume (except of course where a single long work is concerned) are grouped in three classes: first I have placed the major work (or works) belonging to the period—which gives the volume its title; next come the more important writings on a smaller scale; and lastly come the really short (and usually relatively unimportant) productions. The chronology is so far as possible determined by the date of the actual composition of the work in question. Often, however, the only certain date is that of publication. Each item is consequently headed by the date of publication in round brackets, followed by the date of composition in square brackets, where this may reasonably be held to differ from the former. Thus the two last 'metapsychological' papers in Volume 14, though published in 1917, were almost certainly written at the same time as their three predecessors, in 1915. These last two are accordingly included in the same volume as the rest, and are headed '(1916–17 [1915])'. Incidentally, each volume contains its own bibliography and index, though a complete bibliography and an index to the whole series are planned for Volume 24.²

一位编者面对约两百万字的总量，首先遇到的问题便是：如何以最佳方式将它们呈现给读者。这些材料应按分类法还是按编年法编排？第一部德文合集（《弗洛伊德文集》[Gesammelte Schriften]，弗洛伊德生前刊行）试图按题材划分；较晚出的《全集》[Gesammelte Werke] 则力求严格编年。两种方案都不尽如人意。弗洛伊德的著作难以妥帖地归入各类，而严格的编年又意味着打断其思路的紧密序列。因此，此处采用了一种折衷方案。编排大体上按编年，但在某些情形下我破例——例如，弗洛伊德在原作问世多年之后才补写附录（如第 20 卷中的《自传研究》[Autobiographical Study]），或他本人将一组写作年代不一的论文编辑在一起（如第 12 卷中论技术的诸文）。但总的来说，每一卷都收录属于某一特定年代跨度的全部作品。各卷内容（当然，单一长篇专著除外）分为三类：首先，我置入属于该时期的主要作品（一部或数部）——这构成该卷的标题；其次，是篇幅较小的较重要文章；最后，则是真正的短篇（通常也相对不重要）之作。只要可能，编年即依所论作品实际写作的日期而定。然而，通常唯一确凿的日期是出版日期。因此，每一篇目都以圆括号标出出版日期，在其后以方括号标出写作日期——凡可合理认为写作日期与之不同者皆如此。例如，第 14 卷中最后两篇"元心理学"[metapsychological] 论文，虽于 1917 年发表，却几乎可以确定与它们之前的三篇同写于 1915 年。故最后这两篇与其余各篇一并收入同一卷，并标以"（1916–17［1915］）"。附带一提，每一卷都各有其书目与索引，而全系列的总书目与总索引则计划编入第 24 卷。²

The German Sources

The translations in this edition are in general based on the last German editions published in Freud's lifetime. One of my main difficulties, however, has been the unsatisfactory nature of the German texts. The original publications, brought out under Freud's immediate supervision, are as a rule trustworthy; but, as time went on and responsibility was delegated to other hands, errors began to creep in. This even applies to the first collected edition, published in Vienna between the Wars and destroyed by the Nazis in 1938. The second collected edition, which was printed in England under the greatest difficulties during the Second War, is largely a photocopy of its predecessor, but naturally shows signs of the circumstances in which it was produced. This, however, remains the only obtainable German edition of Freud's works with any claim to completeness.¹

本版的译文，一般以弗洛伊德生前所出的最后几版德文本为依据。然而，我的主要困难之一，始终是德文文本本身不尽如人意。在弗洛伊德亲自监督下刊行的初版，通常可信；但随着时间的推移、责任委诸他人，讹误便开始渗入。这一点甚至也适用于第一部合集——它刊行于两次大战之间的维也纳，1938 年被纳粹销毁。第二部合集于第二次世界大战期间在极端困难中于英国印行，大体上是其前身的影印，但自然带有其印制时境况的痕迹。然而，这仍是唯一可获得的、稍可称完备的弗洛伊德著作德文版。¹

From 1908 onwards, Freud preserved his manuscripts; but in the case of works published in his lifetime I have not consulted them except in a few cases of doubt. Where writings have been published posthumously the position is different, and, in a few instances, especially in the case of the 'Project' (as will be seen from the Editor's Introduction to that work), the translation has been made direct from a photostat of the manuscript.

自 1908 年起，弗洛伊德保存了自己的手稿；但对于他生前发表的作品，除少数存疑之处外，我并未查阅这些手稿。至于身后发表的作品，情况则不同；在少数几例中，尤其是《大纲》（如该书的编者导言所示），译文直接译自手稿的影印件。

A serious defect in the German editions is the absence of any attempts at dealing with the very numerous changes in the text made by Freud in successive editions of some of his books. This applies in particular to The Interpretation of Dreams and the Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality, both of which were to a very considerable extent recast in their later editions. For a serious student of the development of Freud's ideas it is of great interest to have the stratification of his views laid bare. Here, accordingly, I have endeavoured to note, for the first time, the dates at which the various alterations were made and to give the earlier versions in footnotes.

德文各版的一个严重缺陷，是完全未曾设法处理弗洛伊德在其某些著作的历次再版中对文本所作的大量改动。这尤其适用于《梦的解析》[The Interpretation of Dreams] 与《性学三论》[Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality]——二者在后来的版本中都作了相当大幅度的改写。对于认真研究弗洛伊德思想发展的人来说，将其观点层层累积的层次揭示出来，具有极大的意义。因此，我在此力求首次注明各次改动所作的时间，并在脚注中给出较早的版本。

The Commentaries

It will be gathered from what has just been said that from first to last I have framed this edition with the 'serious student' in mind. The result has inevitably been a large amount of commentary, by which many readers will be irritated. Here I am inclined to quote Dr Johnson:² 'It is impossible for an expositor not to write too little for some, and too much for others. He can only judge what is necessary by his own experience; and how long soever he may deliberate, will at last explain many lines which the learned will think impossible to be mistaken, and omit many for which the ignorant will want his help. These are censures merely relative, and must be quietly endured.'

从方才所述可以看出，我自始至终都是以"认真的学生"[serious student] 为念来构筑本版的。其结果必然是大段的评注，许多读者或将为之恼怒。此处我禁不住要引约翰逊博士[Dr Johnson] 的话：²"任何注释者都不可能对某些人写得太少、而对另一些人写得太多。他只能凭自己的经验来判断何为必要；无论他斟酌多久，最终总会解释许多博学之士认为绝不可能弄错的文句，又会遗漏许多无知者亟需其帮助之处。这些指责都只是相对的，只能默默忍受。"

The commentaries in the Standard Edition are of various kinds. Firstly there are the purely textual notes to which I have referred just above. Next come elucidations of Freud's very numerous historical and local allusions and literary quotations. Freud was a striking example of a man equally at home in both of what have been called the 'two cultures'. He was not only an expert neuroanatomist and physiologist; he was also widely read in the Greek and Latin classics as well as in the literatures of his own language and in those of England, France, Italy and Spain.³ Most of his allusions may have been immediately intelligible to his contemporaries in Vienna, but are quite beyond the range of a modern English-speaking reader. Often, however, especially in The Interpretation of Dreams, these allusions play an actual part in the line of argument; their explanation could not be neglected, though it has called for considerable, and sometimes unsuccessful, research.

《标准版》中的评注种类不一。首先是方才提到的纯文本校勘注。其次是阐释弗洛伊德为数极多的历史与地方典故及文学引语。弗洛伊德是一个显著的例子：他对所谓"两种文化"[two cultures] 二者同样精通。他不仅是神经解剖学与生理学的专家，还广泛涉猎希腊与拉丁古典，以及其母语和英格兰、法国、意大利、西班牙各国文学的著作。³ 他的大多数典故，对维也纳的同时代人或许一见即懂，却远在现代英语读者的视野之外。然而，尤其在《梦的解析》中，这些典故常在论证线索中起着实实在在的作用；对它们的阐释不容忽视，尽管这需要相当多的——有时甚至是不成功的——研究。

Another class of annotations is constituted by the cross-references. These should be of special value to a student. Freud has often dealt with the same topic several times, and perhaps in different ways, at various widely separated dates. Cross-references between these occasions over the whole range of the edition should help to overcome the objection to the general chronological treatment of the material.¹

另一类注释由交叉参照[cross-references] 构成。这类注释对学生应特别有价值。弗洛伊德常常在相隔甚远的各个时期，多次论及同一主题，或许还以不同的方式论及。在全版范围内于这些不同场合之间建立交叉参照，应有助于消除对材料大体按编年处理的反对意见。¹

Lastly, and more rarely, there are notes explanatory of Freud's remarks. These, however, are usually only extended examples of the cross-references; more elaborate discussions of Freud's meaning are usually reserved to yet another category of comment.

最后，较为少见的是解释弗洛伊德言论的注释。不过，这些通常只是交叉参照的扩展示例；对弗洛伊德含义更为精细的讨论，通常留待另一类评注处理。

For, quite apart from these running explanations in the footnotes, each separate work without exception is provided with an introductory note. This varies in length according to the importance of the work. It opens in every case with a bibliography of the German text and all its English translations. (No notice is taken of translations into other languages; and no attempt has been made to give a complete list of reprints subsequent to Freud's death in 1939.) This is followed by an account of what is known of the date and circumstances of the composition and publication of the work. After this comes some indication of its topic and of its place in the main current of Freud's thought. It is here, of course, that differences will be found. In the case of a short work of slight interest, there will be only a sentence or two. In the case of a major work, there may be an introductory essay covering several pages.

因为，除了脚注中这些随文而下的解释之外，每一部独立的作品都毫无例外地配有一篇导言性的题注。其篇幅依作品的重要程度而不同。每一篇题注都以一份书目开篇，列出德文原本及其全部英译。（不涉及译为其他语言者；亦未试图给出弗洛伊德 1939 年逝世之后重印本的完整清单。）其后是对该作品写作与出版之日期及情形的已知情况的叙述。再往后则略示其主题，及其在弗洛伊德思想主潮中的位置。当然，正是在这里，各篇会有出入。若是一部无关紧要的短篇，便只有一两句话；若是一部重要著作，则可能有一篇长达数页的导论性文章。

All these various kinds of editorial intervention have been governed by a single principle. I have aimed, consistently I hope, at allowing Freud to be his own expositor. Where there are obscurities I have looked for explanations in Freud's own writings; where there seem to be contradictions I have been content with laying the fact before the reader and enabling him to form a judgment of his own. I have done my best to escape being didactic, and have avoided any claim to ex cathedra authority. But, if I have withheld my own opinions, especially on matters of theory, it will be found that I have equally withheld all later commentaries and elaborations and criticisms from any source whatever. So that, almost without exception, this edition contains no references at all to other writers, however distinguished—apart, of course, from those quoted by Freud himself. (The immense proliferation of psycho-analytic literature since his death would in any case have imposed this decision.) The student should thus be able to approach Freud's writings uninfluenced by extraneous opinions.

所有这些不同种类的编者介入，都受同一条原则的统辖。我希望自己始终力求让弗洛伊德成为他自己的阐释者。凡有晦涩之处，我都在弗洛伊德本人的著作中寻找解释；凡似乎存在矛盾之处，我则满足于把事实摆到读者面前，让他自行作出判断。我尽力避免说教，也避免作任何权威式[ex cathedra] 的断言。但如果说我保留了自己的意见（尤其是在理论问题上），那么人们会发现，我同样一概舍弃了来自任何来源的一切后来的评注、引申与批评。因此，几乎毫无例外，本版完全不提及任何其他作者，无论其多么卓著——当然，弗洛伊德本人所引者除外。（弗洛伊德逝世之后精神分析文献的浩繁增殖，无论如何也会迫使我们作出这一决定。）这样一来，学生便应能在不受外来意见影响的情形下接近弗洛伊德的著作。

It is in the matter of commentaries that I am most aware of the deficiencies of this edition, many of them irremediable. The numerous misprints and minor slips may be corrected, I hope, in Volume 24; but the faults I have in mind cannot so easily be put right. They spring in the main from the unripeness of the material. This is exemplified by what I have already mentioned—the absence of any really trustworthy German edition. But in fact, when work was starting on this edition more than fifteen years ago, the whole region was unexplored and unmapped. The publication of Ernest Jones's life of Freud had not even begun; the correspondence with Fliess and the very existence of the 'Project' were unsuspected by most people. It is true that I received assistance from many quarters,¹ especially from Ernest Jones, who kept me abreast of his discoveries as he made them. Nevertheless, the Standard Edition is a piece of pioneering work, with all the inevitable errors and blunders that that involves. I myself became better educated in Freud's ideas as time went on, and it is likely that the later-published volumes give evidence of this.²

正是在评注一事上，我最清楚地意识到本版的缺陷，其中许多已无可补救。众多的印刷错误与小的疏漏，我希望能在第 24 卷中加以更正；但我心中所想的那些毛病却不容易轻易纠正。它们主要源于材料尚未成熟。我方才提到的一点即可为证——缺乏任何真正可靠的德文版。而事实上，当本版的工作在十五年多以前启动之时，整个领域尚未被勘探、未被测绘。欧内斯特·琼斯[Ernest Jones] 的弗洛伊德传记甚至尚未开始出版；与弗利斯的通信以及《大纲》的存在本身，都不为大多数人所知。诚然，我从许多方面得到了帮助，¹ 尤其是来自欧内斯特·琼斯的帮助——他每有发现，便随时告知我，使我得以同步跟进。尽管如此，《标准版》仍是一项拓荒之作，自然带有此类工作所难免的一切差错与疏漏。随着时间推移，我本人对弗洛伊德思想的领会也日趋精进，晚近出版的各卷很可能即可为证。²

The Translations

In considering a revised translation of Freud, the primary aim was bound to be the rendering of his meaning with the greatest possible accuracy. But another, and perhaps more difficult, problem could not be evaded: the problem of style. The literary merits of Freud's writing cannot possibly be dismissed. Thomas Mann, for instance, spoke of the 'purely artistic' qualities of Totem and Taboo—'in its structure and literary form a masterpiece related and allied to all the great examples of German essay writing'.⁴ These merits could scarcely be expected to survive translation, but some effort had to be made in that direction. When the Standard Edition was first planned, it was considered that it would be an advantage if a single hand were responsible for shaping the whole text; and in fact a single hand has carried out the greater part of the work of translation, and even where a former version has been used as a basis it will be found that a large amount of remodelling has been imposed. This unfortunately has involved the discarding, in the interests of this preferred uniformity, of many earlier translations that were excellent in themselves. The imaginary model which I have always kept before me is of the writings of some English man of science of wide education born in the middle of the nineteenth century. And I should like, in an explanatory and no patriotic spirit, to emphasize the word 'English'.

在考虑弗洛伊德的修订译文时，首要目标必然是尽可能准确地传达其含义。但另一个、或许更为困难的问题却无法回避：风格问题。弗洛伊德文笔的文学价值绝不可轻忽。例如，托马斯·曼[Thomas Mann] 就谈到《图腾与禁忌》[Totem and Taboo] 的"纯艺术"品质——"就其结构与文学形式而言，它是一部杰作，与德国随笔写作的一切伟大典范相类而相通"。⁴ 这些价值恐怕难以在翻译中留存，但总须朝这个方向作些努力。当初筹划《标准版》时，曾认为若由同一只手来塑造全部文本，将是一种优势；而事实上，确是由同一只手完成了翻译工作的大部分，即便在以前人的译本为基础之处，也会发现已施加了大量的改造。遗憾的是，为了这种所偏好的统一，此举不得不舍弃了许多本身极佳的早期译本。我始终悬在眼前的想象范本，是某位生于十九世纪中叶、受过广博教育的英国科学人士的著作。而且，我要以一种解释性的、而非爱国的精神，强调"英国"一词。

If I turn now to the primary question of the correct rendering of Freud's meaning, I must come into conflict with what I have just said. For wherever Freud becomes difficult or obscure it is necessary to move closer to a literal translation at the cost of any stylistic elegance. For the same reason, too, it is necessary to swallow whole into the translation quite a number of technical terms, stereotyped phrases and neologisms which cannot with the best will in the world be regarded as 'English'. There is also the special difficulty, which arises, for instance, in The Interpretation of Dreams, The Psychopathology of Everyday Life, and the book on jokes, of the appearance of material involving untranslatable verbal points. Here the easy alternatives are denied us of making a cut or of substituting some equivalent English material. We must fall back on square brackets and footnotes, for we are bound by the fundamental rule: Freud, the whole of Freud, and nothing but Freud.

倘若我现在转向如何正确传达弗洛伊德之含义这一首要问题，我便势必要与我方才所说的话相抵牾。因为凡是弗洛伊德变得艰深或晦涩之处，都必须以牺牲一切文体的雅致为代价，更贴近直译。同样出于这一理由，也势必将相当多的术语、定型词组[stereotyped phrases] 与新造词[neologisms] 囫囵吞入译文——这些无论抱有多么良好的意愿，都不能被视为"英语"。此外还有一种特殊的困难，例如在《梦的解析》、《日常生活的心理病理学》[The Psychopathology of Everyday Life] 以及论诙谐的书[the book on jokes] 中出现的情形，即材料涉及不可翻译的字面文字游戏。此时，我们无从采取简易的替代办法——或删削，或代之以某种对等的英语材料。我们必须退而依靠方括号与脚注，因为我们受制于一条根本准则：弗洛伊德，整个的弗洛伊德，且唯有弗洛伊德。

As regards technical vocabulary, I have in general adopted the terms suggested in A New German-English Psycho-Analytical Vocabulary by Alix Strachey (1943), which was itself based on the suggestions of a 'Glossary Committee' set up by Ernest Jones twenty years earlier. In only a few instances have I departed from these authorities. Some individual words which raise controversial points are discussed in a separate note below.¹

至于专门术语，我大体采用阿利克斯·斯特雷奇[Alix Strachey]《新编德英精神分析词汇》[A New German-English Psycho-Analytical Vocabulary]（1943）所建议的译名，该书本身则依据二十年前欧内斯特·琼斯所设"术语委员会"[Glossary Committee] 的建议。我仅在少数几处背离了这些权威。少数引起争议的个别词语，另在下方一条注释中讨论。¹

I have tried so far as possible to keep to the general rule of invariably translating a German technical term by the same English one. Thus, 'Unlust' is always translated 'unpleasure' and 'Schmerz' is always translated 'pain'. It should be noticed, however, that this rule is liable to lead to misunderstandings. For instance, the fact that 'psychisch' is usually translated 'psychical' and 'seelisch' 'mental' may lead to the notion that these words have different meanings, whereas I believe they are synonymous. The rule of uniform translation has, however, been carried further and extended to phrases and indeed to whole passages. When, as so often happens, Freud puts forward the same argument or tells the same anecdote on more than one occasion (sometimes at long intervals) I have tried to follow him, and to use, if he does, identical words, or, if he varies them, to do the same. Some not uninteresting points are in this way preserved in the translation.

我尽可能恪守一条通则：始终以同一英语词来翻译同一德语术语。这样，'Unlust' 总译作"不快"[unpleasure]，'Schmerz' 总译作"疼痛"[pain]。然而应当注意，这条规则容易导致误解。例如，'psychisch' 通常译作"心理的"[psychical]，'seelisch' 译作"精神的"[mental]，这一事实或会使人以为这两个词含义不同，而我相信它们实为同义。不过，统一译名的规则已被推得更远，扩及短语、乃至整段文字。弗洛伊德常常在不止一个场合（有时相隔很久）提出同一论证或讲述同一轶事，此时我便力求追随他：他若用同样的词，我便用同样的词；他若变换用词，我也照样变换。这样一来，译文中便保留了一些不无趣味的细节。

I ought to say explicitly here that all additions to the text, however small, and all additional footnotes are indicated by square brackets.

我在此应当明确说明：凡对正文的一切增补，无论多么微小，以及一切额外增添的脚注，均以方括号标示。

Acknowledgements

Recognition must be paid, before anything else, to the exceedingly generous support to the scheme in its primordial stages by the members of the American Psychoanalytic Association (of which I am now proud to count myself an honorary member), on the initiative, in particular, of Dr John Murray of Boston, with the support of Dr W. C. Menninger, at that time President of the Association. Every previous attempt to raise the necessary capital had failed, and the whole project would have been abandoned without the magnificent gesture from America in subscribing in advance for some five hundred sets of the proposed edition. The sum was subscribed as an act of pure and indeed unreasonable faith, at a time when no concrete evidence existed of any such thing as the Standard Edition, and the patient subscribers were obliged to wait for as much as four or five years before the first volumes were delivered to them.

首先，必须表彰美国精神分析协会[American Psychoanalytic Association]（我如今荣幸地成为其名誉会员）的成员在本计划最初阶段所给予的极其慷慨的支持——这尤其是出自波士顿的约翰·默里博士[Dr John Murray] 的倡议，并得到当时协会会长 W. C. 门宁格博士[Dr W. C. Menninger] 的襄助。此前为筹集所需资金的一切尝试都告失败，若无美国方面预订约五百套拟议中版本的宏伟之举，整个计划本将搁置。这笔款项是以一种纯粹乃至不近情理的信念认购的——当时对于《标准版》这样一部书的存在尚无任何具体凭据，而耐心的认购者不得不等待长达四五年之久，才收到最初的几卷。

From that time onward, American support has been unswerving and has reached me from many quarters. I have enjoyed constant consultations throughout the years with Dr K. R. Eissler, who has put all the resources of the Sigmund Freud Archives at my disposal, besides giving me the friendliest personal reinforcement. Through him, too, I had the benefit of access to the valuable material in the Library of the New York State Psychiatric Institute. I have, of course, been constantly indebted to Dr Alexander Grinstein and his Index of Psychoanalytic Writings. Before leaving the help I have had from America, I must mention two men, from widely separated regions, each of whom gave their support long ago to the dream of a complete Freud in English, but neither of whom lived to see its fulfilment: Otto Fenichel and Ernst Kris.

自此以后，美国方面的支持未曾动摇，从许多方面及于我。这些年来，我一直有幸与 K. R. 艾斯勒博士[Dr K. R. Eissler] 保持持续的咨询——他将西格蒙德·弗洛伊德档案馆[Sigmund Freud Archives] 的全部资源供我调用，此外还给予我最友好的个人鼓励。也是通过他，我才得以使用纽约州立精神病研究所[New York State Psychiatric Institute] 图书馆的珍贵材料。当然，我始终有赖于亚历山大·格林斯坦博士[Dr Alexander Grinstein] 及其《精神分析著作索引》[Index of Psychoanalytic Writings]。在结束关于美国方面帮助的叙述之前，我必须提及两位来自天南地北的人士，他们都曾很久以前就支持"一部完整的英文弗洛伊德"之梦，却都未能亲见其实现：奥托·费尼谢尔[Otto Fenichel] 与恩斯特·克里斯[Ernst Kris]。

If I come now nearer home, my principal support has of course been from the Institute of Psycho-Analysis and in particular from its Publications Committee which, under changing names, has backed me through thick and thin from the earliest times, and in spite of what must often have seemed the most exorbitant financial demands. It seems a distortion to mention individual names, but I must recall once more my voluminous and instructive correspondence with Ernest Jones. I have special grounds for gratitude to Dr Sylvia Payne who was for a long time Chairman of the Publications Committee.

倘若现在回到近在身旁之处，我的主要支持当然来自精神分析研究所[Institute of Psycho-Analysis]，尤其是其出版委员会[Publications Committee]——该委员会名称屡经变更，却自最早时日起便一如既往地支持我，尽管常有看似最为过分的经费要求。提及个别姓名似乎有失偏颇，但我必须再次追念我与欧内斯特·琼斯之间卷帙浩繁而富于教益的通信。我尤有理由感谢西尔维娅·佩恩博士[Dr Sylvia Payne]，她曾长期担任出版委员会主席。

Turning to the actual germination of the Standard Edition, it goes without saying that my first acknowledgements must be to the collaborator and assistants whose names will be found on the title page of each volume: Miss Anna Freud, my wife and Dr Alan Tyson. Miss Freud, in particular, has been ungrudging in devoting her precious leisure hours to reading through the whole of the translation and providing invaluable criticisms. The name of Miss Angela Richards (now Mrs Angela Harris) also appears on the title page of the present volume. In recent years she has, indeed, been my principal assistant and has taken charge of much of the editorial side of my work. My gratitude is also due to Mrs Ralph Partridge, who has prepared most of the indexes to the individual volumes, and to Mrs Ambrose Price and Mrs D. H. O'Brien, who between them typed out the whole of the material in the edition.¹

至于《标准版》的实际发端，毋庸赘言，我首先必须致谢的是其姓名见于各卷卷首扉页的合作者与助手：安娜·弗洛伊德小姐[Miss Anna Freud]、我的妻子，以及艾伦·泰森博士[Dr Alan Tyson]。尤其是弗洛伊德小姐，她不惜将宝贵的闲暇时光毫不吝惜地用于通读全部译文，并提出极有价值的批评。安杰拉·理查兹小姐[Miss Angela Richards]（现为安杰拉·哈里斯夫人[Mrs Angela Harris]）的名字也见于本卷扉页。近年来，她确已成为我的主要助手，承担了我工作中相当一大部分的编辑事务。我还应感谢拉尔夫·帕特里奇夫人[Mrs Ralph Partridge]，她编制了各卷的大部分索引；并感谢安布罗斯·普赖斯夫人[Mrs Ambrose Price] 与 D. H. 奥布赖恩夫人[Mrs D. H. O'Brien]，二人合力誊清了本版全部材料的打字稿。¹

The difficulties in the preliminary preparations for the edition were exacerbated by the complications arising from Freud's completely unbusinesslike handling of the copyrights in his translations. These troubles, particularly in regard to the American copyrights, were only solved by the energetic intervention of Mr Ernst Freud over a period of several months. The English side of this question was handled by the Hogarth Press and especially by Mr Leonard Woolf. Mr Woolf, who has been publishing the English translations of Freud for some forty years, himself took an active share in the evolution of this edition. I feel that my special, and somewhat guilty, thanks are due to the publishers and to the printers for their tolerance in meeting my requirements.

本版筹备阶段的困难，因弗洛伊德对其译文版权处理之全然不合商业常规而生的种种纠葛而加剧。这些麻烦，尤其是在美国版权方面，全赖恩斯特·弗洛伊德先生[Mr Ernst Freud] 历时数月的奋力斡旋，方告解决。此问题的英国方面，则由霍加斯出版社[Hogarth Press] 处理，尤其有赖于伦纳德·伍尔夫先生[Mr Leonard Woolf]。伍尔夫先生出版弗洛伊德英译已约四十年，他本人也积极参与了本版的演进。我觉得，我对出版者与印刷者负有一种特殊的、且略感愧疚的谢意——感谢他们容忍并满足我的种种要求。

It is right for me to add that, though I have received and profited immeasurably from the advice of many helpers, yet the final decision upon every point whether of the translation or the commentary was bound ultimately to rest with me, and it is therefore upon me that the sole responsibility must rest for the errors which time will no doubt bring to light in plenty.

我还应当补充一点：尽管我从众多助手的建议中获益匪浅，但对无论是译文还是评注的每一点，最终的决定终归要由我作出，因此，凡是时间无疑将大量揭示出来的错误，其全部责任也须由我一人承担。

Finally, perhaps I may be allowed a more personal acknowledgement—of my debt to the companion who has shared my task as a translator for so long. It is nearly half a century now since we spent two years together in Vienna in analysis with Freud, and since, after only a few weeks of our analysis, he suddenly instructed us to make a translation of a paper he had recently written—'Ein Kind wird geschlagen'—a translation now imbedded here in Volume 17. In the present enterprise she has given me constant help by her impartiality both in approval and criticism, and she alone carried me through some periods of physical difficulty when it seemed absurd to imagine that the Standard Edition could ever be brought to completion.

最后，或许请允许我作一番更为私人的致谢——致谢那位长久以来与我分担翻译任务的伴侣。我们将近半个世纪前曾在维也纳一同接受弗洛伊德的分析，为期两年；而就在我们分析开始仅数周之后，他突然嘱我们翻译他新近写成的一篇论文——"一个孩子被打"[Ein Kind wird geschlagen]——该译文现已收入本书第 17 卷。在本项事业中，她以其在赞许与批评两方面同样持平的态度，不断给我帮助；在我一度身体抱恙、几乎觉得《标准版》之完成只是荒诞妄想的那段时期，正是她独自一人支撑我度过。

JAMES STRACHEY
Marlow, 1966

詹姆斯·斯特雷奇[James Strachey]
马洛，1966 年
