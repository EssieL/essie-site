DRAFT K¹ · THE NEUROSES OF DEFENCE
(A Christmas Fairy Tale)

There are four types of these and many forms. I can only make a comparison between hysteria, obsessional neurosis and one form of paranoia. They have various things in common. They are pathological aberrations of normal psychical affective states: of conflict (hysteria), of self-reproach² (obsessional neurosis), of mortification (paranoia), of mourning (acute hallucinatory amentia). They differ from these affects in that they do not lead to anything being settled but to permanent damage to the ego. They come about subject to the same precipitating causes as their affective prototypes, provided that the cause fulﬁls two more preconditions – that it is of a sexual kind and that it occurs during the period before sexual maturity (the preconditions of sexuality and infantilism). About preconditions applying to the individual concerned I have no fresh knowledge. In general I should say that heredity is a further precondition, in that it facilitates and increases the pathological affect – the precondition, that is, which mainly makes possible the gradations between the normal and the extreme case. I do not believe that heredity determines the choice of the particular defensive neurosis.

这类病有四种类型、许多形式。我只能就癔症、强迫性神经症与妄想狂的一种形式作比较。它们有若干共同之处。它们是正常心理情动状态的病理性偏差：冲突（癔症）、自我责备²（强迫性神经症）、受辱（妄想狂）、哀恸（急性幻觉性精神错乱）。它们与这些情动的不同在于：它们不导致任何事情得到了结，而招致对自我的永久损害。它们发生时所受的促发原因与其情动原型相同，只要该原因满足另外两个前提——它属于性的性质，并且发生在性成熟之前的时期（性与婴幼性[infantilism] 这两个前提）。关于涉及当事人个体的前提，我没有新的知识。总的说来我应当说：遗传是进一步的前提，因为它促成并增强病理性情动——也就是说，这一前提主要使正常与极端病例之间的层级过渡成为可能。我不认为遗传决定了所选择的那一种防御性神经症。

There is a normal trend towards defence – that is, an aversion to directing psychical energy in such a way that unpleasure [Unlust]ᵀ results. This trend, which is linked to the most fundamental conditions of the psychical mechanism (the principle of constancy), cannot be employed against perceptions, for these are able to compel attention (as is evidenced by their consciousness); it only comes in question against memories and thoughts. It is innocuous where it is a matter of ideas to which unpleasure was at one time attached but which are unable to acquire any contemporary unpleasure (other than remembered unpleasure), and in such cases too it can be overridden by psychical interest.

存在着一种朝向防御的正常倾向——即一种厌恶：不愿以会产生不快[Unlust]ᵀ 的方式去引导心理能量。这一倾向与心理机制最根本的条件（恒常原则[the principle of constancy]）相联系，却不能用来对付知觉，因为知觉能够强行引起注意（其意识性即为证明）；它只在对付记忆与思想时才成问题。在涉及那些曾经附带不快、但已无法获得任何当下不快（除记忆中的不快之外）的观念时，它是无害的；在这类情形中，它也可以被心理上的兴趣所压倒。

The trend towards defence becomes detrimental, however, if it is directed against ideas which are also able, in the form of memories,¹ to release fresh unpleasure – as is the case with sexual ideas. Here, indeed, the one possibility is realized of a memory having a greater releasing power subsequently [nachträglich]ᵀ than had been produced by the experience corresponding to it.² Only one thing is necessary for this: that puberty should be interpolated between the experience and its repetition in memory – an event which so greatly increases the effect of the revival. The psychical mechanism seems unprepared for this exception, and it is for that reason a necessary precondition of freedom from neuroses of defence that no considerable sexual irritation should occur before puberty, though it is true that the effect of such an experience must be increased by hereditary disposition before it can reach a pitch capable of causing illness.

然而，若防御倾向指向那些同样能够以记忆的形式释放新鲜不快的观念——性的观念即是如此——它便变得有害。这里确实实现了这样一种可能：一个记忆在事后的时点上[nachträglich]ᵀ 所具有的释放力量，大于与之相应的经验当初所产生的。² 为此只需要一件事：青春期应当被插入经验与其在记忆中的重复之间——这一事件极大地增强了复活的效果。心理机制对这类例外似乎毫无准备；正因如此，青春期之前不应有相当程度的性刺激，这乃是免于防御性神经症的一个必要前提——尽管确凿的是，这类经验的效果必须先由遗传素质加以增强，才能达到足以致病的强度。

(Here a subsidiary problem branches off: how does it come about that under analogous conditions, perversion or simple immorality emerges instead of neurosis?)³

（此处分出一个附带的问题：在类似条件下，为什么出现的是性倒错[perversion] 或单纯的伤风败俗，而不是神经症？）³

We shall be plunged deep into psychological riddles if we enquire into the origin of the unpleasure which seems to be released by premature sexual stimulation and without which, after all, a repression cannot be explained. The most plausible answer will appeal to the fact that shame and morality are the repressing forces and that the neighbourhood in which the sexual organs are naturally placed must inevitably arouse disgust during sexual experiences.⁴ Where there is no shame (as in a male person), or where no morality comes about (as in the lower classes of society), or where disgust is blunted by the conditions of life (as in the country), there too no repression and therefore no neurosis will result from sexual stimulation in infancy. I fear, nevertheless, that this explanation will not stand up to deeper testing. I do not think that the release of unpleasure during sexual experiences is the consequence of the chance admixture of certain unpleasurable factors.

如果我们追问那似乎由过早的性刺激所释放的不快来自何处——而没有它，压抑终究无法解释——我们就会陷入深深的心理学谜团。最可信的答案会诉诸这一事实：羞耻与道德是压抑的力量，而性器官天然所处的邻域，在性经验中必定不可避免地引起嫌恶。⁴ 凡没有羞耻之处（如男性身上），或道德无从产生之处（如社会的下层），或嫌恶被生活条件磨钝之处（如乡间），那里便也没有压抑，因而婴幼期的性刺激不会导致神经症。然而我担心，这一解释经不起更深的检验。我不认为性经验中的不快释放，是某些不快因素偶然混杂的结果。

Everyday experience teaches us that if libido reaches a sufﬁcient height disgust is not felt and morality is overridden; and I believe that the generation of shame is connected with sexual experience by deeper links. In my opinion there must be an independent source for the release of unpleasure in sexual life: once that source is present, it can activate sensations of disgust, lend force to morality, and so on. I hold to the model of anxiety neurosis in adults, where a quantity deriving from sexual life similarly causes a disturbance in the psychical sphere, though it would ordinarily have found another use in the sexual process. So long as there is no correct theory of the sexual process, the question of the origin of the unpleasure operating in repression remains unanswered. [See footnote 1, p. 297 below.]

日常经验教导我们：若力比多达到足够的高度，嫌恶便不被感到、道德便被压倒；而我相信，羞耻的发生与性经验之间有着更深的联系。在我看来，性生活中必定有一个独立的不快释放来源：一旦这个来源存在，它便能激活嫌恶之感、给道德赋予力量，如此等等。我坚持成年焦虑神经症的模式——在那里，一个来自性生活的量同样在心理领域造成扰乱，而它本来通常会在性过程中找到别的用途。只要还没有关于性过程的正确理论，压抑中起作用的不快之起源问题就仍然得不到回答。〔见下文 p. 297 脚注 1。〕

The course taken by the illness in neuroses of repression is in general always the same: (1) The sexual experience (or series of experiences) which is traumatic and premature and is to be repressed. (2) Its repression on some later occasion which arouses a memory of it; at the same time the formation of a primary symptom. (3) A stage of successful defence, which is equivalent to health except for the existence of the primary symptom. (4) The stage in which the repressed ideas return, and in which, during the struggle between them and the ego, new symptoms are formed which are those of the illness proper. (5)¹ A stage of adjustment, of being overwhelmed, or of recovery with a malformation.²

在压抑性神经症中，疾病的进程大体总是相同的：（1）那是创伤性的、过早的、并将被压抑的性经验（或一系列经验）。（2）它在某个后来唤起其记忆的场合被压抑；同时形成原初症状。（3）一个防御成功的阶段，除原初症状的存在之外等同于健康。（4）被压抑的观念回归的阶段；在这一阶段，在它们与自我的斗争之中，形成新的症状，这些才是疾病本身的症状。（5）¹ 一个调适的、被压倒的、或以畸形[malformation] 而康复的阶段。²

The main differences between the various neuroses are shown in the way in which the repressed ideas return; others are seen in the manner in which the symptoms are formed and in the course taken by the illness. But the speciﬁc character of a particular neurosis lies in the fashion in which the repression is accomplished.

各种神经症之间的主要差别，表现在被压抑观念回归的方式上；另一些差别见于症状形成的方式与疾病进程之中。但某一特定神经症的特有性质，在于压抑得以完成的那种方式。

The course of events in obsessional neurosis is what is clearest to me, because I have come to know it the best.

强迫性神经症中的事件进程对我来说最为清楚，因为我最熟悉它。

OBSESSIONAL NEUROSIS

Here the primary experience has been accompanied by pleasure [Lust]ᵀ. Whether an active one (in boys) or a passive one (in girls), it was not without pain or any admixture of disgust; and this in the case of girls implies a comparatively high age in general (about eight years). When this experience is remembered later, it gives rise to a release of unpleasure; and, in particular, there ﬁrst emerges a self-reproach, which is conscious. It seems, indeed, as though the whole psychical complex – memory and self-reproach – is conscious to start with. Later, both of them, without anything fresh supervening, are repressed and in their place an antithetic symptom, some nuance of conscientiousness, is formed in consciousness.

这里，原初经验曾伴随快感[Lust]ᵀ。无论是主动的（男孩）还是被动的（女孩），它都并非没有疼痛、也并非没有嫌恶的混杂；而在女孩的情形中，这总体上意味着年龄相对较大（约八岁）。当这经验后来被回忆时，它引起不快的释放；特别是首先浮现出一种自我责备，而它是有意识的。看来确实如此：整个心理复合体——记忆与自我责备——起初都是有意识的。后来，二者在没有任何新因素加入的情况下被压抑；而在意识中，代替它们的是一种对抗性症状，某种谨小慎微[conscientiousness] 的色彩得以形成。

The repression may come about owing to the memory of the pleasure itself releasing unpleasure when it is reproduced in later years; this should be explicable by a theory of sexuality. But things may happen differently as well. In all my cases of obsessional neurosis, at a very early age, years before the experience of pleasure, there had been a purely passive experience; and this can hardly be accidental.¹ If so, we may suppose that it is the later convergence of this passive experience with the experience of pleasure that adds the unpleasure to the pleasurable memory and makes repression possible. So that it would be a necessary clinical precondition of obsessional neurosis that the passive experience should happen early enough not to be able to prevent the spontaneous occurrence of the experience of pleasure. The formula would therefore run:

压抑也可能这样发生：快感本身的记忆在后来岁月中被复现时释放出不快；这本应由性的理论来解释。但事情也可能以别的方式发生。在我所有的 强迫性神经症病例中，在很早的年龄、在快感经验之前数年，都曾有过一次纯粹被动的 经验；这很难是偶然的。¹ 若如此，我们可以设想：正是这被动经验与快感经验后来的汇合，为那愉快的记忆添上了不快，从而使压抑成为可能。因此，强迫性神经症的一个必要临床前提将是：被动经验发生得足够早，以致无法阻止快感经验的自发出现。公式因此将是：

Unpleasure – Pleasure – Repression.

不快——快感——压抑。

The determining factor would be the chronological relations of the two experiences to each other and to the date of sexual maturity.

决定性的因素，将是两个经验彼此之间、以及与性成熟日期之间的年代学关系。

At the stage of the return of the repressed,² it turns out that the self-reproach returns unaltered, but rarely in such a way as to draw attention to itself; for a while, therefore, it emerges as a pure sense of guilt without any content. It usually becomes linked with a content which is distorted in two ways – in time and in content: the former insofar as it relates to a contemporary or future action, and the latter insofar as it signiﬁes not the real event but a surrogate chosen from the category of what is analogous – a substitution. An obsessional idea is accordingly a product of compromise, correct as regards affect and category but false owing to chronological displacement [Verschiebung]ᵀ and substitution by analogy.

在被压抑之物的回归阶段，² 可以看出自我责备 未经改动地回归了，但很少以引人注意的方式回归；因此有一段时间，它作为一种没有任何内容的纯粹内疚感[guilt] 而出现。它通常与一个内容相联结，而这内容在两重意义上被扭曲——在时间上和在内容上：前者在于它涉及当下的或未来的行动；后者在于它所指的并非真实事件，而是从『类似者』这一范畴中选出的替代物——一种替代。因此，一个强迫观念是妥协的产物：在情动与范畴上正确，却由于年代学的移置[Verschiebung]ᵀ 与类比性替代而虚假。

The affect of the self-reproach may be transformed by various psychical processes into other affects, which then enter consciousness more clearly than the affect itself: for instance, into anxiety (fear of the consequences of the action to which the self-reproach applies), hypochondria (fear of its bodily effects), delusions of persecution (fear of its social effects), shame (fear of other people knowing about the objectionable action), and so on.

自我责备的情动可以通过各种心理过程转变为其他情动，这些情动随后比情动本身更清楚地进入意识：例如转变为焦虑（对自我责备所涉行动之后果的恐惧）、疑病（对其身体影响的恐惧）、被害妄想（对其社会影响的恐惧）、羞耻（对他人得知那可恶行动的恐惧），如此等等。

The conscious ego regards the obsession as something alien to itself: it withholds belief from it, by the help, it seems, of the antithetic idea of conscientiousness, formed long before. But at this stage it may at times happen that the ego is overwhelmed by the obsession – for instance, if the ego is affected by an episodic melancholia. Apart from this, the stage of illness is occupied by the defensive struggle of the ego against the obsession; and this may itself produce new symptoms – those of the secondary defence. The obsessional idea, like any other, is attacked by logic, though its compulsive force is unshakeable. The secondary symptoms are an intensiﬁcation of conscientiousness, and a compulsion to examine things and to hoard them. Other secondary symptoms arise if the compulsion is transferred [übertragt]ᵀ¹ to motor impulses against the obsession – for instance, to brooding, drinking (dipsomania), protective ceremonials, folie du doute. ［Marginal note in the MS: Übertragung］

有意识的自我把强迫视为某种与己相异之物：它对之不予相信[withholds belief]，所借助的似乎正是很早以前形成的谨小慎微这一对抗观念。但在这个阶段，有时会发生自我被强迫所压倒的情形——例如当自我受一次发作性忧郁症影响时。除此以外，疾病阶段被自我对强迫的防御斗争所占据；而这本身又可能产生新的症状——次级防御 的症状。强迫观念像任何其他观念一样受到逻辑的攻击，尽管其强迫力量不可动摇。次级症状是谨小慎微的加剧，以及检查与囤积的强迫。若强迫被转移[übertragt]ᵀ¹ 为对抗它的运动冲动，则出现其他次级症状——例如沉思[brooding]、饮酒（酒狂[dipsomania]）、保护性仪式、怀疑狂[folie du doute]。［原稿页边注：Übertragung（转移）。］

Here, then, we arrive at the formation of three kinds of symptoms:
(a) the primary symptom of defence – conscientiousness,
(b) the compromise symptoms of the illness – obsessional ideas or obsessional affects,
(c) the secondary symptoms of defence – obsessional brooding, obsessional hoarding, dipsomania, obsessional ceremonials.

于是，我们在这里得到三类症状的形成：
（a）防御的原初症状——谨小慎微；
（b）疾病的妥协症状——强迫观念或强迫性情动；
（c）防御的次级症状——强迫性沉思、强迫性囤积、酒狂、强迫性仪式。

Those cases in which the content of the memory has not become admissible to consciousness through substitution, but in which the affect of self-reproach has become admissible through transformation, give one the impression of a displacement having occurred along a chain of inferences: I reproach myself on account of an event – I am afraid other people know about it – therefore I feel ashamed in front of other people. As soon as the ﬁrst link in this chain is repressed, the obsession jumps on to the second or third link, and leads to two forms of delusions of observation, which, however, are in fact part of the obsessional neurosis. The defensive struggle terminates in general doubting-mania or in the development of the life of an eccentric with an indeﬁnite number of secondary defensive symptoms – that is, if such a termination is reached at all.

在那些病例中，记忆的内容并未通过替代而变得可以为意识所接纳，但自我责备的情动却通过转变而变得可被接纳；这类病例给人的印象是：沿着一串推论发生了移置——我因某事件而责备自己——我怕别人知道它——因此我在他人面前感到羞耻。一旦这链条的第一环被压抑，强迫便跳到第二环或第三环，并导致两种形式的被观察妄想——它们其实仍是强迫性神经症的一部分。防御斗争一般以怀疑狂告终，或以一个古怪之人[eccentric] 的生活之发展告终，后者带有数目不定的次级防御症状——如果这样的终结真能达到的话。

It further remains an open question whether the repressed ideas return of their own accord, without the assistance of any contemporary psychical force [Kraft]ᵀ,¹ or whether they need this kind of assistance at every fresh wave of their return. My experiences indicate the latter alternative. States of contemporary unsatisﬁed libido, it seems, are what employ the force of their unpleasure to arouse the repressed self-reproach. Once this arousal has occurred and symptoms have arisen through the impact of the repressed on the ego, then, no doubt the repressed ideational material² continues to operate on its own account; but in the oscillations of its quantitative power it always remains dependent on the quota of libidinal tension present at the moment. Sexual tension which, owing to being satisﬁed, has no time to turn into unpleasure remains harmless. Obsessional neurotics are people who are subject to the danger that eventually the whole of the sexual tension generated in them daily may turn into self-reproach or rather into the symptoms resulting from it, although at the present time they would not recognize the primary self-reproach afresh.

还有一个问题悬而未决：被压抑的观念是自行回归、无需任何当下心理力量[Kraft]ᵀ 的协助，¹ 还是在其每一次回归的新浪潮中都需要这类协助？我的经验指向后一种可能。看来正是当下未获满足的力比多状态，动用其不快之力去唤起被压抑的自我责备。一旦这种唤起发生、症状通过被压抑之物对自我的冲击而产生，那么被压抑的观念材料² 无疑便自行继续运作；但在其量的力量的振荡中，它始终依赖于当下存在的力比多紧张之份额。性的紧张若因得到满足而无暇转为不快，便保持无害。强迫性神经症患者是这样的人：他们面临这样一种危险——最终他们每日所产生的全部性紧张都可能转变为自我责备、或更确切地说转变为由之产生的症状，尽管在当下他们不会再重新认出那原初的自我责备。

Obsessional neurosis can be cured if we undo all the substitutions and affective transformations that have taken place, till the primary self-reproach and the experience belonging to it can be laid bare and placed before the conscious ego for judging anew. In doing this we have to work through an incredible number of intermediate or compromise ideas which become obsessional ideas temporarily. We gain the liveliest conviction that it is impossible for the ego to direct on to the repressed material the part of the psychical energy to which conscious thought is linked. The repressed ideas – so we must believe – are present in and enter without inhibition into the most rational trains of thought; and the memory of them is aroused too by the merest allusions. The suspicion that ‘morality’ is put forward as the repressing force only as a pretext is conﬁrmed by the experience that resistance during the therapeutic work avails itself of every possible motive of defence.

如果我们把发生的一切替代与情动转变都撤销，直到原初的自我责备以及属于它的经验得以裸露出来、并置于有意识的自我面前重新评判，强迫性神经症便可治愈。在此过程中，我们必须加工数量惊人的居间观念或妥协观念，它们暂时成为强迫观念。我们会获得最生动的确信：自我不可能把意识思维所联结的那部分心理能量，引导到被压抑的材料之上。被压抑的观念——我们必得相信——是现存的，并且毫无阻碍地进入最理性的思维序列；关于它们的记忆也会被最轻微的暗示所唤起。『道德』只是作为借口而被推举为压抑力量——这一怀疑，被治疗工作中抵抗动用一切可能的防御动机这一经验所证实。

PARANOIA¹

The clinical determinants and chronological relations of pleasure and unpleasure in the primary experience are still unknown to me. What I have distinguished is the fact of repression, the primary symptom and the stage of illness as determined by the return of the repressed ideas.

原初经验中快感与不快的临床决定因素与年代学关系，我仍然不知道。我所区分出来的，是压抑这一事实、原初症状，以及由被压抑观念之回归所决定的疾病阶段。

The primary experience seems to be of a similar nature to that in obsessional neurosis; repression occurs after the memory of it has released unpleasure – it is unknown how. No self-reproach, however, is formed and afterwards repressed; but the unpleasure generated is referred to the patient’s fellow men in accordance with the psychical formula of projection. The primary symptom formed is distrust (sensitiveness to other people). In this, belief has been withheld from a self-reproach.

原初经验似乎与强迫性神经症中的性质相似；压抑在关于它的记忆释放出不快之后发生——如何发生则不明。然而并没有形成自我责备并随后加以压抑；相反，所产生的不快依投射的心理公式而被归诸患者的同人[fellow men]。所形成的原初症状是不信任（对他人的敏感）。在此，信念被从一种自我责备那里收回。

We may suspect the existence of different forms, according to whether only the affect is repressed by projection or the content of the experience too, along with it. So, again, what returns may be merely the distressing affect or the memory as well. In the second case, which is the only one I am closely acquainted with, the content of the experience returns as a thought that occurs to the patient as a visual or sensory hallucination. The repressed affect seems invariably to return in hallucinations of voices.

我们可以推测存在不同的形式，取决于被投射所压抑的只是情动，还是连同经验的内容一起。于是，回归的也可能只是那痛苦的情动，或连同记忆一起。在第二种情形中（也是我唯一熟悉的一种），经验的内容作为一种念头回归，它以视觉或感觉性幻觉的形式呈现于患者。被压抑的情动似乎总是以人声幻觉的形式回归。

The returning portions of the memory are distorted by being replaced by analogous images from the present day – that is, they are simply distorted by a chronological replacement and not by the formation of a surrogate.² The voices, too, bring back the self-reproach as a compromise symptom and they do so, firstly, distorted in its wording to the pitch of being indeﬁnite and changed into a threat; and, secondly, related not to the primary experience but precisely to the distrust – that is, to the primary symptom.

记忆回归的部分被扭曲，其方式是：以来自当下的类似意象取代之——也就是说，它们只是通过年代学的替换而被扭曲，而非通过替代物的形成。² 人声也把自我责备作为妥协症状带回，并且是以两种方式：第一，在其措辞上被扭曲到不确定的程度、并变成一种威胁；第二，不是与原发性经验相关，而恰恰与那不信任——即与原初症状——相关。

Since belief has been withheld from the primary self-reproach, it is at the unrestricted command of the compromise symptoms. The ego does not regard them as alien to itself but is incited by them to make attempts at explaining them which may be described as assimilatory delusions.³

既然信念被从原初的自我责备那里收回，它便听凭妥协症状的无限支配。自我并不把它们视为与己相异，反而受其怂恿去作出解释它们的尝试——这些尝试可以被称为同化妄想[assimilatory delusions]。³

At this point, with the return of the repressed in distorted form, the defence has at once failed; and the assimilatory delusions cannot be interpreted as a symptom of secondary defence but as the beginning of an alteration of the ego, an expression of its having been overwhelmed.⁴

在这一点上，随着被压抑之物以扭曲的形式回归，防御立即失败了；而同化妄想不能解释为次级防御的症状，而应解释为自我的改变 之开端，是自我被压倒的一种表现。⁴

The process reaches its conclusion either in melancholia (a sense of the ego’s littleness), which, in a secondary manner, attaches to the distortions the belief which has been withheld from the primary self-reproach,¹ or – what is more frequent and more serious – in protective delusions (megalomania), till the ego has been completely remodelled.

这一过程或终于忧郁症（一种自我渺小之感）——它以次级的方式，把被从原初自我责备那里收回的信念，附着于那些扭曲之上；¹ 或（更常见也更严重）终于保护性 妄想（夸大狂），直到自我被彻底改造。

The determining element of paranoia is the mechanism of projection involving the refusal of belief in the self-reproach. Hence the common characteristic features of the neurosis: the signiﬁcance of the voices as the means by which other people affect us, and also of gestures, which reveal other people’s mental life to us; and the importance of the tone of remarks and allusions in them – since a direct reference from the content of remarks to the repressed memory is inadmissible to consciousness.

妄想狂的决定性要素，是那包含对自我责备拒绝相信的投射机制。由此产生这种神经症的共同特征：人声的意义——它是他人影响我们的手段；姿势的意义——它向我们来揭示他人的心理生活；以及言语的语调 与其中暗示 的重要性——因为从言语的内容 到被压抑记忆的直接指涉，是不为意识所接纳的。

In paranoia repression takes place after a complicated conscious process of thought (the withholding of belief). This may perhaps be an indication that it ﬁrst sets in at a later age than in obsessional neurosis and hysteria. The preconditions of repression are no doubt the same. It remains a completely open question whether the mechanism of projection is entirely a matter of individual disposition or whether it is selected by particular temporal and accidental factors.

在妄想狂中，压抑发生在一个复杂的有意识思维过程（拒绝相信）之后。这或许提示：它开始得比强迫性神经症与癔症更晚。压抑的前提无疑相同。至于投射机制究竟纯属个体素质，还是由特定的时间性与偶然因素所选定，则完全悬而未决。

Four kinds of symptoms:
(a) primary symptoms of defence,
(b) compromise symptoms² of the return,
(c) secondary symptoms of defence,
(d) symptoms of the overwhelming of the ego.

四类症状：
（a）防御的原初症状；
（b）回归的妥协症状；²
（c）防御的次级症状；
（d）自我被压倒的症状。

HYSTERIA

Hysteria necessarily presupposes a primary experience of unpleasure – that is, of a passive nature. The natural sexual passivity of women explains their being more inclined to hysteria. Where I have found hysteria in men, I have been able to prove the presence of abundant sexual passivity in their anamneses. A further condition of hysteria is that the primary experience of unpleasure should not occur at too early a time, at which the release of unpleasure is still too slight and at which, of course, pleasurable events may still follow independently. Otherwise what will follow will be only the formation of obsessions. For this reason we often ﬁnd in men a combination of the two neuroses or the replacement of an initial hysteria by a later obsessional neurosis. Hysteria begins with the overwhelming of the ego, which is what paranoia leads to. The raising of tension at the primary experience of unpleasure is so great that the ego does not resist it and forms no psychical symptom but is obliged to allow a manifestation of discharge [Abführungsreisserung]ᵀ – usually an excessive expression of excitation [Erregung]ᵀ. This ﬁrst stage of hysteria may be described as ‘fright hysteria’; its primary symptom is the manifestation of fright accompanied by a gap in the psyche. It is still unknown up to how late an age this ﬁrst hysterical overwhelming of the ego can occur.

癔症必然以一次不快——即被动性质的——原初经验为前提。女性天然的性被动性，解释了她们更易倾向癔症。凡我在男性身上发现癔症之处，我都能在其病史中证明大量性被动的存在。癔症的进一步条件是：那原初的不快经验不应发生在过早的时点——那时不快的释放还过于轻微，而愉快的经验当然还可能独立地随后发生。否则随之而来的只会是强迫的形成。正因如此，我们常在男性身上看到两种神经症的结合，或先起的癔症为后来的强迫性神经症所取代。癔症始于自我被压倒——而这也正是妄想狂所导致的。原初不快经验中紧张的升高如此之大，以致自我不作抵抗、也不形成任何心理症状，而不得不容许一种排泄[Abführungsreisserung]ᵀ 的表现——通常是兴奋[Erregung]ᵀ 的过度表现。癔症的这一第一阶段可称为『惊吓性癔症』[fright hysteria]；其原初症状是惊吓的表现，伴以心理中的一个裂隙。这首次癔症性自我压倒可以发生在多大年龄之前，至今不明。

Repression and the formation of defensive symptoms only occur subsequently [nachträglich]ᵀ, in connection with the memory; and thenceforward defence and overwhelming (that is, the formation of symptoms and the outbreak of attacks) may be combined to any extent in hysteria.

压抑与防御症状的形成，只在后来[nachträglich]ᵀ 与记忆相联系时才发生；自此以后，在癔症中，防御 与压倒（即症状的形成与发作的爆发）可以任意程度地结合。

Repression does not take place by the construction of an excessively strong antithetic idea [p. 149 above] but by the intensiﬁcation of a boundary idea, which thereafter represents the repressed memory in the passage of thought. It may be called a boundary idea because on the one hand it belongs to the ego and on the other hand forms an undistorted portion of the traumatic memory. So, once again, it is the result of a compromise; this, however, is not manifested in a replacement on the basis of some category of subject matter, but by a displacement of attention along a series of ideas linked by temporal simultaneity. If the traumatic event found an outlet for itself in a motor manifestation, it will be this that becomes the boundary idea and the ﬁrst symbol of the repressed material. There is thus no need to assume that some idea is being suppressed at each repetition of the primary attack; it is a question in the ﬁrst instance of a gap in the psyche.

压抑并不通过构造一个过强的对抗观念〔见上文 p. 149〕而发生，而是通过强化一个界限观念[boundary idea] 而发生——此后这个界限观念在思维的通路中代表被压抑的记忆。它可以被称为界限观念，因为一方面它属于自我，另一方面它构成创伤记忆中未被扭曲的一部分。于是，它又一次是妥协的结果；然而这并不表现在基于某种内容范畴的替换上，而是表现在注意沿着一系列由时间同时性联系起来的观念而移置。若那创伤事件曾在一种运动表现中为自己找到出口，那么正是这一点成为界限观念、成为被压抑材料的第一个象征。因此，无需假设在原发性发作的每次重复时都有某个观念被压制；首先涉及的是心理中的一个裂隙。
