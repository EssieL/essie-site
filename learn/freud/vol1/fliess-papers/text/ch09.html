LETTER 21²

… I have only collected a few cases this Monday.

……这个星期一我只收集到寥寥几例。

No. 3

Dr Z., a physician, aged 34. Has suffered for many years from organic sensitivity of the eyes: phosphorus [flashes], dazzle, scotomas, etc. This has increased enormously, to the point of preventing him working, in the last four months (since the time of his marriage). Background: masturbation since the age of 14, apparently continued up to recent years. Marriage not consummated, much reduced potency; incidentally, divorce proceedings begun.

Z. 医生，34 岁。多年来患眼的器质性敏感：磷光[phosphorus (flashes)]、畏光、暗点[scotomas] 等。近四个月（自结婚之时起），此症剧增，以至使他无法工作。背景：自 14 岁起手淫，显然延续至近年。婚姻未圆房，性能力大为减退；附带一提，离婚程序已开始。

Clear typical case of hypochondria in a particular organ in a masturbator at periods of sexual excitation. It is interesting that medical education reaches such a shallow depth.

这是手淫者在性兴奋时期发生于某一特定器官的疑病症[hypochondria] 之清晰典型病例。有趣的是，医学教育所及竟如此之浅。

No. 4

Herr D., nephew of Frau A., who died a hysteric. A highly neurotic family. Aged 28. Has suffered for some weeks from lassitude, intracranial pressure, shaky knees, reduced potency, premature ejaculation, the beginnings of perversion: very young girls excite him more than mature ones.

D. 先生，A. 太太之侄；A. 太太死于癔症。一个高度神经质的家族。28 岁。数周来苦于倦怠、颅内压迫感、膝软、性能力减退、早泄，以及性倒错[perversion] 的开端：极年幼的女孩比成熟女子更能激起他的兴奋。

Alleges that his potency has been capricious from the first; admits masturbation, but not too prolonged; has a period of abstinence behind him now. Before that, anxiety states in the evening.

他声称自己的性能力自始便反复无常；承认手淫，但为时不算太久；如今身后有一段禁欲时期。在此之前，傍晚有焦虑状态。

Has he made a full confession?

他是否已完全如实交代？
