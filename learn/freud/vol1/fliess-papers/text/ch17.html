LETTER 46¹

... As the fruit of some tormenting reﬂections, I send you the following solution to the aetiology of the psychoneuroses, which still awaits conﬁrmation from individual analyses. Four periods of life are to be distinguished [Fig. 5]:

……作为一些痛苦思索的果实，我把下面这个关于精神神经症病因学的解答寄给你；它仍待个案分析的证实。生命可区分出四个时期〔图 5〕：

A and B (from about 8–10¹ and 13–17) are the transitional periods² during which repression for the most part occurs.

A 与 B（约 8–10 岁¹ 与 13–17 岁）是过渡时期，² 压抑大多在此发生。

The arousal in a later epoch of a sexual memory from an earlier one produces a surplus of sexuality in the psyche which operates as an inhibition of thought and gives the memory and its consequences an obsessional character – uninhibitability.

在后来的时相中，对来自更早时相的性记忆的唤起，会在心理中产生一种性的过剩[surplus of sexuality]，它起思维抑制的作用，并赋予那记忆及其后果一种强迫的性质——不可抑制性。

The period Ia possesses the characteristic of being untranslated,³ so that the arousal of a Ia sexual scene⁴ leads, not to psychical consequences, but to conversion. The surplus of sexuality prevents translation.

Ia 时期具有『未被翻译』³ 这一特征，因此唤起一个 Ia 期的性情景⁴ 所导致的不是心理后果，而是转换[conversion]。性的过剩阻止了翻译。

Surplus of sexuality alone is not enough to cause repression; the cooperation of defence is necessary for it; but without a surplus of sexuality defence does not produce a neurosis.

单凭性的过剩还不足以造成压抑；为此还需要防御 的协作；但没有性的过剩，防御也不会产生神经症。

The different neuroses have their particular chronological requirements for their sexual scenes [Fig. 6]:

不同的神经症对其性情景各有特定的年代学要求〔图 6〕：

That is, the scenes for hysteria occur in the first period of childhood (up to 4 years), in which the mnemic residues are not translated into verbal images [Wortvorstellungen]ᵀ. It is a matter of indifference whether these Ia scenes are aroused during the period after the second dentition (8–10) or in the stage of puberty. Hysteria always results and in the form of conversion, since the combined operation of defence and surplus of sexuality prevents translation.

也就是说，癔症的情景发生在童年的第一个时期（至 4 岁）——此时记忆痕迹尚未被翻译为词语表象[Wortvorstellungen]ᵀ。这些 Ia 期的情景是在第二次出牙之后的时期（8–10 岁）、还是在青春期阶段被唤起，则无关紧要。癔症总是以转换 的形式产生，因为防御与性的过剩的共同作用阻止了翻译。

The scenes for obsessional neuroses belong to Epoch Ib. They are provided with a translation into words and when they are aroused in II or III, psychic obsessional symptoms are generated.

强迫性神经症的情景属于 Ib 时期。它们获得了向词语的翻译；当它们在 II 或 III 期被唤起时，便产生心理上的强迫症状。

脚注 / Footnotes

p. 256 ¹ [Dated Vienna, May 30, 1896.]

p. 256 ¹ ［系于维也纳，1896 年 5 月 30 日。］

p. 256 ² [‘Praec’ in the MS. This term, apparently never used elsewhere by Freud, has, of course, quite a different meaning from ‘vorbewusst’, for which the regular English translation is ‘preconscious’.]

p. 256 ² ［手稿作『Praec』。这个术语显然在弗洛伊德别处从未使用过，其意义当然与『vorbewusst』大不相同——后者的通行英译才是『preconscious』（前意识）。］

p. 257 ¹ [The period of the second dentition, to which Freud attributed much importance at this time: see below, and cf. the second paper on the neuropsychoses of defence (1896b), RSE, 3, 176 and 177 n. 1, and ‘The Aetiology of Hysteria’ (1896c), ibid., 3, 217 f.]

p. 257 ¹ ［第二次出牙的时期；弗洛伊德此时对它极为重视：见下文，并参较第二篇论防御性神经精神病的论文（1896b），RSE 第 3 卷，176 页与 177 页脚注 1，以及《癔症的病因学》（1896c），同上，第 3 卷，217 页及下页。］

p. 257 ² [The forerunner of Freud’s ‘latency period’ concept.]

p. 257 ² ［弗洛伊德『潜伏期』[latency period] 概念的前身。］

p. 257 ³ [Into verbal images (see below).]

p. 257 ³ ［翻译为词语表象（见下文）。］

p. 257 ⁴ [The term ‘sexual scene’ is the forerunner of the ‘primal scene’ of later years. Cf. the ‘Wolf Man’ analysis (1918b), ibid., 17, 35. In fact, ‘primal scenes’ appears only a year later than the present letter. See p. 274 below.]

p. 257 ⁴ ［『性情景』[sexual scene] 一词是后来『原初情景』[primal scene] 的前身。参较『狼人』[Wolf Man] 分析（1918b），同上，第 17 卷，35 页。事实上，『原初情景』只比本信晚一年出现。见下文 p. 274。］
