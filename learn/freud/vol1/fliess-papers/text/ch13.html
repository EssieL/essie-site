DRAFT I¹ · MIGRAINE: ESTABLISHED POINTS

(1) A matter of summation. There is an interval of hours or days between the trigger and the outbreak of the symptoms. One has a sort of feeling that an obstacle is being overcome and that a process then goes forward.

（1）累加之事。在触发因素与症状爆发之间，有数小时或数日的间隔。人有一种感觉：某个障碍正在被克服，随后某个过程便向前推进。

(2) A matter of summation. Even without a trigger one has an impression that there must be an accumulating stimulus which is present in the smallest quantity at the beginning of the interval and in the largest quantity towards its end.

（2）累加之事。即使没有触发因素，人也有一种印象：必定有一个累积起来的刺激，它在间隔之初量最小，到其末则量最大。

(3) A matter of summation, in which susceptibility to aetiological factors lies in the height of the level of the stimulus already present.

（3）累加之事——其中对病因因素的易感性，在于已经存在的刺激水平之高度。

(4) A matter with a complicated aetiology. Perhaps on the pattern of a chain aetiology, where a proximate cause can be produced by a number of factors² directly and indirectly, or on the pattern of a surrogate³ aetiology, where, alongside of a specific cause, stock causes can act as quantitative substitutes.⁴

（4）带有复杂病因学之事。或许循『链式病因学』的模式：其中近因可由若干因素² 直接与间接地产生；或循『替代性病因学』³ 的模式：其中在特定原因之外，储备性原因[stock causes] 可以作为量的替代物而起作用。⁴

(5) A matter on the model of menstrual migraine and belonging to the sexual group. Evidence:

（5）循月经性偏头痛[menstrual migraine] 的模式、并属于性组之事。证据：

(a) Rarest in healthy males.

（a）在健康男性中最罕见。

(b) Restricted to the sexual time of life: childhood and old age almost excluded.

（b）局限于性生活的年龄段：童年与老年几乎被排除。

(c) If it is produced by summation, sexual stimulus too is something produced by summation.

（c）若它由累加产生，则性的刺激也是由累加产生之物。

(d) The analogy of periodicity.

（d）周期性的类比。

(e) Frequency in people with disturbed sexual discharge (neurasthenia, coitus interruptus).

（e）在性排泄受扰者（神经衰弱、性交中断）中频发。

(6) Certainty that migraine can be produced by chemical stimuli: human toxic emanations,¹ sirocco,² fatigue, smells. Now, sexual stimulus, too, is a chemical one.³

（6）偏头痛可由化学刺激产生的确定性：人体毒性散发[toxic emanations]、西洛可风[sirocco]、² 疲劳、气味。如今，性的刺激也是一种化学刺激。³

(7) Cessation of migraine during pregnancy, when production is probably directed elsewhere.

（7）妊娠期间偏头痛停止——此时产生大概被引向别处。

This would seem to show that migraine is a toxic effect produced by the sexual stimulating substance⁴ when this cannot find sufficient discharge. And perhaps one should add to this that there is a particular path present (whose location has to be determined) which is in a state of special susceptibility. The question about this is the question of the localization of migraine.

这似乎表明：偏头痛是性的刺激物质⁴ 在找不到充分排泄时所产生的一种毒性效果。或许还应当补充：存在着一条特定的通路（其位置有待确定），它处于特别易感的状态。关于这一点的问题，就是偏头痛的定位[localization] 问题。

(8) In regard to this path we have indications that organic diseases of the cranium, tumours and suppurations (without toxic intermediate links?) produce migraine or something similar, further that migraine is unilateral, is connected with the nose and is linked with localized paralytic phenomena. The first of these signs is not unambiguous. The unilaterality, localization over the eye and complication by localized paralyses are more important.

（8）关于这条通路，我们有些提示：颅骨的器质性疾病、肿瘤与化脓（在没有毒性中间环节的情况下？）会产生偏头痛或类似之物；此外，偏头痛是单侧的，与鼻相连，并与局限性的麻痹现象相联系。第一个征象并不明确无歧义。单侧性、定位于眼上方，以及由局限性麻痹所致的并合，更为重要。

(9) The painfulness of migraine can only suggest the cerebral meninges, since affections of the cerebral substance are certainly painless.

（9）偏头痛的疼痛性只能提示脑膜[cerebral meninges]，因为脑实质 的病变肯定是无痛的。

(10) If in this way migraine seems to approach neuralgia, this tallies with summation, sensitivity and its oscillations, the production of neuralgias through toxic stimuli. Toxic neuralgia will thus be its physiological prototype. The scalp is the seat of its pain and the trigeminal is its pathway. Since, however, the neuralgic change can only be a central one, we must suppose that the topical⁵ centre for migraine is a trigeminal nucleus whose ﬁbres supply the dura mater.

（10）如果偏头痛由此显得接近神经痛[neuralgia]，这与累加、敏感及其波动、以及由毒性刺激产生神经痛相合。中毒性神经痛[toxic neuralgia] 于是将是它的生理学原型。头皮是其疼痛的部位，三叉神经是其通路。然而，既然神经痛的改变只能是中枢性的，我们就必须假定：偏头痛的局部[topical]⁵ 中枢是一个三叉神经核，其纤维供应硬脑膜[dura mater]。

Since the pain in migraine is similarly located to that in supraorbital neuralgia, this dural nucleus must be in the neighbourhood of the nucleus of the first division. Since the different branches and nuclei of the trigeminal inﬂuence one another, all other affections of the trigeminal can contribute to the aetiology [of migraine] as concurrent (not as stock) factors.

既然偏头痛的疼痛位置与眶上神经痛[supraorbital neuralgia] 相似，这个硬脑膜核必定在第一支的核附近。既然三叉神经的不同分支与核相互影响，三叉神经的一切其他病变都可以作为并合性（而非储备性）因素，参与〔偏头痛的〕病因学。

偏头痛的症状学与生物学位置

The symptomatology and biological position of migraine

偏头痛的症状学与生物学位置

The pain of a neuralgia usually finds its discharge in tonic tension (or even in clonic spasms). Therefore it is not impossible that migraine may include a spastic innervation of the muscles of blood vessels in the reﬂex sphere of the dural region. We may ascribe to this intervention the general (and, indeed, the local) disturbance of function which does not differ symptomatically from a similar disturbance due to vascular constriction. (Cf. the similarity of migraine to attacks of thrombosis.) Part of the inhibition is due to the pain in itself. It is presumably the vascular area of the choroid plexus which is ﬁrst affected by the spasm of discharge. The relation to the eye and nose is explained by their common innervation by the first division [of the trigeminal].¹

神经痛的疼痛通常以紧张性收缩（甚至阵挛性痉挛）找到其排泄。因此，偏头痛可能包含硬脑膜区反射范围内血管肌肉的痉挛性支配，这并非不可能。我们可以把这种普遍的（其实也是局部的）功能扰乱归因于这一介入——它在症状上与血管收缩所致的类似扰乱并无不同。（参较偏头痛与血栓形成发作的相似性。）一部分抑制来自疼痛本身。首先受排泄性痉挛影响的，大概是脉络丛[choroid plexus] 的血管区。与眼和鼻的关系，由二者共同受〔三叉神经〕第一支的支配来解释。¹

脚注 / Footnotes

p. 241 ¹ [This MS. is undated. It does not form part of the rest of the collection of ‘Fliess Papers’, but was in the possession of Dr Robert Fliess in New York. It initially appeared to be a document referred to as having been recently written, in a portion, not published here, of Letter 22 above. This is dated March 4, 1895. That, however, has subsequently turned out to be something else Freud was writing at the time (Freud, 1895g), NSW, 4. It seems most likely that it was written in the autumn of 1895, when migraine was a frequent topic of discussion between Freud and Fliess. The editors of the 1986 German edition consider it best placed with a letter dated November 8, 1895, not reprinted here. – Both Freud and Fliess were sufferers from migraine and it was a major focus of interest to Fliess. See Jones, 1953, 339, and The Psychopathology of Everyday Life (1901b), RSE, 6, 19.]

p. 241 ¹ ［这份手稿未注明日期。它不属于『弗利斯文稿』收集的其余部分，而曾为纽约的罗伯特·弗利斯[Robert Fliess] 医生所藏。起初它看来像是上文第 22 封信（此信未在此处印出的一部分）中提到的、新近写成的一份文件。那封信系于 1895 年 3 月 4 日。然而后来查明，那指的是弗洛伊德当时正在写的另一样东西（Freud, 1895g），NSW 第 4 卷。最可能的是，本稿写于 1895 年秋，当时偏头痛是弗洛伊德与弗利斯之间时常讨论的话题。1986 年德文版编者认为最好把它与 1895 年 11 月 8 日的一封信（未在此处重印）放在一起。——弗洛伊德与弗利斯都患偏头痛，而这也是弗利斯关注的一大焦点。见 Jones, 1953, 339，以及《日常生活的精神病理学》（1901b），RSE 第 6 卷，19 页。］
