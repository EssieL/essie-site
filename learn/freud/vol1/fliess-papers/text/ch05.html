DRAFT D – ON THE AETIOLOGY AND THEORY OF THE MAJOR NEUROSES

Introduction. Historical. Gradual differentiation of the neuroses. The course of development of my own views.

导论[Introduction]。历史部分。诸神经症之逐渐分化。我本人观点的形成过程。

I. CLASSIFICATION

A. Morphology of the Neuroses
(1) Neurasthenia and the pseudo-neurasthenias.
(2) Anxiety neurosis.
(3) Obsessional neurosis [Zwangsneurose].
(4) Hysteria.
(5) Melancholia, Mania.
(6) The mixed neuroses.
(7) Ramifications of the neuroses and transitions to the normal.

A. 神经症的形态学[Morphology of the Neuroses]
（1）神经衰弱[neurasthenia]与假性神经衰弱[pseudo-neurasthenia]。
（2）焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]。
（3）强迫性神经症[obsessional neurosis，Zwangsneurose]。
（4）癔症[hysteria]。
（5）忧郁症[melancholia]、躁狂[mania]。
（6）混合性神经症。（7）神经症的分支及其向正常状态的过渡。

B. Aetiology of the Neuroses (provisionally restricted to the acquired neuroses)
(1) Aetiology of neurasthenia – Type of congenital neurasthenia.
(2) Aetiology of anxiety neurosis.
(3) of obsessional neurosis and hysteria.
(4) of melancholia.
(5) of the mixed neuroses.
(6) The basic aetiological formula [p. 208 above]. – The thesis of specificity [of aetiology]; the analysis of the medley of neuroses.
(7) The sexual factors in their aetiological significance.
(8) Examination of patients.
(9) Objections and proofs.
(10) Behaviour of asexual people.²

B. 神经症的病因学[Aetiology of the Neuroses]（暂限于获得性神经症[acquired neuroses]）
（1）神经衰弱的病因学——先天性神经衰弱之类型。
（2）焦虑神经症的病因学。
（3）强迫性神经症与癔症的病因学。
（4）忧郁症的病因学。
（5）混合性神经症的病因学。
（6）基本病因学公式［见上文第 208 页］。——病因学上的特定性[specificity]命题；对混合性神经症之分析。
（7）性因素及其病因学意义。
（8）对病人的检查。
（9）反对意见与证据。
（10）无性欲者[asexual people]的行为。²

C. Aetiology and Heredity
The hereditary types. – Relation of aetiology to degeneracy, to the psychoses and to disposition.

C. 病因学与遗传[Aetiology and Heredity]
遗传类型。——病因学与退化[degeneracy]、与精神病[psychoses]、与素质[disposition]的关系。

II. THEORY

D. Points of Contact with the Theory of Constancy¹
Internal and external increase of stimulus; constant and ephemeral excitation. – Summation a characteristic of internal excitation.² – Specific reaction.³ – Formulation and exposition of the theory of constancy. – Intercalation of the ego [Ich]ᵀ, with storing up of excitation.⁴

D. 与恒常性理论的接触点¹
刺激的内在与外在增加；持续性兴奋与短暂性兴奋。——累积[summation]是内在兴奋的一个特征。²——特定反应[specific reaction]。³——恒常性理论的表述与阐发。——自我[Ich]ᵀ之插入，以及兴奋的贮存[storing up]。⁴

E. The Sexual Process in the Light of the Theory of Constancy
Path taken by the excitation in the male and the female sexual process. – Path taken by the excitation in the presence of aetiologically operative sexual noxae. – Theory of a sexual substance.⁵ – The sexual schematic diagram.⁶

E. 恒常性理论观照下的性过程
兴奋在男性与女性性过程中所取之路径。——在病因学上起作用的性有害因素[sexual noxae]存在时，兴奋所取之路径。——性物质[sexual substance]理论。⁵——性过程示意图。⁶

F. Mechanism of the Neuroses
The neuroses as disturbances of equilibrium owing to increased difficulty in discharge [Abfuhr]ᵀ. – Attempts at adjustment, limited in their efficiency. – Mechanism of the different neuroses in relation to their sexual aetiology. – Affects and neuroses.

F. 神经症的机制
神经症是排出[Abfuhr]ᵀ困难增加所致的平衡失调。——调节之尝试，其效力有限。——不同神经症的机制与其性病因学的关系。——情感[affects]与神经症。

G. Parallel between the Neuroses of Sexuality and Hunger

G. 性欲神经症与饥饿神经症之平行

H. Summary of the Theory of Constancy and the Theory of Sexuality and the Neuroses
Place of the neuroses in pathology; factors to which they are subject; laws governing their combination. – Psychical inadequacy, development, degeneracy, etc.

H. 恒常性理论、性理论及神经症之总结
神经症在病理学中的位置；其所受制于的因素；支配其组合的规律。——心理上的不足[psychical inadequacy]、发展、退化等。
