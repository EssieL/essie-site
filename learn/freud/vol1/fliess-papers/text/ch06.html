LETTER 18¹

… There are still a hundred gaps, large and small, in my ideas about the neuroses; but I am getting closer to a comprehensive view and to some general lines of approach. I know three mechanisms: transformation of affect (conversion hysteria), displacement of affect [Affektverschiebung]ᵀ (obsessions) and (3) exchange of affect (anxiety neurosis and melancholia). In every case what seems to undergo these alterations is sexual excitation, but the impetus to them is not in every case something sexual. That is to say, in every case in which neuroses are acquired, they are acquired owing to disturbances of sexual life; but there are people in whom the behaviour of their sexual affects is disturbed hereditarily, and they develop the corresponding forms of hereditary neuroses. The most general aspects from which I can classify the neuroses are the four following:

……我关于诸神经症的想法，仍有上百个或大或小的缺口；但我正逐渐接近一个总览性的观点，也接近几条总的入手路径。我知道三种机制：情感之转化[transformation of affect]（转换性癔症[conversion hysteria]）、情感之移置[displacement of affect，Affektverschiebung]ᵀ（强迫观念[obsessions]），以及（3）情感之交换[exchange of affect]（焦虑神经症与忧郁症）。在每一例中，看似经历这些变更的都是性兴奋[sexual excitation]；但促使这些变更发生的，却并非每一例都是某种性的东西。也就是说，凡神经症为后天获得者，皆因性生活之紊乱而获得；但也有些人，其性情感的表现方式是遗传性紊乱的，他们便发展出相应的遗传性神经症形式。我据以对诸神经症进行分类的最一般的着眼点，有如下四项：

(1) Degeneracy.
(2) Senility.
(3) Conflict.
(4) Conflagration.

（1）退化[degeneracy]。
（2）衰老[senility]。
（3）冲突[conflict]。
（4）骤燃[conflagration]。

What do those mean?
Degeneracy means the innately abnormal behaviour of the sexual affects; so that the processes of conversion, displacement and transformation into anxiety occur in proportion as the sexual affects play a part in the course of life.

这些是什么意思？
退化指性情感[sexual affects]天生异常的表现方式；于是转换、移置与转化为焦虑这些过程，便按性情感在生命历程中所起作用的比例而发生。

Senility is clear. It is, as it were, a degeneracy normally acquired in old age.²

衰老含义自明。它可谓一种在老年正常获得的退化。²

Conflict coincides with my conception of defence [Abwehr]ᵀ; it comprises the cases of acquired neurosis in people who are not hereditarily abnormal. What is fended off [Abgewehrte] is always sexuality.

冲突与我的防御[Abwehr]ᵀ概念相合；它涵盖那些并无遗传异常者身上的获得性神经症病例。被抵御者[Abgewehrte]始终是性。

Conflagration is a new conception. It means what may be called acute degeneration (e.g. in severe intoxications, in fevers, in the preliminary stage of general paralysis) – catastrophes, that is to say, in which there are disturbances of the sexual affects without sexual precipitating causes. Perhaps traumatic neuroses might be approached from this point.³

骤燃是一个新概念。它指可称为急性退化者（例如严重中毒、发热、麻痹性痴呆[general paralysis]的前期）——亦即灾难性事件，其中出现性情感的紊乱，却并无性的诱发原因。或许创伤性神经症[traumatic neuroses]可从这一点入手。³
