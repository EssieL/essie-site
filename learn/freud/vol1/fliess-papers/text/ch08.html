DRAFT F – COLLECTION III²

18 Aug. 94
No. 1
Herr K., aged 24 — Anxiety neurosis: hered. disp.

94 年 8 月 18 日
第 1 例
K. 先生，24 岁——焦虑神经症：遗传素质[hereditary disposition]

Father treated for senile melancholia; sister, O., good case of complicated anxiety neurosis, thoroughly analysed; all the K.s neurotic and temperamentally gifted. A cousin of Dr K. in Bordeaux. – In good health till recently; has slept badly for the last 9 months; in February and March woke frequently with night terrors and palpitations; gradually increasing general excitability; intermission owing to army manoeuvres, which did him a great deal of good. Three weeks ago in the evening a sudden attack of anxiety with no content, with a feeling of congestion from his chest up to his head. Interpreted [by him to mean that] something dreadful was bound to happen; no accompanying oppression and only slight palpitations. Similar attacks afterwards in daytime as well, at his midday meal. Two weeks ago he consulted a doctor; improved on bromide, [condition] still continues, but sleeps well. In addition during the last two weeks short attacks of deep depression, resembling complete apathy, lasting barely a few minutes. Improved here in R[eichenau]. Besides this, attacks of pressure at the back of the head.

父亲曾因老年忧郁症[senile melancholia] 就医；姊妹 O. 是一例典型的复杂性焦虑神经症，曾彻底分析；K. 家所有人皆神经质而天资颖异。有一位堂表亲是波尔多的 K. 医生。——至最近一向健康；过去 9 个月睡眠不佳；2 月与 3 月常因夜惊[night terrors] 与心悸而醒；全身易激性逐渐增高；因军队演习而中断，演习对他大有好处。三周前某晚，突发一次无内容的焦虑发作，伴一种自胸部上涌至头部的壅塞感。他〔将其〕解释为〔意谓〕必有可怕之事发生；无伴随的压闷感，仅轻微心悸。此后白天亦有类似发作，在午饭时。两周前他就诊；服溴剂后好转，〔病情〕仍在持续，但睡眠良好。此外，近两周有短促的深度抑郁发作，类乎完全的淡漠，仅持续数分钟。在赖兴瑙[R(eichenau)] 此地好转。除此以外，尚有后头部压迫感发作。

He himself began with [volunteering] sexual information. A year ago he fell in love with a girl who was a flirt; a great shock when he heard she was engaged to someone else. No longer in love now. – Attaches little importance to it. – He went on: he masturbated between 13 and 16 or 17 (seduced at school) to a moderate extent, he said. Moderate in sexual intercourse; has used a condom for the last 2½ years for fear of infection; often feels limp after it. He described this kind of intercourse as forced. Notices that his libido has quite significantly¹ diminished for about a year. Was very much excited sexually in his relations with the girl (without touching her, etc.). His first attack at night (February) was two days after coitus; his first anxiety attack was after coitus on the same evening; since then (three weeks) abstinent – a quiet, mild-mannered and in other ways healthy man.

他本人〔主动〕开始提供性的情况。一年前他爱上一位轻浮的女子；听说她与他人订婚时，受到极大震动。如今已不再恋爱。——他对此不甚重视。——他接着说：13 岁至 16 或 17 岁之间（在学校被人引诱）手淫，据他说程度中等。性交方面中等；近两年半因怕传染而使用保险套；事后常感疲软。他把这类性交形容为勉强的。他注意到自己的里比多显著地¹ 减退，约已一年。在与那女子的交往中（并未触碰她等），他在性方面极为兴奋。他的首次夜间发作（2 月）在性交后两日；首次焦虑发作则在当晚性交之后；此后（三周）禁欲——一个安静、性情温和、其他方面健康的男子。

18 Aug. 94 · Discussion of No. 1

If we attempt to interpret the case of K., one thing in particular strikes us. The man has a hereditary disposition: his father suffers from melancholia, perhaps anxious melancholia; his sister has a typical anxiety neurosis; I am intimately acquainted with it, but otherwise I should certainly have described it as acquired. This creates ground for thought on their heredity. There is probably only a ‘disposition’ in the K. family (a tendency to fall ill with greater certainty and more seriously in response to the typical aetiology) and not ‘degeneracy’. We may therefore expect that in Herr K.'s case the slight anxiety neurosis developed from a slight aetiology. Where is it to be looked for without prejudice?

若我们试图解释 K. 的病例，有一点格外引人注意。此人有遗传素质：其父患忧郁症，或为焦虑性忧郁症；其姊妹患典型的焦虑神经症；我对之了解甚深，若非如此，我定会将其描述为获得性的。这就为思考他们的遗传提供了依据。K. 家或许只有一种“素质”（一种在面临典型病因时更确定、更严重地罹病的倾向），而非“退化”。因此我们可以预期，在 K. 先生这一例中，轻微的焦虑神经症是由轻微的病因发展而来。这病因该到哪里不带偏见地寻找？

It seems to me in the first place that it is a question of an enfeebled condition of sexuality. The man's libido has been diminishing for some time; the preparations for using a condom are enough to make him feel that the whole act is something forced on him and his enjoyment of it something he was persuaded into. This is no doubt the nub of the whole business. After coitus he sometimes feels limp; he notices this, as he says, and then, two days after a coitus or, as the case may be, on the next evening, he has his first attacks of anxiety.

在我看来，首先所关涉的是性欲的一种虚弱状态。此人的里比多已减退一段时日；使用保险套的那些准备，已足以使他觉得整个行为是强加于他的，而对之的享受则是被说服才有的。这无疑是整件事的症结。性交之后他有时感到疲软；如他所说，他注意到这一点，随后，在性交后两日，或视情形在次晚，他便有首次焦虑发作。

The concurrence of reduced libido and anxiety neurosis fits in with my theory without difficulty. There is a weakness in the psychical mastery of the somatic sexual excitation. This weakness has been present for some time and makes it possible for anxiety to appear if there is an incidental increase in somatic excitation.

里比多减退与焦虑神经症同时出现，与我的理论毫不费力地相符。在对躯体性兴奋[the somatic sexual excitation] 的心理掌控[psychical mastery] 中存在一种虚弱。这虚弱已存在一段时日，并使焦虑得以在躯体兴奋偶有增加时出现。

How was this psychical enfeeblement acquired? There is not much to be got out of his masturbation in his youth; it would certainly not have had such results, especially as it does not seem to have exceeded the usual amount. His relations with the girl, which excited him very much sensually, seem far better calculated to produce a disturbance in the required direction; in fact, the case approaches the conditions in the familiar neuroses of men during long engagements. But above all, it cannot be disputed that the fear of infection and the decision to use a condom laid the foundation for what I have described as the factor of alienation between the somatic and the psychical [p. 221 f. above]. The effect would be the same as in the case of coitus interruptus with men. In short, Herr K. brought psychical sexual weakness on himself because he spoilt coitus for himself, and, his physical health and production of sexual stimuli being unimpaired, the situation gave rise to the generation of anxiety. We may add that his readiness to take precautions, instead of finding adequate satisfaction in a secure relationship, points to a sexuality which was from the first of no great strength. The man has a hereditary disposition; the aetiology that can be found in his case, though it is qualitatively important, would be tolerated as harmless by a healthy – that is, by a vigorous – man.

这心理上的虚弱是如何获得的？从他青年时代的手淫中得不到多少东西；它肯定不会有这样的结果，尤其它似乎并未超出通常的量。他与那女子的交往曾使他肉体上极为兴奋，这远更可能造成所需方向上的紊乱；事实上，此例已接近人们熟知的、男子在漫长订婚期中所患神经症的状况。但最重要的是，无可争辩的是：对传染的恐惧与使用保险套的决定，为我曾描述为躯体与心理之间疏离[alienation] 因素者奠下了基础［见上文第 221 页及以下］。其效果当与男子中断性交的情形相同。简言之，K. 先生自己招致了心理上的性虚弱，因为他为自己败坏了性交；而他的身体健康与性刺激的产生均未受损，这一情形便引起了焦虑的生成。我们还可补充：他乐于采取预防措施，而非在一段安稳的关系中获得充分满足，这表明他的性欲从一开始就不甚强健。此人有遗传素质；在他这一例中所能找到的病因，尽管在性质上重要，对一个健康的——也就是强健的——男子而言，本会被耐受为无害。

An interesting feature of this case is the appearance of a typically melancholic mood in attacks of short duration. This must be of theoretical importance for anxiety neurosis due to alienation; for the moment I can only make a note of it.

此例一个有趣的特征是：在短促的发作中出现了典型的忧郁性心境。这对因疏离而致的焦虑神经症，必具有理论上的重要性；此刻我只能把它记下一笔。

20 Aug. 94 · No. 2

Herr von F., Budapest, aged 44

F. 男爵[von F.]，布达佩斯，44 岁

A healthy man physically, he complains that ‘he is losing his liveliness and zest, in a way that is not natural in a man of his age’. This state – in which everything seems indifferent to him, in which he finds his work a burden and feels peevish and limp – is accompanied by severe pressure on the top and also the back of his head. Moreover, it is regularly characterized by bad digestion – that is, by disinclination for food and by flatulence and sluggish stools. He also seems to sleep badly.

他身体上是个健康人，却诉说自己“正在丧失生气与兴味，其方式在一个他这个年纪的男子身上并不自然”。这一状态——其中一切对他都显得无所谓，他觉得工作成了负担，感到烦躁而易倦——伴有头顶及后头部的剧烈压迫感。此外，它还规律地以消化不良为特征——即厌食、胀气与大便迟缓。他似也睡眠不佳。

But the state is evidently intermittent. Each time it lasts for 4 or 5 days and slowly passes off. He notices from the flatulence that the nervous weakness is coming on. There are intervals of from 12 to 14 days, and he may be well for several weeks. Better periods of months' duration have even occurred. He insists that things have been like this for the last 25 years. As so often, one has to start by building up the clinical picture, for he keeps on monotonously repeating his complaints and declares that he has paid no attention to other events. Thus the indeterminate outline of the attacks forms part of the picture, as does their complete irregularity in time. He naturally puts the blame of his state on his digestion. Benedikt¹ put the diagnosis in writing: Cephalaea cum digestione tarda.

但这状态显然是间歇性的。每次持续四五日，然后慢慢消退。他从胀气察觉神经性虚弱将至。间隔 12 至 14 日，他也可能连续数周安好。甚至出现过长达数月的较好时期。他坚持说，近 25 年来情况一直如此。一如常有的事，人不得不从构建临床图像入手，因为他一味单调地反复诉说自己的抱怨，并宣称对其他事件从未留意。于是，发作轮廓的不确定，与它们时间上的完全不规则一样，都构成临床图像的一部分。他自然把自己状态之咎归于消化。贝内迪克特[Benedikt]¹ 曾把诊断写了下来：Cephalaea cum digestione tarda（头痛伴消化迟滞）。

Organically sound; no serious worries or emotional disturbance. As regards sexuality: masturbated between the ages of 12 and 16; then very regular relations with women; he was not enormously attracted; married for the last 14 years, only two children, the last 10 years ago; in the interval and since then, only a condom and no other technique. Potency has decidedly diminished in the last few years. Coitus every 12 to 14 days or so; often, too, with long intervals. Admits that he feels limp and wretched after coitus with a condom; but not immediately afterwards, only 2 days later – or, as he puts it, he has noticed that 2 days later he gets digestive trouble. Why does he use a condom? One should not have too many children! ([He has] two.)

器官上健全；无严重忧患或情绪扰乱。就性方面而言：12 岁至 16 岁之间手淫；此后与女子的交往极为规律；他并未受到极大的吸引；结婚已 14 年，只有两个孩子，末次在 10 年前；其间及此后，只用保险套，别无其他方式。近数年性能力明显减退。约每 12 至 14 日性交一次；也常间隔良久。他承认，戴保险套性交后感到疲软而难受；但不是紧接着，而只在两日后——或者如他所说，他注意到两日后便出现消化方面的毛病。他为什么用保险套？人不该有太多孩子！（他〔有〕两个。）

Discussion of No. 2

A mild but actually² very characteristic case of periodic depression, melancholia. Symptoms: apathy, inhibition [Hemmung]ᵀ, intracranial pressure, dyspepsia, insomnia – the picture is complete.

一例轻微、但实则² 极为典型的周期性抑郁、忧郁症。症状：淡漠、抑制[Hemmung]ᵀ、颅内压感、消化不良、失眠——图像完整。

There is an unmistakable similarity to neurasthenia, and the aetiology is the same. I have some quite analogous cases: they are masturbators (Herr A.) and also with a hereditary taint. The von F.s from Budapest are well known to be psychopathic.³ Thus the case is one of neurasthenic melancholia; there must be a point of contact here with the theory of neurasthenia.

与神经衰弱有明显的相似，且病因学相同。我有若干十分类似的病例：他们都是手淫者（A. 先生），且都带遗传污点。布达佩斯的 F. 家族众所周知是精神变态的[psychopathic]。³ 故此例属神经衰弱性忧郁症[neurasthenic melancholia]；此处必与神经衰弱理论有一个接触点。

It is quite possible that the starting point of a minor melancholia like this may always be an act of coitus: an exaggeration of the physiological saying ‘omne animal post coitum triste’.¹ The time intervals would fit in. The man is improved by every course of treatment, every absence from home – that is, by every period of relief from coitus. Of course, as he says, he is faithful to his wife. The use of a condom is evidence of weak potency; being something analogous to masturbation, it is a continuous causation of his melancholia.

像这样轻微的忧郁症，其起点完全可能始终是一次性交行为：这是对“omne animal post coitum triste”（凡动物交媾后皆悲哀）这一生理学格言的夸大。¹ 其时间间隔也吻合。此人经每一疗程、每一次离家——也就是每一段免于性交的时期——都有好转。当然，如他所说，他对妻子是忠实的。使用保险套是性能力虚弱的证据；它既与手淫有某种类似，便成为其忧郁症的持续成因。
