NOTE

During the whole of the later part of the year 1895 Freud was largely occupied with the fundamental theoretical problem of the relation between his emerging psychology and established neurology. His reﬂections ﬁnally led to the uncompleted work which we have named a ‘Project for a Scientific Psychology’. This draft was written in September and October, 1895, and should appear, chronologically, at this point in the Fliess papers. It stands so much apart, however, from the rest of them, and constitutes such a formidable and self-contained entity, that it has seemed advisable to print it in a detached shape at the end of this volume. One letter, No. 39, written on January 1, 1896, is so closely connected with the ‘Project’ (apart from which, indeed, it would be unintelligible) that it too has been removed from its proper place in the correspondence and printed as an Appendix to the ‘Project’. That Freud had also during all this period been concerned with clinical matters as well is conclusively shown by the fact that on the very same day on which he despatched this letter (January 1, 1896) he also sent Fliess Draft K, which follows here and is in many respects a full preliminary sketch of his second paper on the neuropsychoses of defence (1896b), completed very soon afterwards.

在 1895 年后半段的整个时期，弗洛伊德主要忙于一个根本性的理论问题：他正在形成中的心理学与既有的神经学之间的关系。他的思考最终导向那部未完成的作品，我们把它命名为《科学心理学大纲》[‘Project for a Scientific Psychology’]。这份草稿写于 1895 年 9 月与 10 月，按年代顺序本应出现在弗利斯文稿的这一位置。然而，它与文稿的其余部分如此远离，又构成一个如此庞大而自足的实体，以致似乎宜于把它以独立的形态印在本卷末尾。第 39 号信写于 1896 年 1 月 1 日，它与《大纲》联系得如此紧密（事实上，离开《大纲》它便不可理解），以致它也被从通信中的原位移出，作为《大纲》的附录印出。弗洛伊德在这整个时期也一直关注临床问题——这一点由以下事实决定性地表明：就在他寄出这封信（1896 年 1 月 1 日）的同一天，他也寄给弗利斯草稿 K；此稿紧随其后，并在许多方面是他在此后不久即完成的第二篇论防御性神经精神病的论文（1896b）的一份完整的初步草图。
