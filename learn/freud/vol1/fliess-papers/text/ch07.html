DRAFT E – HOW ANXIETY ORIGINATES

The core and mainstay of the whole business remains, of course, the fact that, as a result of particular sexual noxae, even healthy people can acquire the different forms of neurosis. The bridge to a wider view [Auffassung]ᵀ is afforded by the fact that, where a neurosis develops without a sexual noxa, a similar disturbance of the sexual affects can be shown to have been present from the first. ‘Sexual affect’ is, of course, taken in the broadest sense, as an excitation of definite quantity.¹

当然，整件事的核心与支柱始终是这样一个事实：由于特定的性有害因素[sexual noxae]，即便是健康人也能获得各种不同形式的神经症。通往更广阔见解[Auffassung]ᵀ的桥梁，则由下述事实提供：凡在并无性有害因素之处发展出神经症者，皆可证明其性情感的类似紊乱自始即已存在。当然，“性情感”[sexual affect] 是在最宽泛的意义上使用的，指的是一种具一定量[quantity]的兴奋。¹

I might bring you my latest example in support of this thesis:

我或可举出最近一例，以支持这一命题：

A man, aged 42, strong and handsome, suddenly developed a neurasthenic dyspepsia at the age of 30 with a loss of 25 kilos, and since then has lived in a reduced and neurasthenic state. At the time at which this occurred, incidentally, he was engaged to be married and was emotionally disturbed by his fiancée's illness. Apart from this, however, there were no sexual noxae. He masturbated, perhaps only for a year, from 16 to 17; from 17 onwards he had normal intercourse; scarcely ever coitus interruptus; no excesses, no abstinence. He himself attributes the cause to the strain he put on his constitution till the age of 30: to his having worked, drunk and smoked a great deal and led an irregular life. But this strong man, subject to [only] stock noxae, was never (never from 17 to 30) properly potent: he could never carry out coitus more than once at a time; he always emitted hurriedly, never made full use of his [initial] successes with women, never found his way into the vagina quickly. What was the origin of this limitation? I cannot tell. But it is remarkable that it was present precisely in him. By the way, I have treated two of his sisters for neuroses; one of them is among my most successful cures of neurasthenic dyspepsia.

一名 42 岁男子，健壮而英俊，30 岁时突发神经衰弱性消化不良[neurasthenic dyspepsia]，体重减轻 25 公斤，此后一直处于虚弱、神经衰弱的状态。附带一提，此事发生时他正订婚，并因未婚妻患病而情绪受扰。除此以外，并无性有害因素。他曾手淫，或许只有一年，自 16 岁至 17 岁；17 岁起有正常性交；几乎从不中断性交[coitus interruptus]；无过度，亦无禁欲。他本人将病因归为 30 岁前对体质的耗损：工作过度、饮酒吸烟甚多、生活不规律。然而这名强壮的男子，所遭遇的〔不过〕是寻常的有害因素，却从未（自 17 岁至 30 岁从未）有过正常的性能力：他一次连续的同房从不能超过一回；总是匆匆射精，从未充分利用他在女人身上的〔最初〕成功，也从未迅速找到进入阴道之途。这一局限从何而来？我说不出。但值得注意的是，它恰恰出现在他身上。顺便说，我曾为他两位姐妹治疗神经症；其中一位属我最成功的神经衰弱性消化不良治愈案例。

HOW ANXIETY ORIGINATES

With an unerring hand you have raised the question at the point which I feel is the weak one. All I know about it is this:

你以不差分毫的手，把问题提到我恰恰觉得薄弱的那一点上。我所知道的全部，只是这些：

It quickly became clear to me that the anxiety of my neurotic patients had a great deal to do with sexuality; and in particular it struck me with what certainty coitus interruptus practised on a woman leads to anxiety neurosis. I then began by following various false scents. I thought that the anxiety from which the patients suffer should be looked on as a continuation of the anxiety felt during the sexual act – that is to say, that it was in reality a hysterical symptom. Indeed, the connections between anxiety neurosis and hysteria are obvious enough. Two things might give rise to the feeling of anxiety in coitus interruptus: in the woman, a fear of becoming pregnant, in the man worry that his [contraceptive] device might fail. I then convinced myself from a number of cases that anxiety neurosis also appeared where there was no question of these two factors, where it was basically of no importance to the people concerned whether they had a baby. Thus the anxiety of anxiety neurosis was not a continued, recollected, hysterical one.¹

我很快就明白，我的神经症病人的焦虑与性有极大的关系；尤其令我震动的是，施加于女子的中断性交[coitus interruptus]何等确定地导致焦虑神经症。我起初循着几条错误的线索走。我以为病人所苦的焦虑，应看作性行为过程中所感到的焦虑之延续——也就是说，它实际上是一个癔症性症状。的确，焦虑神经症与癔症之间的联系已足够明显。在中断性交中，有两件事可能引起焦虑感：在女子是对怀孕的恐惧，在男子是担心其〔避孕〕器具可能失效。随后，我从若干病例中说服自己：焦虑神经症也出现在与这两个因素无关的场合，出现在当事人对有否孩子根本上并不在意之处。所以焦虑神经症的焦虑，并不是一种延续的、回忆起的、癔症性的焦虑。¹

A second extremely important point became established for me from the following observation. Anxiety neurosis affects women who are anaesthetic in coitus just as much as sensitive ones. This is most remarkable, but it can only mean that the source of the anxiety is not to be looked for in the psychical sphere. It must accordingly lie in the physical sphere; it is a physical factor in sexual life that produces anxiety. But what factor?

还有第二个极其重要的要点，由下述观察为我确立。焦虑神经症之侵袭性交中感觉缺失[anaesthetic]的女子，与侵袭感觉敏锐者一样多。这极为可怪，但它只能意味着：焦虑的来源不应在心理领域[psychical sphere]中寻找。它必在身体领域[physical sphere]中；是性生活中的某个身体因素产生了焦虑。但究竟是哪个因素？

With this aim in view, I brought together the cases in which I found anxiety arising from a sexual cause. They seemed at first to be quite heterogeneous:

带着这一目的，我把凡我发现焦虑由性原因而起的病例都汇集起来。它们起初显得十分杂乱异质：

(1) Anxiety in virginal people (sexual observations and information, foreshadowings of sexual life); confirmed by numerous instances of both sexes, predominantly female. Not infrequently there is a hint at an intermediate link – a sensation like an erection arising in the genitals.

（1）童贞[virginal]者的焦虑（性的观察与见闻、对性生活的预感）；两性皆有大量实例可为印证，而以女性居多。不罕见的是，还暗示着一个中间环节——生殖器内升起一种类似勃起的感觉。

(2) Anxiety in intentionally abstinent people, prudes (a type of neuropath), men and women characterized by pedantry and a feeling for cleanliness, who regard everything sexual as horrible. The same people tend to work their anxiety over into phobias, obsessional actions [Zwangshandlungen], folie du doute.²

（2）有意禁欲者的焦虑，即假正经者[prudes]（神经病态者之一型），其人为男女皆有，以迂执与洁癖为特征，视一切性事为可怖。这类人也往往把他们的焦虑加工成恐怖症[phobias]、强迫行为[Zwangshandlungen]、疑虑狂[folie du doute]。²

(3) Anxiety of necessarily abstinent people, women who are neglected by their husbands or are not satisfied on account of lack of potency.

（3）被迫禁欲者的焦虑，即被丈夫冷落的女子，或因对方性能力不足而不得满足的女子。

This form of anxiety neurosis can certainly be acquired, and, owing to subsidiary circumstances, is often combined with neurasthenia.

这一形式的焦虑神经症当然可以是后天获得的，而且由于种种附带情形，常与神经衰弱合并出现。

(4) Anxiety of women living in coitus interruptus, or, what is similar, of women whose husbands suffer from ejaculatio praecox – of people, therefore, who do not obtain satisfaction from physical stimulation.

（4）处于中断性交中的女子的焦虑，或与之相似者，即丈夫患早泄[ejaculatio praecox]的女子——故此皆由身体刺激而不得满足之人。

(5) Anxiety of men practising coitus interruptus, even more of men who excite themselves in various ways and do not employ their erection for coitus.

（5）施行中断性交的男子的焦虑，尤以那些以各种方式自我刺激、却不将勃起用于性交的男子为甚。

(6) Anxiety of men who go beyond their desire or strength, older people whose potency is diminishing,¹ but who nevertheless forcibly bring about coitus.

（6）逾越其欲望或力量的男子的焦虑，即年长者、性能力正在衰退者，¹ 却仍强行促成性交。

(7) Anxiety of men who abstain on occasion: of youngish men who have married older women, by whom they are in fact disgusted, or of neurasthenics who have been diverted from masturbation by intellectual occupation without making up for it by coitus, or of men whose potency is beginning to grow weak and who abstain in marriage on account of sensations post coitum [cf. p. 227 n. 1 below].

（7）偶或禁欲的男子的焦虑：较年轻的男子娶了年长的女子、实际上对之厌恶者；或因从事脑力工作而戒绝手淫、却未以性交相补偿的神经衰弱者；或性能力已开始衰退、因性交后[post coitum]的感受而在婚姻中禁欲的男子［参见下文第 227 页注 1］。

In the remaining cases the connection between the anxiety and sexual life was not obvious. (It could be established theoretically.)

在其余病例中，焦虑与性生活之间的联系并不明显。（此联系可在理论上确立。）

How are all these separate cases to be brought together? What recurs in them most frequently is abstinence. Taught by the fact that even anaesthetic women are subject to anxiety after coitus interruptus, one is inclined to say that it is a question of a physical accumulation of excitation – that is, an accumulation of physical sexual tension. The accumulation is the consequence of discharge being prevented. Thus anxiety neurosis is a neurosis of damming up, like hysteria; hence their similarity. And since no anxiety at all is contained in what is accumulated, the position is expressed by saying that anxiety has arisen by transformation out of the accumulated sexual tension.²

这些彼此分立的病例，要怎样才能归拢到一起？其中反复出现最多的，是禁欲。既然连感觉缺失的女子在中断性交之后也受焦虑之苦，人便倾向于说：这里所关涉的是兴奋在身体上的积累——也就是说，身体性张力[physical sexual tension]的积累。这种积累是排出受阻的结果。所以焦虑神经症是一种壅塞[damming up]而成的神经症，与癔症一样；其相似性即由此而来。又因为所积累之物中根本不含任何焦虑，于是这一立场便表述为：焦虑是由积累的性张力经转化而生。²

Some knowledge arrived at simultaneously about the mechanism of melancholia may be interpolated here. Quite particularly often melancholics have been anaesthetic.³ They have no need for coitus (and no sensation in connection with it). But they have a great longing for love in its psychical form – one might say, psychical erotic tension. Where this accumulates and remains unsatisfied, melancholia develops. Here, then, we should have the counterpart to anxiety neurosis. Where physical sexual tension accumulates – anxiety neurosis. Where psychical sexual tension accumulates – melancholia.

此处可插叙一些同时获得的、关于忧郁症机制的认识。极为常见的是，忧郁症患者曾是感觉缺失者。³ 他们并不需要性交（与之相关亦无感觉）。但他们对爱有一种极大的渴求，渴求其心理形式——或许可称之为心理的性爱张力[psychical erotic tension]。凡此积累而不得满足之处，便发展出忧郁症。如此，我们在此便得到了焦虑神经症的对应物。凡身体性张力积累之处——焦虑神经症。凡心理的性张力积累之处——忧郁症。

But why does this transformation into anxiety occur when there is an accumulation? At this point we ought to enter into the normal mechanism for dealing with accumulated tension. What we are concerned with here is the second case – the case of endogenous excitation [Erregung]. Things are simpler in the case of exogenous excitation. The source of excitation is outside and sends into the psyche an accretion of excitation which is dealt with according to its quantity. For that purpose any reaction suffices which diminishes the psychical excitation by the same quantum. [Cf. footnote 1, p. 199 above.]

但为什么在有积累时会发生这种向焦虑的转化？此处我们本应进入处理积累张力的正常机制。我们在此所关涉的是第二种情形——内生兴奋[Erregung]的情形。外源兴奋的情形较为简单。兴奋之源在外部，它向心理发送一份兴奋增量，此增量按其量而被处理。为此目的，任何能减去同等量心理兴奋的反应便已足够。［参见上文第 199 页注 1。］

But it is otherwise with endogenous tension, the source of which lies in one's own body (hunger, thirst, the sexual drive [Sexualtrieb]ᵀ). In this case only specific reactions are of use¹ – reactions which prevent the further occurrence of the excitation in the end-organs concerned, whether those reactions are attainable with a large or small expenditure [of energy]. Here we may picture the endogenous tension as growing either continuously or discontinuously, but in any case as only being noticed when it has reached a certain threshold. It is only above this threshold that it is turned to account psychically, that it enters into relation with certain groups of ideas,² which thereupon set about producing the specific remedies. Thus physical sexual tension above a certain value arouses psychical libido,³ which then leads to coitus, etc. If the specific reaction fails to ensue, the physico-psychical tension (the sexual affect) increases immeasurably. It becomes a disturbance, but there is still no ground for its transformation. In anxiety neurosis, however, such a transformation does occur, and this suggests the idea that there things go wrong in the following way. The physical tension increases, reaches the threshold value at which it can arouse psychical affect; but for some reasons the psychical linkage offered to it remains insufficient: a sexual affect cannot be formed, because there is something lacking in the psychical determinants. Accordingly, the physical tension, not being psychically bound, is transformed into – anxiety.¹

但内生张力则不然，其源头在于自己的身体之内（饥饿、口渴、性驱力[Sexualtrieb]ᵀ）。在此情形下，只有特定反应才有用¹——这类反应阻止该兴奋在相关终末器官中再度发生，无论达成这些反应所需〔能量〕耗费是大是小。此处我们可设想，内生张力或连续、或不连续地增长，但无论如何，只有当它达到某一阈限[threshold]时才被察觉。只有超过此阈限，它才被心理地加以利用，才与某些观念群[groups of ideas]² 发生关系，而这些观念群随即着手产生特定的补救之道。所以，超过某一数值的身体性张力唤起心理的里比多[libido]，³ 后者进而导致性交，等等。若特定反应未能随之发生，则身体—心理的张力（即性情感）便无限增长。它成为一种扰乱，但仍无据以使其转化。然而在焦虑神经症中，此种转化确实发生，这就提示出这样一种想法：在那里，事情是以下述方式出了差错。身体张力增长，达到能唤起心理情感的阈限值；但由于某些原因，提供给它心理联结仍嫌不足：性情感无法形成，因为心理的决定因素中有某物缺失。于是，身体张力既未被心理地约束[bound]，便转化为——焦虑。¹

If we accept the theory so far, we shall have to insist that in anxiety neurosis there must be a deficit to be noted in sexual affect, in psychical libido. And this is confirmed by observation. If this connection is put before women patients, they are always indignant and declare that on the contrary they now have no desire whatever, etc. Men patients often confirm it as an observation that since suffering from anxiety they have felt no sexual desire.

若我们至此接受这一理论，便须坚持：在焦虑神经症中，性情感、心理的里比多方面必有应予指出的亏缺。而观察证实了这一点。若把这一联系摆到女病人面前，她们总是愤然否认，宣称恰恰相反，她们如今根本毫无欲望，等等。男病人则常以观察的方式加以证实：自患焦虑以来，他们便不感到任何性欲。

We will now see whether this mechanism agrees with the different cases enumerated above.

我们现在来看，这一机制是否与上文所列举的各病例相符。

(1) Virginal anxiety. Here the field of ideas which ought to take up the physical tension is not yet present, or is only insufficiently present; and there is in addition a psychical refusal which is a secondary result of education [Erziehungsresultat]ᵀ. This fits in very well.

（1）童贞的焦虑。此处，本应承接身体张力的观念领域[field of ideas]尚未存在，或仅不足地存在；此外还有一种心理的拒斥，那是教育[Erziehungsresultat]ᵀ 的次生结果。此说十分吻合。

(2) Anxiety of prudes. Here what we have is defence – outright psychical rejection, which makes any working over of the sexual tension impossible. Here too we have the case of the numerous obsessions. This fits in very well.

（2）假正经者的焦虑。此处我们所见的乃是防御——彻底的心理拒斥，使性张力的任何加工[working over]皆不可能。此处亦见众多强迫观念之病例。此说十分吻合。

(3) Anxiety of necessitated abstinence. This is essentially the same; for women of this kind mostly create a psychical rejection so as to avoid temptation. Here the rejection is a contingent one, in (2) it is a fundamental matter.

（3）被迫禁欲的焦虑。这本质上相同；因为这类女子多半会造出一种心理拒斥，以避诱惑。此处拒斥是偶然的，而在（2）中则是根本性的。

(4) Anxiety in women from coitus interruptus. Here the mechanism is simpler. It is a question of endogenous excitation which does not originate [spontaneously] but is induced, but not in an amount sufficient to be able to arouse psychical affect. An alienation² is artificially brought about between the physico-sexual act and its psychical working over. If afterwards the endogenous tension increases further on its own account, it cannot be worked over and generates anxiety. Here libido can be present, but not at the same time as anxiety.³ Thus here psychical rejection is followed by psychical alienation; tension of endogenous origin is followed by induced tension.

（4）女子因中断性交而生的焦虑。此处机制较为简单。所关涉的是内生兴奋，它并非〔自发〕而起，而是被引发的，但其量不足以唤起心理情感。在身体—性的行为与其心理加工之间，人为地造成了一种疏离[alienation]。² 若此后内生张力自行进一步增长，它便无法被加工，并产生焦虑。此处里比多可以在场，但不与焦虑同时在场。³ 所以此处心理拒斥之后继以心理疏离；内生来源的张力之后继以被引发的张力。

(5) Anxiety in men from coitus interruptus or reservatus. The case of coitus reservatus is the clearer; coitus interruptus may in part be regarded as subsumed under it. It is a question once again of psychical diversion, for attention is directed to another aim and is kept away from the working over of physical tension. The explanation of coitus interruptus, however, probably stands in need of improvement.

（5）男子因中断性交或保留性交而生的焦虑。保留性交[coitus reservatus]的情形较为清楚；中断性交或可部分视为归入其下。此处所关涉的仍是心理的转移[diversion]，因为注意被引向另一目标，而被带离对身体张力的加工。不过，对中断性交的解释，大概仍待改进。

(6) Anxiety in diminishing potency or insufficient libido. Insofar as this is not the transformation of physical tension into anxiety owing to senility, it is to be explained by the fact that insufficient psychical desire can be summoned up for the particular act.

（6）性能力衰退或里比多不足时的焦虑。凡此并非因衰老而致的身体张力向焦虑之转化，便须以如下事实解释：为那一具体行为所能唤起的心理欲望不足。

(7) Anxiety in men from disgust, or in abstinent neurasthenics. The former calls for no fresh explanation; the latter is perhaps a specially attenuated form of anxiety neurosis, for as a rule this occurs¹ properly only in potent men. It may be that the neurasthenic nervous system cannot tolerate an accumulation of physical tension, since masturbation involves becoming accustomed to frequent and complete absence of tension.

（7）男子因厌恶而生的焦虑，或禁欲的神经衰弱者的焦虑。前者无需新的解释；后者或许是一种特别减弱形式的焦虑神经症，因为通常此种情形¹ 只在性能力强健的男子身上才恰当出现。或许神经衰弱的神经系统不能耐受身体张力的积累，因为手淫使人习惯于频繁而完全的张力消解。

On the whole the agreement is not so bad. Where there is an abundant development of physical sexual tension but this cannot be turned into affect by psychical working over – because of insufficient development of psychical sexuality or because of the attempted suppression of the latter (defence), or of its falling into decay, or because of habitual alienation between physical and psychical sexuality – the sexual tension is transformed into anxiety. Thus a part is played in this by the accumulation of physical tension and the prevention of discharge in the psychical direction.

大体而言，吻合得还不算差。凡身体性张力有丰足的生成，却不能经由心理的加工而转化为情感之处——或因心理的性欲发展不足，或因企图压抑后者（防御），或因后者趋于衰败，或因身体性欲与心理性欲之间习惯性的疏离——性张力便转化为焦虑。因此，身体张力的积累与心理方向上排出的受阻，在此中皆起一份作用。

But why does the transformation take place precisely into anxiety? Anxiety is the sensation of the accumulation of another endogenous stimulus, the stimulus to breathing, a stimulus which is incapable of being worked over psychically apart from this; anxiety might therefore be employed for accumulated physical tension in general. Furthermore, if we examine the symptoms of anxiety neurosis more closely, we find in it the disjointed pieces of a major anxiety attack: viz. simple dyspnoea, simple palpitations, simple feelings of anxiety and a combination of these. Looked at more precisely, these are the paths of innervation which the psychosexual tension ordinarily traverses even when it is about to be worked over psychically. The dyspnoea and palpitations belong to coitus;¹ and, while ordinarily they are employed only as subsidiary paths of discharge, here they serve, so to speak, as the only outlets for the excitation. There is a kind of conversion in anxiety neurosis just as there is in hysteria (another instance of their similarity [p. 218 above]); but in hysteria it is psychical excitation that takes a wrong path exclusively into the somatic field, whereas here it is a physical tension, which cannot enter the psychical field and therefore remains on the physical path. The two are combined extremely often.

但为什么这转化恰是转化为焦虑？焦虑是另一种内生刺激——呼吸刺激——积累时的感觉，而这一刺激除此以外无法被心理地加工；因此焦虑或可被用于一般的身体张力积累。再者，若更仔细地考察焦虑神经症的症状，我们会从中发现一次重大焦虑发作被拆散的各部件：即单纯性呼吸困难、单纯性心悸、单纯的焦虑感，以及这些的组合。更精确地看，这些正是心理性张力即便在将被心理加工之时也照常走行的支配路径[paths of innervation]。呼吸困难与心悸本属于性交；¹ 而它们通常只被用作辅助的排出路径，此处却可说是充当了兴奋唯一的出口。在焦虑神经症中有一种转化，正如在癔症中一样（此又是二者相似之一例［见上文第 218 页］）；但在癔症中，走错路而全部进入躯体领域的是心理的兴奋，而此处则是身体的张力，它无法进入心理领域，因而留在身体路径上。二者极常结合。

That is as far as I have got today. The gaps need filling in badly. I think it is incomplete, I lack something; but I believe the foundation is right. Of course it is absolutely unripe for publicity. Suggestions, amplifications, indeed refutations and explanations will be received most gratefully.

今日我只能到此为止。这些缺口亟需填补。我以为它并不完整，我缺少某样东西；但我相信基础是对的。当然，它完全尚未成熟到可以公开。建议、补充，乃至反驳与说明，都将极为感激地领受。
