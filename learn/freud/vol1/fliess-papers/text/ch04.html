Letter 14. (October 6, 1893)

RSE 第 1 卷 · 弗利斯文稿摘录 · p.212–213 · 15 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

本篇小节
  1. 脚注

... Things become more and more complicated as confirmation comes in. Yesterday, for instance, I saw four new cases whose aetiology, as shown by the chronological data, could only be coitus interruptus. It may perhaps amuse you if I give a short account of them. They are far from being uniform.³

…事情随着印证陆续到来而变得愈加复杂。譬如，昨日我见到四个新病例，按其时间顺序资料所示，其病因学[aetiology]只能是中断性交[coitus interruptus]。若我略述一二，或许能让你觉得有趣。它们远非整齐划一。³

(1) Woman, aged 41; children, 16, 14, 11 and 7. Nervous trouble for the last 12 years; well during pregnancy; recurrence afterwards; not made worse by the last pregnancy. Attacks of giddiness with feeling of weakness, agoraphobia, anxious expectation, no trace of neurasthenia, little hysteria. Aetiology confirmed: simple [anxiety neurosis].

（1）女子，41 岁；子女 16、14、11 及 7 岁。过去 12 年神经疾患，孕期健康，产后复发，末次妊娠未加重。眩晕发作伴虚弱感、广场恐怖[agoraphobia]、焦虑性期待[anxious expectation]，无神经衰弱[neurasthenia]痕迹，癔症[hysteria]轻微。病因学[aetiology]确认：单纯性〔焦虑神经症〕。

(2) Woman, aged 24; children, 4 and 2. Since the spring of ’93 attacks of pain at night (from back to spine?) with insomnia; otherwise nothing; well during the day. Husband a commercial traveller; was at home for some time during the spring and just now. In the summer, while the husband was away, she was perfectly well. Coitus interruptus and great fear of having children. Hysteria, therefore.

（2）女子，24 岁；子女 4 岁及 2 岁。自 ’93 年春季起，夜间疼痛发作（由背部至脊柱？）伴失眠；此外并无他症；白天良好。丈夫是商业推销员；今春及最近曾在家一段时日。夏季丈夫外出期间，她完全健康。中断性交[coitus interruptus] 且极怕生育。故为癔症。

(3) Man, aged 42; children, 17, 16 and 13. Well till a year ago.¹ Then, on his father's death, sudden attack of anxiety with heart failure, hypochondriacal fears of cancer of the tongue; several months later a second attack, with cyanosis, intermittent pulse, fear of death, etc.; since then weakness, vertigo, agoraphobia, some dyspepsia. This is a case of pure anxiety neurosis accompanied by heart symptoms, after emotion; whereas coitus interruptus was apparently tolerated easily for ten years.²

（3）男子，42 岁；子女 17、16 及 13 岁。至一年前一直健康。¹ 其后因父亲去世，突发焦虑发作伴心力衰竭，以及对舌癌的疑病性恐惧[hypochondriacal]；数月后第二次发作，伴发绀[cyanosis]、脉搏间歇、死亡恐惧等；此后虚弱、眩晕、广场恐怖、轻度消化不良。这是一例单纯性焦虑神经症，伴心脏症状，发生于情绪激动之后；而中断性交则显然十年来都易被耐受。²

(4) Man, aged 34. Loss of appetite for the last three years; dyspepsia for the last year, with loss of 20 kilos, constipation. When these ceased, violent intracranial pressure when a sirocco was blowing;³ attacks of weakness with associated sensations, hysteriform clonic spasms. In this case, therefore, neurasthenia predominates. One child, aged 5. Since then coitus interruptus owing to wife's illness. At about the same time as his recovery from dyspepsia, normal intercourse was resumed.

（4）男子，34 岁。近三年食欲丧失；近一年消化不良，体重减轻 20 公斤，便秘。当这些症状消失后，西洛可风[sirocco] 吹拂时出现剧烈的颅内压增高；³ 虚弱发作伴连带感觉[associated sensations]、癔症样阵挛性抽搐。故此例以神经衰弱为主。一子，5 岁。此后因妻子患病而行中断性交。约在其消化不良痊愈的同时，恢复正常性交。

In view of these reactions to the same noxa, it calls for courage to insist on the specific nature, in my sense, of its effects. And yet it must be so; and there are certain points to go upon even in these four cases (simple anxiety neurosis – simple hysteria – anxiety neurosis with heart symptoms – neurasthenia with hysteria).

面对这些对同一有害因素[noxa] 的反应，仍要坚持其效果具有我所理解的特定性[specific nature]，是需要勇气的。然而事情必然如此；即便在这四例中（单纯性焦虑神经症——单纯性癔症——伴心脏症状的焦虑神经症——神经衰弱合并癔症），也有若干可供依循之点。

In (1), a very intelligent woman, there was no fear of having children; she has a simple anxiety neurosis.

在（1）中，一位极聪慧的女子并无怕生育之心；她患的是单纯性焦虑神经症。

In (2), a nice, stupid young woman, the anxiety was highly developed; after a short while she had hysteria for the first timeᵀ.

在（2）中，一位善良而愚钝的少妇，焦虑发展得极为明显；不久之后她第一次发作了癔症。ᵀ

Case (3), with anxiety neurosis⁴ and heart symptoms, was a very potent man, who was a great smoker.

第（3）例伴焦虑神经症⁴与心脏症状，是一名性能力很强、烟瘾极大的男子。

Case (4), on the contrary, was (without having masturbated) only moderately potent – frigid.

第（4）例则相反，（未曾手淫）性能力只是中等——性冷淡[frigid]。
