Letter 14. (October 6, 1893)

RSE 第 1 卷 · 弗利斯文稿摘录 · p.212 · 4 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠 · （未完）

... Things become more and more complicated as confirmation comes in. Yesterday, for instance, I saw four new cases whose aetiology, as shown by the chronological data, could only be coitus interruptus. It may perhaps amuse you if I give a short account of them. They are far from being uniform.³

…事情随着印证陆续到来而变得愈加复杂。譬如，昨日我见到四个新病例，按其时间顺序资料所示，其病因学[aetiology]只能是中断性交[coitus interruptus]。若我略述一二，或许能让你觉得有趣。它们远非整齐划一。³

(1) Woman, aged 41; children, 16, 14, 11 and 7. Nervous trouble for the last 12 years; well during pregnancy; recurrence afterwards; not made worse by the last pregnancy. Attacks of giddiness with feeling of weakness, agoraphobia, anxious expectation, no trace of neurasthenia, little hysteria. Aetiology confirmed: simple [anxiety neurosis].

（1）女子，41 岁；子女 16、14、11 及 7 岁。过去 12 年神经疾患，孕期健康，产后复发，末次妊娠未加重。眩晕发作伴虚弱感、广场恐怖[agoraphobia]、焦虑性期待[anxious expectation]，无神经衰弱[neurasthenia]痕迹，癔症[hysteria]轻微。病因学[aetiology]确认：单纯性[焦虑神经症]。

(2) Woman, aged 24; children, 4 and 2. Since the spring of ’93 attacks of pain at night (from back to spine?) with insomnia; otherwise

（2）女子，24 岁；子女 4 岁及 2 岁。自 ’93 年春季起，夜间疼痛发作（由背部至脊柱？）伴失眠；此外——（未完）
