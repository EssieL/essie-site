Draft A. (Undated. ? End of 1892)

PROBLEMS

(1) Is the anxiety [Angst]ᵀ of anxiety neuroses derived from the inhibition [Hemmung]ᵀ of the sexual function [Sexualfunktion]ᵀ or from the anxiety linked with their aetiology?

（1）焦虑神经症[anxiety neuroses]的焦虑[Angst]ᵀ，究竟是源于性机能[Sexualfunktion]ᵀ 的抑制[Hemmung]ᵀ，还是源于与其病因学[aetiology]相连的焦虑？

(2) To what extent does a healthy person respond to later sexual traumas differently from someone with a disposition due to masturbation? Only quantitatively? Or qualitatively?

（2）健康的人对日后性创伤[sexual traumas]的反应，在多大程度上不同于因手淫[masturbation]而具有素质[disposition]者？仅仅在量上不同？还是在质上也不同？

(3) Is simple coitus reservatus (condom) a noxa at all?²

（3）单纯的 reservatus 性交[coitus reservatus]（避孕套[condom]）究竟是不是一种损害[noxa]？²

(4) Is there an innate neurasthenia with innate sexual weakness or is it always acquired in youth? (From nurses, from being masturbated by someone else.)³

（4）是否存在一种带有先天性的性衰弱的先天神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，抑或它总在青年期获得？〔源于保姆、源于被他人施行手淫。〕³

(5) Is heredity anything other than a multiplier?⁴

（5）遗传[heredity]除却一种倍增器[multiplier]之外，还可能是别的什么吗？⁴

(6) What plays a part in the aetiology of periodic depression?

（6）在周期性抑郁[periodic depression]的病因学[aetiology]中，何者发挥作用？

(7) Is sexual anaesthesia in women anything other than a result of impotence? Can it of itself cause neuroses?⁵

（7）女性的性感觉缺失[sexual anaesthesia]除却无能[impotence]的后果之外，还可能是别的什么吗？它自身能否引发神经症[neuroses]？⁵

THESES [1–98]

(1) No neurasthenia or analogous neurosis exists without a disturbance of the sexual function.

（1）不存在哪一种神经衰弱[neurasthenia]或类似的神经症，其性机能[sexual function]不曾发生紊乱。

(2) This either has an immediate causal effect or acts as a disposition for other factors, but always in such a way that without it the other factors cannot bring about neurasthenia.

（2）它或具有直接的因果效应，或作为其他因素的素质[disposition]而起作用；但无论如何，若无它，其他因素便不能酿成神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。

(3) Neurasthenia in men, on account of its aetiology, is accompanied by relative impotence.

（3）男子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，由于其病因学[aetiology]，伴有相对的性无能[impotence]。

(4) Neurasthenia in women is a direct consequence of neurasthenia in men, through the agency of this reduction in their potency.

（4）女子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，是男子神经衰弱经由其 potency 之此种减弱[reduction in their potency]而直接导致的后果。

(5) Periodic depression is a form of anxiety neurosis, which, apart from this, is manifested in phobias and anxiety attacks.

（5）周期性抑郁[periodic depression]是焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]的一种形式，除此而外，它亦表现于恐怖症[phobias]与焦虑发作[anxiety attacks]之中。

(6) Anxiety neurosis is in part a consequence of inhibition of the sexual function.

（6）焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]部分地是性机能[sexual function]受抑制[inhibition]的后果。

(7) Simple excess and overwork are not aetiological factors.⁶

（7）单纯的纵欲[excess]与过劳[overwork]并非病因学[aetiological]因素。⁶

(8) Hysteria in neurasthenic neuroses indicates suppression of the accompanying affects.

（8）神经衰弱性神经症[neurasthenic neuroses]中的癔症[hysteria]，标示着对伴随性情感[affect]的压抑[suppression]。

GROUPS [FOR OBSERVATION]

(1) Men and women who have remained healthy.

（1）始终保有健康的男女。

(2) Sterile women, where preventive traumas in marriage are absent.

（2）不孕[sterile]女子——婚姻中不存在防范性创伤[preventive traumas]者。

(3) Women infected with gonorrhoea.

（3）感染淋病[gonorrhoea]的女子。

(4) Loose-living men who are gonorrhoeal, and who are on that account protected in every respect, being aware of their hypospermia.

（4）生活放荡且患淋病[gonorrhoeal]的男子；正因如此，他们于各方面皆受保护，因其自知患有精液减少[hypospermia]。

(5) Members of severely tainted families who have remained healthy.

（5）出身于重度污损[tainted]家族而始终保有健康者。

(6) Observations from countries in which particular sexual abnormalities are endemic.

（6）来自某些特定性异常[sexual abnormalities]呈地方流行性[endemic]之国家的观察。

AETIOLOGICAL FACTORS

(1) Exhaustion owing to abnormal [forms of] satisfaction. Example: masturbation.⁵

（1）由反常[形式]的满足[abnormal satisfaction]所致的耗竭[exhaustion]。例：手淫[masturbation]。⁵

(2) Inhibition of the sexual function. Example: coitus interruptus.

（2）性机能[sexual function]的抑制[inhibition]。例：中断性交[coitus interruptus]。

(3) Affects accompanying these practices.

（3）伴随这些做法的情感[affects]。

(4) Sexual traumas before the age of understanding.

（4）理解年龄[age of understanding]之前的性创伤[sexual traumas]。

脚注 / Footnotes

1 [Undated. The editors of the Anfänge, 74 n., suggest that it was written towards the end of 1892. Freud and Fliess were at that time working on the problem of neurasthenia. Ingeborg Meyer-Palmedo suggests that the dating is confirmed by the fact that Freud promises in a letter dated January 5, 1893, to rewrite the ‘Aetiology of the Neuroses’, which he then submitted to Fliess on February 8, 1893 (Draft B). At all events, Draft A deals with much the same topics as Draft B, in abbreviated form. All these early drafts, up to and including Draft E, are mainly concerned with anxiety neurosis and neurasthenia, questions which were brought to a head in the first paper on anxiety neurosis (1895b), published on January 15, 1895. – This manuscript is almost unique in being written, for some unknown reason, in Roman script instead of Freud’s usual Gothic. In a letter to Ernest Jones of November 20, 1926, Freud wrote: ‘You will be astonished when I disclose what it is that hinders my correspondence with you. It is a classic example of the petty restrictions to which our nature is subject. It is that I find it very hard to write German in Roman characters, as I am now doing. All fluency – inspiration one would say on a higher plane – at once leaves me. You have often told me that you cannot read Gothic handwriting.’ (Jones, 1957, 138.)]

1. ［未注明日期。《开端》[Anfänge] 编者注第 74 页认为，它写于 1892 年底前后。当时弗洛伊德与弗利斯正致力于神经衰弱[neurasthenia]问题。英格博格·迈耶-帕尔梅多（Ingeborg Meyer-Palmedo）指出，如下事实可印证此一系年：弗洛伊德在 1893 年 1 月 5 日的信中承诺重写《神经症的病因学》[Aetiology of the Neuroses]，并于 1893 年 2 月 8 日将该文提交给弗利斯（即草稿 B）。无论如何，草稿 A 所涉及的主题与草稿 B 大致相同，只是更为简略。包括草稿 E 在内的所有这些早期草稿，主要关切焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]与神经衰弱[neurasthenia]；这些问题在 1895 年 1 月 15 日发表的首篇焦虑神经症论文（1895b）中达到顶点。——这份手稿几乎独一无二地——不知何故——以罗马体[Roman script]写就，而非弗洛伊德惯常的哥特体[Gothic]。在 1926 年 11 月 20 日致欧内斯特·琼斯[Ernest Jones]的信中，弗洛伊德写道：“当我说出究竟是什么妨碍了我与你的通信，你定会惊讶。这不过是我们天性所受种种细小限制的一个典型例子。那就是：我发现用罗马字母书写德文极为困难，正如我此刻所为。一切流畅——若说在更高层面，便是灵感——顿时离我而去。你常对我说，你读不了哥特体手书。”（Jones, 1957, 138.）］

2 [Freud seems to be asking whether coitus reservatus is a noxa when there is no previous history of masturbation. He replies to this question in the affirmative in Draft B, p. 209 below.]

2. 弗洛伊德似乎在问：当既往无手淫史时，reservatus 性交[coitus reservatus]是否仍属损害[noxa]。他在草稿 B（下文第 209 页）中对此作了肯定的回答。

3 [This seems to be the first indication of the general hypothesis of sexual seduction in the aetiology of neuroses, assuming the dating of this draft is correct.]

3. 若此草稿的系年无误，这似乎便是关于“性诱惑[sexual seduction]构成神经症病因学[aetiology of neuroses]之一般假设”的最早迹象。

4 [This analogy from an electric circuit appears (among other places) in the second paper on anxiety neurosis (1895f), RSE, 3, 123 and n.]

4. 这一借自电路[electric circuit]的比喻，亦出现于（诸多场合之一）第二篇焦虑神经症论文（1895f），RSE 第 3 卷，第 123 页及注。

5 [Note at the bottom of MS. page:] Delusion of smallness, sexual inadequacy.

5. ［手稿页底注：］弱小妄想[Delusion of smallness]、性能力欠缺[sexual inadequacy]。

6 [This view is expressed repeatedly in Freud’s early writings, e.g. Freud (1895b), RSE, 3, 94, and (1898a), ibid., 3, 267, but was qualified later in (1917c), ibid., 23, 204 n. 2.]

6. 这一观点在弗洛伊德早期著作中屡有表述，例如弗洛伊德（1895b），RSE 第 3 卷，第 94 页；以及（1898a），同前，第 3 卷，第 267 页；但后来在（1917c），同前，第 23 卷，第 204 页注 2 中有所修正。
