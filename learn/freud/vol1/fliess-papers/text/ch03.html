DRAFT B – THE AETIOLOGY OF THE NEUROSES

I am writing the whole thing down a second time, for you, my dear friend, and for the sake of our common labours. You will of course keep the draft away from your young wife.¹

我将整篇重新写下一遍，为你，我的挚友，亦为我们共同的劳作。你自当使这份草稿远离你年轻的妻子。¹

1. It may be taken as a recognized fact that neurasthenia is a frequent consequence of an abnormal sexual life. The assertion, however, which I wish to make and to test by my observations is that neurasthenia is always only a sexual neurosis.

1. 神经衰弱[neurasthenia]是反常的性生活屡见不鲜的后果，此点可视作公认的事实。然而，我欲借自己的观察提出并检验的断言是：神经衰弱[neurasthenia]永远只是一种性神经症[sexual neurosis]。

I have adopted a similar point of view (along with Breuer) in regard to hysteria. Traumatic hysteria was well known; what we asserted beyond this was that every hysteria that is not hereditary is traumatic.² In the same way I am now asserting that every neurasthenia is sexual.

关于癔症[hysteria]，我曾（与布洛伊尔一道）采取类似的观点。创伤性癔症[traumatic hysteria]为人熟知；我们于此之外所断言者，乃是：每一并非遗传而来的癔症[hysteria]都是创伤性的。² 同样，我现在断言：每一神经衰弱[neurasthenia]都是性（神经症）性的。

We will for the moment leave on one side the question of whether hereditary disposition and, secondarily, toxic influences can produce genuine neurasthenia, or whether what appears to be hereditary neurasthenia in fact also goes back to early sexual exhaustion. If there is such a thing as hereditary neurasthenia, the questions arise of whether the status nervosus in the hereditary cases should not be distinguished from neurasthenia, what relation at all it has to the corresponding symptoms in childhood, and so on.

我们暂且将如下问题搁置一旁：遗传素质[hereditary disposition]以及（其次）毒性影响[toxic influences]能否产生真正的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，抑或那看似遗传性的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]实际上同样可回溯至早年的性耗竭[sexual exhaustion]。倘若真有遗传性的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]存在，则随之而来的是这样一些问题：在遗传病例中的神经质状态[status nervosus]是否不应与神经衰弱[neurasthenia]区分开来，它究竟与童年期的相应症状有何关联，等等。

In the first instance, therefore, my contention will be restricted to acquired neurasthenia. What I am asserting can accordingly be formulated as follows. In the aetiology of a nervous affection we must distinguish (1) the necessary precondition without which the state cannot come about at all, and (2) the precipitating factors. The relation between these two can be pictured thus. If the necessary precondition has operated sufficiently, the affection sets in as an inevitable consequence; if it has not operated sufficiently, the result of its operation is in the first place a disposition to the affection which ceases to be latent as soon as a sufficient amount of one of the secondary factors supervenes. Thus what is lacking for full effect in the first aetiology can be replaced by the aetiology of the second order. The aetiology of the second order can, however, be dispensed with, while that of the first order is indispensable.³

因此，首先我的主张将仅限于获得性神经衰弱[acquired neurasthenia]。我之所断言，便可表述如下。在某一神经疾患[nervous affection]的病因学[aetiology]中，我们必须区分：(1) 必要的先决条件——缺之则此状态根本无从产生；以及 (2) 诱发因素[precipitating factors]。二者之间的关系可如此描绘：若必要先决条件已充分起作用，该疾患便作为不可避免的后果而发作；若它未充分起作用，则其运作的结果首先是一种对该疾患的素质[disposition]，一旦有足量的某一次级因素介入，此素质便不再潜伏。由此可知，第一重病因学中所缺而未能达致充分效应者，可由第二序病因学[aetiology of the second order]所替代。然而，第二序病因学可以省去，而第一序病因学则不可或缺。³

If this aetiological formula is applied to our present case, we arrive at this. Sexual exhaustion can by itself alone provoke neurasthenia. If it fails to achieve this by itself, it has such an effect on the disposition of the nervous system that physical illness, depressive affects and overwork (toxic influences) can no longer be tolerated without [leading to] neurasthenia. Without sexual exhaustion, however, all these factors are incapable of generating neurasthenia. They bring about normal fatigue, normal sorrow, normal physical weakness, but they only continue to give evidence of how much ‘of these detrimental influences a normal person can tolerate’.⁴

若将这一病因学[aetiological]公式运用于我们当前的病例，我们便得到如下结论。性耗竭[sexual exhaustion]仅凭自身便足以引发神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。若它未能凭自身做到这一点，它便对神经系统的素质[disposition]产生如此效应，以致身体疾病、抑郁性情感[depressive affects]与过劳[overwork]（毒性影响[toxic influences]）若无（导致）神经衰弱[neurasthenia]便再也无法被容忍。然而，若无性耗竭[sexual exhaustion]，所有这些因素皆无力生成神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。它们只造成正常的疲乏、正常的悲愁、正常的身体虚弱，但它们持续证明的仅仅是：“一个正常人究竟能容忍多少这类有害影响”。⁴

I will consider neurasthenia in men and in women separately.

我将分别考察男子与女子的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。

Neurasthenia in males is acquired at puberty and becomes manifest in the patient’s twenties. Its source is masturbation, the frequency of which runs completely parallel with the frequency of male neurasthenia.⁵ One can observe in the circle of one’s acquaintances that (at all events in urban populations) those who have been seduced by women at an early age have escaped neurasthenia. When this noxa has operated long and intensely, it turns the person concerned into a sexual neurasthenic, whose potency, too, has been impaired; the intensity of the cause is paralleled by a lifelong persistence of the condition. Further evidence of the causal connection lies in the fact that a sexual neurasthenic is always a general neurasthenic at the same time.

男子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]获得于青春期[puberty]，并在患者二十多岁时显现。其源在手淫[masturbation]，而手淫之频度与男子神经衰弱[neurasthenia]之频度完全平行。⁵ 于相识者圈中可观察到：那些在幼年即被女子引诱的人（无论如何，在城市人口中）得以免于神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。当这一损害[noxa]长久而强烈地起作用时，它便将当事人变为一名性神经衰弱者[sexual neurasthenic]，其 potency 亦已受损；原因之强度与病症终身缠绵相并行。因果关系之又一佐证在于：一名性神经衰弱者[sexual neurasthenic]同时总是一名一般性神经衰弱者。

If the noxa has not been sufficiently intense, it will have (in accordance with the formula given above) an effect on the patient’s disposition; so that later, if provoking factors supervene, it will produce neurasthenia, which those factors alone would not have produced. Intellectual work – cerebral neurasthenia; normal sexual activity – spinal neurasthenia, etc.

若该损害[noxa]之强度不足，它便（依上述公式）对患者的素质[disposition]发生作用；以致日后若有诱发因素[provoking factors]介入，它便会酿成神经衰弱[neurasthenia]——而单凭那些因素本不足以酿成。心智劳作[intellectual work]——脑型神经衰弱[cerebral neurasthenia]；正常的性活动——脊髓型神经衰弱[spinal neurasthenia]，等等。

In intermediate cases we find the neurasthenia of youth, which typically begins with and runs its course accompanied by dyspepsia, etc., and which terminates at marriage.

在中间型病例中，我们见到青春型神经衰弱[neurasthenia of youth]——它典型地以消化不良[dyspepsia]等症伴随开始并走完病程，且终止于婚姻。

The second noxa, which affects men at a later age, makes its impact on a nervous system which is either intact or which has been disposed to neurasthenia through masturbation. The question is whether it can lead to detrimental results even in the former case; probably it can. Its effect is manifest in the second case, where it revives the neurasthenia of youth and creates new symptoms. This second noxa is onanismus conjugalis – incomplete coition in order to prevent conception. In the case of men all the methods of achieving this seem to fall into line: they operate with varying intensity according to the subject’s earlier disposition, but do not actually differ qualitatively. Even normal coition is not tolerated by those with a strong disposition or by chronic neurasthenics; and beyond this, intolerance of the condom, of extra-vaginal coition and of coitus interruptus take their toll. A healthy man will tolerate all of these for quite a long time, but even so not indefinitely. After a certain time he behaves like the disposed subject. His only advantage over the masturbator is the privilege of a longer latency or the fact that on every occasion he needs a provoking cause. Here coitus interruptus proves to be the main noxa and produces its characteristic effect even in non-disposed subjects.

第二种损害[noxa]影响较晚年龄的男子，作用于要么完好、要么已因手淫[masturbation]而具有神经衰弱[neurasthenia]素质的神经系统。问题在于：即便在前一种情形中，它是否也能导致有害结果；或许它能。其效应在后一种情形中显而易见——它复活了青春型神经衰弱[neurasthenia of youth]并制造新症状。这第二种损害[noxa]便是婚内遗精[onanismus conjugalis]——为防受孕而不完全的性交。就男子而言，达成此目的的一切方法似乎都归入同一序列：它们依主体早先的素质[disposition]而以不同强度起作用，但在性质上其实并无差异。即便是正常的性交，亦不为具有强烈素质[disposition]者或慢性神经衰弱[chronic neurasthenics]患者所容忍；此外，对避孕套[condom]、阴道外性交[extra-vaginal coition]与中断性交[coitus interruptus]的不耐受亦各有所耗。健康男子能将这一切容忍相当长的时间，但即便如此也非无限。过一段时间后，他便如那具有素质者一般行事。他相对于手淫者唯一的优势，是享有更长的潜伏期[latency]，或在于每一次他都需要一个诱发原因[provoking cause]。在此，中断性交[coitus interruptus]被证明是主要的损害[noxa]，且即便在无素质者身上亦产生其特有之效应。

Neurasthenia in females. Normally, girls are sound and not neurasthenic; and this is true as well of young married women, in spite of all the sexual traumas of this period of life. In comparatively rare cases neurasthenia appears in married women and in older unmarried ones in its pure form; it is then to be regarded as having arisen spontaneously and in the same manner [as in men]. Far more often neurasthenia in a married woman is derived from neurasthenia in a man or is produced simultaneously. In that case there is almost always an admixture of hysteria and we have the common mixed neurosis of women.

女子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。通常，少女是健全的，不患神经衰弱[neurasthenic]；年轻已婚女子亦然，尽管此一人生阶段充斥着各种性创伤[sexual traumas]。在相对罕见的病例中，神经衰弱[neurasthenia]以纯粹形式出现于已婚女子与年长未婚女子身上；此时它应被视为自发产生，且与（男子身上）同一方式。更常见的是，已婚女子的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]源自男子的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，或与之一并产生。在那种情形下，几乎总伴有癔症[hysteria]的混入，于是我们便有了常见的妇女混合型神经症[mixed neurosis of women]。

The mixed neurosis of women is derived from neurasthenia in men in all those not infrequent cases in which the man, being a sexual neurasthenic, suffers from impaired potency. The admixture of hysteria results directly from the holding back of the excitation [Erregung] of the act. The poorer the man’s potency, the more the woman’s hysteria predominates; so that in reality a sexually neurasthenic man makes his wife not so much neurasthenic as hysterical.

妇女的混合型神经症[mixed neurosis of women]源自男子的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，此情形并不罕见：该男子身为性神经衰弱者[sexual neurasthenic]，苦于 potency 受损。癔症[hysteria]的混入直接源于对性行为之兴奋[Erregung]的遏止[holding back]。男子之 potency 越差，女子之癔症[hysteria]便越占主导；以致实际上，一名性神经衰弱的男子使他的妻子与其说是神经衰弱[neurasthenic]，不如说是癔症性[hysterical]的。

This neurosis arises, with neurasthenia in males, during the second onset of sexual noxae, which is of far greater significance for a woman, assuming that she is sound. Thus we come across far more neurotic men during the first decade of puberty and far more neurotic women during the second. In the latter case this is the result of the noxae due to the prevention of conception. It is not easy to arrange them in order and in general none of them should be regarded as entirely innocuous to women; so that even in the most favourable case (a condom) women, being the more exacting partners, will scarcely escape slight neurasthenia. A great deal will obviously depend on the two dispositions: whether (1) she herself was neurasthenic before marriage or whether (2) she was made hysterico-neurasthenic during the period of free intercourse [without contraceptive devices].

这一神经症[neurosis]随着男子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]，发生于性损害[sexual noxae]的第二次发作期间；假定女子是健全的，则这一发作对她意义重大得多。于是我们在青春期[puberty]的头十年中遇见远为更多的神经症[neurotic]男子，而在第二个十年中遇见远为更多的神经症[neurotic]女子。后一种情形乃是防孕[prevention of conception]所致损害[noxae]的结果。要将它们排出次序并不容易，且一般而言，它们无一应被视为对女子全然无害；因而即便在最有利的情形（避孕套[condom]）下，女子作为要求更高的伴侣，也几乎难以逃脱轻微的神经衰弱[neurasthenia]。显然，在很大程度上取决于两种素质[dispositions]：其一，她本人在婚前是否即为神经衰弱[neurasthenic]；其二，她是否在自由性交[free intercourse]（无避孕器具）期间被造成了癔病-神经衰弱[hysterico-neurasthenic]。

11. Anxiety neurosis.⁶ Every case of neurasthenia is no doubt marked by a certain lowering of self-confidence, by pessimistic expectation and an inclination to distressing antithetic ideas.⁷ But the question is whether the prominent emergence of this factor [anxiety], without the other symptoms being specially developed, should not be detached⁸ as an independent ‘anxiety neurosis’, especially as this is to be met with no less frequently in hysteria than in neurasthenia.

11. 焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]。⁶ 每一神经衰弱[neurasthenia]病例无疑都标刻着自信的某种降低、悲观的预期[expectation]以及倾向于令人痛苦的对反观念[antithetic ideas]。⁷ 但问题在于：这一因素[焦虑]之显著浮现，在其它症状未特别发展的情况下，是否不应被分离⁸为一个独立的“焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]”——尤其因为它在癔症[hysteria]中与在神经衰弱[neurasthenia]中同样常见。

Anxiety neurosis appears in two forms: as a chronic state and as an attack of anxiety. The two may readily be combined: and an anxiety attack never occurs without chronic symptoms. Anxiety attacks are commoner in the forms connected with hysteria—more frequently, therefore, in women. The chronic symptoms are commoner in neurasthenic men.

焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]以两种形式出现：作为慢性状态，以及作为焦虑发作[anxiety attack]。二者极易结合：且焦虑发作[anxiety attack]从不脱离慢性症状而发生。焦虑发作[anxiety attack]在关连于癔症[hysteria]的形式中更常见——因而在女子中更频繁。慢性症状则于神经衰弱[neurasthenic]男子中更常见。

The chronic symptoms are: (1) anxiety relating to the body (hypochondria); (2) anxiety relating to the functioning of the body (agoraphobia, claustrophobia, giddiness on heights); (3) anxiety relating to decisions and memory – i.e. one’s own idea of one’s psychical functioning – (folie du doute, obsessive brooding [Grübelzwang]ᵀ, etc.). So far, I have found no occasion for not treating these symptoms as equivalent. Again, the question is (1) to what extent this condition emerges in hereditary cases, without any sexual noxa, (2) whether it is released in hereditary cases by

慢性症状有：(1) 关乎身体的焦虑（疑病[hypochondria]）；(2) 关乎身体机能的焦虑（广场恐怖[agoraphobia]、幽闭恐怖[claustrophobia]、高处眩晕）；(3) 关乎决断与记忆的焦虑——即一个人自身对其心理机能[psychical functioning]的观念——（疑虑妄想[folie du doute]、强迫性穷思竭虑[obsessive brooding][Grübelzwang]ᵀ 等）。迄今我尚未有理由不将这些症状同等视之。再者，问题在于：(1) 这一状态在遗传病例中、于无任何性损害[sexual noxa]的情况下，在多大程度上显现；(2) 它是否在遗传病例中由……所释放

any chance sexual noxa, (3) whether it supervenes as an intensification in common neurasthenia. There is no question but that it is acquired, and especially by men and women in marriage, during the second period of sexual noxae, through coitus interruptus. I do not believe that disposition owing to earlier neurasthenia is necessary for this; but where disposition is lacking, latency is longer. The causal formula is the same as in neurasthenia [p. 208 above].

任何偶然的性损害[sexual noxa]、(3) 它是否作为常见神经衰弱[neurasthenia]中的强化而继起。毫无疑问，它是获得的，尤其为婚姻中的男女，在性损害[sexual noxae]的第二个时期，经由中断性交[coitus interruptus]而获得。我不相信早先神经衰弱[neurasthenia]所致的素质[disposition]为此所必需；但凡素质[disposition]缺乏之处，潜伏期[latency]更长。其因果公式与神经衰弱[neurasthenia]中相同[见上文第 208 页]。

The rarer cases of anxiety neurosis outside marriage are met with especially in men. They turn out to be cases of congressus interruptus in which the man is strongly involved psychically with women whose well-being is a matter of concern to him. This procedure in these conditions is a greater noxa for a man than coitus interruptus in marriage, for this is often corrected, as it were, by normal coitus outside marriage.

婚姻之外的焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]较罕见病例，尤见于男子。它们结果证明是中断接合[congressus interruptus]的病例——其中男子在心理上深切牵系于那些令他关切其福祉的女子。在如此情形下，这一做法对男子是比婚姻中的中断性交[coitus interruptus]更大的损害[noxa]，因为后者往往由婚姻之外的正常性交所矫正，仿佛如此。

I must look upon periodic depression, an attack of anxiety lasting for weeks or months, as a third form of anxiety neurosis. This, in contrast to melancholia proper, almost always has an apparently rational connection with a psychical trauma. The latter is, however, only the provoking cause. Moreover, this periodic depression is without psychical [sexual] anaesthesia, which is characteristic of melancholia [p. 228 below].

我必须把周期性抑郁[periodic depression]——一种持续数周或数月的焦虑发作——视为焦虑神经症[anxiety neurosis]的第三种形式。与真正意义上的抑郁[melancholia]不同，它几乎总与某一心理创伤[psychical trauma]有着显然理性的关联。然而后者不过是其诱发原因[provoking cause]。再者，这一周期性抑郁[periodic depression]并无心理性[性]感觉缺失[psychical [sexual] anaesthesia]，而那正是抑郁[melancholia]的特征[见下文第 228 页]。

I have been able to trace back a number of such cases to coitus interruptus; their onset was a late one, during marriage, after the birth of the last child. In a case of tormenting hypochondria which began at puberty, I was able to prove an assault in the eighth year of life. Another case from childhood turned out to be a hysterical reaction to a masturbatory assault. Thus I cannot tell whether we have here truly hereditary forms without a sexual cause; and on the other hand I cannot tell whether coitus interruptus alone is to be blamed here, whether hereditary disposition can always be dispensed with.

我已能将若干此类病例回溯至中断性交[coitus interruptus]；它们的起病较晚，在婚姻期间、最后一个孩子出生之后。在一例始于青春期的折磨人的疑病[hypochondria]中，我得以证实八岁那年遭受过一次袭击[assault]。另一童年病例结果证明是对一次手淫性袭击[masturbatory assault]的癔症性反应[hysterical reaction]。因此我无从断定，我们在此所见是否确为无性原因的遗传形式；另一方面我也无从断定，此处是否仅应归咎于中断性交[coitus interruptus]，是否总能省去遗传素质[hereditary disposition]。

I will omit occupational neuroses, since, as I have told you, changes in the muscular parts have been demonstrated in them.

我将略去职业性神经症[occupational neuroses]，因为，如我曾告知于你，在其中已证实有肌肉部分的改变。

CONCLUSIONS

It follows from what I have said that neuroses are entirely preventable as well as entirely incurable. The physician’s task is wholly shifted on to prophylaxis.

由我之所言可知：神经症[neuroses]既完全可预防，又完全不可治愈。医师的任务整个转移到了预防[prophylaxis]之上。

The first part of this task, the prevention of the sexual noxa of the first period, coincides with prophylaxis against syphilis and gonorrhoea, since they are the noxae which threaten anyone who gives up masturbation. The only alternative would be free sexual intercourse between young men and respectable girls⁹; but this could only be adopted if there were innocuous methods of preventing conception. Otherwise, the alternatives are: masturbation, neurasthenia in the male, hystero-neurasthenia in the female, or syphilis in the male, syphilis in the next generation, gonorrhoea in the male, gonorrhoea and sterility in the female.

此任务的第一部分——防范第一个时期的性损害[sexual noxa]——与针对梅毒[syphilis]与淋病[gonorrhoea]的预防[prophylaxis]相重合，因为它们是威胁任何放弃手淫[masturbation]者的损害[noxae]。唯一的替代方案，将是青年男子与良家女子之间的自由性交⁹；但这只有在存在无害的避孕方法时才可采用。否则，替代方案便是：手淫[masturbation]、男子之神经衰弱[neurasthenia]、女子之癔病-神经衰弱[hystero-neurasthenia]，或男子之梅毒[syphilis]、下一代之梅毒[syphilis]、男子之淋病[gonorrhoea]、女子之淋病[gonorrhoea]与不育[sterility]。

The same problem—an innocuous means of controlling conception—is set by the sexual trauma of the second period; since the condom provides neither a safe solution nor one acceptable to someone who is already neurasthenic.⁹

同一问题——一种控制受孕的无害手段——亦由第二个时期的性创伤[sexual trauma]所提出；因为避孕套[condom]既非安全的解决，亦非已被神经衰弱[neurasthenic]者所能接受。¹¹

In the absence of such a solution, society appears doomed to fall victim to incurable neuroses, which reduce the enjoyment of life to a minimum, destroy the marriage relation and bring hereditary ruin on the whole coming generation. The lower strata of society know nothing of Malthusianism, but they are in full pursuit, and in the course of things will reach the same point and fall victim to the same fatality.

若无此种解决之道，社会似乎注定要沦为不治神经症[neuroses]的牺牲品——它们将生之享乐降至最低，摧毁婚姻关系，并给整个下一代带来遗传性的毁灭。社会的下层对马尔萨斯主义[Malthusianism]一无所知，却正全力追逐，且事势所趋，终将抵达同一点，沦为同一宿命的牺牲品。

Thus the physician is faced with a problem whose solution is worthy of all his efforts.¹⁰

因此，医师面对的是一个其解决值得他倾尽全力的难题。¹¹
