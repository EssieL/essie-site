LETTER 22¹

... I have nothing to report to you. At most a small analogy to Emma’s dream psychosis that we witnessed.² Rudi Kaufmann, a very intelligent nephew of Breuer’s, and a medical student too, is a late riser. He gets himself called by a servant, but is very reluctant about obeying her. One morning she woke him up a second time and, as he would not respond, called him by his name: ‘Herr Rudi!’ Thereupon the sleeper had a hallucination of a noticeboard over a hospital bed (cf. the Rudolﬁnerhaus)³ with the name ‘Rudolf Kaufmann’ on it, and said to himself: ‘R. K.’s in hospital in any case, then; so I needn’t go there!’ and went on sleeping.⁴

……我没有什么可向你报告的。至多是一个小小的类比，与我们曾目睹的艾玛[Emma] 的梦精神病相似。² 布罗伊尔的侄子鲁迪·考夫曼[Rudi Kaufmann] 是个很聪明的年轻人，也是医学生，他起床很晚。他让人叫醒自己，却又很不情愿听从。一天早上，女仆第二次把他叫醒，见他没有反应，便直呼其名：『鲁迪先生！』于是这睡眠者产生了一个幻觉：医院病床上方挂着一块牌子（参较鲁多尔菲纳医院[Rudolfinerhaus]）³，上面写着『鲁道夫·考夫曼』的名字；他对自己说：『反正 R. K. 已经在医院里了，那我就不必去那儿了！』于是继续睡。⁴

脚注 / Footnotes

p. 240 ¹ [Dated Vienna, March 4, 1895.]

p. 240 ¹ ［系于维也纳，1895 年 3 月 4 日。］

p. 240 ² [Emma reappears in the ‘Project’ (p. 378 f. below). She figures prominently in other parts of the correspondence, not included here. Fliess operated on her nose, with unhappy consequences. The German editors of the 1986 edition of these letters present good evidence to suggest that she appears again in The Interpretation of Dreams (1900a), RSE, 4, 112, as the patient who underwent an unsuccessful ‘jaw’ operation followed by a dream of convenience very similar to the analogous one reported here. She is believed also to be the patient ‘Irma’ in Freud’s (1900a) famous specimen dream (see Schur, 1966a).]

p. 240 ² ［艾玛在《大纲》（下文 p. 378 及下页）中重新出现。她在本书信集未收录的其他部分中占有显著位置。弗利斯曾为她的鼻部动手术，结果不幸。1986 年版这些书信的德文编者提出了有力证据，表明她再次出现于《梦的解析》（1900a，RSE 第 4 卷，112 页），即那位经历过一次不成功的『下颌』手术、随后做了一个与此处所报告的类似『图方便的梦』的病人。人们也相信她就是弗洛伊德（1900a）那个著名标本梦中的病人『伊尔玛』[Irma]（见 Schur, 1966a）。］

p. 240 ³ [A private hospital in Vienna. – ‘Rudi’ is the familiar short form of ‘Rudolf’.]

p. 240 ³ ［维也纳的一所私人医院。——『鲁迪』是『鲁道夫』的亲昵简称。］

p. 240 ⁴ [This, perhaps the earliest recorded wishful ‘dream of convenience’, was introduced by Freud into The Interpretation of Dreams, ibid., 4, 111 f. and 207. He used it again in his last, posthumous, work, An Outline of Psychoanalysis (1940a), ibid., 23, 155.]

p. 240 ⁴ ［这也许是最早被记录下来的、愿望性的『图方便的梦』；弗洛伊德把它引入《梦的解析》，同上，第 4 卷，111 页及下页、207 页。他在最后一部遗著《精神分析纲要》（1940a）中再次使用了它，同上，第 23 卷，155 页。］
