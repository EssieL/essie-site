Extracts from Freud's Footnotes to his Translation of Charcot's Tuesday Lectures

[Charcot had been giving an account of hysterical attacks.] . . . I avail myself of the opportunity offered in the text in order to lay before the reader an independent view of hysterical attacks. Charcot's 'type', with its modifications and the possibility of each stage becoming independent and representing the whole attack, etc., is undoubtedly extensive enough to cover all the observed forms of attack. For that very reason it will perhaps be argued in some quarters that it does not represent a true entity.

[沙可当时正在讲述癔症发作。] ……我借正文所提供的机会，向读者呈述一番关于癔症发作的独立见解。沙可的"类型"[Charcot's 'type']，连同其各种变体，以及每一阶段都可能独立出来、代表整场发作等情形，无疑已足够宽泛，足以涵盖所有观察到的发作形式。正因如此，或许会有人认为它并不构成一个真正的实体。

I have attempted to meet the problem of hysterical attacks along a line other than descriptive, and by examining hysterical patients in a hypnotic state I have arrived at new findings, a few of which I will mention here. The core of a hysterical attack, in whatever form it may appear, is a memory, the hallucinatory reliving of a scene which is significant for the onset of the illness. It is this event which manifests itself in a perceptible manner in the phase of 'attitudes passionnelles'; but it is also present when the attack appears to consist only of motor phenomena. The content of the memory is as a rule either a psychical trauma which is qualified by its intensity to provoke the outbreak of hysteria in the patient or is an event which, owing to its occurrence at a particular moment, has become a trauma.

我尝试以一条非描述性的路径来应对癔症发作这一难题；通过考察处于催眠状态[hypnotic state]中的癔症患者，我获得了一些新的发现，此处仅择其数端略作陈述。一场癔症发作的核心——无论其以何种形式显现——是一段记忆，是对某一于疾病起病[onset of the illness]具有关键意义的场景的幻觉性重历[hallucinatory reliving]。正是这一事件，在"激情姿态"[attitudes passionnelles]阶段以可感知的方式显现自身；然而即便当发作看似仅由运动现象[motor phenomena]构成时，它也同样在场。该记忆的内容通常要么是某种心理创伤[psychical trauma]——其强度足以在该患者身上引发癔症的爆发——要么是一个因其发生于某个特定时刻而转化为创伤的事件。

In cases of what is known as 'traumatic' hysteria this mechanism is obvious to the most cursory observation; but it can be demonstrated also in hysteria where there is no single major trauma. In such cases we find repeated minor traumas or, where the factor of disposition predominates, memories which are often indifferent in themselves magnified into traumas. A trauma would have to be defined as an accretion of excitation [Erregungszuwachs]T in the nervous system,1 which the latter has been unable to dispose of adequately by motor reaction. A hysterical attack is perhaps to be regarded as an attempt to complete the reaction to the trauma. – I may refer here to a work on this subject which has been begun in collaboration with Dr Josef Breuer.

在所谓"创伤性"癔症['traumatic' hysteria]的病例中，这一机制即便最粗略的观察也一目了然；然而在并无单一重大创伤的癔症里，它同样可以得到证明。在此类病例中，我们会发现反复的轻微创伤，或者——在素质[disposition]因素占主导之处——那些本身往往无关紧要的记忆被放大成了创伤。创伤须被定义为神经系统内的一次兴奋积聚[Erregungszuwachs]T¹，而神经系统未能通过运动性反应[motor reaction]对其加以充分处置。一场癔症发作[hysterical attack]或许应被视作完成针对创伤之反应的尝试。——此处我愿提请注意一部关于此主题、正与约瑟夫·布洛伊尔医生[Dr Josef Breuer]合作撰写的著作。

[Charcot had described cases in which well-brought-up boys had hysterical attacks accompanied by outbreaks of obscene language.] Can it be a matter of chance that attacks in young people of whose good upbringing and manners Charcot speaks highly take the form of ravings and abusive language? This is no more the case, I think, than the familiar fact that the hysterical deliria of nuns revel in blasphemies and erotic pictures.2

[沙可曾描述过一些病例：教养良好的男孩出现伴有污言秽语爆发的癔症发作。] 在那些教养与举止深受沙可称许的年轻人身上，发作竟采取胡言乱语与污言秽语[ravings and abusive language]的形式，这难道会是出于偶然吗？我以为，这并不比一个熟知的事实更属偶然——即修女们的癔症性谵妄[hysterical deliria]沉溺于亵渎[blasphemies]与情色画面。²

In this we may suspect a connection which allows us a deep insight into the mechanism of hysterical states. There emerges in hysterical deliria material in the shape of ideas and impulsions to action which the subject in his healthy state has rejected and inhibited [gehemmt]T – has often inhibited by a great psychical effort. Something similar holds good of a number of dreams, which spin out further associations which have been rejected or broken off during the day.3

由此我们或可推想某种关联，它使我们得以深入洞察癔症状态的机制。在癔症性谵妄中浮现出以观念与行动冲动[impulsions to action]为形态的材料，而主体在其健康状态下曾拒斥并抑制[gehemmt]T——且往往曾以巨大的心理努力[psychical effort]加以抑制。类似情形亦适用于若干梦境：它们将白日里遭到拒斥或中断的联想进一步铺展开来。³

I have based on this fact the theory of 'hysterical counterwill'4 which embraces a good number of hysterical symptoms.

我正是基于这一事实，提出了"癔症性反意志"['hysterical counterwill']⁴ 的理论，该理论涵盖了为数不少的癔症性症状[symptoms]。

[Charcot was discussing a case exhibiting symptoms both of tic and of obsessions.] Here I may recall an interesting case which I observed recently which showed a new variant in the relation between tic and obsession [Zwangsvorstellung]T. A man aged 23 consulted me on account of obsessions of a typical kind. From the age of 8 to 15 he had suffered from a lively tic, which has disappeared since then. The obsessions appeared at the age of 12 and have become much more severe recently.

[沙可当时正在讨论一例同时表现出抽动与强迫症状[obsessions]的病例。] 在此我愿回想一例近期观察所得的有趣病例，它展现了抽动与强迫观念[Zwangsvorstellung]T 之间关系的一种新变体[variant]。一位 23 岁的男子因典型类型的强迫观念前来就诊。他从 8 岁到 15 岁期间患有一种活跃的抽动，此后该抽动便消失。强迫观念出现于 12 岁，且近来变得严重得多。

[Freud has a long footnote dealing with an elaborate discussion of Charcot's, who maintained that in certain cases complete hemianaesthesia could occur owing to a particular kind of central organic lesion and was in such cases exactly similar to hysterical hemianaesthesia. In particular, Charcot denied that in these cases hemianopsia was present.] . . . When on one occasion I ventured to question him on this point and to argue that this contradicted the theory of hemianopsia, I was met by this excellent comment: 'La théorie c'est bon; mais ça n'empêche pas d'exister.' If one only knew what exists! . . . 1

[弗洛伊德有一长段脚注，详论沙可的一番论述：沙可主张，在某些病例中，完全的半身感觉缺失[hemianaesthesia]可因某种特定的中枢性器质性病灶[central organic lesion]而发生，且在此类病例中与癔症性半身感觉缺失[hysterical hemianaesthesia]完全相似。尤其是，沙可否认这些病例中存在偏盲[hemianopsia]。] ……有一次，我斗胆就此点向他提出质疑，并争辩说这有悖于偏盲[hemianopsia]理论；他回以一句绝妙的评注："理论固然好，却不妨碍事实存在。"[La théorie c'est bon; mais ça n'empêche pas d'exister.] 倘若人能知晓究竟何者存在，那该多好！……¹

[Charcot had declared that heredity was the 'true cause' of a patient's hysterical attacks, his vertigo and his agoraphobia.] I venture upon a contradiction here. The more frequent cause of agoraphobia as well as of most other phobias lies not in heredity but in abnormalities of sexual life. It is even possible to specify the form of abuse of the sexual function involved. Such disorders can be acquired in any degree of intensity; naturally they occur more intensely, with the same aetiology, in individuals with a hereditary disposition.

[沙可曾宣称，遗传[heredity]是一位患者癔症发作、其眩晕[vertigo]及其广场恐怖[agoraphobia]的"真正原因"。] 在此我斗胆提出异议。广场恐怖[agoraphobia]以及大多数其他恐怖症[phobias]更为常见的原因，并不在于遗传[heredity]，而在于性生活[sexual life]的异常。我们甚至能够指明所涉性功能之滥用的具体形式。此类障碍可在任意强度上获得；自然，在具有遗传素质[hereditary disposition]的个体身上，基于相同的病因学[aetiology]，它们会以更强的程度出现。

[Charcot was discussing a case of Graves' disease.] Some readers will probably, like me, object to Charcot's aetiological theory which does not separate the disposition to neuroses from that to organic nervous diseases, which takes no account of the part played by acquired nervous diseases (which cannot be overestimated) and which makes an arthritic tendency in relatives figure as a hereditary neuropathic disposition. His overestimation of the part played by the factor of heredity may also explain the fact that, in dealing with Graves' disease, Charcot does not mention the organ in whose changes, as weighty indications tell us, we must look for the true cause of the affection. I refer, of course, to the thyroid gland and, in connection with the discussion of the fact that hereditary disposition and psychical trauma play a large part in the development of the disease, I may mention Moebius's excellent paper on Graves' disease in the Deutsche Zeitschrift für Nervenheilkunde, 1 (1891).

[沙可当时正在讨论一例格雷夫斯病[Graves' disease]的病例。] 不少读者大概会像我一样，反对沙可的病因学理论[aetiological theory]——它未将神经症[neuroses]的素质与器质性神经疾病[organic nervous diseases]的素质区分开来，未顾及获得性神经疾病[acquired nervous diseases]所起作用（其重要性再怎么强调也不为过），并且把亲属中的关节炎倾向[arthritic tendency]当作一种遗传性神经病态素质[hereditary neuropathic disposition]来呈现。他高估遗传因素[heredity]所起作用这一点，或许也能解释如下事实：在论及格雷夫斯病[Graves' disease]时，沙可并未提及那个器官——而正如种种有力的迹象所示，我们须在该器官的变化中寻找此病症的真正原因。我指的当然是甲状腺[thyroid gland]；并且，鉴于遗传素质与心理创伤[psychical trauma]在疾病发展[development]中发挥着重大作用这一事实，我愿顺带提及默比乌斯（Moebius）论述格雷夫斯病[Graves' disease]的出色论文，载于《德国神经病学杂志》[Deutsche Zeitschrift für Nervenheilkunde]，第1卷（1891年）。

[Charcot was discussing the distinction between organic and hysterical aphasia.] When I left the Salpêtrière in 1886, Charcot set me the task of carrying out a comparative study of organic and hysterical paralyses on the basis of the observations made by the Salpêtrière. I have carried out the work but not published it. Its outcome was a further extension of the thesis laid down here by Charcot: hysterical paralyses are characterized by two factors and, in particular, beyond that, by a concurrence between them. They are capable, in the first place, of the greatest intensity and, in the second place, of the sharpest isolation, and they diverge especially from organic paralyses when they combine intensity and isolation. A monoplegia of the arm from an organic cause can be limited exclusively to the arm; but in that case it is almost never absolute. As soon as its intensity increases, it also becomes more extensive; it is positively the rule that it is then accompanied as well by a slight degree of paresis in the face and in the leg. If it is limited to the arm alone and at the same time absolute, the paralysis can only be hysterical.1

[沙可当时正在讨论器质性失语症[aphasia]与癔症性失语症之间的区分。] 1886 年我离开萨尔佩特里埃尔医院[Salpêtrière]时，沙可交给我一项任务：基于萨尔佩特里埃尔医院[Salpêtrière]所做的观察，对器质性与癔症性瘫痪[organic and hysterical paralyses]进行比较研究。我已完成这项工作，但尚未发表。其结果是进一步扩展了沙可在此提出的论点：癔症性瘫痪的特征在于两个因素，且尤其在此之外，在于二者之间的并存[concurrence]。首先，它们可达最大的强度；其次，它们可被最高度地孤立；而当强度与孤立性相结合时，它们尤其有别于器质性瘫痪。一条由器质性原因所致的臂部单瘫[monoplegia]可以仅限于臂部；但在那种情形下，它几乎从不是绝对的。一旦其强度增加，它也变得更为广泛；这确是一条规律：此时它同时也伴有面部与腿部的轻度轻瘫[paresis]。若瘫痪仅限于臂部、且同时是绝对的，则该瘫痪只能是癔症性的。¹

[Charcot had been giving technical advice on the use of suggestion: 'The English, who are certainly practical people, have in their language a warning: "Do not prophesy unless you are sure." I should like to associate myself with this and should advise you to do so too. Indeed, if, in an undoubted case of psychical paralysis, you say to the patient with complete confidence: "Stand up and walk!", and if he really does, you may, it is true, ascribe the miracle you have performed to yourself and your diagnosis. But I advise you not to venture too far and to consider from the first how, in the possible case of a failure, you can make certain of a retreat "in good order".'] With these wise words Charcot reveals one of the greatest inconveniences with which the practical use of suggestion in the waking state and under light hypnosis has to reckon. In the long run neither the doctor nor the patient can tolerate the contradiction between the decided denial of the ailment in the suggestion and the necessary recognition of it outside the suggestion.2

[沙可当时正就暗示[suggestion]的运用提供技术性建议："英国人诚然是讲求实际的人，他们的语言中有句告诫：'除非你有把握，否则不要预言。'我愿认同这句告诫，并且也建议你这样做。事实上，若在一个确凿的心理性瘫痪[psychical paralysis]病例中，你满怀信心地对患者说：'站起来，走！'，而他的确照做了，那么——没错——你可以将你所成就的这桩奇迹归功於你自己和你的诊断。但我劝你不要冒进太远，并且从一开始就要考虑：万一失败，你如何能有把握地实现'有序'[in good order]撤退。"] 借着这些睿智的话语，沙可揭示出在清醒状态[waking state]与轻度催眠[hypnosis]下实际运用暗示[suggestion]所必须面对的最大不便之一。长此以往，无论医生还是患者，都无法容忍如下矛盾：在暗示中对病痛的断然否认，与在暗示之外对该病痛的必然承认。²

[Charcot had discussed the case history of a male hysterical patient whose disorder was apparently the outcome of mercurial intoxication.] The readers of these lectures are probably aware that P. Janet, Breuer and I, as well as other authors, have very recently sought to outline a psychological theory of hysterical phenomena, based on Charcot's own writings (on the explanation of hysterotraumatic paralyses). However robust and promising this theory may seem to us, prudence calls for an admission that no step has hitherto been taken towards showing that hysteria through intoxication or the analogy between hysterical and organic hemiplegia or the origin of hysterical contractures can be subsumed under the basic idea of this line of approach. I hope that this task will not prove insoluble or at least that these facts will not turn out to be irreconcilable with the psychological theory.

[沙可曾讨论过一例男性癔症患者的病史，其障碍显然系汞中毒[mercurial intoxication]所致。] 这些讲座的读者大概已经知道，P. 让内（Janet）、布洛伊尔（Breuer）与我，以及其他一些作者，近来都试图基于沙可本人的著述（论癔症性创伤性瘫痪[hysterotraumatic paralyses]的解释）来勾勒出一种癔症现象的心理理论[psychological theory]。无论这一理论在我们看来多么坚实、多么有前景，审慎[prudence]都要求我们承认：迄今为止，在表明如下事项方面尚未迈出任何一步——即经由中毒所致的癔症、或癔症性与器质性偏瘫[hemiplegia]之间的类比、或癔症性挛缩[contractures]之起源，均可被归入这一研究进路的基本观念之下。我希望这项任务不至于无解，或至少这些事实不至于被证明与这一心理理论不可调和[irreconcilable]。

[Charcot was pointing out the differential diagnosis between organic and hysterical monoplegias.] In a short work ('Quelques considérations pour une étude comparative des paralysies motrices organiques et hystériques', Archives de Neurologie, No. 77, 1893) I have tried to enlarge on this remark of Charcot's and have discussed its relation to the theory of the neuroses.1

[沙可当时正在指出器质性单瘫[monoplegias]与癔症性单瘫之间的鉴别诊断[differential diagnosis]。] 在拙作《若干关于器质性与癔症性运动性瘫痪之比较研究的考量》（"Quelques considérations pour une étude comparative des paralysies motrices organiques et hystériques"，载《神经学档案》[Archives de Neurologie]，第77期，1893年）中，我曾试图详述沙可的这一见解，并讨论了它与神经症理论[theory of the neuroses]之间的关系。¹

[Charcot was describing the differing attacks shown by a hysterical girl.] We shall certainly not be misunderstanding Charcot if we conclude from his remarks on 'hystéro-épilepsie à crises mixtes' and 'à crises séparées' that the term 'hystero-epilepsy' is thoroughly objectionable and that its use should be entirely dropped. Some of the patients indicated in this way are suffering simply from hysteria; others are suffering from hysteria and epilepsy, two disorders which have little internal relationship and only meet by chance in a single individual. A statement such as this may not be unnecessary, since many physicians nevertheless seem to be of the opinion that 'hystero-epilepsy' is an aggravation of hysteria or a transition from it to epilepsy. An intention to convey this meaning no doubt originally underlay the creation of the term 'hystero-epilepsy'. But Charcot has long since abandoned any such view, and there is no reason why we should lag behind him on this point.

[沙可当时正在描述一名癔症女孩所表现出的不同发作。] 如果我们从他就"混合型发作[crises mixtes]的癔症性癫痫[hystéro-épilepsie]"与"分离型发作[crises séparées]"所作的评注中得出结论，认为"癔症性癫痫"[hystero-epilepsy]这一术语极其不妥、其使用应完全废止，那么我们肯定不会误解沙可。以此方式被归入此类的一些患者仅仅患有癔症[hysteria]；另一些患者则同时患有癔症与癫痫[epilepsy]——这两种障碍内部关联甚少，只是偶然在同一人身上相遇。这样的陈述或许并非多余，因为许多医生似乎仍持如下看法：即"癔症性癫痫"[hystero-epilepsy]是癔症的一种加重，或是从癔症向癫痫的过渡。创造"癔症性癫痫"[hystero-epilepsy]这一术语时，其本意无疑正是要传达此种含义。但沙可早已放弃了任何此类看法，而我们也没有理由在这一点上落后于他。

[Charcot had expressed his views on overwork as a cause of 'cerebral neurasthenia'.] All these aetiological discussions on questions of neurasthenia are incomplete so long as no consideration is given to sexual noxae, which, in my experience, constitute the most important and only indispensable aetiological factor.

[沙可曾表达过他关于过度劳顿[overwork]作为"脑神经衰弱[cerebral neurasthenia]"之成因的看法。] 所有这些关于神经衰弱[neurasthenia]病因学的讨论都是不完整的，只要没有把性的有害因素[sexual noxae]纳入考量——依我的经验，后者构成了最重要、且唯一不可或缺的[indispensable]病因学[aetiological]因素。

[On a discussion of hereditary determinants of neuroses.] . . . The theory of the 'famille névropathique' is certainly in urgent need of revision.

[关于神经症[neuroses]的遗传性决定因素的一场讨论。] ……"神经病家族"[famille névropathique]理论无疑迫切需要修订。

[In a similar connection.] . . . The conception of the 'famille névropathique' – which, incidentally, embraces almost everything we know in the form of nervous diseases, organic and functional, systematic and accidental – could scarcely stand up to serious criticism.2

[在一段类似的关联论述中。] ……"神经病家族"[famille névropathique]这一概念——顺带一提，它几乎涵盖了我们所知的一切以神经系统疾病[nervous diseases]形式存在的事物，无论器质性还是功能性、系统性还是偶发性——几乎经不起严肃的批评。²

脚注 / Footnotes

2 [In his obituary of Charcot (1893f), NSW, 4; RSE, 3, 21 f., Freud criticizes the theory of the 'famille névropathique' (the group of the nervous disorders), in which Charcot included 'all those disorders of the nervous system which can take one another's place reciprocally in inheritance' – among them both tabes and hysteria.]

2 [在沙可的悼文（1893f，见 NSW 第 4 卷；RSE 第 3 卷，第 21 页及次页）中，弗洛伊德批评了"神经病家族"[famille névropathique]（即神经障碍之群组）理论；沙可将该家族纳入了"所有那些在遗传中可彼此相互替代的神经系统障碍"——其中既包括脊髓痨[tabes]，也包括癔症。]
