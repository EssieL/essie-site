EDITORS' NOTE（编者注）

The three condensed memoranda which follow were included among the posthumous writings of Freud in Volume XVII of the Gesammelte Werke. (More detailed bibliographical data are attached to each separate draft below.) We are informed by the editors of the German edition that all three of these papers had been in Breuer's possession but were returned by him to Freud in 1909, the year after the publication of the second edition of Studies on Hysteria. Freud acknowledged their receipt in a letter dated October 8, 1909: 'Very many thanks for letting me have the old drafts and sketches, which seem to me most interesting. As regards the notes on hysterical attacks [Sketch C below], it must be as you say; but I did not keep the manuscript after it was printed.'

以下这三份浓缩的备忘录[memoranda]被收入《弗洛伊德全集》[Gesammelte Werke]第十七卷的遗著[posthumous writings]之中。（更详尽的文献数据附于下方各份草稿之后。）据德文版编者告知，这三篇论文原本都在布洛伊尔[Breuer]手中，但在《癔症研究》[Studies on Hysteria]第二版出版后的次年——1909 年——由他归还给了弗洛伊德。弗洛伊德在 1909 年 10 月 8 日的信中确认收到："非常感谢你将那些旧稿与草稿[sketches]交还给我，它们在我看来越发有趣。至于关于癔症发作的笔记[Sketch C below]，必定如你所言；不过在付印之后我并未保留手稿[manuscript]。"

Though the second of these sketches is undated, there can be no doubt that all three of them were written in the latter part of 1892, in preparation for the 'Preliminary Communication' – 'On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena' (1893a), RSE, 2, 3. That work, produced in collaboration with Josef Breuer, was published on January 1 and 15, 1893.

尽管这数份草稿[sketches]中的第二份未注明日期，但毫无疑问，三者都写于 1892 年下半年，是为那篇"初步通讯"[Preliminary Communication]——即《论癔症现象的心理机制》['On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena'，(1893a)，见 RSE 第2卷第3页]——所做的准备。这部与约瑟夫·布洛伊尔[Josef Breuer]合作完成的作品，于 1893 年 1 月 1 日与 15 日分两次发表。

Much of these sketches is in a highly condensed form, but it is possible to discover almost every single element of them, more intelligibly stated, in the 'Preliminary Communication'. There is, however, one remarkable exception. The 'principle of constancy' is stated very clearly, and perhaps for the first time, in Section 5 of Sketch C (p. 182 below); but it is entirely omitted, for some unexplained reason, in the 'Preliminary Communication'. A full account of the history of the 'principle of constancy' is given in an Editors' Appendix on 'Freud's Fundamental Hypotheses', RSE, 3, 59.

这些草稿[sketches]中有许多呈高度浓缩的形态，但我们仍能在《初步通讯》['Preliminary Communication']中发现它们几乎每一个要素——且表述得更为晓畅。然而，有一处显著的例外。"恒常原则"[principle of constancy]在草稿 C 的第 5 节（见下文 p. 182）中被表述得极为清晰，或许也是有史以来第一次；但在《初步通讯》['Preliminary Communication']中，它却出于某种未加说明的原因被全然略去。关于"恒常原则"[principle of constancy]历史的完整记述，见编者附录《弗洛伊德的基本假说》['Freud's Fundamental Hypotheses']，RSE 第 3 卷第 59 页。

(A) LETTER TO JOSEF BREUER 1（致约瑟夫·布洛伊尔的信 1）

Honoured friend, The satisfaction with which I innocently handed you over those few pages of mine has given way to the uneasiness which is so apt to go along with the unremitting pains of thinking. I am tormented by the problem of how it will be possible to give a two-dimensional picture of anything that is so much of a solid as our theory of hysteria. The main question, no doubt, is whether we should describe it historically and lead off with all (or two of the best) case histories or whether, on the other hand, we should start by dogmatically stating the theories we have devised as an explanation.2 I incline to the second suggestion and would arrange the material thus:

尊敬的友人，当初我天真地把那几页文稿交到你手上时还满心欢喜，如今这份满足已被不安所取代——那不安总爱与思考的不懈劳苦[unremitting pains]形影相随。我正为一个难题所折磨：该如何为我们关于癔症的理论——它实在如此"立体"——给出一幅二维的[two-dimensional]图景。毋庸讳言，核心问题在于：我们应当从历史的角度去描述它，并以全部（或其中两则最佳）个案史[case histories]开篇；还是反其道而行，先以独断的方式[dogmatically]陈述我们为解释而构想出的那些理论。² 我倾向于第二种主张，并打算将材料作如下安排：

(1) Our theories: (a) The theorem concerning the constancy of the sum of excitation [Erregungssumme]T.3 (b) The theory of memory.4 (c) The theorem which lays it down that the contents of different states of consciousness are not associated with one another.5

(1) 我们的理论：(a) 关于兴奋总量[Erregungssumme]T 恒常性[constancy]的定理。³ (b) 记忆[memory]的理论。⁴ (c) 如下定理：不同意识状态[states of consciousness]的内容彼此之间并不相关联。⁵

(2) The origin of chronic hysterical symptoms: dreams, autohypnosis, affects and the results of absolute traumas. The first three of these factors relate to disposition, the last relates to aetiology.1 The chronic symptoms would seem to correspond to a normal mechanism; they are displacements [Verschiebungen]T [subsidiary topic],2 in part along an abnormal path (internal alteration), of sums of excitation which have not been released. Reason for the displacement: attempt at reaction. Reason for the persistence: theorem (c) [above], concerning associative isolation. – Comparison with hypnosis.3 Subsidiary topic: On the nature of the displacement: localization of the chronic hysterical symptoms.

(2) 慢性癔症性症状[chronic hysterical symptoms]的起源：梦[dreams]、自我催眠[autohypnosis]、情感[affects]，以及绝对创伤[absolute traumas]的结果。这前三项因素关乎素质[disposition]，末项关乎病因学[aetiology]。¹ 慢性症状似乎对应于一种正常机制；它们是未被释放的兴奋之和的移置[Verschiebungen]T [副主题]，² 部分地沿着一条异常路径（内在改变[internal alteration]）而发生。移置的缘由：反应的尝试。持续的缘由：上述定理 (c)，即有关联想性隔绝[associative isolation]者。——与催眠[hypnosis]的比较。³ 副主题：论移置[displacement]之本性：慢性癔症性症状的定位[localization]。

(3) The hysterical attack: also an attempt at reaction, by means of recollection, etc.4 (4) The origin of hysterical stigmata: highly obscure, a few hints.5 (5) The pathological formula of hysteria: Dispositional and accidental hysteria. The series proposed by me.6 The magnitude of the sum of excitation, the concept of trauma, the second state of consciousness.

(3) 癔症发作[hysterical attack]：同样是一种通过回忆[recollection]等手段完成的反应尝试。⁴ (4) 癔症性体征[hysterical stigmata]的起源：极为晦暗，仅有些许线索。⁵ (5) 癔症的病理学公式[pathological formula]：素质性与偶发性[accidental]癔症。我所提出的序列[series]。⁶ 兴奋之和的量[magnitude of the sum of excitation]、创伤[trauma]的概念、第二意识状态[second state of consciousness]。

(B) 'III'

In what we have written above we have had to point out as a fact of observation that the recollections lying behind hysterical phenomena are absent from the patient's accessible memory, whereas under hypnosis they can be awakened with hallucinatory vividness. We have also pointed out that a number of such recollections relate to events that occurred in peculiar states (such as cataplexy due to fright, half-dreaming conditions, autohypnosis, and so on), the content of which is not connected associatively with normal consciousness. Thus it was already to that extent impossible for us to discuss what it is that determines the occurrence of hysterical phenomena without first considering a particular hypothesis which seeks to characterize the hysterical disposition. In hysteria, according to this hypothesis, the content of consciousness easily becomes temporarily dissociated and certain complexes2 of ideas which are not associatively connected easily fly apart. The hysterical disposition is therefore to be looked for where states of this kind either appear spontaneously (from internal causes) or are easily produced by external influences; and we may suppose a series of cases in which these two factors play a part of varying importance.3

在上文的论述中，我们不得不指出一个观察事实：即潜藏于癔症现象[recollections lying behind hysterical phenomena]背后的那些回忆，并不在患者可及的记忆[accessible memory]之中，而在催眠[hypnosis]状态下却能以幻觉般的鲜活[hallucinatory vividness]被唤醒。我们还曾指出，此类回忆中有相当一部分与发生在奇异状态中的事件相关——例如因惊吓所致的猝倒[cataplexy due to fright]、半梦半醒的境况[half-dreaming conditions]、自我催眠[autohypnosis]等——这些事件的内容与正常意识[normal consciousness]之间并无联想性的联结。因此，早在那个程度上，我们便已无法在绕开某个特定假说的前提下讨论究竟是何者决定了癔症现象的发生；那个假说试图刻画癔症性素质[hysterical disposition]的特征。依此假说，在癔症中，意识的内容易于暂时性地解离[dissociated]，某些彼此并无联想联结的观念复合体[complexes]² 也易于彼此飞散开来。因此，癔症性素质[hysterical disposition]应到如下之处去寻找：即这类状态要么自发出现（缘于内在原因），要么易由外在影响所诱发；而我们可设想有一系列病例，在其中这两个因素起着轻重不一的作用。³

We describe these states as 'hypnoid';4 and we emphasize the fact that it is an essential characteristic of them that their content is to a greater or less extent cut off from the remaining content of consciousness and is thus deprived of the possibility of being disposed of associatively1 – just as in dreaming and waking, a model of two states which differ from each other, we are not inclined to make associations from the one to the other but only within each.2 In persons with a hysterical disposition any affect can give rise to a splitting of this kind; and an impression received during the affect would thus become a trauma even though it was not in itself appropriate for acting as one. Moreover, the impression might itself produce the affect. In their fully developed shape these hypnoid states, between which there can be associative connections, form the condition seconde so familiar in case histories. But rudiments of such a disposition would seem to be discernible everywhere, and may be developed by appropriate traumas even in non-disposed subjects. Sexual life is especially well suited to provide the content [of such traumas] owing to the very great contrast it presents to the rest of the personality and to its ideas being impossible to react to.

我们将这些状态称为"催眠样状态"['hypnoid']⁴；并强调如下事实：其内容在或大或小的程度上与意识其余内容相隔绝，从而被剥夺了经由联想性[associatively]处置而排解的可能¹——正如在梦与醒之间，这两种彼此相异的状态构成一个范型：我们并不倾向于从其一到其另一去建立联想，而只在每一状态之内作联想。² 在具有癔症性素质[hysterical disposition]的人身上，任何情感[affect]都能引起这一类的分裂；而情感期间所接收的一个印象，即便就其本身而言并不适宜充当创伤，也会因此转转变创伤。再者，那个印象本身也可能产生出该情感。在其充分发展之形态下，这些催眠样状态[hypnoid states]——它们彼此之间可以有联想性的联结——构成了个案史中极为熟悉的"继发状态"[condition seconde]。不过，此类素质的雏形[rudiments]似乎处处皆可辨识，即便在并无此素质的受试者身上，也可能因恰当的创伤而被发展出来。性生活[sexual life]尤其宜于提供此类创伤的内容[of such traumas]，这既因其与人格其余部分之间呈现出极其巨大的反差，也因其种种观念无从加以反应。

It will be understood that our therapy consists in removing the results of the ideas [Vorstellungen]T that have not been abreacted, either by reviving the trauma in a state of somnambulism, and then abreacting and correcting it, or by bringing it into normal consciousness under comparatively light hypnosis.

可以想见，我们的疗法[therapy]就在于清除那些未经宣泄[abreacted]的观念[Vorstellungen]T 所留下的结果——方法有二：或是在梦游状态[somnambulism]中使该创伤得以复现，继而将其宣泄[abreacting]并加以矫正；或是在较为轻度的催眠[hypnosis]下，将之引入正常的意识[normal consciousness]之中。

(C) ON THE THEORY OF HYSTERICAL ATTACKS（论癔症发作的理论）

So far as we know, there is not as yet any theory of hysterical attacks, but only a description of them, coming from Charcot, which relates to the rare, unabbreviated 'grande attaque hystérique [major hysterical attack]'. According to Charcot, a 'typical' attack of this kind consists of four phases: (1) the epileptoid phase, (2) the phase of large movements, (3) the phase of 'attitudes passionnelles',2 and (4) the phase of terminal delirium. All the multifarious forms of hysteric attacks which the physician has opportunities of observing more frequently than the typical grande attaque arise, so Charcot tells us, insofar as these individual phases make themselves independent or are prolonged or modified or omitted.

就我们所知，迄今尚不存在关于癔症发作[hysterical attacks]的任何理论，而只有源自沙可的一种描述，它针对的是那种罕见、未经删节的"大型癔症发作"[grande attaque hystérique（major hysterical attack）]。依沙可之见，一场此类"典型"发作由四个阶段[phases]构成：(1) 癫痫样阶段[epileptoid phase]，(2) 大幅度运动阶段[phase of large movements]，(3) "激情姿态"[attitudes passionnelles]阶段²，(4) 终末谵妄[terminal delirium]阶段。医生们得以观察到的机会，往往多于那种典型大型癔症发作的种种五花八门的癔症发作形式，据沙可所言，其成因在于：这些个别阶段各自独立出来，或被延长、被修改、或被省略。

This description throws no light at all on any connection there may be between the different phases, on the significance of attacks in the general picture of hysteria, or on the way in which attacks are modified in individual patients. Perhaps we shall not be wrong in supposing that the majority of physicians are inclined to regard the hysterical attack as 'a periodic discharge of the motor and psychical centres of the cerebral cortex'.

这一描述对于不同阶段之间可能存在的一切关联、对于发作在癔症的总体图景[general picture]中的意义、或对于发作在个别患者[individual patients]身上被改变的方式，全都未予任何阐明。或许，我们若假设大多数医生都倾向于把癔症发作[hysterical attack]视为"大脑皮层[cerebral cortex]运动中枢与心理中枢的一种周期性释放[periodic discharge]"，也不会是错的。

We have reached our opinions on hysterical attacks by treating hysterical subjects by means of hypnotic suggestion and by questioning them under hypnosis and thus investigating their psychical processes during the attack. The following is a statement of our views on the hysterical attack; and we must preface them by pointing out that we regard it as indispensable for the explanation of hysterical phenomena to assume the presence of a dissociation – a splitting of the content of consciousness.

我们是通过以催眠性暗示[hypnotic suggestion]治疗癔症性受试者，并在催眠[hypnosis]状态下对他们进行询问，从而得以在他们发作期间考察其心理过程[psychical processes]，才得出关于癔症发作的看法的。以下是我们对癔症发作[hysterical attack]之见解的陈述；而在陈述之先，我们必须预先指出：我们认为，要解释癔症现象，就必须预设一种解离[dissociation]的存在——即意识内容的一种分裂[splitting]。

1) The constant and essential content of a (recurrent) hysterical attack is the return of a psychical state which the patient has already experienced earlier – in other words, the return of a memory.

1) 一场（复发性的）[recurrent]癔症发作之恒常而本质的内容，乃是一种患者早先已经历过的心理状态[psychical state]的复返——换言之，即一段记忆[memory]的复返。

We are asserting, then, that the essential portion of a hysterical attack is comprised in Charcot's phase of attitudes passionnelles. In many cases it is quite obvious that this phase comprises a memory from the patient's life and frequently, indeed, that memory is always the same one. But in other cases this phase seems to be absent and the attack apparently consists only of motor phenomena – of epileptoid spasms or of a cataleptic or sleeplike state of quiescence; yet even in such cases examination under hypnosis provides definite proof of a psychical mnemic process such as is usually revealed openly in the phase passionnelle.

那么，我们是在主张：癔症发作的本质部分，正包含在沙可的"激情姿态"[attitudes passionnelles]阶段之中。在许多病例里，这一阶段包含着患者生活中的一段记忆，这一点相当明显；而且确实常常是同一段记忆。但在另一些病例里，这一阶段似乎缺如，发作显然仅由运动现象[motor phenomena]构成——即癫痫样痉挛[epileptoid spasms]，或一种僵住性的[cataleptic]、睡眠般的静止状态[quiescence]；然而即便在此类病例中，催眠[hypnosis]下的检查也提供了确凿的证明，表明存在一种心理性的记忆过程[mnemic process]，它通常在激情姿态阶段[phase passionnelle]中公开显露。

The motor phenomena of an attack are never unrelated to its psychical content; either they give a general expression of the accompanying emotion or they correspond exactly to the actions involved in the hallucinatory process.

发作的运动现象与其心理内容从不无关；它们要么对所伴随的情绪[emotion]给出一种总体性的表达，要么恰好对应于幻觉性过程[hallucinatory process]所涉及的那些行动。

(2) The memory which forms the content of a hysterical attack is not any chance one; it is the return of the event which caused the outbreak of hysteria – the psychical trauma.

(2) 构成癔症发作内容的记忆并非随手拈来的任意一段；它是导致癔症爆发[outbreak of hysteria]的那个事件——即心理创伤[psychical trauma]——的复返。

This relation is once again manifest in the classical cases of traumatic hysteria such as Charcot demonstrated in male patients, where someone who was not previously hysterical succumbed to a neurosis after a single major fright (such as a railway accident, a fall, etc.). In such cases the content of the attack consists in a hallucinatory reproduction of the event which endangered the subject's life, accompanied perhaps by the trains of thought and sense impressions which passed through his mind at the time. But the behaviour of these patients does not differ from that of common female hysterics; it is an exact model of it. If we examine the content of the attacks of one of the latter in the manner that has been indicated, we come upon events which are equally well calculated in their nature to operate as traumas (e.g. frights, mortifications, disappointments). Here, however, the single major trauma is as a rule replaced by a series of minor ones, which are held together by their similarity or by the fact of forming part of one unhappy story. Such patients, accordingly, often have attacks of different kinds, each with a particular mnemic content. This fact makes it necessary to extend the concept of traumatic hysteria considerably.

这一关系在创伤性癔症[traumatic hysteria]的典范病例中再次显露——如沙可在男性患者身上所演示的那样：一个此前并无癔症的人，在经历一次重大惊吓（诸如铁路事故、跌落等）之后罹患了神经症[neurosis]。在此类病例中，发作的内容在于对那个危及主体生命之事件的幻觉性再现[hallucinatory reproduction]，或许还伴有当时掠过其心头的思绪轨迹[trains of thought]与感觉印象[sense impressions]。但这些患者的行为与寻常的女性癔症者[female hysterics]并无不同；它正是后者的一个精确范型。若我们按前述方式考察后一类患者之一其发作的内容，便会遇到那些就其本性而言同样适宜充当创伤而起作用的事件（例如惊吓、羞辱[mortifications]、失望）。然而在这里，单一的重大创伤通常被一系列轻微创伤所取代——这些轻微创伤由它们的相似性、或由它们构成同一个不幸故事之一部分这一事实而维系在一起。因此，这类患者常常具有不同种类的发作，每一种都带有特定的记忆性[mnemic]内容。这一事实使我们有必要在相当大的程度上扩展创伤性癔症的概念。

In a third group of cases we find that the content of the attacks consists in memories which we should not have judged worthy in themselves of constituting traumas. They evidently owe this to the fact that they happened to be associated by a chance coincidence with a moment at which the subject's hysterical disposition was pathologically intensified and they were thus elevated into traumas.

在第三组病例中，我们发现发作的内容由这样一些记忆构成：它们本身并不足以被我们判定为够格构成创伤。它们之所以成为创伤，显然是缘于如下事实：它们碰巧出于偶然的巧合，与主体癔症性素质[hysterical disposition]被病理性地强化[intensified]的某个时刻关联了起来，从而被提升为创伤。

(3) The memory which forms the content of a hysterical attack is an unconscious one; or, more correctly, it is part of the second state of consciousness which is present, organized to a greater or less degree, in every hysteria. Accordingly, that memory is either wholly absent from the patient's recollection when he is in his normal state, or it is present only in a summary fashion. If we can succeed in bringing such a memory entirely into normal consciousness, it ceases to be capable of producing attacks. During an actual attack the patient is partly or wholly in the second state of consciousness. In the latter case the whole attack is covered by amnesia during his normal life; in the former case he is aware of the change in his state and of his motor behaviour, but the psychical events during the attack remain hidden from him. They can, however, be awakened at any time by hypnosis.

(3) 构成癔症发作内容的记忆是一段无意识[unconscious]的记忆；或者更确切地说，它是第二意识状态[second state of consciousness]的一部分——后者以或大或小的程度被组织于每一例癔症之中。因此，当患者处于其正常状态时，那段记忆要么全然不在其回忆[recollection]之中，要么仅以一种概括性的[summary]方式在场。若我们能成功地把这样一段记忆完全引入正常意识[normal consciousness]之中，它便不再能够产生发作。在一次实际的发作期间，患者部分地或完全地处于第二意识状态之中。在后一情形下，整场发作在其正常生活中被遗忘症[amnesia]所覆盖；在前一情形下，他觉察到自身状态的改变及其运动行为，但发作期间的心理事件对他保持隐匿。然而，这些心理事件随时可借催眠[hypnosis]而被唤醒。

(4) The question of the origin of the mnemic content of hysterical attacks coincides with the question of what it is that determines whether an experience (an idea, intention, etc.) shall be taken up into the second consciousness rather than into the normal one. We have discovered with certainty two of these determinants in hysterical subjects:

(4) 癔症发作之记忆性[mnemic]内容起源的问题，与如下问题相重合：究竟是何者决定了一个经验（一个观念、一个意图等）是被纳入第二意识[second consciousness]，而非被纳入正常意识。我们在癔症性受试者身上已确切地发现了这些决定因素[determinants]中的两个：

If a hysterical subject seeks intentionally to forget an experience or forcibly repudiates, inhibits and suppresses an intention or an idea, these psychical acts, as a consequence, enter the second state of consciousness; from there they produce their permanent effects and the memory of them returns as a hysterical attack. (Cf. hysteria in nuns, continent women, well-brought-up boys,1 people with a hankering after art or the stage, etc.)

若一位癔症性受试者有意寻求遗忘某个经验，或强力地拒斥、抑制[inhibits]并压制[suppresses]某个意图或观念，那么这些心理活动[psychical acts]便会随之进入第二意识状态；它们从那里产生其持久的效果，而关于它们的记忆则作为一场癔症发作而复返。（参较修女、节欲的妇女[continent women]、教养良好的男孩¹、渴慕艺术或舞台的人等等身上的癔症。）

Impressions received during unusual psychical states (such as affective states, states of ecstasy or autohypnosis) also enter the second state of consciousness.

在不寻常的心理状态（诸如情感状态[affective states]、出神状态[ecstasy]或自我催眠[autohypnosis]）期间所接收的印象，同样会进入第二意识状态。

It may be added that these two determinants are often combined by internal links, and that there are probably other determinants in addition to these.

或许还可补充：这两个决定因素常常由内在的联结[internal links]结合在一起；且除这两者之外，很可能还存在其他决定因素。

(5) The nervous system endeavours to keep constant something in its functional relations that we may describe as the 'sum of excitation'. It puts this precondition of health into effect by disposing associatively of every sensible accretion of excitation1 or by discharging it by an appropriate motor reaction.2 If we start out from this theorem, which, incidentally, has far-reaching implications, we find that the psychical experiences forming the content of hysteric attacks have a characteristic in common. They are all of them impressions which have failed to find adequate discharge [Abfuhr]T, either because the patient refuses to deal with them for fear of distressing mental conflicts, or because (as in the case of sexual impressions) he is forbidden to do so by modesty or social conditions, or, lastly, because he received these impressions in a state in which his nervous system was incapable of fulfilling the task of disposing of them.

(5) 神经系统力图使其功能关系中某种可称为"兴奋总量"['sum of excitation']的东西保持恒常。它通过如下方式将这一健康的前提付诸实现：或是把每一可感知的兴奋积聚[accretion of excitation]¹ 联想性地[associatively]加以处置，或是通过一种恰当的运动性反应[motor reaction]² 将其释放。若我们从这条定理出发——顺带一提，它具有深远的意涵——便会发现：构成癔症发作内容的那些心理经验具有一个共同特征。它们全都是未能寻得充分释放[Abfuhr]T 的印象：或是因为患者出于对令人痛苦的心理冲突的畏惧而拒绝处理它们；或是因为（如性印象的情形）羞怯或社会条件禁止他这样做；或，最后，是因为他接收这些印象时正处于一种其神经系统无力完成处置任务的状态。

In this way, too, we arrive at a definition of a psychical trauma which can be employed in the theory of hysteria: any impression which the nervous system has difficulty in disposing of by means of associative thinking or of motor reaction becomes a psychical trauma.

同样以这种方式，我们便得到了一个可用于癔症理论的心理创伤[psychical trauma]定义：任何令神经系统难以通过联想性思维[associative thinking]或运动性反应加以处置的印象，都会成为一段心理创伤。
