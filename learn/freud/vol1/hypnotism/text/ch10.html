EDITORS' NOTE（编者注）

Included (No. XXVIII) in Freud's own collection of abstracts of his early works (1897b; RSE, 3). Also included in NSW, 4. The original is in French. The present translation is a revised reprint of the SE version.

本文被收入弗洛伊德自编的早期著作摘要集[abstracts of his early works]（第 XXVIII 号；1897b；见 RSE 第 3 卷）。亦收入 NSW 第 4 卷。原文为法文。本译文系 SE 版（标准版）译文的修订重印本。

This paper has a long history behind it, which is related in full by Ernest Jones (1953, 255–7). The subject of the present investigation was apparently suggested by Charcot to Freud, in February, 1886, just before his departure from Paris.1 In his 'Report on my Studies in Paris and Berlin' (1956a), written in April, 1886, very soon after his return to Vienna, Freud writes that his discussions with Charcot 'led to my preparing a paper which is to appear in the Archives de Neurologie and is entitled "Vergleichung der hysterischen mit der organischen Symptomatologie" ["A Comparison between Hysterical and Organic Symptomatology"]'. (See pp. 11–12 above.) It thus seems that the paper was already written at this early date;1 but, rather more than two years later, in a letter to Fliess dated May 28, 1888, he writes: 'The first draft of the "hysterical paralyses" is now finished; it is uncertain when the second will be.'

本文背后有着一段漫长的历史，欧内斯特·琼斯（Ernest Jones，1953，第 255–7 页）对此有完整的叙述。本项考察的课题，显然是由沙可于 1886 年 2 月——就在弗洛伊德离开巴黎之前——向弗洛伊德提出的。¹ 在其写于 1886 年 4 月、即返回维也纳后不久的《巴黎和柏林进修报告》['Report on my Studies in Paris and Berlin'（1956a）]中，弗洛伊德写道，他与沙可的讨论"促使我准备撰写一篇将发表于《神经学档案》[Archives de Neurologie]、题为《癔症性与器质性症状学的比较》"Vergleichung der hysterischen mit der organischen Symptomatologie"[（'A Comparison between Hysterical and Organic Symptomatology'）]的论文"。（见上文第 11–12 页。）由此看来，该文在如此早的时间便已写成；¹ 然而，在两年多之后，即 1888 年 5 月 28 日致弗利斯（Fliess）的一封信中，他写道："'癔症性瘫痪'的初稿现已完成；第二稿何时完成则尚不确定。"

(Freud, 1950a, Letter 4.) After another three months he writes again, on August 29: 'I am now at last just finishing the hysterical and organic paralyses, with which I am fairly well pleased' (ibid., Letter 5). Further, in his Preface (also dated 'August, 1888') to his translation of Bernheim's book on suggestion (Freud, 1888–89a), he refers to the present subject and speaks of a work upon it 'which is shortly to appear' (p. 86 above). There then follow five years of complete silence, broken once more by a letter to Fliess of May 30, 1893 (1950a, Letter 12): 'The book I am sending you today is not very interesting.2 The hysterical paralyses, smaller and more interesting, is appearing at the beginning of June.' On July 10 (ibid., Letter 13): 'The hysterical paralyses should have appeared long ago; they will probably come out in the August number. It is a very short paper . . . You may remember perhaps that I was already engaged on the question when you were my pupil, and that I gave one of my University lectures on it at that time.' This would have been in the autumn of 1887, when Fliess attended some lectures of Freud's in Vienna. Finally, in another letter to Fliess, of July 24, 1893: 'The hysterical paralyses have at last appeared.'

（弗洛伊德，1950a，第 4 封信。）又过了三个月，他于 8 月 29 日再次写道："我现在终于快要完成癔症性与器质性瘫痪那篇了，我对此颇为满意"（同上，第 5 封信）。此外，在其为所译贝恩海姆（Bernheim）论暗示[suggestion]之著作（弗洛伊德，1888–89a）而写的序言（亦标注为"1888 年 8 月"）中，他提及本课题，并谈到一部关于该课题、"即将出版"的著作（见上文第 86 页）。此后是长达五年的完全沉寂，直至 1893 年 5 月 30 日致弗利斯的一封信（1950a，第 12 封信）才再度打破："我今天寄给你的那本书不太有意思。² 癔症性瘫痪那篇，篇幅更小、也更有意思，将于六月初面世。"7 月 10 日（同上，第 13 封信）："癔症性瘫痪那篇早就该面世了；大概会登在八月号上。那是一篇很短的论文……你或许还记得，当你还在做我的学生时，我就已着手研究这个问题了，那时我曾以此为题讲过一堂大学课。"这应是在 1887 年秋天，当时弗利斯在维也纳听过弗洛伊德的一些讲课。最后，在 1893 年 7 月 24 日另一封致弗利斯的信中："癔症性瘫痪那篇终于面世了。"

There is nothing to show the nature of the 'accidental and personal reasons' which Freud speaks of here (p. 187 below) to account for the five years' delay in publishing the apparently completed original draft. (Cf. also p. 79 f.) We cannot tell whether it too was written in French; but, in spite of the German title given to it in his 'Paris Report' (p. 12), it seems probable that it was. For, as we have seen, at the time of their original discussion Charcot seems to have promised to publish the outcome of Freud's investigation in the Archives de Neurologie, and he did so seven years afterwards – only a fortnight or so before his own unexpected death.

弗洛伊德在此处（见下文第 187 页）为解释那部显然已完成的原稿竟延迟五年才出版所提到的"偶然的与个人的理由"['accidental and personal reasons']，其性质我们无从得知。（另参较第 79 页及次页。）我们无法判断它是否同样以法文写成；然而，尽管在其《巴黎报告》（第 12 页）中给出了德文标题，它很可能仍是以法文写成的。因为，正如我们已看到的，在当初讨论之时，沙可似乎曾允诺在《神经学档案》[Archives de Neurologie]上发表弗洛伊德此项考察的成果，而他也确在七年之后这样做了——就在他自己意外去世前的一两个星期。

There is, however, a possible explanation of the delay, which is connected with the position occupied by this paper on the watershed between Freud's neurological and psychological writings. The first three sections of the paper are entirely neurological and were no doubt written in 1888, if not in 1886. But the fourth section must date from 1893, if only because it quotes the Breuer & Freud 'Preliminary Communication', which appeared at the beginning of that year. The whole of this last section, indeed, is based entirely on the new ideas with which Breuer and Freud had begun to operate – repression, abreaction, the principle of constancy, are all implied here if not named explicitly. Freud's direct contact with these ideas had begun in about 1887, and in the following years he was becoming more and more absorbed in them. It seems not impossible that when he had finished the first draft of this paper he had already begun to have some faint notion of an explanation of the facts contained in it which involved these new ideas, and he may for that reason have held back its publication while he went into the question more deeply.

然而，对这一延迟有一种可能的解释，它与本篇论文在弗洛伊德的神经学著作与心理学著作之间的分水岭[watershed]位置有关。论文的前三节纯属神经学，无疑写于 1888 年——若非 1886 年。但第四节必定属于 1893 年，哪怕只因为它引用了布洛伊尔与弗洛伊德的《初步通讯》['Preliminary Communication']，该文于那一年的年初问世。事实上，这最后一节的整体，完全奠基于布洛伊尔与弗洛伊德已开始运用的那些新观念之上——压抑[repression]、宣泄[abreaction]、恒常原则[principle of constancy]，凡此种种即便未被明确命名，也都隐含于此。弗洛伊德与这些观念的直接接触约始于 1887 年，而在随后的几年里，他愈来愈沉浸于其中。因此，当他完成本文初稿之时，他很可能已对其中所载事实产生了一种模糊的构想，即以一种牵涉这些新观念的方式去解释它们；他或许正因为此而暂缓发表，以便更深入地探究这一问题。

Finally, a quite minor point may be remarked on, which is of interest as a hint of things to come: the paragraph near the end of the paper which is perhaps Freud's first brief public incursion into social anthropology.

最后，还可顺带指出一个颇为次要之点，它作为一个预示未来之事的迹象而饶有趣味：即论文接近末尾的那一段，它或许是弗洛伊德首次简短地公开涉足社会人类学[social anthropology]。

正文

At the time at which, in 1885 and 1886, I was a pupil of M. Charcot's, he was kind enough to entrust me with the task of making a comparative study of organic and hysterical motor paralyses based on the observations of the Salpêtrière, in the hope that it might reveal some general characteristics of the neurosis and lead to a view of its nature. For accidental and personal reasons I have long been prevented from carrying out his commission, and even now I am only bringing forward some results of my researches, leaving on one side the details necessary for a complete statement of my opinions.

在我于 1885 年与 1886 年间做沙可先生[M. Charcot]的学生时，他好意地将一项任务托付给我：基于萨尔佩特里埃尔医院[Salpêtrière]的观察，对器质性与癔症性运动瘫痪[hysterical motor paralyses]进行一番比较研究，希望借此揭示该神经症[the neurosis]的某些一般特征，并导向对其本性的一种看法。由于一些偶然的与个人的原因，我长久以来一直未能完成他的这一托付；即便在现在，我也只是提出我研究的部分结果，而将那些为完整陈述我的见解所必需的细节搁置一旁。

I must begin with some remarks on organic motor paralyses which, incidentally, are generally accepted. Clinical neurology recognizes two kinds of motor paralysis – periphero-spinal (or bulbar) paralysis and cerebral paralysis. This distinction agrees entirely with the findings of the anatomy of the nervous system, which show that the course of the motor conductive fibres falls into two sections only: of which the first runs from the periphery to the cells of the anterior horns of the spinal cord and the second from there to the cerebral cortex. The modern histology of the nervous system, founded on the work of Golgi, Ramón y Cajal, Kölliker, etc., expresses these facts in the statement that 'the course of the motor conductive fibres is made up of two neurons (cellulo-fibrillary neural units), which meet and enter into relation with each other at the level of the cells known as the motor cells of the anterior horns.' The essential difference between these two sorts of paralysis is, in clinical terms, as follows: periphero-spinal paralysis is a paralysis 'détaillée', cerebral paralysis is a paralysis 'en masse'.

我须先就器质性运动瘫痪[organic motor paralyses]作几点说明，这些说明顺带一提也是被普遍接受的。临床神经学[clinical neurology]承认两种运动瘫痪——周围脊髓性（或延髓性）[periphero-spinal (or bulbar)]瘫痪与大脑性[cerebral]瘫痪。这一区分与神经系统解剖学[anatomy of the nervous system]的发现完全一致：后者表明，运动传导纤维[motor conductive fibres]的走行只分为两段——第一段自外周行至脊髓前角[anterior horns of the spinal cord]的细胞，第二段则从那里通向大脑皮层[cerebral cortex]。以高尔基（Golgi）、拉蒙-卡哈尔（Ramón y Cajal）、克利克尔（Kölliker）等人的工作为基础的现代神经系统组织学[histology]，将上述事实表述为如下论断："运动传导纤维的走行由两个神经元[neurons]（细胞-纤维性神经单位[cellulo-fibrillary neural units]）构成，二者在被称为前角运动细胞[motor cells of the anterior horns]的细胞层面上相遇并彼此发生关系。"这两类瘫痪之间的本质区别，用临床术语来说如下：周围脊髓性瘫痪是一种"细致性"['détaillée']瘫痪，大脑性瘫痪则是一种"整体性"['en masse']瘫痪。

The type of the former is the facial paralysis in Bell's palsy, the paralysis in acute infantile poliomyelitis, etc. In these disorders each muscle – one might say, each muscle fibre – can be paralysed individually and in isolation. What happens depends only on the site and extent of the nervous lesion; and there is no fixed rule according to which one peripheral element escapes paralysis while another suffers from it permanently.

前者的典型实例是贝尔氏麻痹[Bell's palsy]中的面瘫、急性婴儿脊髓灰质炎[acute infantile poliomyelitis]中的瘫痪，等等。在这类障碍中，每一块肌肉——或许可以说，每一条肌纤维——都可以被个别地[individually]、孤立地[in isolation]加以瘫痪。发生什么只取决于神经病灶[nervous lesion]的部位与范围；并不存在一条固定的规则，规定某个周围性成分[peripheral element]得以免于瘫痪，而另一个成分却永久地受其影响。

Cerebral paralysis, on the contrary, is always a disorder that attacks a large portion of the periphery, a limb, a segment of an extremity or a complicated motor apparatus. It never affects an individual muscle – for instance, the biceps of the arm or the tibialis in isolation; and if there are apparent exceptions to this rule (cortical ptosis, for example), we can see clearly that what are in question are muscles which perform by themselves a function of which they are the sole instrument.

与此相反，大脑性瘫痪[cerebral paralysis]始终是一种侵袭外周很大一部分的障碍——侵袭一个肢体、一个肢段或一套复杂的运动装置[motor apparatus]。它从不影响单独一块肌肉——例如单独影响臂部的肱二头肌[biceps]或胫骨肌[tibialis]；而即便这条规则存在表面上的例外（例如皮质性上睑下垂[cortical ptosis]），我们也能清楚地看到，此处所涉及的乃是那些自身即执行某一功能的肌肉，而它们正是该功能的唯一工具。

In the cerebral paralyses of the extremities, it can be observed that the distal segments always suffer more than the proximal ones; for instance, the hand is more paralysed than the shoulder. So far as I know, there is no such thing as an isolated cerebral paralysis of the shoulder with the hand retaining its motility, whereas the contrary is the rule in paralyses which are not complete.

在四肢的大脑性瘫痪[cerebral paralyses]中，可以观察到远端节段[distal segments]总是比近端节段[proximal ones]受损更重；例如，手比肩部瘫痪得更重。就我所知，并不存在这样的情形：肩部发生孤立的大脑性瘫痪[isolated cerebral paralysis]，而手仍保有运动能力[motility]；相反，在那些并不完全的瘫痪中，与之相反的才是常规。

In a critical study of aphasia, published in 1891, I endeavoured to show that the cause of this important difference between periphero-spinal and cerebral paralyses is to be looked for in the structure of the nervous system. Each element in the periphery corresponds to an element in the grey matter of the cord, which, as M. Charcot has said, is its nervous termination; the periphery is, so to say, projected upon the grey matter of the cord, point by point and element by element. I have proposed to give the periphero-spinal paralysis détaillée the name of projection paralysis. But the same is not true of the relations between the elements of the cord and those of the cortex. The number of conductive fibres would no longer be sufficient to give a second projection of the periphery upon the cortex.

在 1891 年发表的一篇关于失语症[aphasia]的批判性研究中，我曾力图表明：周围脊髓性瘫痪与大脑性瘫痪之间这一重要差异的原因，须到神经系统的结构中去寻找。外周中的每一要素，都与脊髓灰质[grey matter of the cord]中的一个要素相对应——正如沙可先生[M. Charcot]所言，后者是它的神经终末[nervous termination]；可以说，外周是一点一点、一个要素一个要素地投射[projected]到脊髓灰质之上的。我曾提议，把这种细致性的[détaillée]周围脊髓性瘫痪命名为投射性瘫痪[projection paralysis]。然而，脊髓诸要素与皮层[cortex]诸要素之间的关系却并非如此。传导纤维[conductive fibres]的数目，将不再足以把外周第二次投射到皮层之上。

We must suppose that the fibres running from the cord to the cortex no longer each represent a single element of the periphery but rather a group of them, and even, on the other hand, that one element of the periphery may correspond to several spino-cortical conductive fibres. The fact is that there is a change in arrangement at the connecting point between the two sections of the motor system. I argue, therefore, that the reproduction of the periphery in the cortex is no longer a faithful reproduction point by point, that it is no longer a true projection. It is a relation by means of what may be termed representative fibres, and for cerebral paralysis I propose the name of representation paralysis.1

我们必须设想：从脊髓通向皮层的那些纤维，已不再各自代表外周中的单一要素，而是代表它们的一个群组；另一方面，甚至必须设想，外周中的一个要素也可能对应着若干条脊髓-皮层性[spino-cortical]传导纤维。事实是，在运动系统这两个部分之间的连接点上，发生了排列方式上的变化。因此我主张，外周在皮层中的复现[reproduction]，已不再是逐点的忠实复现，即不再是一种真正的投射。它是一种凭借或可称为代表性纤维[representative fibres]的东西而建立的关系；而对于大脑性瘫痪，我提议采用表征性瘫痪[representation paralysis]¹ 这一名称。

When projection paralysis is total and very extensive, it too, of course, is paralysis en masse and its main distinctive characteristic is obliterated. On the other hand, cortical paralysis, which is distinguished among cerebral paralyses by its greater tendency to dissociation, nevertheless always presents the character of a representation paralysis.

当投射性瘫痪[projection paralysis]是完全的且范围极广时，它当然同样是一种整体性瘫痪[paralysis en masse]，而其主要的区别性特征便被抹除了。另一方面，皮质性瘫痪[cortical paralysis]——它在大脑性瘫痪中以其更强的解离[dissociation]倾向而著称——却始终呈现出一种表征性瘫痪[representation paralysis]的性质。

The other differences between projection and representation paralyses are well known. I may instance among them the normal nutrition and the integrity of the electrical reactions [of the parts affected] which are associated with the latter. Although they are very important clinically, these signs do not possess the theoretical importance which is to be attributed to the first differential characteristic that we mentioned – paralysis détaillée and paralysis en masse.

投射性瘫痪与表征性瘫痪之间的其他种种差异是人所熟知的。我或许可以举出其中之一：与后者相关联的正常营养状态[nutrition]，以及电反应[electrical reactions]的完整无损（受患部位的）[of the parts affected]。尽管这些征象在临床上极为重要，它们却不具备那种理论上的重要性——后者应归于我们所提到的第一个鉴别性特征[differential characteristic]，即细致性瘫痪[paralysis détaillée]与整体性瘫痪[paralysis en masse]。

Hysteria has fairly often been credited with a faculty for simulating the most various organic nervous disorders. The question arises whether, more precisely, it simulates the characteristics of the two sorts of organic paralyses, whether there are hysterical projection paralyses and hysterical representation paralyses like those in organic symptomatology. Here a first fact of importance emerges. Hysteria never simulates periphero-spinal or projection paralyses; hysterical paralyses only share the characteristics of organic representation paralyses. This is a most interesting fact, since Bell's palsy, radial paralysis, etc., are among the commonest disorders of the nervous system.

癔症[hysteria]相当经常地被赋予一种能力，即能够模拟[simulating]各式各样的器质性神经障碍。于是问题便产生了：更确切地说，癔症所模拟的究竟是这两类器质性瘫痪的特征吗？是否存在着像器质性症状学[organic symptomatology]中那样的癔症性投射性瘫痪与癔症性表征性瘫痪？在此，第一个重要的事实浮现出来：癔症从不模拟周围脊髓性瘫痪或投射性瘫痪；癔症性瘫痪只具有器质性表征性瘫痪的特征。这是一个饶有趣味的事实，因为贝尔氏麻痹[Bell's palsy]、桡神经麻痹[radial paralysis]等，都属神经系统最常见的障碍之列。

It is desirable to point out here, in order to avoid any confusion, that I am only dealing with flaccid hysterical paralyses and not with hysterical contractures. It appears to me impossible to apply the same rules to hysterical paralyses and contractures. It is only of flaccid hysterical paralyses that it can be maintained that they never affect single muscles (except where the muscle concerned is the sole instrument of a function), that they are always paralyses en masse and that in this respect they correspond to representation paralyses or organic cerebral paralyses. Furthermore, in the matter of the nutrition of the paralysed parts and their electrical reactions, hysterical paralyses present the same characteristics as organic cerebral paralyses.

在此有必要指出一点，以免产生任何混淆：我此刻只处理弛缓性癔症性瘫痪[flaccid hysterical paralyses]，而不涉及癔症性挛缩[hysterical contractures]。在我看来，不可能把同样的规则应用于癔症性瘫痪与癔症性挛缩。只有就弛缓性癔症性瘫痪而言，才能主张：它们从不影响单块肌肉（除非所涉肌肉乃是某一功能的唯一工具）；它们始终是整体性瘫痪[paralyses en masse]；并且在这一方面，它们对应于表征性瘫痪或器质性大脑性瘫痪[organic cerebral paralyses]。此外，在瘫痪部位的营养状态及其电反应方面，癔症性瘫痪也呈现出与器质性大脑性瘫痪相同的特征。

If hysterical paralysis is thus associated with cerebral paralysis and in particular with cortical paralysis, which presents a greater aptitude for dissociation, it is no less distinguished from them by important characteristics. In the first place, it is not bound by the rule, which applies regularly to organic cerebral paralyses, that the distal segment is always more affected than the proximal one. In hysteria, the shoulder or the thigh may be more paralysed than the hand or the foot. Movements may appear in the fingers while the proximal segment is still absolutely inert. There is not the slightest difficulty in artificially producing an isolated paralysis of the thigh, the leg, etc., and clinically one can quite often come across these isolated paralyses, in contradiction to the rules of organic cerebral paralysis.

如果癔症性瘫痪[hysterical paralysis]因此与大脑性瘫痪相关联，尤其与具有更强解离[dissociation]禀赋的皮质性瘫痪[cortical paralysis]相关联，那么它同样以若干重要特征而与二者相区别。首先，它并不受制于那条对有器质性大脑性瘫痪规律地适用的规则——即远端节段[distal segment]总是比近端节段[proximal one]受损更重。在癔症中，肩部或大腿部可能比手或足部瘫痪得更重。手指可能已出现活动，而近端节段仍绝对地毫无动静[inert]。人为地造成一种孤立的大腿、小腿等部位的瘫痪，并无丝毫困难；而在临床上，人们也相当经常地可以遇到这类孤立性瘫痪——与器质性大脑性瘫痪的规则相抵触。

In this important respect hysterical paralysis is, so to say, intermediate between organic projection paralysis and representation paralysis. If it does not possess all the characteristics of dissociation and isolation proper to the former, it is far from being submitted to the strict laws that govern the latter – cerebral paralysis. Subject to these qualifications, it may be maintained that hysterical paralysis is also a representation paralysis, but with a special kind of representation whose characteristics remain to be discovered.

在这一重要方面，癔症性瘫痪可以说介于器质性投射性瘫痪与表征性瘫痪之间。如果说它并不具备前者所特有的全部解离与孤立之特征，那么它也远未服从于支配后者的——即大脑性瘫痪的——那些严格规律。在上述限定之下，可以认为癔症性瘫痪同样是一种表征性瘫痪，只不过是一种特殊类型的表征；至于其诸特征，尚有待发现。

As a step in that direction, I propose to study the other distinguishing characteristics between hysterical paralysis and cortical paralysis, the most perfect type of organic cerebral paralysis. We have already mentioned the first of these characteristics – the fact that hysterical paralysis can be more dissociated, more systematized, than cerebral paralysis. The symptoms of organic paralysis appear piecemeal, as it were, in hysteria. Among the symptoms of common organic hemiplegia (paralysis of the upper and lower limbs and of the lower part of the face) hysteria reproduces only the paralysis of the limbs and even dissociates, quite often and with the greatest ease, paralyses of the arm and of the leg in the form of monoplegias.

作为朝这一方向迈出的一步，我提议考察癔症性瘫痪与皮质性瘫痪[cortical paralysis]——器质性大脑性瘫痪最完善的类型——之间其他一些区别性特征。我们已经提及了这些特征中的第一个——即癔症性瘫痪可以比大脑性瘫痪更为解离、更加系统化[systematized]这一事实。器质性瘫痪的症状，可以说在癔症中是零碎地[piecemeal]出现的。在常见的器质性偏瘫[organic hemiplegia]（上肢与下肢以及面部下部的瘫痪）诸症状中，癔症只复现[reproduces]肢体的瘫痪，甚至相当经常且极为轻易地，以单瘫[monoplegias]的形式把臂部与腿部的瘫痪彼此解离开来。

From the syndrome of organic aphasia it reproduces motor aphasia in isolation; and – something unheard of in organic aphasia – it can create total aphasia (motor and sensory) for a particular language without in the slightest interfering with the faculty of understanding and articulating another. (I have observed this in some unpublished cases.)1 This same power of dissociation is manifested in isolated paralyses of one segment of a limb while other parts of the same limb remain completely unimpaired, or, again, in the total abolition of a function (e.g. in abasia and astasia) while another function performed by the same organs remains intact.2 This dissociation is all the more striking when the function that is unimpaired is the more complex one. In organic symptomatology, if there is an unequal weakening of several functions, it is always the more complex function, the one that has been more recently acquired, that is most affected as the result of the paralysis.

从器质性失语症[organic aphasia]这一综合征中，它（癔症）单独地复现出运动性失语症[motor aphasia]；并且——这在器质性失语症中是闻所未闻的——它能够针对某一种特定语言造成完全性失语[total aphasia]（运动性与感觉性），而丝毫不妨碍理解与说出另一种语言的能力。（我在若干未发表的病例中观察过此种情形。）¹ 这种同样的解离能力，也表现在一个肢段的孤立性瘫痪上——此时同一肢体的其他部分完全未受损害；或者，同样表现在某一功能的完全丧失上（例如在步行不能[abasia]与站立不能[astasia]中），而由同样这些器官所执行的另一功能却保持完好。² 当那个未受损害的功能是较为复杂的功能时，这种解离就愈发引人注目。在器质性症状学中，若几个功能出现不均衡的减弱，那么作为瘫痪的结果受损最重的，总是那个较为复杂的功能，即那个较晚近才获得的功能。

Furthermore, hysterical paralysis exhibits another characteristic which is, as it were, the hallmark of the neurosis and which comes as an addition to the first one. Hysteria, as I have heard M. Charcot say, is indeed a disease of excessive manifestations; it tends to produce its symptoms with the greatest possible intensity. This characteristic is shown not only in its paralyses but also in its contractures and anaesthesia. We know to what a degree of distortion hysterical contractures can be carried – a degree almost unequalled in organic symptomatology. We know, too, the frequency in hysteria of absolute, profound anaesthesia, of which organic lesions can reproduce only a feeble sketch. It is the same with paralyses. They are often absolute to the most extreme extent. The aphasic utters not a word, whereas the organic aphasic almost always retains a few words, 'yes' or 'no', a swear word, etc.; the paralysed arm is completely inert – and so on. This characteristic is too well known to need insisting upon. In contrast to this, we know that in organic paralysis paresis is always commoner than absolute paralysis.

此外，癔症性瘫痪还表现出另一个特征，它可以说是这一神经症[the neurosis]的标志，并且是在第一个特征之外的又一补充。正如我曾听沙可先生[M. Charcot]所言，癔症确实是一种以过度表现[excessive manifestations]为特征的疾病；它倾向于以最大可能的强度产生其症状。这一特征不仅见于其瘫痪，也见于其挛缩[contractures]与感觉缺失[anaesthesia]。我们知道癔症性挛缩可以被带到何等程度的变形——那种程度在器质性症状学中几乎无与伦比。我们也知道，在癔症中绝对的、深度的感觉缺失何其常见——而器质性病灶对此只能复现出一幅微弱的勾勒[sketch]。瘫痪的情形亦然。它们往往达到最极端的绝对程度。失语症者一言不发，而器质性失语症者则几乎总能保留若干词语，如"是"或"不"、一个咒骂词等；瘫痪的臂部则完全毫无动静——如此等等。这一特征尽人皆知，无须赘言。与此相反，我们知道在器质性瘫痪中，轻瘫[paresis]总是比绝对性瘫痪更为常见。

Hysterical paralysis is thus characterized by precise limitation and excessive intensity; it possesses both these qualities at once, and it is in this that it shows the greatest contrast to organic cerebral paralysis, in which it is regularly found that these two characteristics are not associated with each other. There are monoplegias in organic symptomatology, but they are almost always monoplegias a potiori1 and not precisely delimited. If the arm is paralysed as the result of an organic cortical lesion, there is almost always a minor concomitant affection of the face and leg; and if this complication is not apparent at a particular moment it will certainly have existed at the start of the illness. The truth is that a cortical monoplegia is always a hemiplegia of which one part or another is more or less obliterated but is still recognizable. To go a little further, let us suppose that the paralysis has affected no part other than the arm, that it is a pure cortical monoplegia; the paralysis will then be seen to be of moderate intensity. As soon as this monoplegia increases in intensity and becomes an absolute paralysis, it will lose its character of a pure monoplegia and will be accompanied by motor disorders in the leg or face. It cannot at the same time become absolute and retain its delimitation.

癔症性瘫痪因此以精确的界限[precise limitation]与过度的强度[excessive intensity]为特征；它同时具备这两种性质，而正是在这一点上，它显示出与器质性大脑性瘫痪最大的反差——在后者中，人们规律地发现这两种特征并不彼此结合。在器质性症状学中确有一些单瘫，但它们几乎总是「a potiori（优先意义上的）」单瘫[monoplegias a potiori]¹，而非精确划界者。若臂部因器质性皮质病灶[cortical lesion]而瘫痪，则几乎总伴有面部与腿部一种较轻微的并发性[concomitant]受累；即便这一并发症在某一特定时刻并不明显，它在疾病之初也必定曾经存在。实情是，皮质性单瘫总是某种偏瘫——其某一部分或多或少被抹除，但仍可辨识。再进一步说，让我们假设瘫痪并未累及臂部以外的任何部位，即它是一例纯粹的皮质性单瘫[cortical monoplegia]；那么这瘫痪将被看作强度中等的。一旦这种单瘫强度增加、变成绝对性瘫痪，它便失去其纯粹单瘫的性质，并伴有腿部或面部的运动障碍[motor disorders]。它不可能同时既成为绝对的，又保持其界限划定的性质。

This, on the contrary, is what can be achieved quite easily by a hysterical paralysis, as clinical experience shows us daily. For instance, it will affect an arm exclusively and without our finding a trace of it in the leg or face. Moreover, at the arm level it is as severe as a paralysis can be, and there we have a striking difference from an organic paralysis – a difference which gives us decided cause for reflection.

与此相反，这恰恰是癔症性瘫痪能够相当轻易地达成的事情，正如临床经验每天都在向我们展示的那样。例如，它会唯独影响一侧臂部，而我们在腿部或面部却找不到任何受累的痕迹。此外，就臂部这一层面而言，其严重程度已达瘫痪所能达到的极限；在此我们便看到与器质性瘫痪之间存在一个触目的差异——这一差异为我们提供了充分的反思理由。

There are, of course, cases of hysterical paralysis in which the intensity is not excessive and in which the dissociation is not in any way remarkable. These can be recognized by other characteristics; but they are cases which do not carry the typical mark of the neurosis, and which, since they can teach us nothing of its nature, are not of any interest from our present point of view.

当然，也有一些癔症性瘫痪的病例，其强度并不过度，其解离也丝毫不引人注目。这些病例可以凭其他特征来识别；但它们并不带有这一神经症的典型标志，并且，既然它们丝毫不能教给我们关于其本性的东西，那么从我们目前的立场来看便不值得关注。

I will add a few comments which are of secondary importance and are even somewhat outside the limits of our topic.

我将再补充几点评论，它们只具有次要的意义，甚至多少超出了我们这一课题的范围。

In the first place I may point out that hysterical paralyses are much more frequently accompanied by disorders of sensibility than are organic paralyses. Such disturbances are in general more profound and more frequent in neuroses than in organic symptomatology. Nothing is commoner than hysterical anaesthesia or analgesia. It will, on the other hand, be recalled with what tenacity sensibility persists where there is a neural lesion. If a peripheral nerve is severed, the anaesthesia will be less in extent and intensity than would have been expected. If an inflammatory lesion attacks the spinal nerves or the centres in the spinal cord, we always find that motility is the first thing to suffer and that sensibility is spared or merely weakened, for somewhere or other neural elements always persist which have not been totally destroyed. Where there is a cerebral lesion, we are familiar with the frequency and duration of the motor hemiplegia, while the accompanying hemianaesthesia is indistinct and transitory and is not present in every case. It is only a few quite special localizations of the lesion which can produce an intense and lasting disturbance of sensibility (confluence of sensory pathways), and even this fact is open to doubt.

首先我可以指出：癔症性瘫痪伴随感觉障碍[disorders of sensibility]的情形，远较器质性瘫痪为多。这类障碍在神经症中一般而言比在器质性症状学[organic symptomatology]中更为深重，也更为常见。没有什么比癔症性的感觉缺失[anaesthesia]或痛觉缺失[analgesia]更常见的了。另一方面，人们也会记得：在存在神经病灶[neural lesion]之处，感觉能力[sensibility]何等顽强地持存。若某一周围神经被切断，其感觉缺失的范围与强度会比预想的要小。若炎症性病灶[inflammatory lesion]侵袭脊神经或脊髓内的各中枢[centres]，我们总会发现运动能力[motility]是最先受损的，而感觉能力则得以保全或仅只减弱——因为某处总还残留着未被完全毁坏的神经要素。在存在大脑病灶[cerebral lesion]时，我们对运动性偏瘫[motor hemiplegia]的常见程度与持续时日都很熟悉，而与之相伴的偏身感觉缺失[hemianaesthesia]却是模糊不清、转瞬即逝的，且并非每个病例都有。只有少数相当特殊的病灶定位能产生强烈而持久的感觉障碍（感觉通路的汇合[confluence of sensory pathways]），而即便是这一事实也仍有疑问。

This behaviour of sensibility, differing in organic lesions and in hysteria, is scarcely explicable at present. We seem to have here a problem whose solution might perhaps throw light on the intimate nature of the phenomena.

感觉能力的这种表现，在器质性病灶与癔症中各不相同，目前几乎无法解释。我们在此似乎遇到了一个问题，其解答或许能为这些现象的内在本质[intimate nature]投下光亮。

Another point which seems to me to deserve mention is that there are certain forms of cerebral paralysis which are not found in hysteria any more than are the periphero-spinal projection paralyses. Such, in the first place, is paralysis of the lower half of the face, the most frequent manifestation of an organic disease of the brain, and (if I may be allowed to pass for a moment to sensory paralyses) homonymous lateral hemianopsia. I am conscious that it is almost risking a wager to assert that such and such a symptom is not found in hysteria, when the researches of M. Charcot and his pupils find in it – one might say every day – fresh symptoms which had not been suspected previously. But I must take things as they are at the moment. The occurrence of hysterical facial paralysis is strongly disputed by M. Charcot, and, even if we believe its partisans, is a phenomenon of great rarity. Hemianopsia has not yet been observed in hysteria, and, I believe, never will be.

另有一点在我看来说得上是值得提及的：有些形式的大脑性瘫痪在癔症中同样找不到，正如周围脊髓性投射性瘫痪在癔症中也找不到一样。首先，这类情形就是下半面部的瘫痪——它是脑器质性疾病最常见的一种表现；以及（如果允许我暂时转到感觉性瘫痪[sensory paralyses]上来）同侧性偏盲[homonymous lateral hemianopsia]。我意识到，断言某某症状在癔症中找不到，几乎是在冒险下赌注——因为沙可先生[M. Charcot]及其学生的研究，总在癔症中发现（几乎可以说每天都有）此前未曾被察觉的新症状。但我只能就目前的状况来谈。癔症性面瘫[hysterical facial paralysis]的发生，遭到沙可先生的强烈否认；而即便我们相信其支持者，它也是一种极为罕见的现象。偏盲[hemianopsia]在癔症中尚未被观察到，而且我相信，也永远不会被观察到。

How is it, then, that hysterical paralyses, while closely simulating cortical paralyses, diverge from them by the distinctive characteristics which I have tried to enumerate? And what is the general character of the special sort of representation with which they must be associated? The answer to this question would include a large and important part of the theory of the neurosis.

那么，癔症性瘫痪何以在紧密模拟皮质性瘫痪[cortical paralyses]的同时，又以我曾试图逐一列举的那些区别性特征而与它们分道而去呢？而它们必定与之相关联的那种特殊类型的表征，其一般性质又是什么呢？对这一问题的回答，将包含这一神经症的理论中一个广大而重要的部分。

（III）

There is not the slightest doubt as to the conditions which dominate the symptomatology of cerebral paralysis. They are the facts of anatomy – the construction of the nervous system and the distribution of its vessels – and the relation between these two series of facts and the circumstances of the lesion. We have pointed out that the lesser number of the fibres running from the spinal cord to the cortex in comparison with the number of fibres running from the periphery to the cord is the basis of the difference between projection and representation paralysis. In the same way every clinical detail of representation paralysis can be explained by some detail of cerebral structure; and, conversely, we can deduce the construction of the brain from the clinical characteristics of the paralyses. I believe there is a complete parallelism between these two series.

支配大脑性瘫痪症状学[symptomatology]的诸条件，是毫无疑义的。它们就是解剖学的事实——神经系统的构造及其血管的分布——以及这两组事实与病灶所处情形之间的关系。我们已经指出：自脊髓通向皮层的纤维数目，相较于自外周通向脊髓的纤维数目为少，这一事实正是投射性瘫痪与表征性瘫痪之间差异的基础。同样地，表征性瘫痪的每一临床细节，都可以由大脑结构的某一细节来解释；反过来说，我们也能从瘫痪的临床特征推出大脑的构造。我相信，这两组事实之间存在着一种完全的平行关系[parallelism]。

Thus, if there is no great facility for dissociation in ordinary cerebral paralysis, that is because the motor fibres run too close together over a long stretch of their intracerebral course for them to be damaged individually. If cortical paralysis shows more tendency to being monoplegic, that is because the diameter of the conducting bundles (brachial, crural, etc.) increases towards the cortex. If paralysis of the hand is the most complete of all the cortical paralyses, that, we believe, is due to the fact that the crossed relation between the cerebral hemisphere and the periphery is more exclusive in the case of the hand than of any other part of the body. If the distal segment of an extremity suffers more from paralysis than the proximal one, we suppose that the representative fibres of the distal segment are far more numerous than those of the proximal one, so that cortical influence becomes more important for the former than it is for the latter. If fairly extensive lesions of the cortex do not succeed in producing pure monoplegias, we infer that the motor centres in the cortex are not sharply separated from one another by neutral territory, or that there are factors operating at a distance (Fernwirkungen¹), which would seem to cancel the effect of a precise separation of the centres.

因此，如果说寻常的大脑性瘫痪并不具有很强的解离能力，那是因为运动纤维在其脑内走行[intracerebral course]的一大段距离上彼此靠得太近，以致无法被个别地损毁。如果皮质性瘫痪表现出更强的成为单瘫[monoplegic]的倾向，那是因为传导束[conducting bundles]（臂束、腿束[brachial, crural]等）的直径朝皮层方向增大。如果说手部瘫痪是一切皮质性瘫痪中最完全者，我们认为，这应归因于：就手而言，大脑半球与外周之间的交叉关系[crossed relation]比身体任何其他部位都更为专有。如果一个肢体的远端节段比近端节段更受瘫痪之苦，我们便设想：远端节段的代表性纤维远多于近端节段，因此皮层的影响对前者比对后者更为重要。如果相当广泛的皮层病灶未能产生纯粹的单瘫，我们便推断：皮层中的诸运动中枢并未借由一块中性地带[neutral territory]彼此截然分开；或者，存在着一些远隔作用[factors operating at a distance]（Fernwirkungen¹），它们似乎会抵消诸中枢精确分离的效果。

In the same way, if in organic aphasia there is always a mixture of disturbances of various functions, that is to be explained by the fact that branches of the same artery nourish all the speech centres, or, if the opinion expressed in my critical study of aphasia [Freud, 1891b; NSW, 4] is accepted, by the fact that we are dealing not with separate centres but with a continuous area of association. However that may be, an explanation derived from anatomy is always to be found.

同样地，如果在器质性失语症中总是混杂着各种不同功能的障碍，那也应当这样来解释：即同一条动脉的分支滋养着所有的言语中枢[speech centres]；或者，如果我在关于失语症的批判性研究[critical study of aphasia]（弗洛伊德，1891b；NSW 第 4 卷）中所表述的意见可以被接受，则是因为我们所面对的并非彼此分离的诸中枢，而是一个连续的联系区[continuous area of association]。无论如何，总能找到一种源自解剖学的解释。

The remarkable associations that are so often observed clinically in cortical paralyses (motor aphasia and right hemiplegia, alexia and right hemianopsia) are explained by the propinquity of the damaged centres. Hemianopsia itself, a symptom that is very curious and strange to a non-scientific mind, is only explicable by the crossing of the fibres of the optic nerve at the chiasma; it is a clinical expression of it, just as every detail of the cerebral paralyses is the clinical expression of a fact of anatomy

在皮质性瘫痪中临床上如此经常观察到的那些值得注意的联合[associations]（运动性失语症与右侧偏瘫、失读症[alexia]与右侧偏盲），可由受损诸中枢的邻近[propinquity]来解释。偏盲本身——这个对非科学头脑而言极为奇特而陌生的症状——只能由视神经[optic nerve]纤维在视交叉[chiasma]处的交叉来解释；它是这一交叉的临床表达，正如大脑性瘫痪的每一细节都是某一解剖学事实的临床表达。

Since there can only be a single cerebral anatomy that is true, and since it finds expression in the clinical characteristics of the cerebral paralyses, it is clearly impossible for that anatomy to be the explanation of the distinctive features of hysterical paralyses. For that reason we must not draw conclusions on the subject of cerebral anatomy that are based on the symptomatology of those paralyses.

既然只能有一种真实的大脑解剖学，而它又在皮质性瘫痪的临床特征中得到表达，那么这种解剖学显然不可能成为癔症性瘫痪诸区别性特征的解释。正因如此，我们切不可基于这些瘫痪的症状学，就大脑解剖学作出结论。

In order to explain this difficult problem we must certainly consider the nature of the lesion concerned. In organic paralyses the nature of the lesion plays a secondary part; it is rather the extent and localization of the lesion which, in the given structural conditions of the nervous system, produce the characteristics of organic paralysis which we have indicated. What might be the nature of the lesion in hysterical paralysis, which dominates the situation, without regard to the localization or extent of the lesion or of the anatomy of the nervous system?

为了解释这一困难的问题，我们确实必须考虑所涉病灶[the lesion concerned]的性质。在器质性瘫痪中，病灶的性质只起次要的作用；毋宁说，正是在神经系统既定的结构条件下，病灶的范围与定位产生了我们已指出的那些器质性瘫痪的特征。那么，在癔症性瘫痪中，那支配着整个局面、却不顾及病灶的定位或范围、也不顾及神经系统解剖的病灶，其性质又可能是怎样的呢？

We have several times heard from M. Charcot that it is a cortical lesion, but one that is purely dynamic or functional. This is a thesis whose negative aspect we can well understand: it is equivalent to asserting that no appreciable tissue changes will be found post mortem. But in its positive aspect its interpretation is far from being unequivocal. What, after all, is a dynamic lesion? I am quite sure that many who read M. Charcot’s works believe that a dynamic lesion is indeed a lesion, but one of which no trace is found after death, such as an oedema, an anaemia or an active hyperaemia. These, however, although they may not necessarily persist after death, are true organic lesions even if they are slight and transitory. Paralyses produced by lesions of this order would necessarily share the characteristics of organic paralyses. Neither oedema nor anaemia, any more than haemorrhage or softening [infarction], could produce the dissociation and intensity of hysterical paralyses. The only difference would be that paralysis due to oedema, by vascular constriction, etc., would be less lasting than paralysis due to destruction of nervous tissue. They have all the other conditions in common, and the anatomy of the nervous system will determine the properties of the paralysis as much in the case of a transitory anaemia as in that of one that is permanent and final.

我们已不止一次听沙可先生[M. Charcot]说过：它是一种皮质性病灶，但纯粹是动力性的[dynamic]或功能性的[functional]。这是这样一个论点，其否定性的一面我们完全能够理解：它等同于断言死后[post mortem]不会发现任何可觉察的组织改变。但在其肯定性的一面，它的解释却远非明确无疑。说到底，究竟什么是动力性病灶[dynamic lesion]呢？我确信，许多读过沙可先生著作的人都以为，动力性病灶确实是一种病灶，只不过死后寻不到它的任何痕迹，例如水肿[oedema]、贫血[anaemia]或主动性充血[active hyperaemia]。然而，这些情形尽管未必能在死后持续存在，却仍是真正的器质性病灶，即便它们轻微而短暂。由这类病灶所产生的瘫痪，必然会具有器质性瘫痪的那些特征。无论是水肿还是贫血，一如出血[haemorrhage]或软化[softening]（梗死[infarction]），都不可能产生癔症性瘫痪所具有的那种解离与强度。唯一的差别只在于：由水肿、由血管收缩[vascular constriction]等所致的瘫痪，其持续时间不如由神经组织毁坏所致的瘫痪那样长。它们在其他一切条件上都是共同的；而神经系统的解剖，将同样地决定瘫痪的性质——无论所涉是一过性的贫血，还是永久而终局的贫血。

These remarks do not seem to me entirely gratuitous. If one reads that ‘there must be a hysterical lesion’ in such and such a centre, the same centre in which an organic lesion would produce a corresponding organic syndrome, and if one recalls that one is accustomed to localize a hysterical dynamic lesion in the same manner as an organic lesion, one is led to believe that behind the expression ‘dynamic lesion’ there is hidden the idea of a lesion like oedema or anaemia, which are in fact transitory organic affections. I, on the contrary, assert that the lesion in hysterical paralyses must be completely independent of the anatomy of the nervous system, since in its paralyses and other manifestations hysteria behaves as though anatomy did not exist or as though it had no knowledge of it.

这些评述在我看来并非全然无端。如果有人读到这样的话：在某某个中枢"必定存在一个癔症性病灶[hysterical lesion]"——而正是在同一个中枢，器质性病灶会产生相应的器质性综合征[organic syndrome]——并且，如果人们回想起自己习惯于像定位器质性病灶那样去定位一个癔症性动力性病灶，那么他便会相信：在"动力性病灶"这一说法背后，隐藏着一种类似水肿或贫血的病灶观念，而这两者在事实上都属于一过性的器质性病变。相反，我要断言：癔症性瘫痪中的病灶必定完全独立于神经系统的解剖，因为在它的瘫痪及其他表现中，癔症的行为方式宛如解剖并不存在，或宛如它对解剖一无所知。

And in fact a good number of the characteristics of hysterical paralyses justify this assertion. Hysteria is ignorant of the distribution of the nerves, and that is why it does not simulate periphero-spinal or projection paralyses. It has no knowledge of the optic chiasma, and consequently it does not produce hemianopsia. It takes the organs in the ordinary, popular sense of the names they bear: the leg is the leg as far up as its insertion into the hip, the arm is the upper limb as it is visible under the clothing. There is no reason for adding paralysis of the face to paralysis of the arm. A hysteric who cannot talk has no motive for forgetting his comprehension of speech, since motor aphasia and word deafness are unrelated to each other in the popular mind, and so on. I can only associate myself fully with the views advanced by M. Janet in recent numbers of the Archives de Neurologie; they are confirmed as much by hysterical paralyses as by anaesthesia and psychical symptoms.

而事实上，癔症性瘫痪的相当多特征都证实了这一断言。癔症对神经的分布一无所知，正因如此，它并不模拟周围脊髓性瘫痪或投射性瘫痪。它对视交叉[optic chiasma]一无所知，因此也不产生偏盲。它按这些器官名称的普通、通俗意义来理解这些器官：腿就是腿，一直到它插入髋部之处为止；臂就是上肢，一如它在衣服之下可见的那样。没有任何理由把面部瘫痪加到臂部瘫痪之上。一个不能说话的癔症患者没有动机去忘记他对言语的理解，因为在常人的心里，运动性失语症与词聋[word deafness]彼此无关——如此等等。我只能完全赞同雅内先生[M. Janet]在最近几期《神经学档案》[Archives de Neurologie]上提出的那些看法；这些看法既由癔症性瘫痪所证实，也同样由感觉缺失与精神症状[psychical symptoms]所证实。

（IV）

I will attempt to indicate, finally, what the lesion that is the cause of hysterical paralyses might be like. I do not say that I will show what it is like; it is merely a question of indicating a line of thought that might lead to a conception which does not contradict the properties of hysterical paralysis insofar as it differs from organic cerebral paralysis.

最后，我将尝试指出，作为癔症性瘫痪之原因的那个病灶可能是怎样的。我并不说我要展示它究竟是怎样的；这仅仅是一个指示一条思路的问题，这条思路或许会导向一种构想，而该构想与癔症性瘫痪——就其区别于器质性大脑性瘫痪而言——的诸性质并不相矛盾。

I shall take the phrase ‘functional or dynamic lesion’ in its proper sense of ‘alteration in function or dynamics’ – alteration of a functional property. Examples of an alteration of this kind would be a diminution in excitability [l’excitabilité] or in a physiological quality which normally remains constant or varies within fixed limits.

我将把"功能性的或动力性的病灶"这一说法按其本有的意义理解为"功能或动力学的改变"[alteration in function or dynamics]——即某种功能属性的改变。这类改变的例子可以是兴奋性[l'excitabilité]的降低，或某种通常保持恒定、或只在固定限度内变化的生理素质[physiological quality]的降低。

But, it will be objected, functional alteration is not a different thing from organic alteration, it is merely another side of it. Let us suppose that nervous tissue is in a state of transitory anaemia; then its excitability will be diminished by this circumstance. It is impossible by this expedient to avoid taking organic lesions into account.

但人们会反驳说：功能性的改变与器质性的改变并非两种不同的东西，它只是后者的另一面。让我们设想神经组织处于一过性贫血的状态；那么，它的兴奋性便会因这一情形而降低。靠这种办法，是无法回避对器质性病灶的考虑的。

I will try to show that there can be a functional alteration without a concomitant organic lesion – or at least without one that is grossly palpable even by the most delicate analysis. In other terms, I will give a suitable example of an alteration of a primitive function; and for that purpose I only ask permission to move on to psychological ground – which can scarcely be avoided in dealing with hysteria.

我将试图表明：可以存在一种功能性的改变，而并不伴随器质性的病灶——或者至少不伴随一种即便以最精细的分析也无法察出的器质性病灶。换言之，我将给出一个关于某种初级功能[primitive function]之改变的恰当例子；为此，我只请求允许我转到心理学的领域[psychological ground]上来——而在处理癔症时，这一领域几乎是无法回避的。

I follow M. Janet in saying that what is in question in hysterical paralysis, just as in anaesthesia, etc., is the everyday, popular conception¹ of the organs and of the body in general. That conception is not founded on a deep knowledge of neuroanatomy but on our tactile and above all our visual perceptions. If it is what determines the characteristics of hysterical paralysis, the latter must naturally show itself ignorant and independent of any notion of the anatomy of the nervous system. The lesion in hysterical paralysis will therefore be an alteration of the conception, the idea, of the arm, for instance. But what kind of alteration must this be in order to produce paralysis?

我在此追随雅内先生[M. Janet]的主张：在癔症性瘫痪中——一如在感觉缺失等情形中——所涉及的乃是关于诸器官、以及一般而言关于身体的日常的、通俗的观念[conception]¹。这一观念并非奠基于对神经解剖学的深切知识，而是奠基于我们的触觉、尤其是我们的视觉。如果正是这一观念决定了癔症性瘫痪的诸特征，那么后者自然必定表现出对神经系统解剖之任何概念的懵然无知与独立无涉。因此，癔症性瘫痪中的病灶，将是对臂（举例来说）的观念、表象[the idea]的改变。但是，为了产生瘫痪，这必须是一种怎样的改变呢？

Considered psychologically, the paralysis of the arm consists in the fact that the conception of the arm cannot enter into association with the other ideas constituting the ego [moi] of which the subject’s body forms an important part. The lesion would therefore be the abolition of the associative accessibility of the conception of the arm. The arm behaves as though it did not exist for the play of associations. There is no doubt that, if the material conditions corresponding to the conception of the arm are profoundly altered, the conception will also be lost. But I have to show that it can be inaccessible without being destroyed and without its material substratum (the nervous tissue of the corresponding region of the cortex) being damaged.

从心理学上考虑，臂部瘫痪在于这样一个事实：臂的观念无法与构成自我[moi]的其他观念进入联想——而主体的身体在自我中构成一个重要部分。因此，病灶将是臂之观念在联想上的可及性[associative accessibility]的废止。臂的行为方式，宛如它并不存在于联想的运作[the play of associations]之中。毫无疑问，如果与臂之观念相对应的物质条件被深刻改变，这一观念也会丧失。但我必须表明：它可以变得不可及，却并未被摧毁，而其物质基底[material substratum]（皮层相应区域的神经组织）也并未受损。

I will begin with some examples drawn from social life. A comic story is told of a loyal subject who would not wash his hand because his sovereign had touched it. The relation of this hand to the idea of the king seemed so important to the man’s psychical life that he refused to let the hand enter into any other relation. We are obeying the same impulse when we break the glass in which we have drunk the health of a young married couple. Savage tribes in antiquity, who burnt their dead chief’s horse, his weapons and even his wives along with his dead body, were obeying this idea that no-one should ever touch them after him. The force of all these actions is clear. The quota of affect² which we attribute to the first association of an object has a repugnance to letting it enter into a new association with another object and consequently makes the idea of the [first] object inaccessible to association.

我将先从社会生活里的一些例子谈起。有一则滑稽的故事讲一个忠心的臣民，他不肯洗自己的手，因为那手曾被君主触碰过。在他看来，这只手与国王之观念的关系如此重要，以至于他拒绝让这只手进入任何其他关系。当我们砸碎那盏曾用来为新婚夫妇祝饮的酒杯时，我们所遵从的是同一种冲动。古代的野蛮部落把死去的酋长的马、他的武器、乃至他的妻子们与他的尸体一同焚烧，他们所遵从的也是这一观念：在他之后，任何人都不应再触碰这些东西。所有这些行为的力量是清楚的。我们归于某对象最初联想的那一份情感量[quota of affect]²，不愿让它与另一个对象结成新的联想，因而使那［最初的］对象的观念变得无法被联想。

It is not a mere comparison, it is almost the identical thing, when we move into the sphere of the psychology of conceptions. If the conception of the arm is involved in an association with a large quota of affect, it will be inaccessible to the free play of other associations. The arm will be paralysed in proportion to the persistence of this quota of affect or to its diminution by appropriate psychical means. This is the solution to the problem we have raised, for, in every case of hysterical paralysis, we find that the paralysed organ or the lost function is involved in a subconscious² association which is provided with a large quota of affect and it can be shown that the arm is liberated as soon as this quota is wiped out. Accordingly, the conception of the arm exists in the material substratum, but it is not accessible to conscious associations and impulses because the whole of its associative affinity, so to say, is saturated in a subconscious association with the memory of the event, the trauma, which produced the paralysis.

这并非仅仅是一种类比；当我们进入观念心理学[the psychology of conceptions]的领域时，它几乎是同一回事。如果臂之观念被卷入一种带有大量情感量[quota of affect]的联想之中，它便无法进入其他联想的自由运作。臂的瘫痪程度，将与此情感量的持续成正比，或与它经由适当的心理手段[appropriate psychical means]而被削减的程度成正比。这就是对我们所提出的那个问题的解答，因为在每一例癔症性瘫痪中，我们都发现：瘫痪的器官或丧失的功能，都被卷入一种下意识[subconscious]²的联想，而这种联想带有大量的情感量；并且可以表明，一旦这一情感量被抹去，臂便获得了解放。据此，臂之观念存在于物质基底[material substratum]之中，但它无法进入意识的联想与冲动，因为可以说，它的全部联想亲和力[associative affinity]都已饱和地投入一种下意识的联想——即与那个产生了瘫痪的事件、即创伤[the trauma]的记忆的联想。

M. Charcot was the first to teach us that to explain the hysterical neurosis we must apply to psychology. Breuer and I have followed his example in a preliminary communication (1893a) 'On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena'. We show in that paper that the permanent symptoms of hysteria which are described as 'non-traumatic' are explained (apart from the stigmata⁴) by the same mechanism which Charcot recognized in traumatic paralyses. But we also give the reason for the persistence of these symptoms and show why they can be cured by a special procedure of hypnotic psychotherapy. Every event, every psychical impression is provided with a certain quota of affect (Affektbetrag) of which the ego divests itself either by means of a motor reaction or by associative psychical activity. If the subject is unable or unwilling to get rid of this surplus, the memory of the impression attains the importance of a trauma and becomes the cause of permanent hysterical symptoms. The impossibility of elimination becomes evident when the impression remains in the subconscious. We have called this theory 'Das Abreagieren der Reizzuwächse'.

沙可先生[M. Charcot]是第一位教导我们：要解释癔症性神经症[hysterical neurosis]，我们必须求助于心理学[psychology]。布洛伊尔[Breuer]与我已追随他的范例，在一篇初步通讯[preliminary communication]（1893a）《论癔症现象的心理机制》['On the Psychical Mechanism of Hysterical Phenomena']中。我们在该文中表明：癔症中那些被描述为"非创伤性"[non-traumatic]的持久症状，可由沙可所认识的、在创伤性瘫痪[traumatic paralyses]中同样的机制来解释（癔症性特征[stigmata]⁴除外）。但我们也同时给出了这些症状何以持久的原因，并表明它们何以能被一种特殊的催眠性心理治疗[hypnotic psychotherapy]程序所治愈。每一事件、每一心理印象[psychical impression]都被赋予一定量的情感量[quota of affect]（Affektbetrag，德文，意为"情感量"），自我[ego]以两种方式之一摆脱它：要么经由一种运动反应[motor reaction]，要么经由联想性的心理活动[associative psychical activity]。如果主体未能或不愿摆脱这一剩余量，那么该印象的记忆便获得了创伤[trauma]般的重要性，并成为持久性癔症症状[permanent hysterical symptoms]的原因。当该印象滞留于下意识[subconscious]之中时，这种无法消除[elimination]的情形便显露出来。我们把这一理论称为"Das Abreagieren der Reizzuwächse"（刺激的宣泄[abreaction of stimulus increments]）。

To sum up, I think that it is in complete agreement with our general view of hysteria, as we have been able to shape it under M. Charcot’s instruction, to suppose that the lesion in hysterical paralyses consists in nothing other than the inaccessibility of the organ or function concerned to the associations of the conscious ego; that this purely functional alteration (even the conception remaining unimpaired) is caused by the fixation² of this conception in a subconscious association with the memory of the trauma; and that this conception does not become liberated and accessible so long as the quota of affect of the psychical trauma has not been eliminated by an adequate motor reaction or by conscious psychical activity. But even if this mechanism does not occur, if a direct autosuggestive idea is always necessary for hysterical paralysis, as it is in M. Charcot’s traumatic cases, we have succeeded in showing what the nature of the lesion, or rather of the alteration, in hysterical paralysis would have to be in order to explain the differences between it and organic cerebral paralysis.

总而言之，我认为，假定癔症性瘫痪中的病灶不外乎在于：所涉器官或功能对于意识自我[conscious ego]的联想而言的不可及性；假定这种纯粹功能性的改变[purely functional alteration]（即便观念[conception]本身未受损伤）是由这一观念在一种下意识联想[subconscious association]中与创伤记忆[memory of the trauma]的固着[fixation]²所致；并假定这一观念只要其心理创伤[psychical trauma]的情感量[quota of affect]尚未经由一种适当的运动反应[motor reaction]或意识性心理活动[conscious psychical activity]而被消除，便不会获得解放与可及——这样的假定，与我们关于癔症的一般看法[general view of hysteria]（我们得以在沙可先生[M. Charcot]的教导下形成它）是完全吻合的。但是，即便这一机制并不出现，即便一种直接的自我暗示性观念[autosuggestive idea]对于癔症性瘫痪总是必需的（正如在沙可先生的创伤性病例中那样），我们也已经成功地表明：为了解释癔症性瘫痪与器质性大脑性瘫痪[organic cerebral paralysis]之间的差异，癔症性瘫痪中的病灶——或更确切地说，那种改变——其本性必须是什么样子的。
