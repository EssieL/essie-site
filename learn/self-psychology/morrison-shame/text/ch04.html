Freud's passing attention to shame was alluded to in chapter 1. Here I will review his writings on shame in greater detail, relying especially on shame's relationship to scopophilic and exhibitionistic drives. I then will attempt to relate Freud's thoughts on narcissism, self-regard, and the ego ideal to the perspective on shame elaborated in this volume.

第1章已提及弗洛伊德[Freud]对羞耻[shame]的短暂关注。在此，我将更详尽地回顾他关于羞耻的著述，尤其倚重羞耻与窥视[scopophilic]及暴露[exhibitionistic]驱力[drives]之间的关系。随后，我将尝试把弗洛伊德关于自恋[narcissism]、自重[self-regard]与自我理想[ego ideal]的思考，与本书所阐发的羞耻视角联系起来。

Because of the central place of the ego ideal in my conception of shame, the work of various authors on this subject will then be reviewed in some detail, particularly from the perspective of psychoanalytic ego psychology.

由于自我理想在我对羞耻的概念中居于核心位置，接下来我将较为详细地回顾各位作者在这一主题上的工作，尤其从精神分析自我心理学[psychoanalytic ego psychology]的视角出发。

While most of these authors did not emphasize the relationship of shame to the ego ideal, I will underscore the relevance of this relationship. In the next two chapters, I will turn to a consideration of the work of two authors (A. Reich, 1953, 1960; Jacobson, 1964) who did deal with shame's place in the framework of primitive narcissism, early object relations, and the ego ideal. I will then consider other contributions on shame from the perspective of object relations and narcissism.

尽管这些作者大多并未强调羞耻与自我理想之间的关系，我仍要凸显这种关系的关联性。在接下来的两章中，我将转而考察两位作者（A. Reich, 1953, 1960；Jacobson, 1964）的著作，他们确实处理了羞耻在原始自恋[primitive narcissism]、早期客体关系[early object relations]与自我理想框架中的位置。之后，我将从客体关系[object relations]与自恋的视角，考量其他关于羞耻的贡献。

FREUD'S VIEWS ON SHAME（弗洛伊德论羞耻）

In 1895, Freud (1892–1899) first wrote about shame in Draft K of a letter to Fliess, which outlined his thoughts about the neuroses of defense and was written at the same time as his famous "Project for a Scientific Psychology" (Freud, 1895). In his letter to Fleiss, shame, along with morality and disgust, was proposed as a cause of repression of sexual experiences (p. 222) and thus was seen to function as a defense.

1895年，弗洛伊德（1892–1899）在写给弗利斯[Fliess]的信之 K 草稿中首次写到了羞耻；该草稿勾勒了他对防御性神经症[neuroses of defense]的思考，与他著名的《科学心理学大纲》[Project for a Scientific Psychology]（Freud, 1895）写于同一时期。在给弗利斯的信中，羞耻与道德[morality]和厌恶[disgust]一道，被提议为性经验[sexual experiences]被压抑[repression]的原因（p. 222），因而被视为发挥着防御[defense]的功能。

Interestingly, in this passage Freud equated the absence of shame with masculinity. Elsewhere in the letter, in discussing puberty, Freud suggested that girls, by contrast, are "seized by a non-neurotic sexual repugnance," which may account for "the flood of shame which overwhelms the female at that period..." (p. 270). In "Further Remarks on the Neuro-Psychoses of Defense," Freud (1896) linked shame with conscientiousness and self-distrust as primary symptoms of defense (p. 169), explicitly with regard to obsessional symptoms (p. 178).

有趣的是，在这段文字中弗洛伊德将"缺乏羞耻"等同于男性气质[masculinity]。在信的另一处，论及青春期[puberty]时，弗洛伊德提出，女孩则相反，会被"一种非神经症的性厌恶[sexual repugnance]"所攫住，这或许可以解释"在那个时期淹没女性的羞耻洪流……"（p. 270）。在《防御性神经心理症的补遗》[Further Remarks on the Neuro-Psychoses of Defense]中，弗洛伊德（1896）将羞耻与良心[conscientiousness]和自我不信任[self-distrust]联系起来，作为防御的首要症状（p. 169），且明确针对强迫性[obsessional]症状（p. 178）。

In discussing the return of the repressed, Freud (1892–1899) wrote in Draft K of "self-reproach" being transformed into other affects, which, in addition to anxiety, hypochondria, and delusions of persecution, included shame (p. 224). In letter 66 to Fliess, Freud later mentioned "an interesting dream of wandering about among strangers, totally or half undressed and with feelings of shame and anxiety" (p. 258).

在讨论被压抑者之返回[return of the repressed]时，弗洛伊德（1892–1899）在 K 草稿中写到"自我谴责[self-reproach]"会转化为其他情感[affects]，除焦虑[anxiety]、疑病[hypochondria]和迫害妄想[delusions of persecution]之外，还包括羞耻（p. 224）。在给弗利斯的第 66 封信中，弗洛伊德后来提到"一个有趣的梦：在陌生人中间游荡，全身或半裸，伴随着羞耻与焦虑的感受"（p. 258）。

Again, in "The Interpretation of Dreams," Freud (1900) described several embarrassing dreams of being naked, and "a distressing feeling in the nature of shame and in the fact that one wishes to hide one's nakedness, as a rule by locomotion, but finds one is unable to do so" (p. 242). Elsewhere, Freud (1898, p. 275) had also equated shame in a young boy with a history of bed-wetting, and the threat by the boy's mother to tell friends and teachers.

同样地，在《梦的解析》[The Interpretation of Dreams]中，弗洛伊德（1900）描述了几个关于裸体[being naked]的尴尬[embarrassing]梦，以及"一种羞耻性质的苦恼[distressing]感受，并伴随着想要隐藏自己裸体——通常藉由移动[locomotion]，却发现无法做到——的念头"（p. 242）。在别处，弗洛伊德（1898, p. 275）也曾将一个小男孩的羞耻等同于其遗尿[bed-wetting]史，以及男孩母亲威胁要把这件事告诉朋友和老师的经历。

Thus, Freud (1892–1899) unequivocally placed shame as an affect within a social context, equating it with "fear of other people knowing about it (the self-reproach]." Similarly, Freud wrote, "I reproach myself on account of an event - I am afraid other people know about it -therefore I feel ashamed in front of other people" (p. 225).

因此，弗洛伊德（1892–1899）毫不含糊地将羞耻作为一种情感[affect]置于社会情境[social context]之中，将其等同于"害怕别人知道此事（自我谴责]"[fear of other people knowing about it (the self-reproach)]。同样地，弗洛伊德写道："我因某件事而谴责自己——我害怕别人知道这件事——因此我在别人面前感到羞耻"（p. 225）。

One of these "events" was the emergence of sexual feelings in young girls, elaborated in Miller's (1985) view of embarrassment noted in the previous chapter.

这些"事件"之一，便是青春期少女中性感受[sexual feelings]的涌现，这一点在上一章提及的米勒[Miller]（1985）关于尴尬[embarrassment]的观点中已有阐发。

From Freud's first mention of shame, then, we note a lack of clarity as he struggled to define the dynamics of defense (and repression, which he equated with defense at that time). Shame was seen (1) as a social (interpersonal) affect, linked with being observed, being found out, by another; (2) as a defense against the memory of a source of unpleasure (that is, prepubertal sexual experience or, later, fantasy); and (3) as a symptom analogous to self-reproach for having been discovered in the early commission of a sexual act. This ambiguity (of shame as affect, defense, or symptom) continued throughout Freud's work and in the contributions of authors who followed. We, too, will continue to wrestle with this ambiguity as our study progresses - for example, can one affect serve at the same time as a defense against other, more noxious affects, and, if so, how does this valence get established? Various authors differ in the emphasis they place on these three qualities of shame; I will attempt to underscore the relevant shifts in emphasis as we proceed.

从弗洛伊德首次提及羞耻起，我们便注意到一种清晰性的缺失——他在努力界定防御（以及压抑，彼时他将压抑等同于防御）的动力机制[dynamics]时举步维艰。羞耻被视为：（1）一种社会的（人际的）情感，与"被另一个人注视、被发现"相关联；（2）一种针对不快[unpleasure]之源（即青春期前的性经验，或更晚些的幻想[fantasy]）之记忆的防御；（3）一种类似于自我谴责的症状——因早年实施性行为而被发现。这种含混性（羞耻究竟是情感、防御还是症状）贯穿了弗洛伊德的整个工作，也存在于其后继者的贡献之中。随着研究的推进，我们也将继续与这种含混性角力——例如，一种情感能否同时充当针对其他更具毒性[noxious]之情感的防御？若能，这种效价[valence]又是如何确立的？不同作者在对羞耻这三种特质[qualities]的强调上各有侧重；在接下来的论述中，我将努力凸显这些侧重点的相关转变。

In "Three Essays on Sexuality," Freud (1905) moved from shame as an affective response to being seen, to shame as a defense against the drives. He described two important sexual drives - exhibitionism, and voyeurism (curiosity, scopophilia) - each of which is visual (seeing and, being seen and is socially embedded in the viewing other. Thus, passive wishes to be seen (exhibitionism) evolve from intense wishes to look (voyeurism) (Wurmser, 1981; p. 147). Freud (1905) introduced shame as a "resistance" against these drives, which "impede the course of the sexual instinct and, like dams, restrict its flow - disgust, feelings of shame, and the claims of aesthetic and moral ideals" (p. 177). Here, shame as affect is linked with another emotion, disgust, and with the more cognitive "ideal" as a defense against drives. These counterforces against the exhibitionistic and scopophilic drives were designated by Freud (1908) as "reaction formations." Nunberg (1955) followed Freud's designation by stating, "Shame is a reaction formation of the ego to the wish to exhibit" (p. 157).

在《性学三论》[Three Essays on Sexuality]中，弗洛伊德（1905）从"羞耻作为被看见的情感性反应"转向"羞耻作为针对驱力[drives]的防御"。他描述了两种重要的性驱力——暴露欲[exhibitionism]与窥视欲[voyeurism]（好奇[curiosity]、窥视[scopophilia]）——二者都是视觉性的（看见与被看见），并社会性[socially]地嵌入于那个观看的他者[other]之中。因此，被动的"被看见"的愿望（暴露欲）由强烈的"观看"愿望（窥视欲）演化而来（Wurmser, 1981; p. 147）。弗洛伊德（1905）将羞耻引入为针对这些驱力的"阻抗[resistance]"，它"阻碍性本能[sexual instinct]的进程，并如同堤坝般限制其流动——厌恶、羞耻感，以及审美与道德理想[ideals]的要求"（p. 177）。在此，作为情感的羞耻与另一种情绪——厌恶——相连，也与更具认知性的"理想"相连，共同作为针对驱力的防御。弗洛伊德（1908）将这些针对暴露与窥视驱力的反作用力[counterforces]命名为"反应形成[reaction formations]"。努伯格[Nunberg]（1955）沿用弗洛伊德的命名，写道："羞耻是自我[ego]针对暴露愿望的一种反应形成"（p. 157）。

Thus, in his 1905 work Freud's emphasis regarding shame shifted significantly from affective experience to defense. When exhibitionism and voyeurism are restricted to their sexual aims, overcome the defenses erected against them (including shame), and supplant genitality, they become perversions rather than normal components of sexuality (Wurmser, 1981, p. 147). From our perspective on shame, it is particularly interesting to note that Freud (1905) linked "the claims of aesthetic and moral ideals" with disgust and shame as the defenses against these visual drives. I believe that this is an early statement about the function of the ego ideal, which was not to be introduced by Freud until his 1914 paper "On Narcissism" and which plays an important part in my view of shame. This relationship of the ego ideal to the experience of shame is consistent with the ego ideal's role, as part of the superego, in controlling the drives. I will consider later various aspects of the ego ideal and its function in shame

因此，在 1905 年的著作中，弗洛伊德对羞耻的强调显著地从情感性经验[affective experience]转向了防御。当暴露欲与窥视欲被限定于其性目标、克服了针对它们的防御（包括羞耻）、并取代生殖性[genitality]时，它们便成了倒错[perversions]，而非性[sexuality]的正常成分（Wurmser, 1981, p. 147）。从我们的羞耻视角来看，尤为有趣的是：弗洛伊德（1905）将"审美与道德理想的要求"与厌恶和羞耻相连，作为针对这些视觉性驱力的防御。我认为这是关于自我理想功能的一个早期表述，而自我理想直到弗洛伊德 1914 年的《论自恋》[On Narcissism]一文才被提出，并在我的羞耻观中占据重要位置。自我理想与羞耻体验之间的这种关系，与自我理想作为超我[superego]的一部分在控制驱力中所扮演的角色是一致的。稍后我将考量自我理想的诸方面及其在羞耻中的功能。

So far, then, Freud's view of shame had evolved into its role as a defense, a reaction formation against the visual/sexual drives. Earlier, he had described it as an affective experience connected to femininity, passivity, and exposure. Shame had been embedded in a social context, relating to others' knowing (as in bed-wetting) or seeing (as in nudity) a personal defect. Such defects apparently included sexuality and sexual desire. In relation to looking and being looked at, Freud considered shame as both affective experience and defense. Freud conceived of shame as the danger from self-exposure to others and also as a sense of inadequacy (Thrane, 1979, p. 331). Freud (1930) wrote, "...man's raising himself from the ground, of his assumption of an upright gait, this made his genitals, which were previously concealed, visible and in need of protection, and so provoked feelings of shame in him" (p. 99n).

至此，弗洛伊德对羞耻的看法已演化为将其视为一种防御——针对视觉/性驱力的反应形成。早前，他曾将羞耻描述为一种与女性气质[femininity]、被动性[passivity]和暴露[exposure]相连的情感性经验。羞耻一直被嵌入社会情境之中，关乎他人"知晓"（如遗尿）或"看见"（如裸体）个人的缺陷[defect]。这类缺陷显然包括了性与性欲[sexual desire]。就"看"与"被看"而言，弗洛伊德将羞耻同时视为情感性经验与防御。弗洛伊德把羞耻构想为来自向他人自我暴露[self-exposure]的危险，也是一种不够格[inadequacy]之感（Thrane, 1979, p. 331）。弗洛伊德（1930）写道："……人从地面直立起来、采取直立步态，这使得此前被遮蔽的生殖器变得可见且需要保护，从而在他身上引发了羞耻感"（p. 99n）。

This statement reflected a reaction to danger and fear, although Freud did not explicitly spell out why the visible genitals provoke shame and thus exemplified shame as an affective response to another affect (that is, fear).¹ Here, one is ashamed of feeling afraid.

这一表述反映出对危险与恐惧[fear]的反应，尽管弗洛伊德并未明确说明为何可见的生殖器会引发羞耻，从而将羞耻例证为针对另一种情感（即恐惧）的情感性反应¹。在此，一个人是因感到害怕而羞耻。

Freud (1933) also noted that shame can reflect, and defend against, feelings of inadequacy. As quoted in the previous chapter, he stated, "Shame, which is considered to be a feminine characteristic par excellence but is far more a matter of convention than might be supposed, has as its purpose, we believe, concealment of genital deficiency. We are not forgetting that at a later time shame takes on other functions" (p. 132).

弗洛伊德（1933）也注意到，羞耻既能反映、也能防御不够格的感受。如上一章所引，他陈述道："羞耻被视为一种卓越的女性特征[feminine characteristic]，但比起人们的设想，它更多是一种习俗[convention]之事；我们相信，它的目的在于隐藏生殖器的缺陷[genital deficiency]。我们并未忘记，在更晚的时期羞耻会承担其他功能"（p. 132）。

Here Freud noted the relationship between shame and concealment, and genital deficiency, thus highlighting shame's central place in the framework of a social context (that is, being seen), of hiding, and of inferiority feelings, and its inevitable relationship to narcissism and a sense of self-defect. Shame's generation of a wish to hide presumably is aimed at defending the self from rejection, contempt, or loss of love.

在此，弗洛伊德指出了羞耻与隐藏[concealment]、与生殖器缺陷之间的关系，从而凸显了羞耻在社会情境（即被看见）、隐藏、以及自卑感[inferiority feelings]这一框架中的核心位置，也凸显了它与自恋及自体缺陷[self-defect]感之间不可避免的联系。羞耻所生成的隐藏愿望，其目的大概在于防御自体免受拒绝[rejection]、轻蔑[contempt]或失爱[loss of love]。

Freud's (1914) paper, "On Narcissism," is meaningful here for his views on the ego ideal and self-regard and their relationship with the voyeuristic/exhibitionistic drives. Evolving from his consideration of the instincts, the essay first discussed an agency of mind that was to be the anlage of "conscience," whose function was to watch the ego (or self) - again, the visual, and hold it to criteria of behavioral and cognitive control over the drives. This agency, the ego ideal, was to be the forerunner of the superego in the evolution of Freud's structural model.

弗洛伊德（1914）的《论自恋》[On Narcissism]一文在此具有重要意义，因为它阐述了关于自我理想与自重，以及它们与窥视/暴露驱力之间关系的观点。从他对本能[instincts]的思考演化而来，该文首先讨论了一种心智的机能[agency]，它将是"良心[conscience]"的始基[anlage]，其功能是监视[watch]自我[ego]（或自体[self]）——再次是视觉性的——并使其符合对驱力之行为与认知控制的标准。这一机能，即自我理想，在弗洛伊德结构模型[structural model]的演化中将是超我的先驱[forerunner]。

However, in working toward introduction of this concept, Freud noted the relationship of libidinal instincts to culture and morality in the quest to maintain self-esteem.

然而，在逐步引入这一概念的过程中，弗洛伊德注意到力比多本能[libidinal instincts]与文化及道德[morality]之间的关系，关乎维持自尊[self-esteem]的追求。

Repression, then, is a result of conflict between the libidinal instincts and cultural and ethical values and reflects the formation of an internalized "ideal" based on these values. It represents the internalization of culture - external precepts- and formation of ethics - internal precepts. In this way, Freud continued to vacillate between external and internal factors, considering shame, along with cultural and ethical ideals, as a source of defense against the drives.

因此，压抑是力比多本能与文化及伦理[ethical]价值之间冲突的结果，也反映出基于这些价值而内化的"理想[ideal]"之形成。它代表着文化（外部戒律[precepts]）的内化，以及伦理（内部戒律）的形成。由此，弗洛伊德继续在外部与内部因素之间摇摆[vacillate]，将羞耻连同文化与伦理理想一起，视为针对驱力的防御之源。

"On Narcissism" and The Ego Ideal（《论自恋》与自我理想）

With these few confounding thoughts, Freud's explicit attention to shame came to an end. Why, then, spend time on the "Narcissism" paper, which did not deal specifically with shame?

伴随着这几分令人困惑[confounding]的思考，弗洛伊德对羞耻的明确关注告一段落。那么，为何要花时间讨论这篇并未专门处理羞耻的《自恋》论文？

脚注（Notes）

1. Because shame is about the self. I believe that an understanding of narcissism in its various forms is essential to an appreciation of the development and manifestations of shame. I have already stated my conviction that shame forms the affective underpinning of narcissistic vulnerability. In "On Narcissism," Freud sketched the outlines of narcissism against which all subsequent considerations of narcissistic manifestations and vulnerability would be measured.

1. 因为羞耻关乎自体[self]。我相信，理解各种形态的自恋，对于把握羞耻的发展与表现[manifestations]至关重要。我已表明我的信念：羞耻构成了自恋脆弱性[narcissistic vulnerability]的情感性基础[underpinning]。在《论自恋》中，弗洛伊德勾勒了自恋的轮廓，此后所有关于自恋表现与脆弱性的考量都将以此为准绳来度量。

2. Because shame represents failure and defect in relation to culture and ethics and their created and fantasied ideals of behavior, structure, and self-regard, it is important to understand the evolution of thinking about ideals and the psychic structure that "houses" them. In "On Narcissism" Freud first elaborated the ego ideal, which is that structure. Other issues relating to the ego ideal and shame will be considered later in our discussion of other authors.

2. 因为羞耻代表着相对于文化与伦理、及其所创造与幻想的行为、结构、自重之理想而言的失败与缺陷，理解关于理想的思考之演化、以及"容纳[houses]"这些理想的心理结构[psychic structure]便十分重要。在《论自恋》中，弗洛伊德首次详述了自我理想，那便是这一结构。其他有关自我理想与羞耻的问题，将在我们讨论其他作者时一并考量。

3. In his movement from narcissism to structural theory, Freud introduced guilt where shame would more appropriately have been placed (for example, with respect to inferiority feelings). This same tendency occurred in later writings as well, and, since Freud's avoidance of the subject of shame influenced subsequent inattention to this feeling, it is relevant here to document his ambiguity regarding shame and guilt.

3. 在他从自恋转向结构理论[structural theory]的过程中，弗洛伊德在更应安置羞耻之处引入了内疚[guilt]（例如，就自卑感而言）。这一相同倾向也出现在后期的著述中；由于弗洛伊德对羞耻主题的回避[avoidance]影响了后世对此感受的忽视[inattention]，在此记录他在羞耻与内疚之间的含混便有其意义。

In "On Narcissism," Freud suggested that the "ideal ego" is invested with narcissism lost from the sense of original perfection emanating from the infantile ego and determines the subjective sense of self-respect (i.e., self-regard, and self-esteem). Here Freud again implied both external and internal sources of this ego ideal as represented by "admonition of others" and by "his own critical judgment." He then proceeded to his well-known formulation of the ego ideal and its relationship to what was to become the superego: "It would not surprise us if we were to find a special psychical agency which performs the task of seeing that narcissistic satisfaction from the ego ideal is ensured and which, with this end in view, constantly watches the actual ego and measures it by that ideal" (Freud, 1914, p. 95).

在《论自恋》中，弗洛伊德提出，"理想自我[ideal ego]"被投注了从婴儿期自我[infantile ego]所生发之原始完美[original perfection]感中失落的自恋，并决定着主观的自尊[self-respect]感（即自重与自尊）。在此，弗洛伊德再次暗示了自我理想的两种来源——外部的与内部的——分别体现为"他人的训诫[admonition of others]"与"他自己的批判性判断[critical judgment]"。随后，他提出了其著名的关于自我理想、及其与日后之超我关系的表述："倘若我们发现有一种特殊的心理机能[psychical agency]，其任务是确保来自自我理想的满足；并且出于这一目的，持续地监视实际的自我，并以其理想来度量它，我们并不会感到惊讶"（Freud, 1914, p. 95）。

Freud then explicitly related the ego ideal to self-regard and to its dependence on narcissistic libido. The inability to love, to invest in an object, lowers self-regard (i.e., self-esteem) and leads to feelings of inferiority. Also, at this point in his essay Freud discussed Adler's concept of organ inferiority and resultant overcompensation.

接着，弗洛伊德明确地将自我理想与自重、及其对自恋力比多[narcissistic libido]的依赖联系起来。无法去爱、无法投注[invest]于一个客体[object]，会降低自重（即自尊）并导致自卑感。同样，在文章的此一处，弗洛伊德讨论了阿德勒[Adler]的器官自卑[organ inferiority]概念及其所导致的过度补偿[overcompensation]。

He went on to dispute Adler's conclusions, which, as I suggested in the previous chapter, may well have played a part in his neglecting to elaborate further on the ego ideal, narcissism, and a significant study of shame.

他进而反驳了阿德勒的结论；正如我在上一章所暗示的，这很可能在一定程度上导致他疏于进一步阐发自我理想、自恋，以及对羞耻的深入研究。

Freud ended his essay with a discussion of object love as a means of rediscovering lost narcissism through narcissistic idealization of, and investment in, the libidinal object. He spoke of the liberation of homosexual libido, which is transformed into a sense of guilt out of a fear of the loss of love of the parents, and, later, of the social group (Freud, 1914, pp. 101–102).⁴ In "On Narcissism," Freud elucidated the ego ideal, inferiority feelings, and loss of self-esteem, thus paving the way to an appreciation of shame.

弗洛伊德以对客体爱[object love]的讨论结束其文章，将其视为藉由对力比多客体[libidinal object]的自恋性理想化[narcissistic idealization]与投注而重新发现失落自恋的途径。他谈到了同性恋力比多[homosexual libido]的解放，它出于对失去父母之爱、以及后来失去社会群体[social group]之爱的恐惧，而转化为内疚感（Freud, 1914, pp. 101–102）⁴。在《论自恋》中，弗洛伊德阐明了自我理想、自卑感与自尊的丧失，从而为理解羞耻铺平了道路。

From Ego Ideal to Ego（从自我理想到自我）

Freud's subsequent writing on the ego ideal, before it "disappeared" into the superego, are of interest in relation to shame. In "Group Psychology and the Analysis of the Ego," Freud (1921) summarized what he had said previously about the ego ideal, indicating functions including "... self-observation, the moral conscience, the censorship of dreams, and the chief influence in repression" (p. 110). The state of being in love may lead to idealization of the love object, through which unattained qualities and perfection of the ego ideal are realized through "overvaluation of the object" in an attempt to regain the lost narcissism (pp. 112–113). Such overvaluation may lead to a substitution of the object for the ego ideal; in this essay, Freud introduced the concepts of identification with, and internalization of, the object. However, in describing this state, he observed that "... with this 'devotion' of the ego to the object, which is no longer to be distinguished from a sublimated devotion to an abstract idea, the functions allotted to the ego ideal entirely cease to operate" (p. 113, emphasis added).

弗洛伊德在自我理想"消失"于超我之前关于自我理想的后续著述，对理解羞耻颇有兴味。在《群体心理学与自我的分析》[Group Psychology and the Analysis of the Ego]中，弗洛伊德（1921）总结了此前关于自我理想的论述，指出其功能包括"……自我观察[self-observation]、道德良心[moral conscience]、梦的审查[censorship of dreams]，以及压抑中的主要影响力"（p. 110）。恋爱[being in love]状态可能导致对爱之客体[love object]的理想化，藉此，自我理想中未达成的特质与完美通过"对客体的过高评价[overvaluation of the object]"而实现，以图重获失落的自恋（pp. 112–113）。这种过高评价可能导致以客体替代自我理想；在此文中，弗洛伊德引入了认同[identification with]客体与内化[internalization of]客体的概念。然而，在描述这一状态时，他观察到："……随着自我对客体的这种'献身[devotion]'，它已不再能与对某个抽象观念的升华性献身相区分，分配给自我理想的功能便完全停止运作"（p. 113，着重号为原文所加）。

With this statement, Freud acknowledged that "an abstract idea" (that is, an ethical ideal ), as well as an object, may replace the ego ideal.

通过这一陈述，弗洛伊德承认，"一个抽象观念"（即一种伦理理想[ethical ideal]），与客体一样，都可能取代自我理想。

This suggestion allows for the concept of "falling short of ideals" as a source of inferiority feelings and shame, which I will discuss subsequently. For Freud, the shared replacement by a given object for the ego ideals of a collective of individuals defined a primary group (p. 116).

这一提法为"达不到理想[falling short of ideals]"这一概念开辟了空间，视其为自卑感与羞耻的一个来源，我将在后文讨论这一点。对弗洛伊德而言，某个给定客体对一群个体之自我理想的共同替代，界定了一个原始群体[primary group]（p. 116）。

He suggested that euphoria of the ego may result from blotting out the restrictions of the ego ideal or from successfully living up to its demands. But it seems to me, Freud again confounded guilt with shame: "And the sense of guilt (as well as the sense of inferiority) can also be understood as an expression of tension between the ego and ego ideal" (p. 131).

他提出，自我的欣快[euphoria]可能源于抹去[blotting out]自我理想的约束，或源于成功地达到[living up to]其要求。但在我看来，弗洛伊德再次将内疚与羞耻混为一谈[confounded]："内疚感（以及自卑感）也可被理解为自我与自我理想之间张力的一种表达"（p. 131）。

As Freud later explored the functions of the superego, the role of guilt was to be clarified. However, as Piers (1953) has argued, that tension between ego and ego ideal is more accurately seen as the experience of shame. This is probably the equivalent of the "sense of inferiority" noted by Freud. A final point about Freud's 1921 essay plays a part in my viewpoint on shame, namely, the relationship of the ego ideal and shame to manic-depressive illness (see chapter 11). Freud (1917) had indicated that the ego ideal was involved as a cause of melancholia, through the ego's identification with, and its internalization of, the rejected object. This leads to self-reproaches reflecting condemnation of the internalized object by the ego ideal. In his 1921 work, however, he spoke of mania as a reflection of "... the ego and the ego ideal having been] fused together, so that the person, in a mood of triumph and self-satisfaction, disturbed by no self-criticism, can enjoy the abolition of his inhibitions, his feelings of consideration for others, and his self-reproaches" (p. 132, emphasis added).

随着弗洛伊德后来探究超我的功能，内疚的角色得以澄清。然而，正如皮尔斯[Piers]（1953）所论，自我与自我理想之间的张力更确切地应被视为羞耻的体验。这大概等同于弗洛伊德所提及的"自卑感"。关于弗洛伊德 1921 年文章的最后一点，在我的羞耻观中占有一席之地，即自我理想与羞耻同躁郁性疾患[manic-depressive illness]的关系（见第11章）。弗洛伊德（1917）曾指出，自我理想作为忧郁症[melancholia]的一个成因而卷入其中，经由自我对遭拒客体[rejected object]的认同与内化。这导致了自我谴责[self-reproaches]，反映出自我理想对内在化客体的谴责[condemnation]。然而在其 1921 年的著作中，他谈到躁狂[mania]是"……自我与自我理想已]融合[fused]在一起"的反映，于是"此人处于胜利与自我满足的心境中，不受自我批评[self-criticism]干扰，得以享受其抑制[inhibitions]、其对他人的体恤[consideration for others]之感、及其自我谴责的消解[abolition]"（p. 132，着重号为原文所加）。

Are not self-criticism and self-reproaches to be viewed as equivalents of shame in relation to manic-depressive illness? Unaware of Freud's statement, I have made this point elsewhere (Morrison, 1983): "I believe that shame is a central affect for the manic-depressive, which the manic flight attempts to hide through fantasied merger with the ideal self" (p. 306n).

难道自我批评与自我谴责，不应被视为羞耻在躁郁性疾患中的等价物吗？在未察觉弗洛伊德此一陈述的情况下，我曾于别处提出过这一点（Morrison, 1983）："我相信，羞耻是躁郁者[manic-depressive]的一种核心情感，而躁狂性逃避[manic flight]试图藉由与理想自我的幻想性融合[merger]来隐藏它"（p. 306n）。

With regard to melancholia, Freud (1921) wrote, "It is not so obvious, but nevertheless very probable, that the misery of the melancholic is the expression of a sharp conflict between the two agencies of his ego, a conflict in which the ideal, in an excess of sensitiveness, relentlessly exhibits its condemnation of the ego in delusions of inferiority and in self-depreciation" (p. 132, emphasis added). This quote suggests a relationship between shame and depression, in which the latter may also result from failures to live up to the demands of the ego ideal. I will expand upon this point in chapters 5 and 7 in relating the work of Kohut to that of Bibring (see also Morrison, 1983, p. 312).

关于忧郁症，弗洛伊德（1921）写道："这并不那么显而易见，却极有可能：忧郁者的痛苦是其自我的两种机能之间尖锐冲突的表达；在这场冲突中，理想以其过度的敏感[sensitiveness]，在自卑妄想[delusions of inferiority]与自我贬低[self-depreciation]中，毫不留情地展露它对自我的谴责"（p. 132，着重号为原文所加）。这一引文暗示了羞耻与抑郁[depression]之间的关系——后者同样可能源于未能达到自我理想的要求。我将在第5章与第7章中，联系科胡特[Kohut]与比布林[Bibring]的工作，对此点加以展开（另见 Morrison, 1983, p. 312）。

In "The Ego and the Id," Freud (1923) introduced the concept of the superego by equating it with the ego ideal and again reiterated guilt (rather than shame, which seems more appropriate) as the result of tension between the ego and ego ideal, with guilt assumed once more to relate to feelings of inferiority (p. 51). However, as this essay proceeded, with delineation of the superego and the relationship of guilt to "repressed impulses" and to the Oedipus complex, Freud's interest in the ego ideal seemed to fade away. In subsequent writings (for example, 1930) he related guilt to the more familiar aggressive and sexual impulses and the response of the superego, with less mention of "inferiority feelings." Attention to shame and related phenomena was pushed to the periphery of Freud's interest.

在《自我与本我》[The Ego and the Id]中，弗洛伊德（1923）通过将超我等同于自我理想而引入了超我的概念，并再次重申内疚（而非似乎更贴切的羞耻）是自我与自我理想之间张力的结果，且再次假定内疚与自卑感相关（p. 51）。然而，随着文章的推进，随着超我被勾勒出来、以及内疚与"被压抑的冲动[repressed impulses]"和俄狄浦斯情结[Oedipus complex]之关系的确立，弗洛伊德对自我理想的兴趣似乎消退了。在后续的著述中（例如 1930 年），他将内疚联系于更为人熟知的攻击性与性冲动，以及超我的反应，较少提及"自卑感"。对羞耻及相关现象的关注，被推到了弗洛伊德兴趣的边缘[periphery]。

Summary（小结）

Freud's view of shame was neither consistent nor clearly defined. He did not focus on shame as a principal concern. Instead, he developed the structural theory, with guilt and the Oedipus complex at its center. I have suggested that this relative lack of interest may have stemmed from the proximity of the key concepts of narcissism (inferiority, self-regard, and self-criticism) to the contributions of Adler. Another speculation might be that shame was an affect that struck too close to Freud's own personal vulnerabilities and that he eschewed study of it, therefore, in favor of other pursuits.

弗洛伊德对羞耻的看法既不一致，也不清晰明确。他并未将羞耻作为主要关注点。相反，他发展出结构理论，以内疚与俄狄浦斯情结为核心。我曾提出，这种相对的兴趣缺失或许源于自恋的关键概念（自卑、自重与自我批评）与阿德勒贡献之间的过于相近[proximity]。另一种揣测[speculation]是：羞耻是一种过于切近弗洛伊德自身个人脆弱性[vulnerabilities]的情感，因此他回避[eschewed]了对它的研究，转而投身其他追求。

Nonetheless, Freud did write about shame, early as a painful affect related to audience, discovery, and inferiority, and later as a defense against voyeuristic/exhibitionistic drives and the sense of genital defect in women. These different viewpoints relate to questions I raised in the first chapter, namely, whether shame can be experienced in isolation or only in a social context; and whether shame is to be viewed primarily as affective experience or as defense. Certainly the latter two attributes do coexist, but Freud's emphasis seems to have shifted over time (Hazard, 1969). It seems that, for Freud, affective experience and defense were intertwined, in that a social context is inferred for the painful affect of shame (for example, in conflict with cultural norms), whereas an internal, intrapsychic context is posited for shame as a reaction-formation against the drives (for example, ethical ideals).

尽管如此，弗洛伊德确实写过羞耻：早期将其视为一种与观众[audience]、被发现[discovery]和自卑相关的痛苦情感；后期则视为针对窥视/暴露驱力、以及女性生殖器缺陷感的防御。这些不同观点关联到我在第1章提出的两个问题，即：羞耻是可以在孤立中体验，还是只能在社會情境中体验？羞耻应主要被视为情感性经验，还是防御？当然，后两种属性确实共存，但弗洛伊德的强调似乎随着时间发生了转移（Hazard, 1969）。对弗洛伊德而言，情感性经验与防御似乎相互交织[intertwined]：痛苦羞耻情感被推定为处于社会情境之中（例如，与文化和规范[norms]的冲突），而作为针对驱力之反应形成的羞耻，则被置于一种内在的、内在心理[intrapsychic]的情境之中（例如，伦理理想）。

In addition, I have suggested that Freud (1914) might have offered a framework for an elaboration of shame had he chosen to do so; that a consideration of shame as a central affect underlying narcissistic phenomena, in combination with Freud's (1914) consideration of the ego ideal and self-regard, might have led to further work on shame.

此外，我曾提出，弗洛伊德（1914）本可以——若他选择如此——提供一个阐发羞耻的框架；若将羞耻视为自恋现象之底层的核心情感，并结合弗洛伊德（1914）对自我理想与自重的考量，本可导向对羞耻更深入的研究。

SUBSEQUENT WORKS RELATED TO SHAME（与羞耻相关的后续研究）

I have searched in vain for work explicitly devoted to shame prior to the contributions of Piers (1953) and Lynd (1958). For a historical perspective on shame, therefore, we must locate comments about shame embedded in writings, particularly those of the ego psychologists. As suggested earlier, I believe that the limited attention to shame reflected the theoretical direction set by Freud. Shame, however, is mentioned by several authors who concern themselves with feelings of inferiority and with the ego ideal. It will become apparent that these works relate directly to an evolving interest in the self and in narcissism, reflecting a resurgence of interest in Freud's "On Narcissism" (1914) and in other issues he had left essentially incomplete.

在皮尔斯（1953）与林德[Lynd]（1958）的贡献之前，我曾徒劳地[searched in vain]搜寻过明确致力于羞耻的研究。因此，对于羞耻的历史视角，我们必须到著述中、尤其是自我心理学家[ego psychologists]的著述里，去寻索那些嵌入其中的关于羞耻的评论。如前所述，我相信对羞耻的有限关注反映了弗洛伊德所设定的理论方向。然而，确有几位关注自卑感与自我理想的作者提及了羞耻。将不难看出，这些著作直接关联到一种对自体与自恋不断增长的兴趣，反映出对弗洛伊德《论自恋》（1914）及其基本留白[incomplete]之其他问题的兴趣复兴[resurgence]。

After discussing several psychoanalytic writers who address shame independently of the ego ideal, I will turn to an assessment of the ego ideal and its relationship to shame, as implied in Freud's 1914 work. Shame takes its place within a matrix of the self and narcissistic vulnerability, particularly in the writings of A. Reich (1960) and Jacobson (1964). A more complete elaboration of shame and narcissism is offered in chapter 4.

在讨论了若干独立于自我理想而论述羞耻的精神分析作者之后，我将转向对自我理想及其与羞耻之关系的评估——正如弗洛伊德 1914 年著作所暗示的。羞耻在自体与自恋脆弱性的母阵[matrix]中占有一席之地，尤其在 A. Reich（1960）与 Jacobson（1964）的著述中。第4章将对羞耻与自恋给出更完整的阐发。

Voyeurism and Inferiority Feelings（窥视与自卑感）

Abraham's (1913) study of scopophilia (voyeurism) expanded upon Freud's conceptualizations, with scopophilia being transformed into compulsive curiosity and a tendency to brood. Defenses against this drive lead to visual symptoms, neurotic blindness, and thought blocking; shame is not a factor in these transformations. Fenichel (1945) also considered scopophilia, but he introduced shame as a motive for defense against it and against exhibitionism. He related shame to visual stimuli; "'I feel ashamed' means 'I do not want to be seen'" (p. 139). Therefore, people who feel ashamed hide themselves or at least avert their faces. However, they also close their eyes and refuse to look: "This is a kind of magical gesture, arising from the magical belief that anyone who does not look cannot be looked at" (p. 139). As noted, Nunberg (1955) also stated that "Shame is a reaction formation of the ego to the wish to exhibit" (p. 157).

亚伯拉罕[Abraham]（1913）对窥视[scopophilia]（窥视欲[voyeurism]）的研究拓展了弗洛伊德的概念化[conceptualizations]，将窥视转化为强迫性好奇[compulsive curiosity]与沉思[brood]倾向。针对这一驱力的防御导致视觉症状、神经症性盲[neurotic blindness]与思维阻断[thought blocking]；羞耻在这些转化中并非要素。费尼谢尔[Fenichel]（1945）也考量了窥视，但他将羞耻引入为针对窥视与暴露欲的防御之动机[motive]。他将羞耻与视觉刺激[visual stimuli]相联系："'我感到羞耻'意味着'我不想被看见'"（p. 139）。因此，感到羞耻的人会隐藏自己，或至少转开[avert]他们的脸。然而，他们也会闭上眼睛、拒绝去看："这是一种魔幻式的姿态[gesture]，源于一种魔幻信念——不看的人就不会被看"（p. 139）。如前所述，努伯格（1955）也说过："羞耻是自我针对暴露愿望的一种反应形成"（p. 157）。

Fenichel (1945) emphasized as well the relationship between shame and urethral eroticism (as implied in the episode of bed-wetting mentioned by Freud, 1898).

费尼谢尔（1945）还强调了羞耻与尿道性欲[urethral eroticism]之间的关系（正如弗洛伊德 1898 年提及的遗尿情节所暗示的）。

"... failures in urethral cleanliness are usually punished by putting the child to shame-much more so than failures in rectal cleanliness. ... shame is the specific force directed against urethral-erotic temptations. Ambition, so often described as an outcome of urethral-erotic conflicts, represents the fight against this shame" (p. 69).

"……在尿道清洁[urethral cleanliness]上的失败通常会以令孩子蒙羞来惩罚——远甚于直肠[rectal]清洁上的失败。……羞耻是针对尿道—性欲[urethral-erotic]诱惑[temptations]的特定力量。雄心[ambition]——常被描述为尿道—性欲冲突的一个结果——代表着对抗这种羞耻的斗争"（p. 69）。

Fenichel's equating ambition and defense against shame is an interesting reversal of Kohut's view of shame as a response to ambition and grandiosity. Here, Fenichel posits shame both as a defense and as a painful affective experience against which ambition serves as the defense; thus, shame may function as an affect that itself generates defense.

费尼谢尔将雄心等同于针对羞耻的防御，这与科胡特将羞耻视为对雄心与夸大[grandiosity]之反应的观点形成了有趣的逆转[reversal]。在此，费尼谢尔将羞耻既设定为防御，也设定为一种痛苦的情感性经验——而雄心充当了其防御；因此，羞耻可以作为一种情感而发挥功能，它自身生成防御。

Alexander (1938) wrote about the role of inferiority feelings, which he seemed to equate with shame in contrast to guilt. He suggested that inferiority feelings are experienced in comparison with other people, leading to a sense of weakness which itself stimulates competition and aggression. Thus, whereas guilt inhibits aggression, shame unleashes it. Also, according to Alexander, inferiority feelings represent responses to the regressive pull of dependency, leaving one narcissistically vulnerable and ashamed.

亚历山大[Alexander]（1938）写到了自卑感的作用，他似乎将其等同于羞耻，以区别于内疚。他提出，自卑感是在与他人的比较[comparison]中体验到的，导向一种虚弱[weakness]感，而它本身刺激竞争[competition]与攻击性[aggression]。因此，内疚抑制[inhibits]攻击性，羞耻却释放[unleashes]它。同样，据亚历山大所言，自卑感代表了对依赖[dependency]之退行性拉动[regressive pull]的反应，使人陷入自恋性的脆弱并感到羞耻。

THE EGO IDEAL（自我理想）

As suggested earlier, an investigation of ego psychology from our perspective on shame leads inevitably to the reemergence of issues of narcissism and the ego ideal. Freud (1914) viewed the ego ideal as an attempt to recapture the lost narcissism of infancy. For Freud, the ego ideal merged with conscience and ultimately with the superego as he created the structural model. At this point, I will consider certain seminal papers on the ego ideal that raise important issues about shame's genesis but do not specifically bear on the role of shame. Such issues, especially with regard to idealization, will be seen to be relevant in our later consideration of shame. Since the ego ideal is that structure which contains the internalizations, identifications, and creations forming the individual's guiding ideals, failure to attain or to live up to these ideals is the primary source of shame.

如前所述，从我们的羞耻视角出发对自我心理学[ego psychology]的考察，不可避免地导向自恋与自我理想议题的重新浮现[reemergence]。弗洛伊德（1914）将自我理想视为重获[recapture]婴儿期失落自恋的一种尝试。对弗洛伊德而言，随着他创建结构模型，自我理想与良心融合，并最终与超我融合。在此，我将考量关于自我理想的若干开创性[seminal]论文，它们提出了关于羞耻起源[genesis]的重要议题，却并未专门触及羞耻的角色。这类议题——尤其关乎理想化[idealization]——将在我们后面对羞耻的考量中被证明切题。由于自我理想是这样一种结构：它容纳着构成个体指导性理想的内化、认同与创生[creations]，未能达成或未能符合这些理想，便是羞耻的主要来源。

Return to the Ideal（回到理想）

Certain other authors returned to a study of the ego ideal, with (at times almost parenthetical) attention to shame and a continuing attempt to differentiate the functions and structure of the ego ideal from those of the superego. We shall see that these authors were not satisfied with elements of Freud's understanding of the superego, and especially with the degree to which the superego of the structural model swallows up and dedifferentiates the ego ideal. For them, Freud's structural theory tended to recast the developmental factors eventuating in the ego ideal in terms of oedipal issues revolving primarily around guilt, and thus failed to delineate adequately specific functions of the ideal, including feelings of shame. For example, Schafer (1960) separated the loving from the hostile functions of the superego. The loving functions, according to Schafer, protect the ego from excessive guilt, from abandonment by significant objects, and facilitate adaptation through identification with the parental superego

另一些作者回到了对自我理想的研究，带着（有时近乎附带的[parenthetical]）对羞耻的关注，并持续尝试将自我理想的功能与结构同超我区分开来。我们将看到，这些作者并不满意弗洛伊德对超我之理解的某些要素，尤其不满意结构模型中的超我吞噬[swallows up]并去分化[dedifferentiates]自我理想的程度。对他们而言，弗洛伊德的结构理论倾向于用主要围绕内疚的俄狄浦斯议题，来重铸[recast]导致自我理想的发展性因素，从而未能充分勾勒理想（包括羞耻感）的特定功能。例如，谢弗[Schafer]（1960）将超我的关爱性[loving]功能与敌意性[hostile]功能区分开来。据谢弗，关爱性功能保护自我免受过度的内疚、免受重要客体[significant objects]的抛弃[abandonment]，并通过认同父母超我而促进适应[adaptation]

These functions also include ideals and pride, through identification with the admired parents. Schafer's viewpoint was similar to mine when he stated, "It is in failing to reach these goals that the ego feels inferior. ... [inferiority feelings] correspond to feelings of loss of the superego's love, just as guilt corresponds to feelings of the superego's hatred" (pp. 178–179).

这些功能还包括理想与骄傲[pride]，藉由对令人钦佩的父母的认同。当谢弗陈述道："正是在未能达成这些目标时，自我感到自卑。……[自卑感]对应于失去超我之爱的感受，正如内疚对应于超我之恨的感受"（pp. 178–179），他的观点与我的相仿。

Schafer (1967) further considered the development of ideals in relation to the ego ideal and the ideal self. While reviewing in detail the relationship of the ego ideal and ideal-formation to the psychic structures (that is, ego, superego, and id), he moved further into an examination of the self and its representations, including ideals and the ideal self. These were considered in terms of patients' experience; "An ideal self-representation is an image or concept of oneself as one would be if one had satisfied a specific ideal" (p. 15).

谢弗（1967）进一步考量了与自我理想及理想自我相关的理想之发展。在详细回顾自我理想与理想形成[ideal-formation]同心理结构[psychic structures]（即自我、超我与本我[id]）的关系时，他进一步深入考察了自体及其表征[representations]，包括理想与理想自我。这些是从病人的经验[experience]角度来考量的："理想自我表征[ideal self-representation]是一种关于自己的意象或概念——即若自己满足了某个特定理想时将会成为的样子"（p. 15）。

Schafer contrasted this view of the self with that of the "experienced self-representation" (reflecting a view of the self as one thinks one is) and suggested that one continually aspires to achieve the ideal self. Ideals may be created or internalized through identification with the "perfection" of the parents. Failure to attain the state of the ideal self (or the ego's standards) may lead to various negative feelings (for example, guilt, unworthiness, being unloved and forsaken, helplessness), which might be experienced as shame. This is the only reference to shame in Schafer's paper.

谢弗将这种自体观与"被经验到的自体表征[experienced self-representation]"（反映一种"自体如自己所认为的样子"之观点）相对照，并提出人持续地渴望[aspires]去达成理想自我。理想可以藉由对父母之"完美"的认同而被创造或内化。未能达到理想自我（或自我的标准[standards]）的状态，可能导致各种负面感受（例如内疚、无价值感[unworthiess]、不被爱与遭弃[forsaken]、无助[helplessness]），而这些可能被体验为羞耻。这是谢弗文章中唯一一次提及羞耻。

Sandler and his colleagues (1963) were also troubled by inconsistencies in the concept of the ego ideal, beginning first with those in Freud's (1914) writings on the subject. These inconsistencies included an ideal for oneself in an attempt to regain the lost narcissism; an ideal for oneself, combined with conscience; a mental structure synonymous with the superego; and the superego as the vehicle for the ego ideal. Thus Sandler noted Freud's early equation of the ideal with narcissism and the subsequent disappearance of both into the mechanistic ambiguities of the structural superego. Presumably, these authors were dissatisfied with the theoretical and clinical explanatory power of the superego with regard to ideals, found this to be an important limitation, and sought to elucidate a separate structure for them. They also considered later work on the ego ideal, noting Nunberg's 1955 differentiation of it from the superego. For Nunberg, the ego ideal represented internalized love objects, and the superego reflected the internalized hated and feared objects. Annie Reich's (1953) distinction between the two was mentioned: "[T]he ego ideal expresses what one desires to be; the superego, what one ought to be"

As we shall see later, Reich (1960) suggested as well that self- and object representations are magically fused to enable the person to feel "as though he were his own ego ideal" (p. 226). Jacobson (1954) also viewed the ego ideal as integrating idealized self- and object features to gratify the longing for merger between loved self and object.

正如我们后文将看到的，赖希（1960）还提出，自体表征与客体表征被魔幻地融合[fused]，以使人体验到"仿佛他自己就是自己的自我理想"（p. 226）。雅各布森[Jacobson]（1954）也将自我理想视为整合[integrating]理想化的自体与客体特征，以满足[gratify]被爱的自体与客体之间融合[merger]的渴望。

Like Schafer (1967), Sandler and his co-workers (1963) went on to suggest that the self-representation takes on different "shapes" at different times, one of which is referred to as the "ideal self," "i.e., that which, at any moment, is a desired shape of the self-the 'self-as-I want-to-be!' "(pp. 152–153).

与谢弗（1967）相似，桑德勒及其合作者（1963）进而提出，自体表征在不同时刻呈现出不同的"形状[shapes]"；其中一种被称为"理想自我"，"即，在任一时刻，自体所渴望的形状——'作为我想成为之样子的自体！'"（pp. 152–153）。

The ideal self would provide the greatest sense of well-being at any given time (p. 154). The shape of the ideal self is being far more fluid and flexible than the ideals held up to the child by his introjects. The shape of the ideal self is also influenced by knowledge of the reality of the self's potentialities and limitations and of the environment. But what happens when the actual self fails to conform to the shape of the ideal self? Sandler et al. dealt explicitly with such failure, stating, "If the individual cannot change the shape of his self so as to identify it with his ideal self, then he will suffer the pangs of disappointment, and the affective states associated with lowered self-esteem" (p. 156).

理想自我在任何给定时刻都会提供最大的安适[well-being]感（p. 154）。理想自我的形状远比其内摄物[introjects]向孩子高举的理想更为流动[fluid]与灵活[flexible]。理想自我的形状也受自体之潜能[potentialities]与局限、以及环境之现实知识的影响。但当实际的自体未能符合[conform to]理想自我的形状时，会发生什么？桑德勒等人明确地处理了这种失败，陈述道："若个体无法改变其自体的形状，使之与其理想自我认同，那么他将遭受失望的苦痛[pangs of disappointment]，以及伴随自尊降低的情感状态"（p. 156）。

They then suggested that "the affect of shame arises when the individual perceives himself (or believes himself to have been perceived by others) as having failed to live up to ideal standards which he accepts, whereas guilt is experienced when his ideal self differs from that which he feels to be dictated by his introjects" (p. 157). Here, shame was explicitly considered to reflect failure to approximate the shape of the ideal self. It is interesting to note that the authors indicated both an internal (self-perception) and an external/social (perceived by others) view of shame.

他们进而提出："当个体知觉到自身（或相信自己被他人所知觉）未能符合他所接受的理想标准时，羞耻的情感便产生；而当他的理想自我不同于他觉得由内摄物所命令[dictated]的样子时，内疚便被体验到"（p. 157）。在此，羞耻被明确地视为反映了未能接近[approximate]理想自我之形状。有趣的是，这些作者同时指出了羞耻的内在（自我知觉[self-perception]）与外在/社会（被他人所知觉）两种视角。

DISCUSSION（讨论）

In this consideration of the ego ideal and the ideal self, I have tried to lay the groundwork for a structural understanding of shame and its relationship to ideals. Starting with Freud's (1914) elucidation of primary narcissism, the ego ideal, and self-regard, I moved to Hartmann and Loewenstein's (1962) metapsychological attempt to root the ego ideal in the superego. While these authors posited the ego ideal as a "rescue operation" for narcissism, they did not further elaborate the role of narcissism and its relationship to structural theory. They also failed to find a meaningful difference between shame and guilt from the perspective of psychoanalytic theory. I believe that this lack of distinction between shame and guilt reflected a greater emphasis on theory than on clinical observation. With Schafer's (1967) and Sandler et al.'s (1963) movement toward a more clinical view of the ego ideal, and their ultimate preference for the phenomenological, experience-near notion of the ideal self, a place for shame within the theoretical context of the ego ideal became possible. Since our interest in shame moves toward affective experience, and the ideal self is a clinically relevant subjective experience that attends to the nature and boundaries of failure, shame could then be introduced as a response to failure in attaining the shape of the ideal self.

在对自我理想与理想自我的这一考量中，我试图为理解羞耻及其与理想之关系的结构观点[structural understanding]奠定基础。从弗洛伊德（1914）对原发自恋[primary narcissism]、自我理想与自重的阐明出发，我转至哈特曼与勒文斯坦（1962）将自我理想植根[root]于超我的元心理学[metapsychological]尝试。尽管这些作者将自我理想设定为对自恋的一场"救援行动"，他们并未进一步阐发自恋的角色及其与结构理论的关系。他们也没能从精神分析理论的视角找到羞耻与内疚之间有意义的差别。我相信，这种对羞耻与内疚不加区分，反映了对理论而非临床观察的更大强调。随着谢弗（1967）与桑德勒等人（1963）转向对自我理想更具临床性的看法，并最终偏好现象学的[phenomenological]、贴近经验[experience-near]的理想自我概念，羞耻在自我理想之理论语境中的位置才成为可能。由于我们对羞耻的兴趣指向情感性经验，而理想自我是一种临床相关的、关注失败之性质与边界[boundaries]的主观经验，羞耻便得以被引入为对"未能达成理想自我之形状"的一种反应。

This view is similar to Schafer's (1967) contention that the ideal self is phenomenologically a more useful concept than the ego ideal. Thus, Sandler et al. (1963) attempted to clarify the ambiguity over the ego ideal evolving from oedipal/structural emphasis on the superego; they introduced (with Schafer, 1967) an experiential, "fluid shape of the ideal self more closely approximating subjective goals than does the "check-list of internalizations" implied by the more abstract ego ideal; and they have pointed to the connection between failure, the ideal self, and shame. Why does this not adequately encompass a theoretical understanding of shame, allowing us to move on to our clinical consideration of shame and its manifestations? Because, I suggest, these authors stopped short of relating their observations to narcissistic phenomena and vulnerability, upon which a more complete understanding of shame must be built. It is to these considerations we will turn next.

这一观点与谢弗（1967）的论断[contention]相似：理想自我在现象学上是一个比自我理想更有用的概念。由此，桑德勒等人（1963）试图澄清围绕自我理想的含混——这一含混源自对超我的俄狄浦斯/结构式强调；他们（与谢弗 1967 一道）引入了经验性的、"流动的理想自我之形状"，它比更抽象的自我理想所暗示的"内化清单[check-list of internalizations]"更切近主观目标[subjective goals]；并且他们指出了失败、理想自我与羞耻之间的联系。为何这仍不足以充分涵盖[encompass]对羞耻的理论理解，使我们得以推进到对羞耻及其表现的临床考量？因为，我认为，这些作者止步[stopped short]于将他们的观察联系到自恋现象与脆弱性——而对羞耻更完整的理解必须建立于此之上。我们接下来要转向的，正是这些考量。

I believe that the ego ideal-and particularly the ideal self provides a framework for understanding shame from an internal perspective. The values, idealizations, and internalized parental expectations of perfection, which form the content of the ego ideal, have been structuralized and no longer require the presence of the external object as guide. The shape of the ideal self is determined by this internal perspective. It is failure to live up to this ideal self - experienced as a sense of inferiority, defeat, flaw, or weakness- that results in the feeling of shame. In chapter 5, we will examine the contributions of self psychology, and particularly the role of rupture and failure of the self-selfobject bond, in understanding that sense of internal failure. Failure to achieve the ideal may be defensive, as I shall consider with regard to neurotic conflict over libidinal wishes or success in competition. However, it is the affective response to that failure, the searing shame, that is experienced clinically (even when that failure is defensive), and that therefore should be the first focus of therapeutic intervention.

我相信，自我理想——尤其是理想自我——提供了一种从内在视角理解羞耻的框架。构成自我理想之内容的那些价值、理想化，以及内化的父母式完美期待，已被结构化[structuralized]，不再需要外部客体的在场作为指引。理想自我的形状正是由这一内在视角所决定。正是未能符合这一理想自我——被体验为自卑、挫败[defeat]、瑕疵[flaw]或虚弱之感——导致了羞耻感。在第5章，我们将考察自体心理学[self psychology]的贡献，尤其是自体—自体客体[self-selfobject]纽带的断裂[rupture]与失败在理解那种内在失败感中的作用。未能达成理想可能是防御性的，正如我将在关于力比多愿望或竞争成功之神经症性冲突时所考量的。然而，在临床上被体验到的，是对那种失败的情感性反应——灼人的[searing]羞耻（即便那失败是防御性的），因而它应当成为治疗性干预[therapeutic intervention]的首要焦点。

Our consideration of the ego ideal has so far been confined to classical/structural theory. Certainly, the suggestion that shame and guilt cannot readily be differentiated psychoanalytically reflects a theoretical emphasis in which the ego ideal is firmly embedded within the superego. Several authors attempted to differentiate the loving function of the ego ideal from the punitive, critical functions of the morally restrictive superego, which threatens criticism and punishment by internalized objects and leads to guilt. We then saw movement toward a more phenomenological view of the ego ideal, represented by the "shape" of an ideal self, creating a clinical place for shame and an opening toward the self and narcissism.

我们对自我理想的考量迄今仅限于古典/结构理论。当然，"羞耻与内疚无法在精神分析上轻易区分"这一提法，反映出一种理论强调——在其中自我理想被牢牢地嵌入[embedded]于超我。若干作者试图将自我理想的关爱性功能，同那惩罚性的[punitive]、批判性的道德限制性超我功能区分开来；后者以内在化客体的批评与惩罚相威胁，并导致内疚。随后我们看到了朝向更具现象学性的自我理想观的运动，它以理想自我的"形状"为代表，为羞耻创造了临床位置，并开启了通往自体与自恋的入口[opening]。

Shame's relationship to guilt will not form a major part of this book, as it has been considered extensively elsewhere (see, for example, Piers, 1953; Lynd, 1958; Levin, 1967; Lewis, 1971, 1987; Thrane, 1978; and Wurmser, 1981). However, the issue of diagnostic and developmental primitiveness of shame arises in the nature of its relationship to guilt. These issues played a major role in the thinking of Reich (1960) and, particularly, of Jacobson (1964).

羞耻与内疚的关系不会构成本书的主要部分，因为它已在别处被广泛考量（例如见 Piers, 1953；Lynd, 1958；Levin, 1967；Lewis, 1971, 1987；Thrane, 1978；以及 Wurmser, 1981）。然而，羞耻在诊断与发育上的原始性[primitiveness]问题，出现于它与内疚之关系的性质之中。这些问题在赖希（1960）、尤其是雅各布森（1964）的思想中扮演了主要角色。

Now we turn to primitive object relations and pathological narcissism, to enrich our understanding of the ego ideal. With these additions, the meaning of shame will come into sharper focus, and I will then consider in greater detail shame's relationship to narcissism.

现在我们转向原始客体关系[primitive object relations]与病理性自恋[pathological narcissism]，以丰富我们对自我理想的理解。有了这些补充，羞耻的含义[meaning]将变得更为清晰[sharper focus]，届时我将更详细地考量羞耻与自恋的关系。

1. I will suggest later that shame frequently is a response to need and desire.

1. 我后面将提出，羞耻常常是对需要与欲望的一种反应。

We have learnt that libidinal instinctual impulses undergo the vicissitude of pathogenic repression if they come into conflict with the subject's cultural and ethical ideas ... he recognizes them as a standard for himself and submits to the claims they make on him. Repression, we have said, proceeds from the ego; we might say with greater precision that it proceeds from the self-respect of the ego" (Freud, 1914, p. 93); and "For the ego the formation of an ideal would be the conditioning factor of repression" (p. 94).
