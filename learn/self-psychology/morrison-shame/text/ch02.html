第1章　Introduction and Overview（导论与概览）

Andrew P. Morrison · 第1章　Introduction and Overview（导论与概览） · pp. 1–17 · 62 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

本篇小节
  1. · GUIDING QUESTIONS（引导问题）
  2. · SUMMARIZING REMARKS（总结性评论）

We have all felt shame. We have all suffered feelings of inferiority, inadequacy, incompetence; known a sense of defect and flaw, of failure; been scorned by others—such feelings are among the most painful we can experience. We hide them from ourselves and from others. Yet, shame has, until very recently, been little explored in the psychoanalytic literature.

我们都有过羞耻[shame]的体验。我们都曾感受过自卑[inferiority]、无能[inadequacy]、不胜任[incompetence]；体会过缺陷与瑕疵、失败；曾被他人蔑视——这些感受是我们能体验到的最痛苦的情绪之一。我们对自己、也对他人隐藏它们。然而，直到最近，羞耻在精神分析文献[psychoanalytic literature]中都还鲜被探究。

My own interest in studying shame began a few years ago with a patient of mine, a man of my own age who had suffered from several episodes of severe depression and mania. He had never fulfilled the promise of his early talents or reached the pinnacles of achievement expected by his parents. The son of two concert musicians, he was "supposed" to reach great artistic heights; instead, he had become a middle-level technocrat in a business concern. He had married, but his wife and two children had left him following a manic episode. During treatment he gradually uncovered deep feelings of shame, mortification, and humiliation at his failures to attain his (and his parents') goals or to form lasting and intimate human relationships.

我对羞耻的研究兴趣始于几年前的一位病人。他与我同龄，曾几度经历严重的抑郁[depression]与躁狂[mania]。他从未兑现早年才华所许诺的前途，也未能达到父母期望的成就巅峰。作为两位音乐会音乐家的儿子，他"本该"达到伟大的艺术高度；相反，他却成了一家商业机构里的中层技术官僚。他曾结婚，但在一次躁狂发作后，妻子和两个孩子都离开了他。治疗中，他逐渐发掘出深刻的羞耻、屈辱[mortification]与羞辱[humiliation]感——源于他未能实现自己（以及父母）的目标，也未能建立持久而亲密的人际关系。

This sense of failure was pervasive and reflected profoundly narcissistic expectations and vulnerabilities. Yet because of the deep pain these feelings caused, it was difficult to ferret them out and examine them in psychotherapy.

这种失败感无处不在，深刻地反映出自恋[narcissistic]的期待与脆弱性[vulnerabilities]。然而，由于这些感受带来的剧痛，要在心理治疗[psychotherapy]中把它们挖掘出来并加以审视，十分困难。

He hid his shame both from himself and from me, his therapist, behind a wall of lethargy and depression, with occasional outbursts of mania to express his defensive grandiosity. This patient, then, introduced me to the ravages of shame. It was no accident that I found him likable, that I readily identified with the issues of his age and gender, and with his artistic aspirations. The shame of patients interacts with that of their therapists, and it was through my own identification with his goals and pain—a recognition of elements of my own shame feelings—that I was able to understand the importance of shame in his life experience. For this man, the experience of shame was embedded in his narcissistic vulnerability and manic-depressive illness. (For an exploration of the relationship of shame to narcissism and to manic-depressive illness, see chapter 11.).

他把自己的羞耻既对自己、也对我——他的治疗师——隐藏起来，藏在倦怠与抑郁的墙后，偶尔以躁狂的爆发来表达其防御性的夸大[grandiose]。正是这位病人，让我见识了羞耻的肆虐。我会觉得他可爱，会如此轻易地认同于他这个年龄与性别所面临的问题、他的艺术抱负，这并非偶然。病人的羞耻与治疗师的羞耻会相互作用；正是通过我对他的目标与痛苦的认同——也就是承认我自己羞耻[shame feelings]的元素——我才得以理解羞耻在他生命经验中的重要性。对这个人而言，羞耻的体验嵌入在他的自恋脆弱性与躁郁性疾患[manic-depressive illness]之中。（关于羞耻与自恋、羞耻与躁郁性疾患之间关系的进一步探讨，见第11章。）

My work with this patient alerted me to shame in other patients. And, indeed, I found it in most of the people I was treating—men and women, neurotic as well as psychotic and character-disordered. In fact, as Wurmser (1981) has pointed out, hardly an analytic or therapeutic session goes by without the appearance of some expression or manifestation of shame, humiliation, embarrassment, mortification, despair, or disgrace. These shame phenomena are sometimes openly and consciously experienced at the heart of human unhappiness but at other times are hidden from experience because they are so painful. As. Levin (1971) has noted, shame frequently causes one to hide, to avoid interpersonal contact as a protection against rejection, and to conceal the affective experience from one's own awareness. As guilt invites confession and forgiveness (Thrane, 1979), shame generates concealment out of a fear of rendering the self unacceptable (Morrison, 1983).

这位病人的工作让我注意到其他病人身上的羞耻。事实上，我发现它存在于我所治疗的大多数人中——无论男女，无论神经症性[neurotic]、精神病性[psychotic]还是人格障碍[character-disordered]者。事实上，正如乌尔姆泽[Wurmser]（1981）所指出的，几乎每一次分析性或治疗性会谈都会呈现出某种羞耻、羞辱、尴尬[embarrassment]、屈辱、绝望[despair]或耻辱[disgrace]的表达或显现[manifestation]。这些羞耻现象有时公开而清醒地处于人类不幸的核心，有时却因过于痛苦而被隐藏于经验之外。正如莱文[Levin]（1971）所言，羞耻常常使人躲藏、回避人际接触以保护自己免受拒绝，并把情感性经验[affective experience]隐藏在自己的意识之外。正如内疚[guilt]会招致坦白与宽恕（Thrane, 1979），羞耻则因害怕使自体[self]变得不可接受而生成隐藏（Morrison, 1983）。

Throughout this book, I will be exploring manifestations of the tendency to hide, to protect the self from rejection-a social manifestation—and from self-loathing—an intrapsychic, internal one.

在本书中，我将探讨隐藏倾向的诸种显现：保护自体免受拒绝——这是一种社会性显现[social manifestation]；以及免受自我厌恶[self-loathing]——这是一种内在心理性[intrapsychic]、内在的显现。

Yet, as obvious as these observations may seem, I have been struck by the surprise and novelty they elicit from most clinicians. In discussions or presentations on shame to various groups of therapists, I have noted a common response: they acknowledge the relevance of shame to their practices, but then they remark that "Somehow I never thought about it in that way" or "It hadn't occurred to me as an important focus." How can we account for this seemingly paradoxical situation, in which a clearly relevant and significant human experience is so frequently relegated to the psychological "back burner" in clinical practice?

然而，尽管这些观察看似显而易见，我却惊讶于它们给大多数临床工作者带来的新奇感。在各种面向治疗师的羞耻讨论或报告中，我注意到一种常见反应：他们承认羞耻与自己的临床实践相关，但随即又说" Somehow 我从未那样想过"，或"它从未作为重要焦点进入我的意识"。我们该如何解释这种看似矛盾的情形——一种明显相关且重要的人类经验，在临床实践中却如此频繁地被 relegated to 心理的"后备 burner"？

First, we must consider why shame for so long has been paid so little attention in the psychoanalytic and psychotherapeutic literature.

首先，我们必须思考，为何羞耻长久以来在精神分析与心理治疗文献中受到如此少的关注。

As the clinical vignette described earlier showed, shame is a central affective experience of narcissism (Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983). In On Narcissism, Freud (1914) introduced the concepts of the ego ideal and self-regard, both of which are central to my perspective on shame. Freud described the ego ideal as that intrapsychic agent (later to be associated with the superego) which contains values and ideals. Had he gone further with this concept, Freud might well have proceeded to an investigation of shame and its relationship to conflicts and failures in the attainment of the perfection of the ego ideal. In the next chapter, I examine in more detail Freud's views on shame, but for now let me suggest that he left the ego ideal and self-regard essentially unexamined after his 1914 work, choosing instead to pursue guilt as a response to oedipal conflict, the intrapsychic tension between ego, id, and superego, between wish and defense.

如前文临床小插曲[vignette]所示，羞耻是自恋的核心情感性经验[affective experience]（Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983）。在《论自恋》[On Narcissism]中，弗洛伊德（Freud, 1914）提出了自我理想[ego ideal]与自重[self-regard]的概念，二者对我关于羞耻的视角[perspective on shame]至关重要。弗洛伊德把自我理想描述为这样一种内在心理性动因[intrapsychic agent]（后来与超我[superego]相关联）：它内含价值与理想。倘若他进一步沿着这一概念推进，弗洛伊德很可能就会研究羞耻，以及羞耻与实现自我理想之完美的冲突和失败之间的关系。在下一章中，我会更详细地考察弗洛伊德关于羞耻的观点；但此刻我想说的是，在1914年的著作之后，他基本未再审视自我理想与自重，转而选择把内疚作为对俄狄浦斯冲突[oedipal conflict]的回应——那是自我、本我[id]与超我之间的内在心理性张力，是愿望与防御之间的张力。

Schafer (1960) has noted this distinction by referring to the ego ideal as "the loving function" of the superego in contrast with its "punitive function."

谢弗[Schafer]（1960）通过把自我理想称为超我的"慈爱功能[loving function]"、以对照其"惩罚功能[punitive function]"，指出了这一区分。

It was the punitive superego, however, that dominated the conflict-drive psychology of Freud's seminal work, as he turned from narcissism, the ego ideal, and self-esteem to the neuroses in the evolution of the structural theory. Wurmser (1981) has made a similar point. Thus, signal anxiety, oedipal conflicts, repression, and the drives were taken as the cornerstones of psychoanalytic theory, with guilt and anxiety underscored as the manifestations of conflict between the intrapsychic components. Shame was not readily assimilated into psychoanalytic conflict theory as it might have been had. Freud pursued a fuller elaboration of narcissism. It was not until the more recent developments of ego psychology that interest returned to the study of narcissism and the self, and with this an opening to the understanding of shame emerged.

然而，正是惩罚性的超我支配了弗洛伊德开创性著作中的冲突-驱力[conflict-drive]心理学；随着他从自恋、自我理想和自尊转向结构理论[structural theory]演化中的神经症[neuroses]，他不再关注自恋。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）也提出过类似观点。于是，信号焦虑[signal anxiety]、俄狄浦斯冲突、压抑[repression]与驱力[drives]被当作精神分析理论的基石，内疚与焦虑被强调为内在心理成分之间冲突的显现。羞耻未能轻易地被纳入精神分析冲突理论——倘若弗洛伊德曾对自恋做更充分的阐发，情况或许本可不同。直到自我心理学[ego psychology]的近期发展，对自恋与自体的研究兴趣才得以回归，而对羞耻的理解也随之打开了一扇门。

Why did Freud not significantly pursue his study of narcissism and its rich manifestations as sketched in his 1914 paper? Certainly, second guessing motivation about such a historical turn is at best hazardous. The manifest fruit of Freud's labors may be justification enough to explain the direction of his interests. Yet, from the perspective of one interested in shame and narcissism, the question remains intriguing, and a sociopolitical answer suggests itself. It seems likely that Freud's relative lack of interest in shame might have been an unintentional but inevitable outcome of the historical evolution of psychoanalysis.

为何弗洛伊德没有进一步探究他在1914年论文中勾勒出的自恋及其丰富显现？当然，对这样一种历史转向的动机进行事后揣测，充其量也是冒险的。弗洛伊德劳作所结出的明显果实，或许已足以解释他兴趣的方向。然而，从一个关注羞耻与自恋的人的角度来看，这个问题依然引人入胜，而一个社会政治性的答案也呼之欲出。弗洛伊德的相对忽视羞耻，很可能并非有意，却是精神分析历史演化的必然结果。

For example, Freud's attention to libidinal manifestations, their repression in response to intrapsychic conflict, and their representation in the unconscious-the essence of his conceptualization of psychoanalysis-is known to have reflected his defense of these discoveries against dilution by premature incursions from ego psychology, conscious mental phenomena, and social interaction. Protecting "the gold of psychoanalysis," he may well have shown less interest in the elaboration of such other phenomena as narcissism and its manifestations. This historical evolution may have related also to Freud's conflict with Alfred Adler, whom Freud had expelled from the psychoanalytic movement in 1911.

例如，弗洛伊德对力比多[libidinal]显现、它们对内在心理冲突的压抑，以及它们在无意识[unconscious]中的表征的关注——这本质上是他对精神分析的概念化——据说反映了他对这些发现的捍卫，以防被自我心理学、意识心理现象和社会互动的过早入侵所稀释。为了保护"精神分析的黄金"，他对自恋及其显现等其他现象的阐发很可能兴趣索然。这一历史演化也可能与弗洛伊德和阿尔弗雷德·阿德勒[Alfred Adler]之间的冲突有关——弗洛伊德曾于1911年将阿德勒驱逐出精神分析运动。

In a scholarly review of the development of Adler's theories, and Adler's relationship to Freud in the organization of the Vienna Psychoanalytic Society, Stepansky (1983) has elucidated the course of the inevitable split between the two men. Without elaborating here the details of Adler's thinking outlined in that study, we can summarize. Adler's theory as focusing on "organ inferiority," leading to generalized inferiority feelings; resultant "masculine protest" as a manifestation of an aggressive drive to deal with these feelings; and the universal wish to defend against the feminine and assert the masculine ("psychic hermaphroditism"). While Freud accepted for some time Adler's contributions as supplementary to psychoanalytic principles, he ultimately rejected them as antagonistic to the theory of libido and repression and spent subsequent years elaborating the content of unconscious processes and the structure and defenses of the mental apparatus.

斯捷潘斯基[Stepansky]（1983）在一篇关于阿德勒理论发展、以及阿德勒与弗洛伊德在维也纳精神分析学会[Vienna Psychoanalytic Society]组织中关系的学术综述中，阐明了两人之间必然分裂的轨迹。无需在此详述那项研究勾勒出的阿德勒思想细节，我们可以把阿德勒的理论概括为聚焦于"器官自卑[organ inferiority]"，它导致泛化的自卑感；随之产生的"男性抗议[masculine protest]"则表现为一种攻击性驱力，用以应对这些感受；此外还有普遍的欲望——抵御女性气质、彰显男性气质（"精神雌雄同体[psychic hermaphroditism]"）。虽然弗洛伊德一度把阿德勒的贡献视为对精神分析原则的补充，最终却将其视为与力比多和压抑理论相 antagonistic，并在随后数年中致力于阐发无意识过程的内容，以及心理装置[mental apparatus]的结构与防御。

One need not fully endorse Adler's formulations to see how close they come to a framework that would allow for shame and its manifestations (although, interestingly, Adler himself never did so).

一个人不必完全认同阿德勒的表述，也能看出它们多么接近一个足以容纳羞耻及其显现的框架（虽然有趣的是，阿德勒本人从未这样做）。

Organ inferiority and inferiority feelings, masculine protest and aggression (which have been viewed as typical reactions instigated by shame, see Gilligan, 1976); and femininity (related by Freud to passivity and passive longings, discussed with regard to shame by Lewis, 1971, and Anthony, 1981)-each may be viewed as a major expression of, or defense against, the experience of shame. Seen from a different perspective, these qualities relate also to the attributes of primary and secondary narcissism, with libidinal cathexis of the ego, the beginnings of internalized object relations, and elucidation of the ego ideal and self-regard, which Freud (1914) described in "On Narcissism." However, the relationship of these constructs to Adler's "Individual Psychology" (Adler, 1907, 1912, 1929) may well have consolidated. Freud's subsequent turn away from narcissism as he built the edifice of psychoanalysis. It was not that Freud explicitly denied the importance of shame in psychic development, but rather that he turned elsewhere to develop a system that was clearly differentiated from those of his various detractors. Thrane (1979) has made a similar point with regard to Adler.

器官自卑与自卑感、男性抗议与攻击性（它们被视作由羞耻激发的典型反应，见Gilligan, 1976）；以及女性气质（弗洛伊德将其与被动性[passivity]和被动渴望联系起来，刘易斯[Lewis, 1971]与安东尼[Anthony, 1981]曾联系羞耻讨论过）——这些每一项都可被视为羞耻经验的主要表达，或防御。从另一个角度看，这些品质还与原发性自恋[primary narcissism]和继发性自恋[secondary narcissism]的属性相关，涉及自我的力比多贯注[libidinal cathexis]、内化的客体关系[internalized object relations]的开端，以及自我理想与自重的阐发——弗洛伊德（1914）在《论自恋》中描述了这些。然而，这些构念[constructs]与阿德勒"个体心理学[Individual Psychology]"（Adler, 1907, 1912, 1929）之间的关系，很可能巩固了弗洛伊德随后从自恋的转向，因为他在建立精神分析大厦。

Whether or not we fully accept this speculative hypothesis about the influence of historical forces on the direction of Freud's theoretical foundation for psychoanalysis, the conclusion is clear: Freud's structural theory emphasized the importance of guilt rather than shame as a central dysphoric affect, clearly related as it was to intrapsychic conflict. This emphasis assured a delay in attention to shame as an important focus of study until the structural theory itself was modified and opened up by the next generation of analysts. (This process of theoretical change will be considered in chapters 2 through 5, following a consideration of shame in Freud's writings.) I believe, however, that there are also additional explanations for the low profile of shame.

无论我们是否完全接受这一关于历史力量如何影响弗洛伊德精神分析理论方向的推测性假设，结论都很清楚：弗洛伊德的结构理论强调内疚而非羞耻，将其作为核心的痛苦情感[dysphoric affect]——这与内在心理冲突密切相关。这种强调导致对羞耻作为重要研究焦点的关注被推迟，直到结构理论本身被下一代分析师修正并拓展。（这一理论变迁的过程将在第2至第5章中讨论，届时会先考察弗洛伊德著作中的羞耻。）不过，我相信羞耻之所以长期处于低 profile，还有其他解释。

Building upon the values of psychoanalysis propounded by Freud, guilt was viewed as the more worthy affective experience compared with shame. Guilt is the result of activity, a transgression against a superego barrier (Piers, 1953), and a response to intrapsychic conflict.

基于弗洛伊德所倡导的精神分析价值，内疚被视为比羞耻更"值得"的情感性经验。内疚是活动[activity]的结果，是对超我屏障的逾越（Piers, 1953），也是对内在心理冲突的回应。

Palliation comes through confession and forgiveness. Shame, on the other hand, reflects passivity, a failure or defect of the whole self. Shame has been viewed as the developmentally more primitive affect (Jacobson, 1964), a product of oral need and anal failure (in contrast to oedipal longings); Tomkins (1962-63) even included shame as one of the innate, inborn emotions. Others have viewed shame disparagingly as inevitably embedded in a social matrix, in contrast to the intrapsychic origins of guilt. Each of these factors has, I believe, influenced the feeling among psychoanalysts that shame is somehow more superficial, and thus less worthy of attention, than guilt. Such assumptions will be considered later, for they certainly play a part in the relatively distant place accorded shame in most clinical considerations.

缓解[palliation]来自坦白与宽恕。另一方面，羞耻反映的是被动性[passivity]，是整个自体的失败或缺陷。羞耻被视作发展上更原始的情感（Jacobson, 1964），是口腔需求与肛门失败的产品（与俄狄浦斯渴望相对）；汤姆金斯[Tomkins]（1962-63）甚至把羞耻列为先天、与生俱来的情绪之一。另一些人则以贬抑的方式看待羞耻，认为它不可避免地嵌入社会矩阵[social matrix]，这与内疚的内在心理起源形成对比。我相信，这些因素都影响了精神分析师中这样一种感觉：羞耻不知何故比内疚更肤浅[superficial]，因此更不值得关注。这些假设将在后文考察，因为它们无疑在羞耻在临床考量中相对边缘的地位上起了作用。

I have already suggested that shame induces hiding and concealment, thus making it more difficult to ferret out in clinical work.

我已经指出，羞耻会引发隐藏与隐瞒[concealment]，因而在临床工作中更难被发掘。

Patients recoil from facing their shame-and the failures, senses of defect, inferiority, and passivity that engender it. Patients often express, instead, defenses against, and displaced manifestations of, shame-certain depressions, mania, rage, envy, and contempt (see chapters 8 and 9). Because shame is so often unspoken, many therapists have not appreciated its importance in analytic and therapeutic work. Frequently it is hidden behind the clearly defensive manifestations of distress, and these are usually investigated alone-often from the perspective of intrapsychic conflict and related dynamics—without appreciation of the underlying or accompanying shame.

病人会回避面对自己的羞耻——以及催生羞耻的失败、缺陷感、自卑与被动性。病人常常表达的，反而是对羞耻的防御与置换性显现[displaced manifestations]——某些抑郁、躁狂、愤怒、嫉妒与轻蔑（见第8、9章）。由于羞耻常常不被言说，许多治疗师并未意识到它在分析与治疗工作中的重要性。它常常隐藏在明显防御性的痛苦显现背后，而这些显现通常被单独考察——往往从内在心理冲突及相关动力的视角——却未意识到其下或伴随的羞耻。

Moreover, the shame of patients is contagious, often resonating with the clinician's own shame experiences—the therapist's own sense of failure, self-deficiency, and life disappointments. Painful countertransference feelings may thus be generated in the analyst/therapist, feelings that he or she, like the patient, would just as soon avoid, feelings that not infrequently lead to a collusion, preventing investigation of the shame experience. Frequently the therapist's own analysis or psychotherapy will not have explored shame, and thus both patient and therapist share various methods of concealment, blind spots unavailable to analytic exploration. As Levin (1971) also noted, then, the pain of shame, and the fear of rejection and abandonment, may affect the therapist as well as the patient.

此外，病人的羞耻具有传染性[contagious]，常常与治疗师自身的羞耻经验产生共鸣——治疗师自己的失败感、自体缺陷感[self-deficiency]与人生失望。痛苦的反移情[countertransference]感受由此在分析师/治疗师身上生成；他或她像病人一样，宁愿回避这些感受；这些感受并不罕见地导致合谋[collusion]，阻碍对羞耻经验的探究。治疗师自己的分析或心理治疗往往也未探索羞耻，于是病人与治疗师共享各种隐瞒方法、盲点[blind spots]，而这些都无法进入分析探索。正如莱文（Levin, 1971）也指出的，羞耻之痛、对被拒绝与被抛弃的恐惧，可能影响治疗师，也同样影响病人。

In order for shame to gain prominence within psychoanalytic study, elaboration of the structural theory was necessary. Hartmann's (1939) contributions to ego psychology amplified and enriched classical psychoanalytic theory; he (Hartmann, 1950) again examined narcissism, defining it as "the libidinal cathexis not of the ego but of the self" (p. 85). Hartmann and Lowenstein (1962) detailed the construct of the ego ideal within the superego. Jacobson (1964) regarded the self as an experiencing entity more global than the intrapsychic structures; she introduced self and object representations (that is, the symbolic, internalized personae of external reality) into ego psychology. Finally, more recent recognition of narcissism by Kohut (1971, 1977, 1984), Kernberg (1975), and others has opened a path for the optimal investigation of shame, as indicated by many recent contributions to the shame literature (Thrane, 1979; Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983).

为了让羞耻在精神分析研究中获得突出地位，结构理论的阐发是必要的。哈特曼[Hartmann]（1939）对自我心理学的贡献放大并丰富了经典精神分析理论；他（Hartmann, 1950）再次考察自恋，将其定义为"对自我而非自体的力比多贯注"（p. 85）。哈特曼与洛文斯坦[Lowenstein]（1962）详细阐述了超我之内的自我理想构念。雅各布森[Jacobson]（1964）把自体视为一种比内在心理结构更 global 的经验性实体；她引入了自体与客体表征[self and object representations]（即外部现实的象征性、内化了的 persona）进入自我心理学。最后，科胡特[Kohut]（1971, 1977, 1984）、克恩贝格[Kernberg]（1975）等人对自恋的近期认识，为羞耻的最优研究开辟了道路，这在羞耻文献的许多近期贡献中都有体现（Thrane, 1979; Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983）。

With this brief historical review of some of the salient themes in psychoanalysis that inform my approach to the study of shame, we come to some principal questions about shame to which we will return again and again in this book. These questions reappear and shape various portions of the Theoretical and Clinical Sections (though not necessarily in tidy fashion.) I will restate them in the Epilogue and will there reconsider them on the basis of material that, by then, will be familiar to the reader.

带着对精神分析中若干显著主题的简短历史回顾——这些主题构成了我研究羞耻的方法——我们来到了一些关于羞耻的核心问题，这些问题将在本书中反复出现。它们会重现，并塑造理论与临床部分的各个章节（虽然未必以整齐的方式）。我将在尾声[Epilogue]中重述它们，并在届时基于读者已然熟悉的材料重新思考。

GUIDING QUESTIONS（引导问题）

1. Is shame to be viewed as a defense against exhibitionism/ grandiosity (and other drive manifestations), or is it best understood as an affect? Freud (1895, 1896, 1900, 1905) was ambiguous on this point. He viewed shame as a reaction formation against the wish for genital exhibitionism and expression of other sexual impulses and perversions; saw shame as socially useful, directing sexual energy away from the self and toward tasks necessary for the preservation of the species. Lowenfeld (1976) has made a similar point about the potential utility of shame in preserving the mores of society. Freud also considered shame to be a result of anxiety, which is "objective" and social, reflecting fear of rejection and disapproval by others.

1. 羞耻应被视作对露阴癖/夸大[exhibitionism/grandiosity]（及其他驱力显现）的防御，还是最好被理解为一种情感[affect]？弗洛伊德（1895, 1896, 1900, 1905）在这一点上态度暧昧。他把羞耻视为对生殖器露阴及表达其他性冲动与性变态的反应形成[reaction formation]；认为羞耻具有社会效用，能把性能量从自体导向为保存物种所必需的任务。洛文菲尔德[Lowenfeld]（1976）也提出过类似观点，强调羞耻在维护社会习俗[mores]方面的潜在功用。弗洛伊德还认为羞耻是焦虑[anxiety]的结果，这种焦虑是"客观的"、社会性的，反映了对被拒绝与他人反对的恐惧。

Thus, shame can be viewed as a defense, a reaction formation against the wish/drive to exhibit. Interestingly, Kohut's position on shame (see chapter 5) was in many ways similar to Freud's. Kohut (1971, 1972) considered shame to be a reflection of what he called the "vertical split," in which the ego is in danger of being overwhelmed by unbridled grandiosity. Hence, shame protects against the breakthrough of grandiosity and consequently serves a defensive function. For Levin (1967, 1971) also, shame is a signal, alerting the ego, through anticipatory shame anxiety, to the danger of future shame if action is not taken to protect the self.

因此，羞耻可被视为一种防御，一种对展示愿望/驱力的反应形成。有趣的是，科胡特关于羞耻的立场（见第5章）在许多方面与弗洛伊德相似。科胡特（1971, 1972）认为羞耻反映了他所谓的"垂直分裂[vertical split]"——在这种状态中，自我[ego]面临被无拘无束的夸大[unbridled grandiosity]淹没的危险。因此，羞耻保护夸大不致突破，从而发挥着防御功能。对莱文（Levin, 1967, 1971）而言，羞耻也是一种信号，通过预期性羞耻焦虑[anticipatory shame anxiety]警示自我：如果不采取行动保护自体，未来羞耻的危险便存在。

Clearly, though, shame is also a painful affect, even when viewed as a signal to modify behavior, to repress wishes, or to hide. As indicated earlier, Tomkins (1962-63) viewed shame as one of the basic affects, generating its own set of facial expressions (see chapter 4).

然而显然，羞耻也是一种痛苦的情感，即便被视为改变行为、压抑愿望或躲藏的信号时也是如此。如前所述，汤姆金斯（Tomkins, 1962-63）把羞耻视为基本情感[affects]之一，它生成其特有的一套面部表情（见第4章）。

Many authors (for example, Piers, 1953; Lynd, 1958; Lewis, 1971; Thrane, 1979) have stressed the relationship between shame and guilt.

许多作者（例如Piers, 1953; Lynd, 1958; Lewis, 1971; Thrane, 1979）都强调过羞耻与内疚的关系。

I have suggested elsewhere (Morrison, 1983, 1984a, b) that shame is a painful feeling central to narcissistic disorders and plays a role analogous to that which guilt plays in neurosis.

我在别处提出（Morrison, 1983, 1984a, b）：羞耻是一种痛苦感受，居于自恋障碍[narcissistic disorders]的核心，并扮演着类似内疚在神经症[neurosis]中所扮演的角色。

Are these viewpoints contradictory, or can shame be seen simultaneously as a painful affect, as a signal of danger from underlying grandiosity and exhibitionism, and as a defense against these (and other) phenomena and drive derivatives? I consider this matter further in chapters 2, 7, and 8.

这些观点是否相互矛盾？抑或羞耻可以同时被视为一种痛苦的情感、一种来自潜在夸大与露阴危险的信号，以及防御这些（及其他）现象与驱力衍生物[drive derivatives]的防御？我将在第2、7、8章中进一步讨论这一问题。

(2.) Can shame be fully understood within the context of structural (conflict-defense) psychology, or must a broader psychology of the self and its deficits be invoked? Here the controversy is joined about whether self-psychology adds anything new to conflict theory, or whether it is really old wine in a new bottle. Certainly the early discussions of shame were from a classical, ego-psychological perspective. Piers (1953) defined shame as a reflection of tension between the ego and the ego ideal, representing a failure to attain a goal of the ego ideal. One manifestation of this tension is the negative comparison of the self and its body parts to others, which comparison leads to feelings of inferiority; the relationship of this perspective to Adler's (1907) "organ inferiority" is obvious. Feldman (1962) postulated a drive "to be the center of attention" (p. 382), analogous to Freud's thoughts about exhibitionism. Thus, Feldman introduced the role of external reality as a source of conflict with id drives leading to the shame response. As noted earlier, even Kohut (1971) originally viewed shame from the perspective of a conflict model, although shame was seen not as the result of the failure of the ego to meet the demands of a rigid ego ideal, but as the response of the ego flooded by unneutralized grandiosity, with the ego ideal standing as guardian and protector against grandiosity. Wurmser (1981) also considered an exhibitionistic drive as he underscored the relationship of shame to a defensive need to hide (a reaction formation) and its reflection in patients' feelings of unlovability. Annie Reich (1960) related shame to narcissism by noting oscillations of feelings from grandiosity to worthlessness and dejection, alternations that reflect wide shifts in self-esteem for certain narcissistic patients. Reich believed that objects are used by such patients in an attempt to stabilize self-esteem, leading to fluctuation of idealization and contempt toward self and others.

（2.）羞耻能否在结构（冲突-防御）心理学[structural (conflict-defense) psychology]的框架内得到充分理解，抑或必须诉诸一种更广泛的自体心理学[psychology of the self]及其缺陷？这里存在的争议是：自体心理学[self-psychology]究竟为冲突理论增添了新内容，还是 merely 旧酒装新瓶。诚然，早期对羞耻的讨论采取的是经典的、自我心理学[ego-psychological]视角。皮尔斯[Piers]（1953）将羞耻定义为自我与自我理想之间张力的反映，代表未能达到自我理想之目标。这种张力的一种显现是：把自体及其身体部位与他人做负性比较，这种比较导致自卑感；这一视角与阿德勒（1907）的"器官自卑"之间的关联显而易见。费尔德曼[Feldman]（1962）假设了一种"成为注意中心"的驱力（p. 382），类似于弗洛伊德关于露阴的想法。于是，费尔德曼引入了外部现实作为与本我驱力冲突的来源，从而导致羞耻反应。如前所述，即便科胡特（1971）最初也是从冲突模型的视角看待羞耻，尽管羞耻不被视为自我未能满足刚性自我理想要求的结果，而是自我被未中性化的夸大[unneutralized grandiosity]淹没的回应——自我理想在此充当抵御夸大的守护者。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）也考虑了一种露阴驱力，同时强调羞耻与防御性隐藏需求（一种反应形成）的关系，以及它在病人"不可爱[unlovability]"感受中的反映。安妮·赖希[Annie Reich]（1960）通过注意到从夸大至无价值与沮丧[dejection]的情感摆荡，把羞耻与自恋联系起来；这种摆荡反映了某些自恋病人自尊[self-esteem]的剧烈波动。赖希认为，这些病人利用客体来稳定自尊，从而导致对自体与他人的理想化[idealization]与轻蔑[contempt]的波动。

Clearly, then, the consideration of shame by many psychoanalytic thinkers has been rooted in the structural model, but, I suggest, these formulations are limited in their scope. A different, wider pathway to viewing shame derives from Erikson's (1950) elaboration of autonomy and shame and doubt. Lynd (1958) related shame to the search for identity, and Lewis (1971) further contended that shame reflects feelings about the whole self. Thus, with a consideration of identity and the self as central to the experience of shame, I believe that the analytic emphasis shifted from structural conflict to problems of narcissism. Certainly there remains much disagreement within the psychoanalytic community about the relationship of conflict and drives to narcissism (Morrison, 1986a), but most people would agree that some construct of "self" is essential in considerations about narcissism. It is a major contention of this book that shame is a crucial dysphoric affect in narcissistic phenomena. If this is so, can shame best be explained within the framework of drive-defense (conflict) theory, or is a separate emphasis on the self and its vicissitudes necessary?

因此，许多精神分析思想家对羞耻的考量都植根于结构模型；但我认为，这些表述在范围上有限。另一条更宽广的羞耻视角之路来自埃里克森[Erikson]（1950）对自主[autonomy]与羞耻和怀疑[shame and doubt]的阐发。林德[Lynd]（1958）把羞耻与身份[identity]追寻联系起来；刘易斯（Lewis, 1971）进一步主张，羞耻反映的是关于整个自体的感受。于是，随着身份与自体被视作羞耻经验的核心，我相信分析的强调重点从结构冲突转向了自恋问题。精神分析共同体关于冲突与驱力同自恋之关系仍有许多分歧（Morrison, 1986a），但大多数人会同意：某种"自体"构念对于考量自恋是必不可少的。本书的一个主要论点是：羞耻是自恋现象中一种关键的痛苦情感[dysphoric affect]。若果真如此，羞耻是能在驱力-防御（冲突）理论的框架中得到最佳解释，还是必须单独强调自体及其变迁[vicissitudes]？

Kohut (1966) introduced the concept of a separate developmental line for narcissism, but even in 1971 he was attempting to work within the parameters of conflict-drive theory. However, in his paper on narcissistic rage (Kohut, 1972), he moved away from the concept of drives toward a view of rage as a response to narcissistic injury. I have already noted that Kohut continued to view shame exclusively from the perspective of overwhelming grandiosity or empathic failure by selfobjects, minimizing the relationship of failure in relation to an ego ideal. The challenge to integrate an ego ideal (or ideal self) concept into a psychology of shame and self will be evident throughout this volume. I will try to show, however, that the more recent papers on shame have included a perspective on narcissism, identity, and the self (for example, Thrane, 1979; Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983). So, we must continue to evaluate the usefulness of competing frameworks in the integration of shame and narcissism-a conflict-drive theory or a theory that incorporates the self and its vicissitudes.

科胡特（1966）引入了自恋的单独发展线[separate developmental line]概念；但即便在1971年，他仍试图在冲突-驱力理论的参数内工作。然而，在他关于自恋性暴怒[narcissistic rage]的论文中（Kohut, 1972），他逐渐脱离驱力概念，转向把暴怒视为对自恋损伤[narcissistic injury]的回应。我已经指出，科胡特继续只从压倒性夸大或自体客体[selfobjects]共情失败[empathic failure]的视角看待羞耻，从而最小化了与自我理想相关的失败关系。把自我理想（或理想自体[ideal self]）概念整合进羞耻与自体心理学的挑战，将在本书中随处可见。不过，我将试图说明，近期关于羞耻的论文已纳入对自恋、身份与自体的视角（例如Thrane, 1979; Broucek, 1982; Kinston, 1983; Morrison, 1983）。因此，我们必须继续评估竞争性框架在整合羞耻与自恋方面的用处——是冲突-驱力理论，还是一种纳入自体及其变迁的理论。

Further, we must explore whether these theories have to be seen as mutually exclusive, or whether they can usefully be integrated. Our consideration of shame, then, arrives at the heart of one of the most engrossing issues facing psychoanalysis today: the relationship between intrapsychic conflict and self-deficit.

此外，我们必须探讨这些理论是否必须被视为相互排斥，抑或可以被有益地整合。于是，我们对羞耻的考量触及了今日精神分析最引人入胜的问题之一：内在心理冲突[intrapsychic conflict]与自体缺陷[self-deficit]之间的关系。

(3.) Can shame arise in isolation as an internal experience (as a manifestation of intrapsychic/exhibitionistic drives, grandiosity, or failure with regard to a goal or ideal), or must it be seen in a social context (that is, as a product of comparison with others, the presence of an audience, or as a reflection of active shaming, leading to feelings of inadequacy, inferiority, or unlovability)? I believe that this question is encompassed even in Freud's preliminary observations about shame (for example, 1896, 1898, 1900). For instance, if shame is viewed as a reaction formation against an exhibitionistic drive-an urge to "show off," to display—it is an intrapsychic defense mechanism. On the other hand, exhibitionism itself implies an audience, a social group of one or more who view what is being exhibited. Wurmser (1981) emphasized the visual element of shame which, again, calls attention to the viewing "other." He also equated shame with a feeling of "unlovability," underscoring the interpersonal context of shame. Nathanson (1987) asserted that with shame, there is always a shamer. I have suggested earlier that shame was underemphasized in psychoanalytic writings because it was viewed as essentially social and environmental, and thus less relevant to intrapsychic processes. Even Lewis (1971), while relating shame to the whole self, viewed it in terms of field dependency, which she equated with social investment and attachment.

（3.）羞耻能否作为孤立的内化经验而产生（作为内在心理/露阴驱力、夸大，或相对于某一目标或理想的失败的显现），抑或必须被置于社会情境[social context]中看待（亦即，作为与他人比较、有观众在场、或主动被羞辱[active shaming]的反映，从而导致无能、自卑或不可爱的感受）？我相信，这一问题甚至已包含在弗洛伊德关于羞耻的初步观察中（例如1896, 1898, 1900）。例如，若羞耻被视为对露阴驱力——一种"炫耀"、展示的驱力——的反应形成，它便是一种内在心理性防御机制。另一方面，露阴本身意味着观众，意味着一个或多个观看被展示之物的人的社会群体。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）强调了羞耻的视觉元素，这再次使人注意到观看的"他者[other]"。他还将羞耻等同于"不可爱"的感受，从而凸显了羞耻的人际情境[interpersonal context]。纳桑森[Nathanson]（1987）主张，只要有羞耻，就总有羞辱者[shamer]。我早些时候提出，羞耻在精神分析著作中被低估，是因为它被视为本质上是社会性的、环境性的，因而与内在心理过程不那么相关。即便刘易斯（Lewis, 1971）在把羞耻与整个自体联系起来时，也是从场依存[field dependency]的角度看待它，她将其等同于社会投入与依恋[attachment]。

Alternatively, shame may be viewed as an affective response to the self alone, the self experienced relatively independent of its social matrix. For instance, while identity formation certainly involves identifications and object internalizations, the emphasis is on a core experience of selfhood, an orientation inward that can, I suggest, be separated from a social context. Shame reflecting failure of the self to attain the goals of its ego ideal can be seen as essentially intrapsychic and nonsocial, representing internalized ideals that are the products of a relatively well-formed self.

另一种可能是，把羞耻视为对独自存在的自体的情感性回应——这种自体经验相对独立于其社会矩阵。例如，虽然身份形成当然涉及认同[identifications]与客体内化[object internalizations]，但重点在于一种核心的自体性[selfhood]经验，一种可以与社会情境分离的内向取向。羞耻反映自体未能达到其自我理想的目标，这可以被视为本质上是内在心理性、非社会性的，代表内化了的理想，这些理想是相对成形良好的自体的产物。

This view of shame as an internal, intrapsychic manifestation underscores shame's relationship to narcissism, and particularly to the contributions of Kohut. One of the major issues in the study of narcissism has been that of the relationship of the object to narcissistic phenomena—that is, to what degree is functional impairment of the self independent of configurational objects and the interpersonal surround? Kohut (1966) took an extreme position on this question, suggesting that object relations theory is essentially a "social psychology" and that narcissism has a developmental line independent of object love. Thus, grandiosity to Kohut-and the shame that results from it—may be totally independent of a social context and is a truly intrapsychic phenomenon. The Kohutian concept of the selfobject is relevant here in defining whether shame occurs intrapsychically (in social isolation) or whether, indeed, it functions within the broader matrix of self-selfobject relations. This matter will be explored in chapter 5. I believe that Kohut's perspective on grandiosity differs from Freud's concept of exhibitionism, which requires the presence of a viewing or experiencing other and hence is more interpersonal.

这种把羞耻视为内在、内在心理性显现的观点，凸显了羞耻与自恋、尤其是与科胡特贡献的关系。自恋研究中的主要问题之一是客体与自恋现象的关系——也就是说，自体的功能损害在多大程度上独立于构型性客体[configurational objects]和人际环境[interpersonal surround]？科胡特（1966）在这一问题上采取了极端立场，提出客体关系理论本质上是一种"社会心理学"，而自恋有一条独立于客体爱[object love]的发展线。因此，对科胡特而言，夸大——以及由此产生的羞耻——可能完全独立于社会情境，是真正内在心理性的现象。科胡特式的自体客体[selfobject]概念在此处相关：它界定了羞耻是内在心理性地发生（在社会孤立中），还是确实在自体-自体客体关系[self-selfobject relations]的更广阔矩阵中运作。这一问题将在第5章探讨。我认为，科胡特关于夸大的视角不同于弗洛伊德的露阴概念，因为后者需要观看或体验着的他者的在场，因而更具人际性[interpersonal]。

So, to Kohut, grandiosity reflects a developmental failure of self-cohesion from inadequate selfobject mirroring, and the shame it engenders (by threatening to overwhelm the self) is essentially independent of a social context. I will develop this theme in chapter 5, but here I suggest simply that failure to reach the goals and values of the ego ideal (or, as I prefer to view it, the ideal self) also generates shame and that this shame reflects both internal and nonsocial, as well as interpersonal elements.

因此，对科胡特而言，夸大反映的是自体凝聚[self-cohesion]的发展性失败，源于不充分的自体客体镜映[selfobject mirroring]；而它引发的羞耻（通过威胁淹没自体）本质上独立于社会情境。我将在第5章展开这一主题，但在此我只想简单指出：未能达到自我理想的目标与价值（或者，如我倾向于看待的，理想自体）同样会产生羞耻；而这种羞耻既反映内在的、非社会的元素，也反映人际性元素。

We have, then, contrasting perspectives on shame with regard to its social (interpersonal) or internal (intrapsychic) context. That shame can be experienced interpersonally is beyond doubt. The real question is whether or not shame can exist independent of a social context. I have implied above that, indeed, it can be; this question will recur throughout the remainder of our study.

于是，我们有了关于羞耻之社会（人际）或内在（内在心理）情境的对照视角。羞耻可以人际性地被体验，这一点毋庸置疑。真正的问题是：羞耻能否独立于社会情境而存在？我在上文已暗示， indeed 它可以；这一问题将在我们研究的余下部分反复出现。

(4.) What is the relationship of shame to guilt? Are they separate affects, or are they inexorably intertwined through an inevitable cycle or alternation? Which is the more primitive affect, and which lends itself more easily to psychotherapeutic intervention? I have noted that Freud turned from elaboration of the ego ideal toward the structural theory and the superego, and, with this orientation, to an emphasis on guilt in response to oedipal conflict. Piers (1933) differentiated guilt from shame by relating guilt to transgression of a superego barrier, leading to the danger of annihilation and dismemberment (castration). Shame, on the other hand, results from a tension between the ego and ego ideal, reflecting failure to meet a goal of the ego ideal, which failure leads to a threat of rejection and abandonment. Thrane (1979) reiterated the relationship of shame to identity, and hence to the experience of the self (or "failure of the whole self," as Lewis [1971, p. 40] has phrased it). Guilt over an action, a transgression, can be felt abstractly, as "alien" to the self; by contrast, shame always has an immediate, personal, searing quality. Wurmser (1981) emphasized that guilt is always a reflection of action toward, "hurting," another, and thus refers to an expression (and abuse) of power whereas shame reflects a failure of the self, and thus an absence, a withholding of power.

（4.）羞耻与内疚[guilt]的关系是什么？它们是分离的情感，还是通过不可避免的循环或交替而不可避免地交织在一起？哪一种更原始，哪一种更易于心理治疗干预？我已指出，弗洛伊德从自我理想的阐发转向结构理论与超我，并在此取向中强调对俄狄浦斯冲突的内疚回应。皮尔斯（Piers, 1933）通过把内疚关联于对超我屏障的逾越，将其与羞耻区分开来；这种逾越带来湮灭与肢解（阉割[castration]）的危险。另一方面，羞耻源于自我与自我理想之间的张力，反映未能达到自我理想的目标；这种失败导致被拒绝与抛弃的威胁。斯兰恩[Thrane]（1979）重申了羞耻与身份的关系，从而也与自体经验（或如刘易斯[Lewis, 1971, p. 40]所言的"整个自体的失败"）相关。对某一行为的内疚，一种逾越，可以被抽象地感受，对自体而言是"异己的[alien]"；相比之下，羞耻总具有直接、个人、灼痛的特质。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）强调，内疚总是对指向、"伤害"他人的行动的反映，因而指涉权力的一种表达（及滥用）；而羞耻反映的是自体的失败，因而是一种缺席，一种权力的 withholding。

What is the developmental difference between guilt and shame? Which is the earlier to develop, the more primitive, the less differentiated? Most authors consider shame to be developmentally the earlier affect, with guilt requiring the evolution of a differentiated superego for its full expression. Tomkins (1962-63) described shame as one of the nine affects present at birth. By contrast, guilt was described as a more complex feeling, differentiated out of the earlier shame matrix. This perspective is logical developmentally, in that it emphasizes an early experience of the "self-as-entity," with later differentiation of self from other, and triadic differentiation awaiting the development of oedipal configurations and the superego. However, this viewpoint does not imply that primitive shame is necessarily replaced by guilt. In fact, Wurmser (1981) stated that "the complex affect of shame-in contrast to shame anxiety, its core-always involves the superego" (p. 73).

内疚与羞耻的发展性差异是什么？哪一个更早出现、更原始、分化更少？大多数作者认为羞耻在发展上更早，而内疚则需要分化的超我演化成熟后才能充分表达。汤姆金斯（Tomkins, 1962-63）把羞耻描述为出生即具有的九种情感之一。相比之下，内疚被描述为一种更复杂的感受，从更早的羞耻基质[shame matrix]中分化出来。这一视角在发展逻辑上是合理的：它强调早期"作为实体的自体[self-as-entity]"经验，随后自体与他者的分化，以及等待俄狄浦斯构型与超我发展的三元分化[triadic differentiation]。然而，这一观点并不意味着原始羞耻必然被内疚取代。事实上，乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）指出："羞耻的复杂情感——与其核心，羞耻焦虑相对——总是涉及超我"（p. 73）。因此，原始羞耻（或羞耻焦虑）会演化为一种倾向，随着超我的发展以及自我理想（或理想自体）的精制，产生更分化的羞耻。这一演化与内疚的产生和精制同时发生，但相互独立。

Thus, primitive shame (or shame anxiety) evolves into a propensity for more differentiated shame with superego development and refinement of the ego ideal (or ideal self). This evolution of shame implicitly occurs independent of, but concomitantly with, the generation and refinement of guilt.

因此，原始羞耻（或羞耻焦虑）随着超我的发展以及自我理想（或理想自体）的精制，演化为一种产生更分化之羞耻的倾向；羞耻的这一演化虽然与内疚的产生和精制同时发生，却独立于后者。

But are shame and guilt so easily differentiated from one another? Piers (1953) described a guilt-shame cycle as a useful conceptualization by which guilt and shame interact and can be interchanged. According to Piers, an id impulse (that is, libidinal or aggressive) is inhibited (guilt), leading to passivity (shame), and subsequent overcompensation and reactive impulse expression, which again leads to guilt and inhibition of the impulse. Thus, the two affects can be integrated, and their mutual (or simultaneous) expression understood, minimizing the reductionistic tendency in attempting to keep them entirely separate.

然而，羞耻与内疚是否如此容易被区分？皮尔斯（Piers, 1953）描述了一种内疚-羞耻循环[guilt-shame cycle]，作为有用的概念化：内疚与羞耻相互作用、可以互换。根据皮尔斯，一种本我[id]冲动（即力比多或攻击性的）被抑制（内疚），导致被动性（羞耻），随后出现过度补偿[overcompensation]与反应性冲动表达，而这又再次导致内疚与对冲动的抑制。因此，两种情感可以被整合，它们相互（或同时）的表达可以被理解，从而减少试图将二者完全分离的还原论倾向。

Wurmser (1981, p. 206) spoke of a "dialectic" between shame and guilt, in which the two affects oscillate rapidly, reflecting an action and its inhibition. I (Morrison, 1984a) have suggested that shame in neurotic patients frequently reflects inhibition of competitive, oedipal strivings out of guilt and feared retaliation, in favor of less threatening passivity and the resultant shame (see also chapter 10).

乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981, p. 206）谈到了羞耻与内疚之间的"辩证法[dialectic]"：两种情感迅速摆荡，反映一个行动及其抑制。我（Morrison, 1984a）提出，神经症病人的羞耻常常反映了对竞争性、俄狄浦斯性追求的抑制——这种抑制出于内疚与对报复的恐惧，转而选择威胁较小的被动性，以及由此产生的羞耻（亦见第10章）。

There seems to be, then, a meaningful difference between shame and guilt and a potential for an oscillating dialectic between them in clinical situations. Hartmann and Lowenstein (1962), however, doubted this distinction (see chapter 2, this volume). Nonetheless, shame appears to predominate in narcissistic states, whereas guilt is more clearly experienced in the neuroses. This contrast will inform a more detailed examination of shame in chapters 9 and 10.

于是看来，羞耻与内疚之间存在着有意义的差异，并且在临床情境中二者之间还可能存在一种摆荡的辩证法[dialectic]。然而，哈特曼与洛伊恩施泰因（Hartmann and Lowenstein, 1962）怀疑这一区分（见本书第2章）。尽管如此，羞耻似乎在自恋状态中占主导，而内疚则在神经症[neuroses]中被更清楚地体验到。这一对照将为第9、10章中对羞耻更细致的考察提供线索。

(5.) Is shame a manifestation of passivity, dependency, and, therefore, of inherent femininity (as suggested by Freud, and by several recent authors), or does this formulation reflect an outmoded notion of femininity? Shame is a reflection of feelings about the whole self in failure, as inferior in competition or in comparison with others, as inadequate and defective. As such, shame relates to low self-esteem, to faulty identity-formation. Just as guilt is a manifestation of harmful action, a transgression, shame can be experienced as a result of inaction, of passivity. Lewis (1971, 1987) equated shame with passivity, femininity, and what she had studied as "field dependence"—that is, individual concern about the surround, with self-esteem dependent on feedback from the social environment. Guilt, on the other hand, tends more to be a function of activity, masculine identification, and "field-independence" (that is, independence of the social context).

（5.）羞耻是被动性、依赖性的显现，因而也是内在女性气质[inherent femininity]的显现吗（如弗洛伊德及若干晚近作者所暗示的）？抑或这一表述反映的只是一种过时的女性气质观念？羞耻是对作为失败者的整个自体的感受的反映——在竞争中、在与他人的比较中显得低劣，显得不充分而有缺陷。如此，羞耻关系到低自尊[low self-esteem]与有缺陷的身份形成[faulty identity-formation]。正如内疚是有害行动、是一种逾越[transgression]的显现，羞耻也可以作为不行动、作为被动性的结果而被体验到。刘易斯（Lewis, 1971, 1987）把羞耻等同于被动性、女性气质与她所研究的"场依存[field dependence]"——亦即个体对周遭的关切，其自尊依赖于来自社会环境的反馈。另一方面，内疚则更多地是行动、男性认同[masculine identification]与"场独立[field-independence]"（亦即独立于社会情境）的功能。

While the sources of shame in passivity-and in feelings of inferiority, inadequacy, deficiency, and low self-esteem—are clear and consonant with the view of shame I have been developing, what is to be made of the equation of these qualities with femininity? Historically, Freud (1933) early equated shame with femininity and with genital inferiority-that issue (i.e., penis envy) which has been heartily contested by students of female development. Freud stated. "Shame, which is considered to be a feminine characteristic par excellence but is far more a matter of convention than might be supposed, has as its purpose, we believe, concealment of genital deficiency. We are not forgetting that at a later time shame takes on other functions" (p. 132). In so stating, Freud indicated that this equation of shame with femininity is "a convention" and that it later "takes on other functions." Freud might well have elaborated on these points, but here he was certainly equating shame with female genital inferiority.

虽说羞耻源于被动性——以及自卑、不充分、缺陷与低自尊之感——这一点清晰且与我一直在发展的羞耻观相一致，但该如何看待把这些特质等同于女性气质？从历史上看，弗洛伊德（Freud, 1933）很早就将羞耻等同于女性气质与生殖器自卑[genital inferiority]——那便是"阴茎嫉羡[penis envy]"这一议题，它已被女性发展[female development]的研究者们大力质疑。弗洛伊德说道："羞耻被视为一种卓越的女性特征，但它远比人们所设想的更像是一种习俗[convention]；我们相信，它的目的在于遮掩生殖器的缺陷。我们不会忘记，在稍后的时期羞耻会承担其他功能"（p. 132）。在这样说时，弗洛伊德指出，把羞耻等同于女性气质是一种"习俗"，并且它在后来"承担其他功能"。弗洛伊德本可以就这些要点进一步展开，但在此他确实把羞耻等同于女性的生殖器自卑。

Anthony (1981) spoke of secondary shame in women, which produces passivity, secretiveness, and a predominantly preoedipal cast to their analyses, as a result of prolonged and complex attachment to the mother. He suggested that the "identity theme" imprinted on the girl is one of shame, leading to prolonged analyses of paranoia and depression which relate to shame. Lewis (1981) also discussed shame as a field-dependent mode of functioning that predominates in women, because of their unchanging, same-sex anaclitic identifications. She also related shame to depression, a conclusion with which I agree. Lewis suggested that the superego of narcissistic patients is shame prone, representing the relation of the self to another in unrequited love and reflecting attempts to restore these lost attachments.

安东尼（Anthony, 1981）谈到女性的次级羞耻[secondary shame]：由于与母亲持久而复杂的依恋，它带来被动性、隐秘性，并使她们的分析主要带有前俄狄浦斯[preoedipal]的色调。他提出，印刻在女孩身上的"身份主题[identity theme]"是一种羞耻，导致与羞耻相关的偏执[paranoia]与抑郁[depression]的长期分析。刘易斯（Lewis, 1981）也把羞耻讨论为一种场依赖的运作模式，这种模式在女性中占主导，因为她们有着恒定不变的、同性的依附性认同[anaclitic identifications]。她同样把羞耻与抑郁联系起来，这一结论我表示同意。刘易斯提出，自恋病人的超我是易羞耻[shame prone]的，它代表自体与另一个处于单恋[unrequited love]中的人的关系，并反映着试图修复这些失落依恋[attachments]的努力。

Thus, for these authors, the connection between femininity and shame goes beyond Freud's "genital inferiority" hypothesis and relates more to feminine concern with intimacy, attachments, and object relatedness. This approach seems to reflect Wurmser's (1981) view of shame's relationship to feelings of "unlovability," as well as to Gilligan's (1982) work on female moral development. I think that current changes in women's views of themselves should have significant impact on field dependence and self-esteem, and undoubtedly will influence these factors in relation to shame. Thus, Freud's (1933) comment about the "matter of convention" takes on added significance in contemporary society. In addition, I believe that the equation of activity, field independence, and guilt with maleness is an oversimplification. I am not impressed that the majority of shame experiences are to be found in women, as suggested by Anthony (1981), but I will attempt to demonstrate that shame-in relation to narcissistic phenomena and retreat from oedipal and sexual feelings-exists equally strongly in men and women. Similarly, as men experience elements of themselves as "feminine"—needing intimacy and attachment, recognizing and expressing feelings in general-they are likely to struggle with pangs of shame.

因此，对这些作者而言，女性气质与羞耻之间的联系超越了弗洛伊德的"生殖器自卑"假说，更多地关系到女性对亲密[intimacy]、依恋[attachments]与客体关系性[object relatedness]的关切。这一进路似乎既反映了乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）关于羞耻与"不可爱[unlovability]"感受之关系的看法，也关系到吉利根（Gilligan, 1982）关于女性道德发展[female moral development]的研究。我认为，女性对自我看法当前的改变，理应对场依存与自尊产生显著影响，并且无疑会影响这些因素与羞耻的关系。因此，弗洛伊德（1933）关于"习俗之事[matter of convention]"的评论，在当代社会中获得了额外的意义。此外，我相信，把行动、场独立与内疚等同于男性气质，是一种过度简化[oversimplification]。我并不认同如安东尼（Anthony, 1981）所暗示的那样——多数羞耻经验存在于女性身上；但我将试图表明，羞耻——就其与自恋现象及从俄狄浦斯与性感受[sexual feelings]中的退避[retreat]的关系而言——在男女身上同样强烈地存在。同样地，当男性体验到自身某些元素为"女性化的[feminine]"——需要亲密与依恋、普遍地识别并表达感受——他们也很可能要与阵阵羞耻感[pangs of shame]相挣扎。

(6.) What is the relationship of shame to anger/rage, contempt, envy, vulnerability, and humiliation: Before we consider these affects and feelings about the self, it should be noted that shame itself can be expressed as several related feelings, such as mortification, humiliation, despair, remorse, apathy, embarrassment, and lowered self-esteem. Some authors believe that these represent separate feelings.

（6.）羞耻与愤怒/暴怒[anger/rage]、轻蔑[contempt]、嫉妒[envy]、脆弱性[vulnerability]与羞辱[humiliation]的关系是什么？在考察这些情感与关于自体的感受之前，应当指出：羞耻本身可以表现为若干相关的感受，例如屈辱[mortification]、羞辱、绝望[despair]、懊悔[remorse]、淡漠[apathy]、尴尬[embarrassment]与降低的自尊[lowered self-esteem]。有些作者认为这些代表分离的感受。

I suggest, however, that they each express some element of shame, with variations in the object, aim, or intensity of the affective experience. In subsequent discussions, it will be seen that different patients may experience one or another of these shame equivalents as being most descriptive of their own feeling state.

然而，我主张它们各自表达了羞耻的某种元素，所不同的只是情感性经验[affective experience]的对象、目的或强度。在后续的讨论中将会看到，不同的病人可能把这一或那一"羞耻等价物[shame equivalents]"体验为对自身感受状态最贴切的描述。

I believe that underlying many expressions of rage is a feeling of shame-a feeling that reflects a sense of failure or inadequacy so intolerable that it leads to a flailing out, an attempt to rid the self of the despised subjective experience. Kohut's (1972) view of narcissistic rage as a response of the vulnerable patient to narcissistic injury—echoing a need for absolute control over an archaic environment-is relevant here. Kohut's formulation of rage, and its contrast with that of Kernberg (1975), will be discussed later. For now, however, it is worth considering the possibility that shame's role in generating rage has been overlooked because of the tendency to hide from shame in the transformation of passive to active, from self-hatred to outward expression of hatred and anger. I differ from Kohut in that I believe that rage frequently reflects shame over self-failure and inferiority, not only as a response to injury from another (for example, from the selfobject).

我相信，在许多暴怒的表达之下，潜藏着一种羞耻感——这种感受反映着一种失败或无能[inadequacy]感，它如此难以忍受，以至于导致一种猛烈的发作[flailing out]，试图把自体从那被鄙视的主观经验中摆脱出来。科胡特（Kohut, 1972）把自恋性暴怒[narcissistic rage]视为脆弱的病人对自恋损伤[narcissistic injury]的回应——呼应着对一种古老环境[archaic environment]之绝对控制的需要——在此是相关的。科胡特对暴怒的表述，以及它与科恩伯格（Kernberg, 1975）观点的对比，将在后文讨论。不过眼下，值得考虑这样一种可能性：羞耻在生成暴怒中的作用被忽视了，因为我们倾向于从羞耻中隐藏自身——在从被动到主动、从自我憎恨[self-hatred]到向外表达憎恨与愤怒的转化中。我与科胡特的不同在于：我相信暴怒常常反映了关于自我失败与自卑的羞耻，而不仅仅是对来自他者（例如来自自体客体[selfobject]）之损伤的回应。

Contempt represents, I suggest, an attempt to "relocate" the shame experience from within the self into another person, and, thus, like rage, it may be an attempt to rid the self of shame. Wurmser (1981) stated that "contempt [is] the form of aggression intrinsic to shame affects" (p. 18). I argue (chapter 7) that this attempt to reestablish self-esteem through relocation of shame into contempt for others occurs through the process of projective identification. Nathanson (1987) referred to this process as "affect broadcasting." In essence, subjective shame, over failure, inferiority, or defect, is disavowed or repressed and is "placed" into another, who must then "accept" and "contain" the projection. Therefore, the object of contempt must bear a certain similarity to the subject, at least with respect to the source of the projected shame, and must also be willing to interact with the subject on the basis of the implicit contempt. From this perspective, then, contempt can be seen as an interactive externalization of the shame experience, and thus, ultimately, as a defense against it.

我主张，轻蔑代表了一种把羞耻经验从自体内"重新安置[relocate]"到另一个人身上的尝试；因此，如同暴怒一样，它也可能是一种把自体从羞耻中摆脱出来的尝试。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981）指出："轻蔑[是]内在于羞耻情感[shame affects]的攻击性形式"（p. 18）。我主张（第7章）：这种通过把羞耻重新安置为对他人的轻蔑以重建自尊的尝试，是经由投射性认同[projective identification]的过程发生的。纳桑森（Nathanson, 1987）把这一过程称为"情感广播[affect broadcasting]"。本质上，关于失败、自卑或缺陷的主观羞耻被否认[disavowed]或压抑，并被"放置"进另一个人，而后者必须"接受"并"容纳[contain]"这一投射。因此，轻蔑的对象必然与主体带有某种相似，至少就所投射之羞耻的来源而言；并且还必须愿意基于那隐含的轻蔑与主体互动。由此视角出发，轻蔑便可被视为羞耻经验的一种互动性外化[externalization]，因而最终是一种针对它的防御。

Shame vulnerability, like shame anxiety, is a sensitivity to, and readiness for, shame. As Levin (1967) has shown, shame anxiety is a signal of impending shame, evoking the defenses of hiding and withdrawal to protect against imminent rejection and abandonment. Wurmser (1981, pp. 82-84) has made a similar point. Levin elaborated his views on shame and shame anxiety from the perspective of ego psychology. I suggest that shame vulnerability is closely related to narcissistic vulnerability and, again, underscores the inevitable relationship of shame to narcissism. Narcissistic vulnerability is the "underside" of exhibitionism, grandiosity, and haughtiness—the low self-esteem, self-doubt, and fragility of self-cohesion that defines the narcissistic condition. I believe that shame is the principal ubiquitous affect that accompanies and defines that condition. Shame vulnerability is, then, the readiness and sensitivity to experience shame and is a frequent affective counterpart of narcissistic vulnerability. Just as shame anxiety relates to intrapsychic conflict and structural theory, shame vulnerability represents an affective component of narcissism and its clinical manifestations.

羞耻脆弱性[shame vulnerability]，如同羞耻焦虑，是对羞耻的敏感与就绪[readiness]。正如莱文（Levin, 1967）所示，羞耻焦虑是迫近之羞耻的信号，它唤起隐藏与退缩[withdrawal]的防御，以抵御迫在眉睫的拒绝与抛弃。乌尔姆泽（Wurmser, 1981, pp. 82-84）提出了类似的观点。莱文从自我心理学[ego psychology]的视角详述了他关于羞耻与羞耻焦虑的看法。我主张，羞耻脆弱性与自恋脆弱性[narcissistic vulnerability]密切相关，并再次凸显了羞耻与自恋之间不可避免的关系。自恋脆弱性是露阴、夸大与傲慢[haughtiness]的"背面[underside]"——正是那种低自尊、自我怀疑[self-doubt]与自体凝聚[self-cohesion]之脆弱性，界定了自恋状态。我相信，羞耻是伴随并界定这一状态的主要的、无处不在的情感[ubiquitous affect]。于是，羞耻脆弱性便是体验羞耻的就绪与敏感，并且是自恋脆弱性频繁的情感对应物。正如羞耻焦虑关系到内在心理冲突与结构理论，羞耻脆弱性代表了自恋及其临床显现[clinical manifestations]的一种情感性成分。

Finally, humiliation deserves a special place in this overview of shame. Humiliation specifically reflects the social, interpersonal manifestation of shame, or internalized representation of the "humiliator." In the presence of humiliation, there must be someone who humiliates or who has, at some time, humiliated. Humiliation is that manifestation of shame which is the product of action perpetrated against the self by someone else. As I have noted elsewhere (Morrison, 1984a), "... humiliation represents the strong experience of shame reflecting severe external shaming or shame anxiety at the hands of a highly cathected object 'a significant other' (p. 488). Rothstein (1984) has made a similar point with regard to humiliation. Embarrassment is another manifestation of shame in a social context, but it differs from humiliation in intensity and in the activity necessary for its generation. Embarrassment is a less searing, less intense form of shame that is more readily acceptable to most people and that, therefore, may more readily be acknowledged in psychotherapy or analysis. Also, to experience embarrassment, there need not necessarily be an "embarrasser," but rather the presence at least of a social context, one or more observers of the self in weakness or failure.

最后，羞辱[humiliation]在羞耻的概览中值得占有一个特殊的位置。羞辱特别反映着羞耻的社会性、人际性显现，或"羞辱者[humiliator]"的内化表征[internalized representation]。在羞辱出现之处，必然有某个人在羞辱，或在某个时刻曾羞辱过。羞辱是这样一种羞耻显现：它是他人对自体所施加行动[action]的产物。正如我在别处所指出的（Morrison, 1984a）："……羞辱代表着强烈的羞耻经验，它反映着严重的外部羞辱，或在一位高度投注[highly cathected]的客体——'一个重要的他者[significant other]'——手中的羞耻焦虑"（p. 488）。罗斯坦（Rothstein, 1984）就羞辱提出了类似的观点。尴尬是羞耻在另一种社会情境中的显现，但它在强度与生成所必需的行动方面不同于羞辱。尴尬是一种不那么灼痛、不那么强烈的羞耻形式，对大多数人更易被接受，因而也更易在心理治疗或分析中得以承认。此外，要体验尴尬，未必需要一个"尴尬者[embarrasser]"，而只需要至少存在一个社会情境——一个或更多注视着处于软弱或失败中的自体的观察者。

Embarrassment underscores the special relationship of the visual—seeing and being seen-to shame. Alternatively, Miller (1985) has related embarrassment to the emergence of hidden sexual longings.

尴尬凸显了视觉——观看与被观看——与羞耻之间的特殊关系。另者，米勒（Miller, 1985）把尴尬与隐秘性渴望[hidden sexual longings]的浮现联系起来。

This discussion of humiliation and embarrassment calls attention again to the question of the "social embeddedness" of the shame experience. As noted earlier, many authors believe that shame occurs only in an interpersonal context, as the reflection of an active shamer or observer, or representing the danger of "unlovability" or abandonment by a significant object who views the personal shortcomings. On the intrapsychic, internal experience. Of course, internalization of objects and their representations in the formation of the ego ideal and ideal self, along with the need for the selfobject function in self-development, ultimately puts these intrapsychic structures into an interpersonal, or intersubjective, framework. But this is true as well for all human development, including the identifications that generate the superego and lead ultimately to guilt as well as to shame, with inevitable reflections of an object-filled environment.

关于羞辱与尴尬的讨论，再次提请我们注意羞耻经验之"社会嵌入性[social embeddedness]"的问题。如前所述，许多作者相信羞耻只发生在人际情境中，作为主动的羞辱者或观察者的反映，或代表来自一位重要客体[significant object]的"不可爱"或抛弃的危险——这位客体注视着个人的缺陷[shortcomings]。就内在心理的、内在的经验而言。当然，客体及其表征在自我理想与理想自体形成中的内化，连同自体发展过程中对自体客体功能[selfobject function]的需要，最终把这些内在心理结构置入一种人际的、或主体间性[intersubjective]的框架。但这对于一切人类发展同样是成立的，包括那些生成超我、并最终导向内疚与羞耻的认同[identifications]，它们不可避免地反映着一个充满客体的环境[object-filled environment]。

(7.) Finally, what is the explicit relationship of shame to narcissism? I will attempt to examine this question thoroughly because I believe that shame and narcissism do coexist and define each other, emerging together in what I shall describe as the Dialectic of Narcissism (see chapter 4). I have already commented on shame in the context of narcissistic vulnerability and manic-depressive illness; on the importance of the ego ideal and self-regard—first articulated by Freud (1914)-to my conceptualization of shame; on the lack of psychoanalytic interest in both shame and narcissism until relatively recently; and on the relationship of shame to various experiences of defect in the self. Most recent works on shame have related it, in one way or another, to narcissistic phenomena. This is especially true of the work of Kohut (1971, 1977), who suggested a separate line of narcissistic development and for whom shame represented experiences of overwhelming grandiosity and repeated selfobject failure to meet (age-appropriate) self-needs. (These points will be covered in detail in chapters 4 and 5.).

（7.）最后，羞耻与自恋的明确关系是什么？我将试图彻底考察这一问题，因为我相信羞耻与自恋确实共存并相互定义，一同浮现于我将称之为"自恋的辩证法[Dialectic of Narcissism]"的现象中（见第4章）。我已经评论过：在自恋脆弱性与躁郁性疾患[manic-depressive illness]背景中的羞耻；自我理想与自重[self-regard]（最早由弗洛伊德[Freud, 1914]阐明）对我的羞耻概念化的重要性；精神分析对羞耻与自恋二者直至相对晚近都缺乏兴趣；以及羞耻与自体种种缺陷[defect]经验的关系。近期关于羞耻的著作大多以这样或那样的方式把它与自恋现象联系起来。科胡特（Kohut, 1971, 1977）的著作尤其如此——他提出了自恋发展的独立线索[separate line]，并且对他而言，羞耻代表着压倒性夸大[overwhelming grandiosity]的经验，以及反复的自体客体失败——即未能满足（与年龄相适的）自体需要[self-needs]。（这些要点将在第4、5章详细论述。）

I implied in question 4 that shame may be experienced alone, in isolation, as an intrapsychic rather than an interpersonal phenomenon and that, as such, it touches on a core of narcissism. This relationship between shame and narcissism seems firmly established, but whether shame is the sole cause of narcissistic distress must remain an open question at this time. To phrase it differently, can there be narcissism or its manifestations without the presence of shame? Might not other dysphoric affects—for example, depression, anxiety, or guilt—be at the center of narcissistic distress?

我在问题4中暗示，羞耻可以单独地、孤立地被体验，作为一种内在心理的而非人际的现象，并且正因如此，它触及了自恋的一个核心。羞耻与自恋之间的这种关系似乎已牢固确立，但羞耻是否自恋性困扰[narcissistic distress]的唯一原因，此时仍须作为一个开放的问题。换一种说法：能否存在没有羞耻在场的自恋或其显现？难道其他痛苦情感[dysphoric affects]——例如抑郁、焦虑或内疚——就不可能处于自恋性困扰的中心吗？

SUMMARIZING REMARKS（总结性评论）

In this introductory overview, I have considered some of the broad themes that recur throughout the discussions that follow. These themes fall into place as contrasts and ambiguities that have accompanied previous writings on shame and that reflect subtleties inherent in it. Is shame a defense, or is it an affect? Can it be understood within the framework of structural (conflict-defense) psychology, or is a theory of the self and developmental arrest more useful? Can shame arise exclusively as an intrapsychic phenomenon, or must a social context be invoked to explain its presence? Does shame differ from guilt, and, if it does, how do they relate to one another? Is shame related to passivity, dependency, and femininity? From another perspective, might "femininity" be viewed as reflecting an outmoded "convention," as implied even by Freud (1933)? I have suggested a relationship between shame and several other key phenomena, including rage, contempt, shame vulnerability, humiliation, and, especially, narcissism. In addition to these guiding themes and questions, our perspective on shame inevitably brings into focus some of the major issues currently confronting psychoanalytic psychology. These include, most importantly, whether or not Kohut's contributions and the self-psychological perspective are a significant addition to classical psychoanalytic theory, or whether these observations can better be integrated into existing theory. What is the role of the drives (including exhibitionism) in human existence? Can an individual ever be viewed primarily as an isolated organism, or must he or she inevitably be seen in a broader social context? What are the implications of shame for our current interest in narcissism? Finally, what are the implications for treatment in this emphasis on shame? More pointedly, has shame been overlooked in traditional work with patients, and, if so, how can this failure be addressed?

在这篇导论性概览中，我考量了一些贯穿后续讨论的宽泛主题。这些主题作为对比与含混[ambiguities]而各就各位，它们伴随着先前关于羞耻的著述，并反映了内在于其中的微妙之处。羞耻是一种防御，还是一种情感？它能在结构（冲突-防御）心理学[structural (conflict-defense) psychology]的框架内得到理解，还是一种自体理论与发展停滞[developmental arrest]更有用？羞耻能否仅作为内在心理现象而出现，抑或必须诉诸社会情境来解释其存在？羞耻与内疚是否不同，若是，它们彼此如何关联？羞耻是否与被动性、依赖性与女性气质相关？换个视角，"女性气质"是否可被视为反映着一种过时的"习俗"，正如弗洛伊德（1933）本人也暗示过的？我已提出羞耻与若干其他关键现象之间的关系，包括暴怒、轻蔑、羞耻脆弱性、羞辱，尤其是自恋。除了这些引导性主题与问题，我们对羞耻的视角不可避免地使精神分析心理学当前所面对的一些主要议题成为焦点。其中最重要的包括：科胡特的贡献与自体心理学视角究竟是对经典精神分析理论的重大增补，还是这些观察能更好地被整合进既有理论。驱力（包括露阴）在人类生存[human existence]中扮演什么角色？个体能否主要被视作孤立的有机体[organism]，抑或他/她不可避免地要被置于更广阔的社会情境中看待？羞耻对我们当前对自恋的兴趣意味着什么？最后，这种对羞耻的强调对治疗[treatment]意味着什么？更尖锐地说，在传统的与病人的工作中，羞耻是否已被忽视，若是，这一失败又该如何弥补？

Obviously, I raise some of these questions rhetorically. I believe that shame has conspicuously been overlooked and underemphasized, and I shall try to demonstrate how it can be, and ought to be, worked with clinically. I also believe that Kohut's contributions are significant additions to the classical perspective. Many of the other questions remain ambiguous, recurring throughout the subsequent chapters of our study of shame—the underside of narcissism.

显然，我提出其中某些问题时用的是修辞[rhetorically]的口吻。我相信，羞耻已被显著地忽视与低估[overlooked and underemphasized]，并且我将试图表明它如何能够、也应当被运用于临床工作。我也相信，科胡特的贡献是对经典视角的重要增补。其余许多问题仍然含混不清，并将在我们关于羞耻——自恋的背面[underside of narcissism]——的研究之后续各章中反复出现。
