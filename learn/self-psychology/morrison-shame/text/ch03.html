第一部分　A Theoretical Framework（理论框架）· 部分导言

Andrew P. Morrison · 第一部分　A Theoretical Framework（理论框架）· 部分导言 · pp. 19–20 · 5 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

In this section, I shall consider some closely related areas of psychoanalytic theory and their ultimate relationship to shame. An integration of previous psychoanalytic contributions will provide a framework for our understanding of shame.

在本部分中，我将探讨精神分析理论中若干密切相关的领域，以及它们与羞耻感[shame]的终极关联。对以往精神分析贡献的整合，将为我们理解羞耻感提供一个框架。

The theoretical issues to be considered include creations and fantasies of the ideal (and the structures that "contain" them—the ego ideal and ideal self, primitive object representations and object relations; and narcissism. We will see that shame plays a varyingly important role in the thinking of the authors to be reviewed.

所要考察的理论议题包括：理想[ideal]的创生与幻想，以及"承载"这些理想的结构——自我理想[ego ideal]与理想自我[ideal self]、原始客体表征[primitive object representations]与客体关系[object relations]；还有自恋[narcissism]。我们将看到，羞耻感在诸位将被评述的作者的思想中扮演着不同程度的重要角色。

I will be building on their formulations to show that shame is the central response to failure with respect to the ideal, to flaws in the experience of self. Failures in early object relationships—either because of active, humiliating attacks or as a result of disruptions in empathic attunement by the significant selfobject—lead to shame sensitivity in the later construction of the self.

我将在他们的理论阐述基础上进一步说明：羞耻感是对"相对于理想而言的失败"、对"自体[self]体验中的缺陷"的核心反应。早期客体关系[object relationships]的失败——无论是由于主动的、羞辱性的攻击，还是由于重要自体客体[selfobject]的共情协调[empathic attunement]受到破坏——都会在后来的自体建构中导致羞耻敏感性[shame sensitivity]。

These considerations inevitably generate formulations about narcissism, which will be examined throughout this volume but are the explicit focus of chapters 4 and 5. Narcissism has been viewed in different ways by the authors to be considered, but, I suggest, we can organize these various approaches in terms of dialectics: between feelings of inferiority and (defensive) grandiosity, between the ideal of autonomy and self-sufficiency and merger with the fantasied, omnipotent "other." Kohut (1966, 1971) suggested a separate line of self-development for narcissism, independent of that of object love and the ego ideal, which relies heavily on the sustaining function of the (mirroring and idealizable) selfobjects. As we progress, I will try to demonstrate that shame is the principal experienced affect of narcissism, from whichever perspective the dialectic is approached.

这些思考必然引发关于自恋的种种理论阐述；自恋将在全书各处被检视，但第4章和第5章将对其进行专门讨论。诸位将被评述的作者以不同方式看待自恋，但我建议，我们可以用一系列辩证[dialectics]来组织这些不同进路：自卑感[inferiority]与（防御性的）夸大[grandiosity]之间的辩证，自主与自足的理想与「融入幻想的全能[omnipotent]他者[other]」之间的辩证。科胡特[Kohut]（1966, 1971）提出自恋有其独立的自体发展[self-development]脉络，独立于客体爱[object love]与自我理想[ego ideal]的发展；它严重依赖（镜映[mirroring]与可理想化的[idealizable]）自体客体[selfobjects]的维系功能。随着论述推进，我将尝试论证：无论从辩证的哪一端切入，羞耻感都是自恋最主要的主观体验情感[affect]。

This review of previous writings on the ideal, primitive object relationships and narcissism will provide the foundation for a discussion of shame informed by clinical perspectives. I will try to relate my understanding of shame to the work of earlier contributors. In Part II, I will offer my own understanding of shame's varied clinical manifestations (including its relationship to resistance and defense) and its relevance to manic-depressive psychosis and will present clinical material on the treatment of shame in neurotic and narcissistic patients.

这些关于理想、原始客体关系与自恋的既往文献回顾，将为一种带有临床视角的羞耻感讨论奠定基础。我将尝试把自己的羞耻感理解与前人的工作联系起来。在第二部分中，我将阐述自己对羞耻感多种临床表现[clinical manifestations]的理解（包括其与阻抗[resistance]和防御[defense]的关系），以及它与躁郁性精神病[manic-depressive psychosis]的相关性，并呈现关于神经症性[neurotic]与自恋性[narcissistic]病人羞耻感治疗的临床材料。
