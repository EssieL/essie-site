Acknowledgments（致谢）

I want to express special thanks to my editors and colleagues at The Analytic Press, who have been helpful, encouraging, and supportive throughout the process of preparing this manuscript. My thanks and admiration go especially to Dr. Paul E. Stepansky, who, as Editor-In-Chief, has set a tone of scholarly professionalism, quiet encouragement, and the rigorous ideal of preparing the best manuscript I could manage. Making helpful and challenging suggestions from the beginning, he also expected that I would strive and work to clarify, structure, and improve the several drafts I sent his way.

我要特别感谢 The Analytic Press 的编辑与同事们，他们在本书稿的准备过程中始终给予我帮助、鼓励与支持。我的谢意与敬意尤其归于 Paul E. Stepansky 博士；作为主编[Editor-In-Chief]，他树立了学术性的专业精神、不动声色的鼓励，以及"尽我所能交出最佳文稿"这一严格理想之风。从一开始，他就提出有益且具挑战性的建议，并期望我努力澄清、组织并改进我呈给他的几版草稿。

Also helpful at TAP was Eleanor Starke Kobrin, whose careful manuscript editing assisted enormously in shaping my thoughts and lending some grace to craggy and obscure phrases. She also advised and "problem-solved" on many matters. Through the good offices of TAP, Dr. Joan Lang read various versions of the manuscript and helped me to deepen and clarify concepts and the shape of their presentation. Kathryn M. Scornavacca has been most responsive and professional in preparing the final format, design, and anticipated marketing approach of the book as we have moved along in production. To these capable and friendly women I extend my gratitude and respect. Mr. Lee Fischer, of Fine Print in Cambridge, was exacting and professional in helping with the final tasks of proofreading and preparation of the indices.

Eleanor Starke Kobrin 在 TAP 同样帮了大忙，她细致的书稿编辑极大地帮助我把想法成形，也让一些崎岖而晦涩的句子多了几分优雅。在许多事情上，她也为我出谋划策、排忧解难。通过 TAP 的安排，Joan Lang 博士阅读了本书稿的多个版本，帮助我深化并澄清概念，也理顺了它们呈现的形态。Kathryn M. Scornavacca 在本书付梓过程中，于最终版式、设计与预期的营销方案上始终反应迅速、专业尽责。对这些能干而友善的女性，我致以感激与敬意。Lee Fischer 先生供职于剑桥的 Fine Print，他在校对与索引编制的收尾工作中一丝不苟、非常专业。

Thanks too to Shirley Allison for her endless revisions at the Compaq.

也要感谢 Shirley Allison，她在 Compaq 上做了无数遍修订。

Along the way, many others have helped as I struggled to clarify and present my ideas about shame. I want to acknowledge and thank Dr. Michael Basch, who early invited me to Chicago to discuss my ideas with the self psychology study group; Dr. Arnold Goldberg, who made clear where my ideas deviated from those of Heinz Kohut and stimulated me to take responsibility for those differences; Dr. Jason Aronson, who first planted the idea that I write my own book on shame; Dr. Silvan Tomkins, who patiently and thoroughly corresponded about similarities and differences between our views on shame; and, finally, the members of the Eastern Division of the National Council on Self Psychology (in particular, Dr. Frank Lachmann), who helped me crystallize and elaborate ideas relating shame and defense.

一路上，还有许多人在我努力澄清并呈现关于羞耻[shame]的想法时伸出援手。我要在此感谢 Michael Basch 博士，他很早就邀请我去芝加哥，与自体心理学[self psychology]研讨小组讨论我的想法；Arnold Goldberg 博士，他让我清楚看到我的想法在何处偏离了海因茨·科胡特[Heinz Kohut]的观点，并促使我为这些差异承担责任；Jason Aronson 博士，他最早种下我自己写一本关于羞耻的书的念头；Silvan Tomkins 博士，他耐心而详尽地与我通信，探讨我们关于羞耻的看法之间的异同；最后还有美国自体心理学委员会（National Council on Self Psychology）东区成员（特别是 Frank Lachmann 博士），他们帮助我把关于羞耻与防御[defense]之间关系的想法结晶成形并予以阐发。

I especially want to greet my analyst, to whom I returned for what I thought would be merely some "fine tuning." In fact, she provided a wise and affirming environment that enabled the unfolding of my own particular transferences; encouraged me as I pursued my own personal and theoretical thoughts about shame; and reminded me—with force, when necessary—of the importance as well of conflict and "The Oedipus." I feel sure that she knows of my gratitude.

我特别想向我的分析师[analyst]致意；我回到她那里，本以为只是做些"微调[fine tuning]"。事实上，她提供了一个睿智而肯定的环境，使我得以展开自己特有的移情[transferences]；鼓励我追寻关于羞耻的个人思考与理论思考；并在必要时有力地提醒我，冲突与"俄狄浦斯[The Oedipus]"同样重要。我确信她知道我怀有感激。

As Helen Block Lewis noted, there is a devoted group of us "shameniks" who tend to be, as we like to think, not only bright and articulate, but also uncommonly supportive of, and nice to, one another! I have had the good fortune to swap ideas with many of them, and I want to note here and express appreciation to this group of clinicians and theoreticians, who include: Frances Broucek, James Gilligan, Sidney Levin, Helen Block Lewis, Nancy Morrison, Donald Nathanson, John O'Leary, Silvan Tomkins, Fred Wright, and Leon Wurmser. Others, of course, have contributed to our understanding of shame, and I hope that they will forgive me for not including them in this list of people with whom I have communicated more directly. It is a club of which I am proud to be a member.

正如 Helen Block Lewis 所言，我们这群"shameniks"是一群热忱的人；我们愿意相信，自己不仅聪明而善辩，而且彼此间给予异乎寻常的支持，也对彼此十分友善！我有幸与其中许多人交换想法，在此我想向这群临床工作者与理论家们表达感谢，他们是：Frances Broucek、James Gilligan、Sidney Levin、Helen Block Lewis、Nancy Morrison、Donald Nathanson、John O'Leary、Silvan Tomkins、Fred Wright 和 Leon Wurmser。当然，还有其他人增进了我们对羞耻[shame]的理解，我希望他们能原谅我没有把他们都列入这份与我有过更直接交流的人的名单。能成为这个俱乐部的一员，我深感自豪。

Inevitably, writing a book is a difficult undertaking for a full-time clinician. People often ask, "Where did you find the time?" A good question. The answer, alas, is that the time comes out of evenings, weekends, and summers, many of which might have been spent relaxing with family and friends. For the many hours "stolen" from my family I want to express particular thanks (I have already expressed the apologies!) to my wife, Amy, and my daughter, Rachel. Their support, humor, and, at times, direct expression of frustration and annoyance have humanized this difficult task. I expect that now they will share with me the relief that it is finally over!

对一位全职临床工作者来说，写一本书不可避免地是一项艰难的工程。人们常问："你哪儿来的时间？"这是个好问题。可惜，答案是：时间来自夜晚、周末和夏天——那些本可以用来与家人朋友放松的时光。对于从家庭中"偷走"的许多时光，我要特别感谢（当然，我也已经道过歉了！）我的妻子 Amy 和女儿 Rachel。她们的支持、幽默，以及时而直接表达的沮丧与恼火，让这项艰巨的任务变得更有人情味。我相信，现在她们也会和我一样，为终于完成而感到如释重负！

Preface（前言）

As with most interests that captivate us, my curiosity about shame evolved from an attempt to make sense out of my own personal experience. I think that that experience first drew my attention to psychoanalysis and, within the analytic framework, to the differences between the writings and emphases of Freud and Alfred Adler. In high school I wrote my senior "source theme" on the development of psychoanalysis. In college, one of the most engrossing papers I toiled over was a comparison of Adler and Freud entitled, as I recall, "Adler and Freud: The Ego and Id of Psychoanalysis." My emphases would be different now, but I think—as I mention in the text of the present book—that I was responding to the sensitivity of Adler to issues relating to shame (and, incidentally, to their relative paucity in Freud's writings).

正如大多数吸引我们的事物一样，我对羞耻[shame]的好奇心源于试图理解自己的个人经验。我认为，正是那段经验最早把我引向精神分析[psychoanalysis]，并在分析框架[analytic framework]之内，引向弗洛伊德[Freud]与阿尔弗雷德·阿德勒[Alfred Adler]在著作与强调重点上的差异。高中时，我的毕业"主题论文[source theme]"写的就是精神分析的发展。大学时，我煞费苦心写过一篇最引人入胜的论文，比较阿德勒与弗洛伊德，题目我记得是《阿德勒与弗洛伊德：精神分析的自我与本我》[Adler and Freud: The Ego and Id of Psychoanalysis]。我现在的强调重点会不同，但我想——正如我在本书正文中提到的——我当时是在回应阿德勒对羞耻问题的敏感（顺带一提，也回应了弗洛伊德著作中对此相对稀少[relative paucity]的论述）。

Having acknowledged my recognition of shame sensitivity in my own personal makeup, I want to note the relative absence of discussion of shame in psychoanalytic writings prior to 1971 (with Helen Block Lewis's epic book). Since that time, and particularly in the last decade, papers and books have begun to appear about shame. It is as though this long-neglected affect has finally been taken "out of the closet" by psychoanalytically informed writers and therapists, after its neglect during the dominance of drive/defense psychology in traditional psychoanalytic thought. As I try to demonstrate in the body of this book, I believe that such a change is due to a broadening of psychoanalytic theory to include the study of narcissism and the self, and due as well to the assumption of a braver stance by therapists. This new stance allowed them to examine the searing, painful experience of shame, which goes beyond the more familiar guilt feelings of intrapsychic conflict. As it did with me, in my practice as psychoanalyst and therapist, work with shame may begin with encounters with patients, but it inevitably leads to a confrontation by the therapist with his or her own shame, which frequently follows different paths from those uncovered in personal analyses or therapies.

承认了自己个性构成中对羞耻的敏感[shame sensitivity]之后，我想指出，在 1971 年以前的精神分析文献中，对羞耻的讨论相对缺位（Helen Block Lewis 的那部史诗般的著作除外）。自那时起，尤其在过去十年里，关于羞耻的论文与书籍开始涌现。仿佛这种长期被忽视的情感[affect]终于在精神分析取向[psychoanalytically informed]的写作者与治疗师手中被"带出衣柜[out of the closet]"，而此前它在传统精神分析思想中驱力/防御心理学[drive/defense psychology]占主导地位时期一直备受忽视。正如我试图在本书主体中展示的，我相信这种变化源于精神分析理论的拓宽——把自恋[narcissism]与自体[self]的研究纳入其中；也源于治疗师采取了更为勇敢的立场。这种新立场使他们能够审视羞耻那种灼痛而痛苦的经验，它超出了人们所熟悉的内在心理冲突[intrapsychic conflict]带来的内疚感。正如它曾发生在我身上一样，在我作为精神分析师与治疗师的实践中，对羞耻的工作或许始于与病人的接触，却不可避免地引向治疗师与自己羞耻感的对峙，而这条路径往往与个人分析或个人治疗中所揭示的路径有所不同。

It seemed for me personally a daunting experience to contemplate writing my own book, and I gave the matter serious consideration before beginning such an undertaking. At first, I planned to collect some previously published papers on shame, writing some pithy introductions, and leaving elaboration of the subject to the voices of others. As a matter of fact, I interrupted the process of work on this book to edit such a volume of published papers about narcissism, in part, I think, to move away from the challenge of committing my own voice to the subject of shame. However, as Nathanson notes in his Preface to The Many Faces of Shame, each approach is somewhat different from the other—capturing a piece of the elephant—leaving the author confused and vaguely dissatisfied with the completeness of his own discussion. Some of the papers on shame that caused such vague feelings of incompleteness were my own, but I feel that my understanding of shame has become clearer and been sharpened as I have thought about and responded to the challenge of preparing this book.

对我个人而言，想到要写一本自己的书似乎是一项令人生畏的经历，在开始这样一项事业之前，我认真考虑过这件事。起初，我打算收集一些已发表的关于羞耻的论文，写几篇精辟的导言，把对这一主题的进一步阐发留给其他人的声音。事实上，我曾中断本书的写作过程，去编辑一部关于自恋[narcissism]的已发表论文集，部分原因——我想——是为了回避把自己的声音献给羞耻这一主题的挑战。然而，正如 Nathanson 在他为《羞耻的多重面貌》[The Many Faces of Shame]所作的前言中所说，每一种方法彼此之间都有些不同——就像盲人摸象，各执一端——这让作者对自己讨论的完整性感到困惑与隐约不满。那些引起这种不完整感的羞耻论文中，有一些正是我自己的；但我觉得，随着我对筹备这本书的挑战进行思考与回应，我对羞耻[shame]的理解已变得更清晰、更锐利。

In a work about an affective experience like shame, it is not easy to determine how much attention should be paid to clinical theory. However, as I became interested in shame, I searched for relevant theoretical discussions and found these to be few. Many of the readings were interesting although not directly related to the theory of shame, but my conceptualizations about shame began to come together around these preliminary theoretical readings. In fact, my own introduction to self psychology and the work of Kohut came about through my investigation of shame. As I proceeded to work on early drafts of this book, I determined that a focus on, and a review of, relevant theoretical perspectives was necessary to provide a lens through which to view and consider the clinical manifestations of shame.

在一本关于像羞耻[shame]这样的情感性经验[affective experience]的著作中，要确定应对临床理论给予多少笔墨并不容易。然而，随着我对羞耻产生兴趣，我寻找相关的理论讨论，却发现为数甚少。许多读物虽然有趣，却并非直接与羞耻理论相关；但我关于羞耻的概念化[conceptualizations]开始在这些初步的理论阅读中逐渐成形。事实上，我正是通过对羞耻的探究，才得以接触自体心理学[self psychology]与科胡特[Kohut]的著作。随着我着手本书的早期草稿，我认定有必要聚焦并回顾相关的理论视角，以便为审视与思考羞耻的临床显现[clinical manifestations]提供一副透镜。

My conceptualization of the theory of shame boils down to three fundamental areas: those relating to the self, to the ideal, and to narcissism.

我对羞耻理论的概念化可归结为三个基本领域：与自体[self]相关的、与理想[ideal]相关的，以及与自恋[narcissism]相关的。

Although much has been written about these areas, many of the seminal papers speak not at all, or very little, about shame. Thus, I found that I had to piece together, for example, the relationships to shame of the ego ideal, of primary narcissism, of the hypothesized supraordinate self or selfobject, and of empathic failure. Other writings included shame in their discussions of these constructs, but sometimes in ways different from those in which I understood the relationship, sometimes almost parenthetically as they focused on other factors. It seemed to me that the shame literature had not clearly and systematically succeeded in relating relevant theoretical contributions about self, ideal, and narcissism to an understanding of shame.

尽管关于这些领域已经有很多著述，但许多开创性论文对羞耻[shame]要么只字不提，要么极少提及。因此，我发现自己不得不把这些片段拼凑起来，例如：自我理想[ego ideal]、原发性自恋[primary narcissism]、假设性的上位自体[supraordinate self]或自体客体[selfobject]，以及共情失败[empathic failure]与羞耻之间的关系。其他著作在讨论这些构念[constructs]时也涉及了羞耻，但有时是以与我所理解的关系不同的方式，有时几乎是附带地、在关注其他因素时一笔带过。在我看来，羞耻文献尚未清晰而系统地把关于自体、理想与自恋的相关理论贡献同对羞耻的理解联系起来。

Part One of this book, then, is devoted to such a presentation. Throughout its first five chapters, I attempt to relate my own understanding of shame to Freud's thoughts about shame and narcissism; to those of the ego psychologists and their edification of the ego ideal; to primitive object relations and narcissism; and to later approaches to narcissism, ending with the thinking of Heinz Kohut. In the Introduction, I offer some guiding questions and issues to delineate our excursion into shame, and I attempt throughout to consider these issues as I annotate others' theoretical work. I try to present a roadmap to the understanding of where such thought leads in relation to our own considerations about shame.

本书第一部分[Part One]便致力于这样的呈现。在其前五章中，我试图把自己对羞耻[shame]的理解同弗洛伊德关于羞耻与自恋[narcissism]的想法联系起来；同自我心理学[ego psychology]家们及其对自我理想[ego ideal]的阐发[edification]联系起来；同原始客体关系[primitive object relations]与自恋联系起来；并与后来对自恋的诸种进路联系起来，最终落脚于海因茨·科胡特[Heinz Kohut]的思想。在导论[Introduction]中，我提出一些引导性问题与议题，以界定我们进入羞耻领域的探询；并在整本书中尝试在我评注他人理论工作的同时思考这些议题。我试图提供一张路线图[roadmap]，以理解这种思想会把我们关于羞耻的思考引向何方。

In Part Two, I turn to clinical matters, beginning with a case presentation and theoretical considerations deriving from ego psychology, object relations, and, finally, self psychology. I then review shame-related clinical manifestations, which are subsequently reframed as the shame defenses (that is, anger and rage, contempt and envy, and depression), or what Kohut has considered as adaptive defensiveness. The following chapters consider in greater detail shame in narcissistic characters, in more structured (neurotic) characters, and, finally, shame in manic-depressive psychosis.

在第二部分[Part Two]中，我转向临床议题，先从一个案例呈现[case presentation]与源自自我心理学[ego psychology]、客体关系[object relations]、最终是自体心理学[self psychology]的理论思考入手。随后我回顾与羞耻相关的临床显现[clinical manifestations]，它们后来被重新框定为羞耻防御[shame defenses]——亦即愤怒与暴怒[anger and rage]、轻蔑与嫉妒[contempt and envy]、抑郁[depression]——或者如科胡特[Kohut]所认为的适应性防御[adaptive defensiveness]。接下来的章节将更详细地讨论自恋型人格[narcissistic characters]中的羞耻、更为结构化的（神经症性的）人格[more structured (neurotic) characters]中的羞耻，最后是躁郁性精神病[manic-depressive psychosis]中的羞耻。

In the Epilogue, I return full circle to a more anecdotal, expository example of an imaginary experience which I, as author, might have experienced; let me here acknowledge that any relation of this "shame fantasy" to the writing of the present book is far from imaginary! This final chapter also considers shame in the broader contexts of philosophy and literature, societal issues, suicide, and the differences between men and women. More, I am sure, could have been included, but other authors will have to respond as I have to the incompleteness and to questions arising from my particular vision of shame in comparison with their own. I invite you, the reader, then, to follow my review of previous theoretical considerations as they relate to shame; of clinical examples of shame and its treatment in patients with varying clinical conditions; and of clinical factors that relate to, or defend against, shame. I hope that you will find this journey worthwhile and stimulating and that you will recognize elements of shame that relate to your own clinical work with patients and, inevitably, to your own experience.

在尾声[Epilogue]中，我将回到起点，转向一个更具轶事性、说明性的例子：一个作为作者的"我"可能经历过的想象性经验；在此我要承认，这个"羞耻幻想[shame fantasy]"与本书写作之间的任何关联都绝非想象！这最后一章还会把羞耻[shame]置于更广阔的背景中考察：哲学与文学、社会议题、自杀，以及男女差异。我确信还可以纳入更多内容，但其他作者也将不得不像我一样，回应这种不完整，以及回应我的羞耻观与他们的观点相比较时所产生的问题。那么，我邀请你——读者——跟随我回顾那些与羞耻相关的理论思考；回顾不同临床状况下羞耻及其治疗的临床案例；以及回顾那些与羞耻相关、或防御羞耻的临床因素。我希望你会觉得这段旅程值得且富有启发，并能在其中认出与自己同病人的临床工作、乃至不可避免地与你自身经验相关的羞耻元素。
