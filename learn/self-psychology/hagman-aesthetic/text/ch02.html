The Philosophy of Aesthetic Experience（审美体验的哲学）

Western philosophers and psychologists have always been interested in art, the appreciation of art, and the psychology of artists. Plato argued that aesthetic experience involved the apprehension of the good in nature. The nonsensuous pleasure, perhaps awe, of aesthetic feeling resulted from the reflection of an ideal form in the object (a flower, a mountain glade, a beautiful face, a painting) that only hinted at the profound perfection and beauty of its higher model. For Plato, it was the apperception of this hint, the glimpse of the ideal, which was the source of aesthetic experience.

西方哲学家与心理学家历来对艺术、艺术鉴赏[the appreciation of art]以及艺术家的心理感兴趣。柏拉图[Plato]主张，审美体验[aesthetic experience]涉及对自然中"善"[the good]的领会。审美感受[esthetic feeling]中那种非感官性的愉悦[nonsensuous pleasure]——或许是敬畏[awe]——源自理想形式[ideal form]在对象（一朵花、一片山林空地、一张美丽的脸、一幅画）中的映现，而这对象仅仅暗示了其更高范本[higher model]那深沉的完美与美。对柏拉图而言，正是对这种暗示的统觉[apperception]、对理想的一瞥，构成了审美体验的源头。

Later, religious thinkers believed that aesthetic experience was linked to the revelation of divinity in the world, the sense of worldly beauty reflecting the eternal beauty of God. Like Plato's views was the belief that some objects, most especially art, expressed God's love and perfection more than others – or at least, the divinity was more easily glimpsed in some objects than in others. It was not until the eighteenth century that a true psychology of aesthetic experience began to emerge. Starting with David Hume and Immanuel Kant, these modern thinkers tried to explain aesthetic experience in psychological terms. The objective nature of "the good" and "the beauty of God" came to be replaced by psychological processes by which our experience of the world is given aesthetic qualities and values.

后来，宗教思想家相信审美体验与神性[divinity]在世界中的启示[revelation]相关联，尘世之美感映照着上帝永恒的至美。与柏拉图观点相似的一种信念是：某些对象——尤其是艺术——比其它对象更能表达上帝的爱与完美；或者至少，神性在某些对象中比在其它对象中更容易被瞥见。直到十八世纪，一门真正的审美体验心理学才开始浮现。以休谟[David Hume]与康德[Immanuel Kant]为开端，这些近代思想家试图以心理学的方式解释审美体验。"善"的客观本质与"上帝之美"逐渐被心理过程所取代——正是经由这些过程，我们对世界的经验才被赋予审美的性质与价值。

Hume (1775) argued that aesthetic experience was associated with sensitivity to the association between a perception and a feeling. The aesthetic feelings were those of refined pleasure, delight, awe, admiration, joy, and so on – in other words, affects and passions considered to be of special, positive value. Hume believed that certain types of experiences, those possessing beauty, attained higher qualities in the formal expression of these feelings. Thus, for Hume, human sensibility and emotion replaced divinity and ideal form as the basis for aesthetic experience.

休谟（1775）主张，审美体验与对"知觉[perception]和感受[feeling]之间联想[association]"的敏感性有关。审美的感受是那些精致的愉悦[refined pleasure]、欣喜[delight]、敬畏[awe]、赞赏[admiration]、喜悦[joy]等等——换言之，是被视为具有特殊、正面价值的情感[affects]与激情[passions]。休谟相信，某些类型的体验——那些拥有美的体验——在这些感受的形式表达[formal expression]中达到了更高的品质。因此，对休谟而言，人的感受性[sensibility]与情感[emotion]取代了神性与理想形式，成为审美体验的基础。

Art, as opposed to natural sources of beauty, expressed certain associated feelings in refined and highly valued ways. Hume argued that a person could develop his or her critical judgment in aesthetic matters by means of experience and study. He also stressed the need for the audience member to keep "his mind free from all prejudice and allow nothing to enter into consideration but the very object that is submitted to his examination" (1775, pp. 114–115). Hume claimed that the audience must be comfortable and without other intentions when viewing something aesthetically. This was one of the initial arguments for the role of "disinterest" in aesthetic experience (this would become important to later aesthetic theory, especially for Kant).

与自然的美的来源相对，艺术以精致且被高度珍视的方式表达了某些相关联的感受。休谟主张，一个人可以借由经验与研习来培养自己在审美事务上的批判判断力。他还强调，观者需要保持"心灵不受任何偏见[prejudice]的束缚，除了呈交自己审视的那个对象本身，不让任何别的东西进入考量"（1775, pp. 114–115）。休谟主张，观者在以审美方式观看某物时，必须是自在的、且不带其它意图。这是关于"无利害"[disinterest]在审美体验中作用的最初论证之一（这一点对后来的美学理论日益重要，尤其对康德而言）。

What Hume was describing was a type of empathy, an ability to put aside one's normal position and needs and to "place oneself in that point of view that the artwork supposes" (p. 114). Thus, aesthetic experience assumed a special form of relationship with the object in which the audience members would approach the experience with benign neutrality and a willingness to give themselves over to the experience without prejudice. This relationship would then ideally result in a pleasant emotional state evoked by the specialness and refinement of the object.

休谟所描述的，是一种共情[empathy]，一种搁置自身惯常立场与需求、并"把自己置入艺术作品所设想的那种视角"的能力（p. 114）。因此，审美体验预设了一种与对象的特殊关系形式：观者以善意的中立[benign neutrality]与不带偏见地投入这一体验的意愿来接近它。这种关系在理想情形下会带来一种愉悦的情绪状态，由对象的独特性与精致所唤起。

Immanuel Kant (1790) postulated that aesthetic experience was a type of subjective judgment distinct from other human emotions, referring to this as taste. Essentially, taste was a type of universal and natural human capability like other modes of perception. As one experiences something aesthetically, there are sensations of pleasure within an attitude of disinterest. In fact, for Kant, taste was closer to reason than to emotion or sensation, it constituted recognition of a priori truths (such as beauty) in the concrete, "objective purposiveness" (1790, p. 187) of objects.

康德（1790）假定审美体验是一种有别于其它人类情感的主观判断，并将其称为鉴赏[taste]。本质上，鉴赏是一种普遍而自然的人类能力，如同其它知觉模式一样。当人以审美方式体验某物时，愉悦的感觉是在无利害[disinterest]的态度中产生的。事实上，对康德而言，鉴赏更接近理性[reason]而非情感或感觉；它构成了对具体对象中"客观合目的性"[objective purposiveness]（1790, p. 187）里先验真理[a priori truths]（诸如美）的确认。

This was Kant's attempt to separate out aesthetic taste from other emotions or sensations and to claim it as a primary form of mental experience. Thus, the source of aesthetic experience lay not in the enjoyment of the beautiful object, but in the recognition of the subjective knowledge of universal ideals. These ideals do not exist in some abstract realm (as with Plato) or in divinity (as with Augustine, for example), rather, they are a priori universal conceptualizations that form the basis of our rational judgments. For example, though we have all had innumerable experiences of individual men of various statures, sizes, and shapes, we also possess a mental model of man, the perfect, formal concept of the beautiful man. Aesthetic experience is based on this understanding of inner ideals. Thus, Kant attempted to develop a theory of aesthetic experience that was both human and rational, a form of perception that formed the very ground of rationality and judgment.

这是康德试图把审美鉴赏从其它情感或感觉中分离出来，并主张它是一种基本的心智体验形式。因此，审美体验的源头并不在于对美的对象的享受，而在于对普遍理型[universal ideals]的主观认识的确认。这些理型并不存在于某种抽象的领域（如柏拉图那样），也不存在于神性之中（例如如奥古斯丁[Augustine]那样），相反，它们是先验的普遍概念化，构成我们理性判断的基础。例如，尽管我们都有过无数次关于身材、体格、形态各异的个体的经验，我们同时也拥有一个关于人[man]的心智模型——关于美的那个完美而形式化的人的概念。审美体验就建立在这种对内在理型的理解之上。于是，康德试图发展出一套既属人又理性的审美体验理论，一种构成理性与判断之根基的知觉形式。

Hume and Kant were the most important contributors to the modern approach to aesthetics and the psychology of art. For our purposes, the key elements of their models can be summarized as follows: (1) the source of aesthetic experience is within the mind of the artist and audience, not inherent in the object, (2) the formal characteristics of the object are important to the extent that they evoke an inner knowledge or feeling associated with an idea or emotion, and (3) aesthetic experience involves an attitude of "disinterest," or distance, that is characterized by suspension of prejudice, psychological security, and the willingness to give oneself over to the experience of the object.

休谟与康德是近代美学进路与艺术心理学最重要的贡献者。就我们的目的而言，他们模型的关键要素可概括如下：（1）审美体验的源头在艺术家与观者的心灵之内，而非对象本身所固有；（2）对象的形式特征之重要，取决于它们能在多大程度上唤起与某个观念或情感相关联的内在认识或感受；（3）审美体验涉及一种"无利害"或距离的态度，其特征为偏见的悬置[suspension of prejudice]、心理安全感，以及把自己交付给对对象的体验的意愿。

These elements presupposed several assumptions that psychoanalysis would challenge. First, Kant believed that the educated and sensitive man can know his own mind and be fully aware of his feelings. Second, he saw an association between the representation and the concept that is open rather than defensively motivated. Third, he believed that disinterest is simply a suspension of prejudice rather than a complex process involving specific psychic mechanisms (e.g., sublimation).

这些要素预设了若干假设，而精神分析将会对此提出挑战。第一，康德相信，受过教育且敏感的人能够认识自己的心灵、并充分觉察自己的感受。第二，他认为表象[representation]与概念[concept]之间的联想是开放的，而非由防御所驱动。第三，他相信无利害仅仅是偏见的悬置，而非一个涉及特定心理机制（例如升华[sublimation]）的复杂过程。

The history of psychoanalytic aesthetics must be seen in the context of the theories promulgated by Hume and Kant and elaborated by their followers. Most importantly, psychoanalysis developed the notion of art and aesthetics as expression, as derivative of psychic concepts and specifically of unconscious fantasies. Freud saw aesthetic experience as equivalent in function to a neurotic symptom or the manifest content of a dream. The psychoanalytic approach to aesthetics was concerned with the symbolic nature of art and its instinctual sources rather than with the aesthetic experience itself.

精神分析美学的历史必须放在休谟与康德所提出、并由其追随者加以阐发的理论脉络中来理解。最重要的是，精神分析发展出了"艺术与美学即表达[expression]"的观念——它们是心理概念、特别是无意识幻想[unconscious fantasies]的衍生物。弗洛伊德把审美体验视为在功能上等同于神经症症状[neurotic symptom]或梦的显意[manifest content]。精神分析对美学的进路所关注的，是艺术的象征性质[the symbolic nature]及其本能来源[instinctual sources]，而非审美体验本身。

This symbolic/interpretive approach to aesthetics has dominated the psychoanalytic literature. However, recent authors have been interested in the primary function of aesthetic experience in addition to its defensive role. They argue that its true meaning and purpose are not merely determined by unconscious conflict, rather, there is a primary function of aesthetic experience, and one can trace its source to the earliest phases of human development.

这种对美学的象征性/解释性[symbolic/interpretive]进路，主导了精神分析文献。然而晚近的作者们除了关注审美体验的防御性作用外，也开始关注它的原初功能[primary function]。他们主张，审美体验的真正意义与目的并不单纯由无意识冲突所决定；相反，审美体验有一个原初功能，人们可以将其源头追溯至人类发展的最早阶段。

This chapter will explore and elaborate on this developmental approach. But first, considering our discussion of the development of the modern approach to psychological aesthetics, let me offer a brief definition of aesthetic experience that will be helpful in the discussion we will engage in over the next two chapters.

本章将探索并阐发这一发展性进路[developmental approach]。但首先，考虑到我们对心理美学[psychological aesthetics]的近代进路之发展的讨论，请允许我给出一个关于审美体验的简要定义，它将对接下来两章中我们所要展开的讨论有所助益。

Aesthetic experience involves a phenomenon (an object, event, sound, or other perception) or a set of phenomena (a group of objects, a sequence of events, a melody, or a complex structure of perceptions) that is/are felt to possess perfection or ideal form. Aesthetic experience is fundamentally subjective, but also grounded in the objective qualities of the object. It may include affects such as joy, sadness, wonder, and awe, or a calm sense of quiescence. The quality of self-experience is also part of it; the individual feels whole, vitalized, more positive, and closely engaged with the world.

审美体验涉及一个现象（一个对象、事件、声音或其它知觉），或一组现象（一群对象、一连串事件、一段旋律，或一种复杂的知觉结构），它们被感受为拥有完美[perfection]或理想形式[ideal form]。审美体验根本上是主观的，但也植根于对象的客观性质之中。它可能包含诸如喜悦、悲伤、惊叹[wonder]、敬畏等情感，或一种平静的寂静感[quiescence]。自我体验[self-experience]的品质也是它的一部分：个体感到完整、有活力、更为积极，并与世界紧密相连。

Furthermore, aesthetic experience varies in intensity. The tragic drama of King Lear is an unsurpassed aesthetic experience, an example of perfection and idealization. However, aesthetic experience also characterizes the enjoyment of a sunny day or the more mundane beauty of an oily puddle in a city street. While aesthetic experience may be passionate, it must also evoke feelings of safety for the observer. Consistent with Kant's viewpoint, aesthetic experience involves a balance between personal investment and emotional disinterest.

此外，审美体验在强度上各有不同。悲剧《李尔王》[King Lear]是一种无与伦比的审美体验，是完美与理想化[idealization]的一个例子。然而，享受一个晴朗的日子、或城市街道上一滩油污那种更为平凡的美，也属于审美体验。尽管审美体验可以是充满激情的，但它也必须唤起观察者的安全感。与康德的观点一致，审美体验涉及个人投入与情感无利害[emotional disinterest]之间的平衡。

In a broad sense, a person's aesthetic is the formal dimension of the unique way in which he or she experiences, responds to, and engages the world. It is that aspect of experience in which the quality of the formal organization of a person's relationship to an object, activity, or sensation is manifest. Aesthetic experience can occur in either the foreground or the background of subjectivity. In the background, it is the source of our sense of the world as having value and perhaps beauty. Otto Rank (1932) discussed how this ongoing, low level of aesthetic experiences helps us endure life's problems. When in the foreground, aesthetic experience is compelling; we experience ourselves as being in the presence of great truths and vivid realities. H. B. Lee (1948) described this level of aesthetic experience as “spiritual,” noting that the object is felt to be “divinely perfect and ideal” (p. 511). Alternation between foreground and background reflects the ongoing struggle to reconcile fantasy and reality, the ideal and the real, the archaic and the mature (Kainer, 1999).

从广义上说，一个人的审美[aesthetic]，是他或她体验、回应并投身世界的那种独特方式的形式维度。正是在经验的这一面向中，一个人与某个对象、活动或感觉之关系的形式组织[formal organization]的品质[quality]得以显现。审美体验既可以发生在主体性的前台[foreground]，也可以发生在后台[background]。在后台，它是我们感到世界具有价值、或许还有美的源头。奥托·兰克[Otto Rank]（1932）讨论了这种持续不断的、低水平的审美体验如何帮助我们承受生活中的种种问题。当处于前台时，审美体验是扣人心弦的；我们体验到自身正置身于伟大的真理与鲜活的实在面前。H. B. Lee（1948）把这一层次的审美体验描述为"灵性的"[spiritual]，指出对象被感受为"神圣般的完美与理想"（p. 511）。前台与后台之间的交替，映照着调和幻想与现实、理想与真实、古老[archaic]与成熟之间那持续不断的挣扎（Kainer, 1999）。

Freud viewed aesthetic experience as the sublimation of forbidden sexual desires, a displacement and transformation (desexualization or neutralization) of libido that, denied direct expression, is allowed discharge in alternative, culturally valued ways. Aesthetic pleasure is the result. The close link between art (a highly idealized cultural institution) and regressive processes and fantasies seems to support the sublimation approach. From this viewpoint, symbolism, a fundamental component of most forms of aesthetic expression, is the same process as that occurring in dreamwork, and thus it opens art to psychoanalytic interpretation.

弗洛伊德把审美体验视为对被禁的性欲望的升华[sublimation]，是力比多[libido]的一种移置[displacement]与转化（去性化[desexualization]或中和[neutralization]）：力比多被拒绝直接表达，却被允许以替代性的、具文化价值的方式获得释放。审美愉悦便是其结果。艺术（一种被高度理想化的文化建制）与退行过程[regressive processes]及幻想之间的紧密联系，似乎支持了升华这一进路。从这个观点看，象征化[symbolism]——大多数审美表达形式的基本组成——与梦的工作[dreamwork]中发生的过程是同一过程，因而它使艺术向精神分析的解释敞开。

For example, in several references, Sandor Ferenczi (1952) viewed aesthetic experience as arising from defenses against anal eroticism, the preoccupation with messy feces being transformed into structural elegance and formal beauty. Limiting themselves to the investigation of the symbolic nature of art, Ferenczi and Freud felt that the relation of analysis to art was interpretive, uncovering the unconscious meanings of imagery or the covert instinctual motivations of the artist. Freud did not identify any specific, defining process in aesthetic experience, focusing instead on the functions and mechanisms that it shared with other psychic processes. This approach, while illuminating the hidden meanings of art, also limited the explanatory use of drive psychology in understanding aesthetic experience – a point Freud (1914b) freely admitted. In fact, he remarked on several occasions that he could not appreciate art unless he could interpret its meaning. In his paper on the Moses of Michelangelo, he wrote:

例如，桑多尔·费伦齐[Sandor Ferenczi]（1952）在多处论述中把审美体验视为源于对肛门性欲[anal eroticism]的防御：对凌乱粪便的执念被转化为结构上的优雅[structural elegance]与形式之美。费伦齐与弗洛伊德局限于考察艺术的象征性质，认为分析[analysis]与艺术的关系是解释性的[interpretive]——揭示意象的无意识意义、或艺术家隐秘的本能动机。弗洛伊德并未在审美体验中辨识出任何特定的、界定性的过程，而是聚焦于它与其它心理过程所共有的功能与机制。这一进路虽然照亮了艺术的隐秘意义，却也限制了驱力心理学[drive psychology]在理解审美体验上的解释力——这一点弗洛伊德（1914b）坦率地承认。事实上，他多次提到，除非他能够解释艺术的意义，否则便无法欣赏它。在他关于米开朗基罗的《摩西》[Moses]的论文中，他写道：

I have often observed that the subject matter of works of art has a stronger attraction for me than their formal and technical qualities, though to the artist their value lies first and foremost in these latter. I am unable rightly to appreciate many of the methods used and the effects obtained in art. (p. 89)

我常常注意到，艺术作品的主题[subject matter]对我的吸引力，要强于其形式与技巧上的品质，尽管对艺术家而言，价值首先且首要地在于后者。对于艺术中所使用的许多方法以及所取得的效果，我无法恰当地欣赏。 （p. 89）

Freud further noted that he was deeply moved by some art (the Moses is one example), but only when he could explain its impact on him. On the other hand, music gave him no pleasure whatsoever. He noted that “some rationalistic, or perhaps analytic, turn of mind in me rebels against being moved by a thing without knowing why I am thus affected and what it is that affects me” (Freud, 1914b, p. 89).

弗洛伊德还指出，他会被某些艺术深深触动（《摩西》即是一例），但只在他能解释它对自己产生的影响时才会如此。另一方面，音乐则完全不能给他带来愉悦。他指出："我心中某种理性主义的、或许可称为分析的倾向，抗拒被某样东西所感动，却不明白我为何如此被触动、究竟是什么触动了我"（Freud, 1914b, p. 89）。

This raises a question: why is it that Freud could not appreciate either the formal quality of art or its content unless he could analyze it? I believe the answer is that aesthetic experience has its roots in early childhood experience (something that Freud did not emphasize) and that it involves a form of knowing that is nonrational and irreducible, at least according to the metaphysics of classical psychoanalysis. (See also Bollas [1978], for his discussion of aesthetic experience as part of the unthought known.)

这就引出一个问题：为什么弗洛伊德除非能够分析艺术，否则既无法欣赏其形式品质、也无法欣赏其内容？我相信，答案在于审美体验植根于早年的童年经验（这一点弗洛伊德并未强调），并且它涉及一种非理性的、不可化约的认知形式——至少按经典精神分析的形而上学[metaphysics]来说是如此。（另参见 Bollas [1978]，他把审美体验作为"未经思考的已知"[the unthought known]的一部分加以讨论。）

The classical approach to the psychology of aesthetic experience was primarily content oriented. That is, it considered the interpretation of the unconscious message as it is encoded in the imagery and expressed meanings of the artwork. The drive psychology model of aesthetic experience as sublimation also highlighted one of the primary functions of art – and certainly there is an affective, even passionate, aspect of most forms of artistic expression. But Freud's viewpoint failed to explore the crucial role of quality and form in aesthetic experience.

经典进路对审美体验心理学的处理，主要是内容取向的[content oriented]。也就是说，它考察的是对编码于艺术作品的意象[imagery]与所表达的意义之中的无意识讯息的解释。把审美体验视为升华的驱力心理学模型，也凸显了艺术的一项原初功能——而且，大多数艺术表达形式中确实存在着一种情感的、乃至激情的面向。但弗洛伊德的视角未能探索品质[quality]与形式[form]在审美体验中的关键作用。

In other words, Freud argued that the sublimating person channels instinctual drive energies through valued cultural channels, but he did not investigate the formal nature or quality of value that characterized those channels, the cultural forms themselves. If we believe that aesthetic experiences possess special qualities that make them distinct from other psychological phenomena (dreams, work, or play), I think that the most important area to consider is the nature of aesthetic form and the sources of the experience of quality or value that the forms seem to possess. When an artist expresses meaning through perfected cultural forms, or a person experiences a sunrise as extraordinarily beautiful, he or she is tapping into archaic aspects of human experience by which the formal aspects of our relationship to the world are felt to be both special and valuable.

换言之，弗洛伊德主张，进行升华的人把本能驱力能量疏导进有价值的文化渠道，但他并未探究那些渠道——即文化形式本身——所具有的形式本质或价值品质。如果我们相信审美体验拥有使其有别于其它心理现象（梦、工作或游戏）的特殊品质，那么我认为最值得考察的领域，就是审美形式的本质，以及这些形式似乎拥有的品质或价值经验的来源。当一位艺术家通过臻于完美的文化形式来表达意义，或当一个人把一次日出体验为非凡之美时，他或她正触及人类经验中的古老面向——借由这些面向，我们与世界之关系的形式方面被感受为既特殊又有价值。

From Sublimation to Regression（从升华到退行）

In 1961, Robert Fliess published an interesting elaboration of Freud's sublimation model of aesthetic experience. Fliess believed that in aesthetic experience, especially in the sense of beauty, there is a normal regression to an “early perceptory relation” involving the “modality of primary perception,” which he conceived of (after Rene Spitz) as the combined perceptions of the labyrinth – outer skin, hands, and mouth unified into a single, nondistinguishable experience.

1961年，罗伯特·弗利斯[Robert Fliess]发表了对弗洛伊德审美体验升华模型的一项有趣的阐发。弗利斯相信，在审美体验中——尤其在美感中——存在一种正常的退行[normal regression]，退行至一种"早期的知觉关系"[early perceptory relation]，涉及"原初知觉的模式"[modality of primary perception]；他（在勒内·斯皮茨[Rene Spitz]之后）将其构想为迷路[labyrinth]的诸知觉的联合——外皮、双手与口腔统一为一种单一而不可分辨的体验。

According to Fliess, in aesthetic enjoyment, there is a muscular discharge of neutral energy. Rather than aggressively cannibalizing the object, the person experiences an empathic introjection of it. Fliess located the developmental level of aesthetic experience in the first oral phase prior to the mobilization of aggression that results in incorporative actions and fantasies. He argued that the sublimation of this neutral oral libido is “inseparable from aesthetic enjoyment” and includes muscular discharges involved in the “modality of primary perception” – “a modality that continues to function throughout life” (1961, p. 283). Thus, for Fliess, sublimation is not simply a defense, but a normal aspect of the vicissitudes of libido that are essentially neutral during the first oral phase.

据弗利斯所言，在审美享受中，存在着中性能量[neutral energy]的肌肉性释放。人并非以攻击性地吞并对象的方式，而是体验到对对象的共情性内摄[empathic introjection]。弗利斯把审美体验的发展水平定位于第一个口腔期[oral phase]，即在攻击性被动员——并导致吞并性行动与幻想——之前。他主张，这种中性口欲力比多[neutral oral libido]的升华"与审美享受不可分离"，并且包含涉及"原初知觉的模式"的肌肉性释放——"一种终生持续发挥功能的模式"（1961, p. 283）。因此，对弗利斯而言，升华不单纯是一种防御，而是力比多变迁的一个正常面向，而这种力比多在第一个口腔期本质上是中性的。

It is the activity of this neutralized libido throughout life that accounts for the ubiquity of aesthetic experience. In an interesting series of case reports, Fliess illustrated the relationship between sex and aesthetic enjoyment, and even argued for the simultaneous experience of both in mature, healthy sexual relations.

正是这种被中性的力比多[neutralized libido]遍及一生的活动，解释了审美体验的无所不在。在一系列有趣的案例报告中，弗利斯阐明了性与审美享受之间的关系，甚至主张在成熟、健康的性关系中，二者是同时被体验的。

Space does not permit an in-depth discussion of Fliess's wonderful contribution. However, given that he did elaborate on Freud's model of aesthetic experience in several important ways, I will briefly touch on some of his points. Unlike Freud, whose reaction to art was by his own admission lukewarm at best, Fliess viewed aesthetic experience as a passionate sublimation. For him, neutralization does not take the physicality or drive out of the libido; it merely changes its aim. This helps to explain something that Freud's sublimation model failed to address: the frequently passionate nature of many aesthetic experiences.

篇幅不允许对弗利斯这一精彩的贡献展开深入讨论。然而，鉴于他确实在若干重要方面阐发了弗洛伊德的审美体验模型，我将简要触及他的某些观点。弗洛伊德对艺术的反应，按他自己的说法至多是不冷不热的；与之不同，弗利斯把审美体验视为一种充满激情的升华。对他而言，中和并未抽去力比多的身体性或驱力，而仅仅是改变了它的目标[aim]。这有助于解释弗洛伊德的升华模型未能处理的一点：许多审美体验往往具有的激情性质。

Although art is never equivalent to actual coital discharge, some successful and aesthetically perfect forms of beauty are frankly erotic and/or aggressive. Therefore, Fliess proposes that art as sublimation allows for a form of discharge regulation in which drive is channeled rather than simply disguised. This suggests that aesthetic experience may be linked to early vicissitudes of sexuality and aggression that are contained and expressed through ego mechanisms having more to do with containment, regulation, and expression than defense. In this light, Fliess viewed aesthetic experience not as a defense but rather as a normal regressive process having developmental sources in the early (nonaggressive) oral phase of the libido. He argued that aesthetic experience involves the multiple sensory modalities of interaction with the nursing mother – hand, eye, mouth, skin, and ear – all linked together in a sensory mosaic. In these ways, Fliess gave the drive perspective on aesthetic experience a developmental bent, but he continued to see it as essentially a regression and therefore did not elaborate a truly developmental approach to aesthetic experience.

尽管艺术从不等同于实际的性交释放，但某些成功的、在审美上完美的美的形式，坦率地说是色情的和/或攻击性的。因此，弗利斯提出，作为升华的艺术允许一种释放调节[discharge regulation]的形式，其中驱力是被疏导、而非仅仅被伪装。这表明审美体验可能与性和攻击性的早期变迁相关联，而这些变迁经由自我机制[ego mechanisms]被容纳与表达——这些机制更多地关乎容纳[containment]、调节与表达，而非防御。就此而言，弗利斯并不把审美体验视为一种防御，而视为一种正常的退行过程，其发展源头在于力比多的早期（非攻击性）口腔阶段。他主张，审美体验涉及与哺乳母亲互动的多重感觉模式[multiple sensory modalities]——手、眼、口、皮肤与耳——它们全部连结为一个感觉的马赛克[sensory mosaic]。以这些方式，弗利斯赋予关于审美体验的驱力视角一种发展性的倾向，但他仍继续把它基本上视为一种退行，因而并未阐发一种真正的发展性进路来理解审美体验。

As discussed later in this chapter, I strongly support the notion that aesthetic experience has its source in early infantile experience. One of the problems with regression models such as Fliess's is that they do not go far enough to explain the developmental elaboration of aesthetic experiences from the archaic processes to mature forms of artistic appreciation and other sophisticated aesthetic judgments. As with any other culturally sanctioned and articulated form of behavior, the refinement and elaboration of early forms of aesthetic experience into the complex and refined forms found in adult life need to be explained.

正如本章后文所讨论的，我强烈支持审美体验源于早期婴儿期经验的看法。诸如弗利斯这类退行模型的问题之一在于：它们走得不够远，未能解释审美体验如何从古老过程发展性地阐发为成熟的艺术鉴赏形式及其它精微的审美判断。如同任何其它受文化认可并得到清晰表达的行为形式一样，审美体验的早期形式如何精炼、阐发为成人生活中所见的复杂而精致的形式，是需要被解释的。

From Regression to Reparation（从退行到修复）

Unlike Freud and Fliess, who stressed the derivative nature of aesthetic experience, object relations theorists posited a progressive function for it. Melanie Klein (1929, 1930) and Hanna Segal (1952) both argued that aesthetic experience assists in the resolution of internal psychological distress arising from the feared consequences of aggression felt toward good objects. However, this resolution is not accomplished by means of denial or other defensive mechanisms; rather, the aesthetic experience can contain both the aggressive fantasy (the bad object) and the libidinal fantasy (the good object) in a single experience.

与强调审美体验之衍生物性质的弗洛伊德和弗利斯不同，客体关系[object relations]理论家为它设定了一种前进性的功能[progressive function]。梅兰妮·克莱因[Melanie Klein]（1929, 1930）与汉娜·西格尔[Hanna Segal]（1952）都主张，审美体验有助于化解源自"对好客体所感到的攻击性之可怕后果"的内部心理痛苦。然而，这种化解并非通过否认或其它防御机制来实现；相反，审美体验能够在单一体验中同时容纳攻击性幻想（坏客体[the bad object]）与力比多幻想（好客体[the good object]）。

According to object relations theory, in some cases, there is an amalgam in which beauty and ugliness are expressed with simultaneous vividness, with neither canceling out the other. In others, there is a balanced but separate depiction of evil and goodness within a single aesthetic field. In either case, instead of suppressing, neutralizing, or eliminating aggression, the aesthetic experience allows for the safe, even reparative, recognition that aggression and libido can exist within the same psychological realm without annihilation of the good. Thus, object relations theory was the first psychoanalytic theory to offer the possibility of a developmental aesthetic. Specifically, the idea that aesthetic experience is an aspect of the depressive position supports the notion that it has early developmental roots.

按客体关系理论，在某些情形中存在着一种融合[amalgam]，其中美与丑以同时的鲜明性被表达，二者互不抵消。在另一些情形中，则是在单一审美场域[aesthetic field]内对恶与善做出均衡却彼此分离的描绘。无论在哪种情形下，审美体验都不是去压抑、中和或消除攻击性，而是允许一种安全的、乃至修复性的承认：攻击性与力比多可以在同一个心理领域中并存，而好并不会因此被毁灭。因此，客体关系理论是第一个为一种发展性美学[developmental aesthetic]提供可能性的精神分析理论。具体来说，"审美体验是抑郁心位[depressive position]的一个面向"这一观念，支持了它拥有早期发展根源的看法。

Lee (1948, 1950) was also critical of Freud's model of sublimation and developed an approach to aesthetic experience like the object relations one. Lee believed that aesthetic experience arises from efforts to achieve atonement for guilt associated with destructive fantasy. He summarized the aesthetic experience of the artist:

Lee（1948, 1950）同样批判弗洛伊德的升华模型，并发展出一种与客体关系进路相似的审美体验理论。Lee 相信，审美体验源于"为与破坏性幻想相关联的罪疚[guilt]寻求赎偿[atonement]"的努力。他这样概括艺术家的审美体验：

In redeeming himself with conscience, the artist achieves not only a rare sense of unification among the institutions of his mind and with the alienated world; but more than this, for the restoration of love and approval from conscience is felt as a lyrical kind of at-one-ness, and as an exhilarating rapture called spiritual pleasure. A marked alleviation of self-esteem results from recapturing the flavor of the moments in early life when, guilty to his mother over destructive rage, the child regained her love, approval and gratitude in the reconciliation that occurred when appropriate action demonstrated his renewed allegiance to her teachings of pity. He experiences not only a moving sense of order, peace and wholeness, but is flooded with his most passionate experience of spiritual pleasure with his most intense experience of vitality and of becoming. (1950, p. 240)

在凭良心地自我救赎的过程中，艺术家不仅获得了一种罕见的心灵诸机构[institutions of his mind]之间、以及与异化世界之间的统一感；不止于此，因为良心的爱与认可得到恢复，被感受为一种抒情式的合一[at-one-ness]，以及一种被称为灵性愉悦[spiritual pleasure]的令人振奋的狂喜[rapture]。自我价值感[self-esteem]的显著缓解，源于重新捕捉到早年生活中那些时刻的滋味：当孩子因破坏性的暴怒而对母亲心怀罪疚时，在恰当的行动表明他重新效忠于母亲那怜悯[pite]的教诲之后所达成的和解中，他重获了母亲的爱、认可与感激。他不仅体验到一种动人的秩序感、平和与完整感，还被其最炽烈的灵性愉悦体验所淹没，伴随着他最强烈的活力与生成[becoming]的体验。 （1950, p. 240）

What Lee described was a psychologically confirming experience that followed the restored sense of connection with the attuned, loving, empathic parental object. Thus, Lee also supported the notion that aesthetic experience was linked to early infantile experiences of connection to parental objects or imagoes.

Lee 所描述的，是一种心理上的确证性体验[psychologically confirming experience]，它紧随"与协调的、慈爱的、共情的父母客体之连结感得以恢复"之后。因此，Lee 同样支持审美体验与"早期婴儿期与父母客体[parental objects]或内化形象[imagoes]的连结经验"相关联的看法。

Developmental Aesthetics（发展性美学）

In the last twenty years, psychoanalytic developmental theory has also been used to explore aesthetic experience. From this perspective, aesthetic experience is a type of regressive state in which early developmental phases (such as symbiotic fusion with caregivers) are revived. Gilbert Rose (1992) was the first to address the bridging of contemporary and archaic developmental states in The Power of Form (1992). He wrote that aesthetic experience is rooted in phylogenetic adaptations to internal and external reality. The way in which aesthetic experience organizes affect and perception and transforms occasions for anxiety into transcendent pleasure and insight compels us to speculate on its biological function. In this regard, Rose noted that:

在过去二十年里，精神分析的发展理论也被用于探索审美体验。从这一视角看，审美体验是一种退行状态，其中早期发展阶段（例如与照护者的共生融合[symbiotic fusion]）被重新唤醒。吉尔伯特·罗斯[Gilbert Rose]（1992）在《形式的权力》[The Power of Form]（1992）中，首次处理了当代与古老发展状态的桥接问题。他写道，审美体验植根于对内部与外部现实的种系发生适应[phylogenetic adaptations]。审美体验组织情感与知觉、并把引发焦虑的场合转化为超越性愉悦与洞见的方式，促使我们去揣度它的生物学功能[biological function]。就此，罗斯指出：

Art performs a valuable biological function: at least it provides a normative mode or opportunity for stimulating and assimilating potentially dangerous degrees of affect – in short, extending the limits of the bearable. Instead of traumatic reexperiencing of affective storms, or a repression of affective signals with regressive resomatization and fragmentation, a greater degree of integration and differentiation of affects can take place within the safe holding presence of the aesthetic structure. (p. 227)

艺术履行着一种宝贵的生物学功能：至少，它提供了一种规范性的模式或机会，用以激发并同化潜在危险程度的情感[affect]——简言之，延展可承受之物的界限。取代对情感风暴的创伤性再体验、或以退行性的再躯体化[resomatization]与碎裂化[fragmentation]来压抑情感信号，在审美结构那安全的抱持性在场[safe holding presence]之内，情感可以达到更高程度的整合与分化。 （p. 227）

Rose's thesis is highly consistent with the psychoanalytic model of aesthetic experience that we will be discussing. He argued that there is a biological, even evolutionary, basis for the creation of art and the sense of beauty (which he defined as the most refined and perfected expression of the aesthetic state of mind). The sense of beauty arises out of the observer's need to ensure consistent orientation in a relatively fluid reality and to regulate disruptive affective states. This view supports the notion that aesthetic experience integrates and binds potentially traumatic and disintegrative affective states, as well as facilitating the adaptation to and attunement with reality – and, most significantly, the complex interpersonal environment of the parent/child relationship. Rose wrote:

罗斯的论题与我们将要讨论的审美体验精神分析模型高度一致。他主张，艺术的创造与美感[the sense of beauty]（他把美感定义为审美心智状态最精炼、最完美的表达）有其生物学的、乃至演化的基础。美感产生于观察者的一种需要：在相对流动的现实中确保一贯的定向，并调节具破坏性的情感状态。这一观点支持如下看法：审美体验整合并约束潜在创伤性与解组性[disintegrative]的情感状态，同时也促进了"对现实的适应与调谐[attunement]"——而且最重要的是，促进了对父母/子女关系这一复杂人际环境的适应与调谐。罗斯写道：

The controlled ambiguity of aesthetic form highlights and magnifies the interaction of primary and secondary processes. Aesthetic experience has more to do with this congruence than with emotions per se. Ambiguity eases the tension of maintaining separate boundaries and restricting discharge, it fuses separate moments of time changed into a sense of timeless constancy. And all this is augmented by the distant resonance with the past, with the narcissistic fusion with the mother. Secondary-process control, delineating spatial, temporal, and logical distinctions, restraining feelings and impulses, builds tension moment by moment and echoes the first building of reality in the separation from the mother. (1992, p. 201)

审美形式那种被控制着的模糊性[controlled ambiguity]，凸显并放大了初级过程[primary processes]与次级过程[secondary processes]之间的相互作用。审美体验与此种一致性[congruence]的关联，甚于与情感本身的关联。模糊性缓和了维持分离边界与限制释放所带来的张力，它把被改变了的、彼此分离的时间瞬间融合为一种无时间性的恒常感。而所有这一切，又因与过去的遥远共鸣、与母亲的自恋性融合[narcissistic fusion]而得到增强。次级过程的控制——勾画出空间、时间与逻辑的区分，约束感受与冲动——一刻接一刻地积累张力，并回响着在与母亲分离中现实的最初建构。 （1992, p. 201）

For Rose, aesthetic experience is a complex psychological activity. This includes oscillations between primary and secondary processes, as well as between fusion and separateness. He argued that, in aesthetic experience, a triangulation exists between the present self, the evoked memory of archaic states, and the art object. This, he felt, resonates with the basic structure of the mind itself, reflecting the mind's imaginative activity or movement. Aesthetic experience recapitulates the mind's fusion with and emergence from the maternal bond. But the problem is that many psychological phenomena might have these functions and characteristics, and what is it that makes aesthetic experience distinct? In the chapters that follow, I will discuss my belief that it is the idealization of the formal aspects of experience that underlie aesthetic processes.

对罗斯而言，审美体验是一种复杂的心理活动。这包括初级过程与次级过程之间的振荡，以及融合与分离之间的振荡。他主张，在审美体验中，存在着"当下的自我、被唤起对古老状态的记忆、以及艺术对象"三者之间的三角关系[triangulation]。他觉得，这与心灵自身的基本结构产生共鸣，映照着心灵的想象性活动或运动。审美体验重演了心灵与母性连结的融合及其从中的浮现。但问题在于：许多心理现象都可能拥有这些功能与特征，究竟是什么使审美体验与众不同？在接下来的章节中，我将讨论我的信念：正是对经验之形式方面的理想化[idealization]，构成了审美过程的基底。

Like Rose's work, other developmental approaches to psychoanalytic aesthetics have focused on the problem of merger and de-differentiation that is commonly associated with aesthetic experience. This has been noted by philosophers not directly connected to psychoanalysis, such as John Dewey (1934), who wrote:

与罗斯的工作类似，其它精神分析美学的发展性进路也聚焦于"融合[merger]与去分化[de-differentiation]"这一问题，而它们常与审美体验相关联。并非直接关联精神分析的哲学家们——例如约翰·杜威[John Dewey]（1934）——也注意到了这一点，他写道：

The uniquely distinguishing feature of [a]esthetic experience is exactly the fact that no such distinction of self and objects exists in it. . . . [T]he two are so integrated that each disappears. (p. 249)

审美体验独一无二的区别性特征，恰恰在于其中并不存在自我与对象之间的这种区分。……二者如此整合，以至于各自都消失了。 （p. 249）

Some psychoanalysts have approached this problem in terms of archaic mental states, particularly regarding the child's first experiences of connection to the mother. They argue that the adult's experience of art is a form of regression to the powerful, emotionally charged, and psychologically ambiguous way in which the infant experiences the caretaking mother. These analysts have utilized the aesthetic approaches of the British Middle School, especially the writings of D. W. Winnicott. Unlike the Kleinians, who stressed the vicissitudes of defense against aggression, Winnicott emphasized the positive developmental thrust of illusion and creativity. Rather than viewing the mother/infant bond as a cauldron of rage and envy, he argued that the child enters the world eager to create, not to destroy. Winnicott saw this creativity as arising out of an original state of pleasurable and omnipotent nondifferentiation.

一些精神分析家从古老心理状态的角度来处理这一问题，尤其是关涉儿童最初与母亲连结的经验。他们主张，成人对艺术的体验，是一种退行——退行至婴儿体验照护母亲的那种强烈、充满情感负载且心理上含混的方式。这些分析家运用了英国中间学派[British Middle School]的审美进路，尤其是温尼科特[D. W. Winnicott]的著作。与强调"对攻击性的防御之变迁"的克莱因派不同，温尼科特强调幻觉[illusion]与创造力[creativity]的积极发展推力。他并不把母婴连结视为一个盛满暴怒与嫉妒[envy]的鼎镬[cauldron]，而是主张，儿童是带着创造的渴望、而非摧毁的渴望进入世界的。温尼科特把这种创造力视为产生于一种原初状态：愉悦而全能的无分化[nondifferentiation]。

Building on this idea, Ellen Handler Spitz (1985) argued for the importance of idealization in these preoedipal merger experiences in aesthetic experience. In Art and Psyche, she wrote:

在此基础上，艾伦·汉德勒·斯皮茨[Ellen Handler Spitz]（1985）主张，理想化在这些前俄狄浦斯期[preoedipal]的融合经验中、在审美体验中占有重要地位。在《艺术与心灵》[Art and Psyche]中，她写道：

The aesthetic ideal, then, is like symbolic fusion in that encounters with the beautiful may temporarily obliterate our sense of inner and outer separateness by drawing us into an orbit in which boundaries between self and other, and categories into which we divide the world, dissolve. This occurs, perhaps, with the return to consciousness, the reavailability to us, during moments of creativity or responsiveness in the arts, of aspects of our preoedipal life, with its pleasurable sense of merging and union. (p. 142)

那么，审美的理想[aesthetic ideal]就如同象征性融合[symbolic fusion]：与美的相遇可能暂时抹去我们内外分离之感，把我们吸入一个轨道，在其中自我与他者之间的边界、以及我们据以划分世界的诸范畴都消融了。这或许发生在创造力涌现、或在艺术中有所感应的时刻——我们前俄狄浦斯期生活的诸面向重新回到意识、重新为我们所及，连同其愉悦的融合与合一之感。 （p. 142）

For Spitz, aesthetic experience recaptures early memories of the preoedipal relationship with the mother. Most important is the experience of the maternal bond as a special and highly valued state that possesses a profound quality of perfection. In addition, aesthetic experience possesses intense affective qualities, such as pleasure, security, and fulfillment. Spitz describes a type of symbiotic relating that is evoked in the aesthetic response, in which the relationship to the object is invested with qualities that hark back to the archaic bond with the mother. Spitz believed that this reexperience is the source of aesthetic pleasure.

对斯皮茨而言，审美体验重新捕捉了与母亲前俄狄浦斯期关系的早期记忆。最重要的是把母性连结体验为一种特殊且被高度珍视的状态，它拥有一种深沉的完美品质。此外，审美体验拥有强烈的情感品质，诸如愉悦、安全与满足[fulfillment]。斯皮茨描述了在审美反应中被唤起的一种共生性关联[symbiotic relating]：与对象的关系被投注了那些回溯到与母亲之古老连结的品质。斯皮茨相信，这种再体验就是审美愉悦的源头。

In a more recent paper, Patricia Lipscomb (1997) extends the discussion of the developmental basis of aesthetic experience into the realm of Winnicott's transitional phenomenon:

在晚近的一篇论文中，帕特里夏·利普斯科姆[Patricia Lipscomb]（1997）把对审美体验之发展基础的讨论，延伸至温尼科特的过渡现象[transitional phenomenon]的领域：

Aesthetic experience functions as a transitional process, allowing the viewer of a work of art to enter a transitional space between reality and fantasy that is suggested by the various dialectics involved in aesthetic experience (past and future, concrete and abstract, passive and active, near and far), but is illustrated most clearly by the sense of ambiguity about fusion versus separateness that is typically induced in the viewer as part of aesthetic experience. (p. 154)

审美体验发挥一种过渡过程[transitional process]的功能，它允许艺术作品的观者进入一个介于现实与幻想之间的过渡空间[transitional space]；这一空间由审美体验所涉及的种种辩证关系（过去与未来、具体与抽象、被动与主动、近与远）所暗示，但最清晰地体现在观者身上通常被引发的、关于"融合抑或分离"的模糊感之中，而这也是审美体验的一部分。 （p. 154）

Lipscomb argued that aesthetic experience induces a revisiting of “the developmental phase in which the young child first began to achieve a sense of psychological separateness from mother and the rest of the world” (p. 154). She asserted that the viewer experiences two sources of pleasure: (1) the temporary experience of boundarilessness and fusion, where opposites coexist and harmony reigns, and (2) the return to a sense of one's psychological separateness and the recognition of one's capacity to withstand loss.

利普斯科姆主张，审美体验引发对"幼儿最初开始获得与母亲及世界其余部分在心理上分离之感的那一发展阶段"的重新造访（p. 154）。她断定，观者体验到两种愉悦来源：（1）暂时性的无边界[boundarilessness]与融合体验，其中对立之物并存、和谐主宰；（2）回归到自身心理分离之感，以及对自身承受丧失之能力的确认。

This approach to understanding aesthetic experience emphasizes the sense of nondifferentiation in which fantasy and reality seem to interpenetrate, even to codetermine each other. The argument goes that this experience in maturity essentially recapitulates the developmentally primitive self and object relations and that the type of emotional resonance and unconscious communication that seems to occur is directly linked to the psychological processes in the mother/infant bond.

这种理解审美体验的进路，强调无分化之感——在其中幻想与现实似乎相互渗透，甚至相互共同决定[codetermine]。其论点是：成熟期的这一体验，本质上重演了发展上原始的自我与客体关系；而似乎发生的那种情感共鸣与无意识沟通，直接关联于母婴连结中的心理过程。

Lipscomb directs her attention to the area of transitional experience, arguing that the work of art is like the transitional object in that the viewer is psychologically inseparable from the object. “There is no such thing as a work of art,” Winnicott might say; there is only the viewer and the art as they share a co-constructed psychic reality, an area of illusion. I would elaborate this viewpoint by pointing out the oscillating positions of the viewer and the artwork. The viewer experiences him- or herself at times as the active creator and, at other times, as the receptive subject of the aesthetic illusion. This shifting identification contributes to the dynamic tension in aesthetic experience.

利普斯科姆把注意力指向过渡经验[transitional experience]的领域，主张艺术作品如同过渡客体[transitional object]，因为观者与对象在心理上不可分离。温尼科特或许会说："并不存在所谓的艺术作品"；存在的只是观者与艺术，它们共享一个共同建构的心理现实、一个幻觉的领域。我想通过指出观者与艺术作品彼此振荡的位置，来阐发这一观点。观者时而把自己体验为主动的创造者，时而又把自己体验为审美幻觉的接受性主体[receptive subject]。这种游移的认同[shifting identification]，构成了审美体验中的动力性张力。

While I feel that developmental theorists such as Spitz and Lipscomb have much of value to contribute, their emphasis on the experience of fusion seems to ignore the fact that experiences of fusion can also be a source of terror and fragmentation. And it is well recognized that separation can result in feelings of loss and abandonment, so the fluctuation or ambiguity between fusion and separateness may not necessarily be a source of aesthetic pleasure. This is an area that has been amply explored by object relations theorists in their discussions of borderline phenomena. Since aesthetic experience is almost invariably positive and self-confirming (no matter what its content may be), I would argue that the experience of fusion and separateness is not just with any type of parental object, but with an idealized one.

尽管我觉得像斯皮茨与利普斯科姆这样的发展理论家有许多有价值的贡献，但他们对融合体验的强调似乎忽略了一个事实：融合体验也可能成为恐惧[terror]与碎裂化的来源。而且人们公认，分离可能导致丧失与被遗弃的感受，因此融合与分离之间的波动或模糊，未必一定是审美愉悦的来源。这一领域已被客体关系理论家在关于边缘性现象[borderline phenomena]的讨论中充分探索过。既然审美体验几乎总是正面的、自我确证的（无论其内容为何），我就要主张：融合与分离的体验并非与任何类型的父母客体相关，而是与一个被理想化的客体[an idealized one]相关。

In this regard, self psychology offers a model of aesthetic experience that emphasizes the loss and recovery of the tie to the idealized parent. In his paper entitled “The Artist and Selfobject Theory,” Charles Kligerman (1980) noted that the psychology of the artist is linked to idealization and aesthetic experience, describing how the artist's self-crisis leads to the development of lifelong efforts to recapture a lost state of perfection. One of the important qualities of idealization is the sense of “formal” perfection and value known as beauty. The idealized object or the grandiose self is experienced as possessing in essence a perfection of form and mode of being that is beautiful; therefore, aesthetic experience forms a part of idealization. In his formulation, Kligerman speculates that the prototypical artist is someone who experienced consistent mirroring of his or her grandiosity in childhood. Inevitably, this archaic selfobject experience fails and the artist-to-be is cast out from this state of perfection. Kligerman noted:

就此而言，自体心理学[self psychology]提供了一种审美体验模型，它强调与"被理想化的父母[the idealized parent]"之连结的丧失与重获。在其题为《艺术家与自体客体理论》[“The Artist and Selfobject Theory”]的论文中，查尔斯·克利格曼[Charles Kligerman]（1980）指出，艺术家的心理与理想化和审美体验相关联；他描述了艺术家的自体危机[self-crisis]如何导致其发展出终生的努力，以重新捕捉一种丧失了的完美状态。理想化的一个重要品质，就是那种被称为美的"形式上的"完美与价值之感。被理想化的客体[the idealized object]或夸大自体[the grandiose self]被体验为本质上拥有一种美的形式完美与存在方式；因此，审美体验构成了理想化的一部分。在其构想中，克利格曼推测，原型的艺术家[prototypical artist]是这样一种人：他在童年经历过对其夸大自体的持续镜映[mirroring]。这种古老的自体客体经验[archaic selfobject experience]不可避免地失败了，于是那个将要成为艺术家的人被逐出这种完美状态。克利格曼指出：

The ensuing fall from grace is followed by a passionate need to recover the original beauty and perfection and later to present the world with a work of beauty (really the artist himself) that will evoke universal awe and admiration. There are thus at least three main currents to the creative drive. (pp. 387–388)

随之而来的失宠[fall from grace]之后，是一种炽烈的需要：去重新获得原初的美与完美，并在日后向世界呈现一件美的作品（实际上就是艺术家本人），它将唤起普遍的敬畏与赞赏。因此，创造性驱力[the creative drive]至少有三种主要潮流。 （pp. 387–388）

An intrinsic joy in creating, related to what has been termed “functional” pleasure. This is perhaps the most important factor, but the one we know the least about: the exhibitionistic grandiose ecstasy of being regarded as the acme of beauty and perfection and the nearly insatiable need to repeat and confirm this feeling: It is the need to regain a lost paradise – the original bliss of perfection – to overcome the empty feeling of self-depletion and to recover self-esteem. In the metapsychology of the self, this would amount to healing the threatened fragmentation and restoring firm self-cohesion through a merger with the self-object – the work of art – and a bid for mirroring approval of the world. We can also add a fourth current to the creative drive – the need to regain perfection by merging with the ideals of the powerful selfobjects, first the parents, then later revered models that represent the highest standards of some great artistic tradition. (pp. 387–388)

创造中一种内在的喜悦，与被称为“功能性”愉悦[functional pleasure]者相关。这或许是最重要的因素，却是我们了解得最少的一个：那种被视作美与完美之极致[acme]的展示性夸大狂喜[exhibitionistic grandiose ecstasy]，以及几乎无法满足的、去重复并确认这一感受的需要：它是重新获得一座失落乐园——原初的完美极乐——的需要，以克服自体枯竭[self-depletion]的空虚感、并恢复自我价值感。在自体的元心理学[metapsychology of the self]中，这将等同于：通过与自体-客体[self-object]——即艺术作品——的融合，以及向世界寻求镜映性认可[mirroring approval]的努力，来疗愈受威胁的碎裂化、并恢复坚实的自体凝聚[self-cohesion]。我们还可以为创造性驱力增添第四种潮流——通过与强大的自体客体的理想相融合来重获完美：最初是父母，之后则是代表着某一伟大艺术传统最高标准的、受尊崇的典范。 （pp. 387–388）

Selfobject experience in childhood inevitably leads to failures in attunement and self-crisis. The child confronted by the loss of the sustaining, developmentally essential tie to the parent responds psychologically by taking into the self-structure the selfobject functions of the parent. This transmuting internalization is repeated innumerable times as the ongoing, normal selfobject failures accumulate. In his discussion of artistic creativity just quoted, Kligerman pointed out that art is an attempt to restore a lost self-state prior to the empathic failure of the parent(s). Aesthetic experience is the restoration of the early selfobject tie to an idealized parent and thus of a renewed sense of self.

童年期的自体客体经验不可避免地导致调谐的失败与自体危机。面对与父母之间那支撑性的、发展上至关重要的连结之丧失，儿童以心理方式回应：把父母的自体客体功能纳入自体结构[self-structure]。随着持续不断的、正常的自体客体失败不断累积，这种转换性内化[transmuting internalization]被重复无数次。在其刚刚引述的关于艺术创造性的讨论中，克利格曼指出，艺术是一种尝试：去恢复在父母共情失败[empathic failure]之前的那个丧失了的自体状态。审美体验就是"对与理想化父母之间早期自体客体连结"的恢复，因而是对自体之感的更新。

I believe that Kligerman's perspective adds an essential component to the developmental model of aesthetic experience – that its sources lie not only in the evocation of early experiences of psychological connection with the mother, but also in the idealized nature of that relationship and accompanying self-states and that these are central to the aesthetic sense. In other words, a key aspect of aesthetics is an intuitive judgment (an appreciation) of quality, and this type of judgment is linked to the most elemental memories of idealization. This point will be discussed in greater depth in chapter four.

我相信，克利格曼的视角为审美体验的发展模型增添了一个本质性的组成——审美体验的源头不仅在于唤起与母亲早期心理连结的经验，还在于那种关系的被理想化的性质、以及随之而来的自体状态，而这些对审美感[aesthetic sense]而言是核心的。换言之，美学的一个关键面向是对品质的直觉性判断（一种赏识[appreciation]），而这类判断与最原初的理想化记忆相关联。这一点将在第四章更深入地讨论。

A New Psychoanalytic Model of Aesthetic Experience（审美体验的新精神分析模型）

Man is, then, alone of all objects in the world, susceptible of an ideal of beauty, as it is only humanity in his person, as an intelligence, that is susceptible of the ideal of perfection. (Kant in Aschenbrenner and Isenberg, 1970, p. 187)

那么，人是世上一切对象中唯一能够感受美之理想的存在，因为唯有在他身上、作为智性[intelligence]的人性，才能感受完美之理想。 （康德，见 Aschenbrenner and Isenberg, 1970, p. 187）

A Human Thing（一件属人的事）

For Kant, the source of aesthetic experience lies in the rational apprehension of man's inner goodness through perception of the human form. Thus, aesthetic pleasure is a response to the moral goodness found at the core of humanity in relation to God. Beauty for Kant was a human thing, reflecting an ideal grounded in truth beyond everyday aesthetic taste.

对康德而言，审美体验的源头在于"通过对人的形貌的知觉，理性地领会人内在的善"。因此，审美愉悦是对"在人性核心处、就其与上帝的关系而言所发现的道德之善"的回应。对康德来说，美是一件属人的事[human thing]，它映照着一种植根于真理、超越日常审美鉴赏的理想。

Later, Francis Jeffrey (1816) also argued for the human source of the aesthetic. He believed:

后来，弗朗西斯·杰弗里[Francis Jeffrey]（1816）也为审美的属人来源辩护。他相信：

It is man and man alone that we see in the beauties of the earth which he inhabits; it is the idea of the enjoyment of feelings that animates the existence of sentient beings, that calls forth all our emotions, and is the parent of beauty. (p. 286)

我们在他所栖居的大地之美中所看到的，是人，且只有人；正是“对感受的享受”这一观念，赋予了有感觉之存在[the existence of sentient beings]以生机，唤起了我们全部的情感，并成为美之父[the parent of beauty]。 （p. 286）

The depiction of aesthetic experience we have been discussing is also fundamentally human. If there is an abstract quality, it is found in the biological readiness to organize experience of the world according to certain patterns, colors, sounds, and rhythms. This inner readiness to create form is the psychological equivalent of Plato's ideal form, but, unlike Platonic form, those that begin as potentials in the neurons must be evoked and elaborated in interaction with the world, especially with other human beings. According to the model to be discussed in this book, aesthetic form is an emergent mental phenomenon in which psychobiological-neuronal propensities are stimulated and sculpted through relational experiences and are gradually internalized as structured expectations and organizing principals that reflect the form and particularly the quality of interpersonal experience. Recent psychoanalytic aesthetic theories echo Kant and Jeffrey by showing that beauty has a human core, but that core is not found in some abstract inner goodness or individual affective state; rather, it adheres in the idealized experience of the parent/child interaction that is elaborated, refined, and spread out over the individual's experience of the world. In this way, the new analytic aesthetic, a developmental-relational perspective, is a fundamentally different model from that of the philosophers we have discussed and from those of the schools of classical psychoanalysis.

我们一直在讨论的这种对审美体验的描绘，从根本上说也是属人的。如果说存在某种抽象的品质，它就存在于"按照某些模式、色彩、声音与节奏来组织对世界之经验"的生物学准备之中。这种创造形式的内在准备，是柏拉图理想形式在心理学上的对应物；但与柏拉图式形式不同，那些在神经元中以潜能形式开始的东西，必须在与世界的互动中——尤其是与其它人类的互动中——被唤起并得到雕琢。按本书将要讨论的模型，审美形式是一种涌现的心智现象[emergent mental phenomenon]：在其中，心理生物-神经的倾向[psychobiological-neuronal propensities]经由关系性经验被激发与塑造，并逐渐内化为结构化的期待与组织原则[organizing principals]，它们映照着人际经验的形式、尤其是其品质。晚近的精神分析美学理论与康德和杰弗里相呼应，表明美具有一个属人的核心，但这个核心并不存在于某种抽象的内在之善或个体的情感状态中；相反，它附着于"父母/子女互动的被理想化的经验"之中，而这些经验被阐发、精炼，并铺展到个体对世界的经验之上。以这种方式，这种新的分析性美学[a new analytic aesthetic]——一种发展-关系性[developmental-relational]的视角——与我们所讨论的哲学家们的模型、以及经典精神分析诸学派的模型都根本不同。

A developmental-relational approach to aesthetic experience (including that of self psychology) answers several important questions that classical psychoanalysis despairs of answering, as is evidenced by the following points taken from a developmental-relational perspective:

一种发展-关系性的审美体验进路（包括自体心理学的进路）回答了经典精神分析绝望于回答的几个重要问题，以下从发展-关系性视角提出的几点即为证明：

1. Even though aesthetic experience can include symbolic content, it is not limited to this; rather than precluding analytic investigation, however, this factor demands an expanded paradigm. Such a paradigm views aesthetic experience not in terms of content, symbolic or otherwise, but according to its structure, form, and quality.

1. 尽管审美体验可以包含象征性内容，但它并不局限于此；然而，这一因素非但不排除分析性探究，反而要求一种扩展的范式[expanded paradigm]。这样一种范式并不从内容（象征性的或其它）的角度、而是依据其结构、形式与品质来看待审美体验。

2. Aesthetic experience is evocative of archaic, preverbal experiences of self-inrelation. Thus, it is prereflective and involves procedural forms of knowing (an attribute that led to Freud's frustration).

2. 审美体验唤起古老的、前语言[preverbal]的自体-关系[self-in-relation]经验。因此，它是前反思的[prereflective]，并涉及程序性的认知形式[procedural forms of knowing]（这一属性曾导致弗洛伊德的挫败）。

3. Aesthetic experience arises not from defensive motives, but from strivings to experience developmentally healthy states of self-experience and relationship.

3. 审美体验并非源于防御性动机，而是源于去体验"发展上健康的自体体验与关系状态"的努力。

4. Aesthetic experience expresses, rather than conceals, fantasy and perception. It seeks to capture and concretize many things that are most frightening or otherwise stimulating to us. It does this by expressing and/or containing these intense affective states in formally structured and perfected forms. In this way, rather than being disguised, fantasy and drive are experientially heightened, even as they are modulated and purged of the acute sense of threat or unbearable arousal.

4. 审美体验表达、而非隐藏幻想与知觉。它试图捕捉并具体化许多对我们而言最为可怕、或在其它意义上最具刺激性的东西。它这样做的方式，是在形式上结构化且臻于完美的形式中表达和/或容纳这些强烈的情感状态。以这种方式，幻想与驱力不是被伪装，而是在体验上被提升，即便它们同时也被调节、并被清除掉那种急迫的威胁感或难以承受的唤起[unbearable arousal]。

5. The core quality of perfection that virtually defines aesthetic experience is derived from the developmental vicissitudes of archaic fantasies of idealization that are tied to equally grandiose self-states. This is the only way that we can explain the supreme value we grant to aesthetic experience, as well as the accompanying heightening of self-esteem and well-being. We do not simply reexperience an early state of oneness with the mother, rather, in aesthetic experience, we relate to something of fantastic value and exquisite form, something that evokes the type of enthrallment that we felt (or longed to feel) as infants with our parents.

5. 那种几乎界定着审美体验的核心完美品质，源自"古老的理想化幻想"的发展性变迁，而这些幻想与同样夸大的自体状态[grandiose self-states]相系。这是唯一能解释"我们赋予审美体验的至高价值"、以及随之而来的自我价值感与幸福感提升的方式。我们并不是单纯再体验与母亲合一的早期状态；相反，在审美体验中，我们与某种具有非凡价值与精美形式的东西发生关联，它唤起我们作为婴儿与父母相处时曾感受到（或曾渴望感受到）的那种着迷[enthrallment]。

The new authors whom we have discussed see aesthetic experience as involving processes and psychological states that are grounded in early infancy. In this context, aesthetic experience does not arise fully formed in adulthood. In fact, if we posit that the capacity for aesthetic experience is an important psychological phenomenon – not just a later-acquired affectation or regressive condition – then we should assume that some developmental elaboration of the experience occurs during an individual's lifetime. E. Jacques (1965) has discussed specific elaborations in aesthetic experience and creativity that occur because of midlife psychological developmental changes.

我们所讨论的这些新作者，把审美体验视为涉及植根于早期婴儿期的过程与心理状态。在这一脉络中，审美体验并非在成年期完整成形地出现。事实上，如果我们设定"审美体验的能力"是一种重要的心理现象——而不只是后天习得的做作[affectation]或退行状况——那么我们就应当假定，这种体验在个体一生中会经历某种发展性的阐发。E. Jacques（1965）讨论了因中年心理发展变化而出现的、在审美体验与创造力方面的具体阐发。

While object relational and self psychological theorists argue convincingly for the presence of an archaic substrate in adult aesthetics, they have not yet explored the possibility that aesthetic experience may have its own developmental line – one that is intertwined with other early childhood processes, but that can also be identified and discussed on its own terms.

尽管客体关系与自体心理学的理论家们令人信服地论证了"在成人美学中存在着一个古老基底[archaic substrate]"，但他们尚未探索这样一种可能性：审美体验或许拥有它自己的发展路线[developmental line]——一条与其它幼儿期过程交织、却也可以依其自身而被辨识与讨论的发展路线。

Aesthetic experience, as noted at the beginning of this chapter, is a basic way in which we organize and experience ourselves and our world. Artists manipulate aesthetic experience; they play with its manifestations and familiar forms and use it secondarily as a medium for communication. The meanings of art (those characteristics that interested Freud) – while important to the purpose of art – do not explain it. What makes an aesthetic experience different from other psychological phenomena is the centrality of form and quality/value, not its content. This is an area of human psychological life that is preverbal, somatically grounded, and powerfully related to the earliest forms of psychological organizing activities and attachment motivations.

正如本章开头所指出的，审美体验是我们据以组织并体验自身与自身世界的一种基本方式。艺术家操纵审美体验；他们玩味它的种种表现与熟悉的形式，并把它次要地用作沟通的媒介。艺术的意义（那些让弗洛伊德感兴趣的特征）——尽管对艺术的目的而言很重要——并不能解释艺术。使一种审美体验区别于其它心理现象者，是形式与品质/价值的核心地位，而非其内容。这是人类心理生活的一个领域，它是前语言的、以身体为基础的[somatically grounded]，并且与最早形式的心理组织活动和依恋动机[attachment motivations]有着强有力的关联。

To further elaborate this viewpoint, we will now turn to a discussion of how the capacity for aesthetic experience comes to be elaborated during psychological development.

为进一步阐发这一观点，我们现在将转向一个讨论：审美体验的能力在心理发展过程中是如何被阐发的。
