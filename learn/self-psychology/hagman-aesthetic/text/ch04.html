Chapter 4　Idealization and Aesthetic Experience（理想化与审美体验）

George Hagman · Chapter 4　Idealization and Aesthetic Experience（理想化与审美体验） · pp. 37–54 · 95 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

本篇小节
  1. · Idealization: A Developmental/Relational Model（理想化：一个发展性/关系性模型）
  2. · Clinical Illustration（临床例证）
  3. · Idealization in Creativity and Aesthetic Experience（创造力与审美体验中的理想化）

This chapter explores the place of idealization in human psychological life, in psychoanalytic treatment, and in aesthetic experience. Though traditionally viewed as a defense, a displacement, or a protection from one's own or the other's aggressive wishes, idealization is fundamental to our valuation of self, our relationships, and our experience of the world. Its source lies in the early affective connection between infant and caregiver, during which the communication of mutual idealization arises and is elaborated. It is the extension of idealization beyond the dyad that contributes to our feelings about life's value, being part of the foundation of self-esteem, love, and moral judgment. The most elaborated and refined embodiment of idealization is in our aesthetic sensibility, reflected in our creation and appreciation of art.

本章考察理想化[idealization]在人类心理生活、在精神分析治疗、以及在审美体验[aesthetic experience]中的位置。尽管理想化历来被视为一种防御[defense]、一种移置[displacement]，或是对自身或他人攻击性愿望的一种防护，但它实则是我们评价自体[self]、评价我们的关系、评价我们对世界之体验的根本基础。它的源头在于婴儿与照料者[caregiver]之间早期的情感联结[affective connection]——正是在其中，相互理想化[mutual idealization]的沟通得以产生并不断被精致化。正是理想化超出二元关系[dyad]的延展，促成了我们对生命价值的感受，并构成自尊[self-esteem]、爱与道德判断的根基之一。理想化最精致、最成熟的体现，就在我们的审美感受力[aesthetic sensibility]之中，映现于我们对艺术的创造与欣赏。

Idealization is the psychological investment of an object, event, or experience with a high degree of value. The thing is felt to be exceptional, the best of a group, perhaps even perfect. Even though idealization is recognized by most as a subjectively based judgment, the idealizer experiences it as a trait of the object, not as an illusion. Idealization is both a healthy developmental need, fueling the early parent/infant tie, and a psychological strategy that can function in both a defensive and compensatory manner. In maturity, idealization is a source of loving feelings and a general sense that life has value; it can also be used in a narrow sense to fend off aggressive fantasies or feelings of helplessness.

理想化是把高度的价值投注[investment]于某个对象、事件或体验之上的心理过程。被理想化之物被感受为例外的、同类中最好的，甚至可能是完美的。尽管大多数人承认理想化是一种以主体为根据的判断[subjectively based judgment]，理想化者却将它体验为对象自身的一种属性，而非一种幻觉[illusion]。理想化既是一种健康的发展性需要[developmental need]，为早期亲子纽带[parent/infant tie]提供动力；也是一种心理策略，可同时以防御性与补偿性[compensatory]的方式运作。在成熟状态中，理想化是爱的感受[loving feelings]以及"生命有其价值"这一总体感受的来源；它也可能被以一种狭隘的方式用来抵挡攻击性幻想或无助感[feelings of helplessness]。

Psychoanalysis has traditionally viewed idealization as a misrepresentation or a pathological distortion of reality; in other words, idealization makes a person unable to judge something (or some person) for what it really is. This presupposes that there is an actual value that the world possesses apart from what we grant to it. Stephen Mitchell (2002) pointed out that this notion (supported by Freud) has its roots in naturalistic science and modernism. One of the assumptions of the present chapter is that we are the ones who grant value to what we experience and that idealization is a construction whose sources, determinants, and functions are both interpersonal and intrapsychic.

精神分析传统上把理想化视为一种误现[misrepresentation]，或是对现实的病理性扭曲；换言之，理想化使人无法按某物（或某人）本来的样子去判断它。这一看法预设：世界自身拥有某种实际价值，独立于我们所赋予它的价值。斯蒂芬·米切尔[Stephen Mitchell]（2002）指出，这一（得到弗洛伊德支持的）观念根植于自然主义科学与现代主义。本章的一个前提是：正是我们赋予自己的体验以价值；理想化是一种建构[construction]，其来源、决定因素与功能既在人际层面[interpersonal]，也在心理内部[intrapsychic]。

Another assumption here is that our idealization of the world is not necessarily good or bad, functional or dysfunctional, appropriate or inappropriate – however, it is essential. I will show that idealization is a motive for human attachment and relatedness and that it is elaborated over time into other forms of healthy and pathological valuations.

此处的另一个前提是：我们对世界的理想化未必是好的或坏的、功能性的或功能失调的、恰当的或不恰当的——然而，它是根本性的。我将表明，理想化是人类依恋[attachment]与关联[relatedness]的一种动机[motive]，并且随着时间推移，它会被精致化为其他形式的健康或病理性评价[valuations]。

Some readers may feel that I am "idealizing idealization," that I am going light on the areas where idealization can be faulty or pathological, as in the overidealization seen in sects, in some religious or political ideologies, or (in the relational area) where people over-idealize others at the expense of their own welfare. Admittedly, idealization can have various functions in psychopathology, even in extreme pathology such as delusional disorders or narcissistic attachments, where one murders or rejects another because life is felt to be unlivable without that person. However, to say that idealization may be enlisted to serve certain pathological functions is not to say that pathology is inherent to it.

有些读者或许会觉得我在"理想化理想化"——觉得我对理想化可能出错或致病的那些领域一带而过，诸如教派中、某些宗教或政治意识形态中所见的过度理想化[overidealization]，或者（在关系层面）人们以牺牲自身福祉为代价去过度理想化他人。无可否认，理想化在精神病理学[psychopathology]中可以发挥各种功能，甚至在妄想性障碍[delusional disorders]或自恋性依恋[narcissistic attachments]这样极端的病理中也是如此——在那类情形里，一个人会杀害或抛弃另一个人，因为觉得若没有那个人，生活就活不下去。然而，说理想化可能被征用来服务于某些病理功能，并不等于说病理是理想化本身所固有的。

In this way, idealization might be seen as analogous to sexual desire. Although sex is involved in many different forms of psychological conflict, I would not claim that sex is in any way inherently pathological, although it can be involved in pathology, as can numerous other normal psychological phenomena. Considering this, I will not deny that there are many forms of disorders in idealization, but those are not my focus here. Rather, I want to emphasize the healthy elaboration of idealization as an important component of human experience and its place in our relationships and our aesthetic sense.

从这个角度看，理想化或许可以与性欲[sexual desire]相类比。尽管性卷入许多不同形式的心理冲突，我不会主张性在本质上有任何病理之处——虽然它可以卷入病理，正如众多其他正常的心理现象一样。有鉴于此，我并不否认理想化存在多种形式的紊乱，但那并非我此处关注的重点。我更想强调的是理想化健康的精致化，它是人类经验的一个重要组成部分；我也强调它在我们的人际关系与审美感[aesthetic sense]中的位置。

This chapter is, therefore, an exploration of the developmental line of idealization. In agreement with Tyson and Tyson (1990), I believe that the concept of developmental lines is "a useful metaphor to convey the interwoven, overlapping, branching network of simultaneously evolving psychic systems that constitute developmental processes" (p. 36).

因此，本章是对理想化发展线[developmental line]的一次探索。我赞同泰森夫妇[Tyson and Tyson]（1990）的看法，认为发展线这一概念是"一个有用的隐喻，用以传达那些同时演化、共同构成发展过程的心理系统之间相互交织、彼此重叠、不断分岔的网络"（p. 36）。

Inevitably, the discussion touches on other developmental areas, such as object relations, the ego and superego, the self-structure, and affect; however, the focus here will be on the source of idealization in early attachment and its elaboration into mature forms of valuation. To examine these, I will start with a review of the literature, leading up to recent proposals to view idealization as a normal mode of relating to important others. Next, I will discuss the vicissitudes of idealization in the early parent/infant interaction and its elaboration and extension over time. I will then briefly discuss a case report in which the restoration of a patient's capacity to idealize led to a revitalization of adult development. I will close this chapter with a discussion of the connection between idealization and aesthetic experience.

讨论难免会触及其他发展领域，例如对象关系[object relations]、自我[ego]与超我[superego]、自体结构[self-structure]以及情感[affect]；但此处的焦点将放在理想化在早期依恋中的起源，以及它如何被精致化为成熟的评价形式。为考察这些内容，我将先作一番文献回顾，进而引出近来把理想化视为与重要他人建立关联之正常方式[normal mode of relating]的主张。接下来，我将讨论理想化在早期亲子互动中的变迁[vicissitudes]，以及它随时间的精致化与延展。随后，我会简要讨论一个个案报告，其中病人理想化能力的恢复带来了成年发展的复苏。本章最后，我将讨论理想化与审美体验之间的联系。

Freud (1914a, 1923) claimed that idealization was an unrealistic exaggeration of the positive qualities of an object. The prototype of idealization is the act of falling in love, when the object and, by association, the self become elevated in value, felt by the lover to be perfect and even sublime. The subject feels excitement, awe, veneration, and enthrallment toward the idealized one. The self is experienced as more powerful, cohesive, and continuous as it shares in the object's grandeur.

弗洛伊德（1914a, 1923）主张，理想化是对对象积极品质的一种不切实际的夸大[exaggeration]。理想化的原型[prototype]是坠入爱河这一行为：此时对象、并连带自体都在价值上被抬高，被爱者感受为完美的、甚至崇高的[sublime]。主体对理想化对象感到兴奋、敬畏[awe]、尊崇[veneration]与迷恋[enthrallment]。由于分有了对象的宏伟[grandeur]，自体被体验为更有力量、更具凝聚性、更为连续。

A key point in Freud's thinking is the notion of idealization as inappropriate to the true nature of the object. Freud's working assumption was that there was a reality "out there" that possessed some inherent value. This approach was related to his grounding in rationalism and scientism. Thus, the "exaggeration" of idealization could only arise from non-normal psychology. Freud grounded idealization in narcissism, the early state in which the infant feels merged with the omnipotent mother. Over time, the child's growing recognition of powerlessness and vulnerability leads to the projection of omnipotent fantasies onto the parents, who then become objects not just of external support and protection (an anaclitic relationship), but, through internalization, they also serve as the energy reservoir for internal psychic structurization.

弗洛伊德思想中的一个关键点，是把理想化视为与对象的真实本性不相称。弗洛伊德的工作假设是：存在一个"在那里的"[out there]现实，它自身拥有某种固有价值。这一进路与他在理性主义和科学主义上的根基有关。因此，理想化的这种"夸大"只可能出自非正常的心理。弗洛伊德把理想化奠基于自恋[narcissism]之上，即婴儿感到与全能母亲融合的早期状态。随着时间推移，儿童对自身无力与脆弱的认识日益增长，导致全能幻想被投射[projection]到父母身上；父母于是不仅成为外部支持与保护的对象（一种 依附性 [anaclitic]关系），而且通过内化[internalization]，还充当了内部心理结构化的能量储存库[energy reservoir]。

In On Narcissism: An Introduction (1914a), Freud discussed the internal creation of the ego ideal as the repository of the original infantile narcissism. Identification with the ego ideal results in a sense of values, positive role modeling, ambitions, and aspirations. A later, more important elaboration of this idea was the concept of the superego. The superego, unlike the ego ideal, is charged with aggressive energy, and in addition to its ideal attributes, it is also the source of internalized authority, fear, and punishment. Eventually, psychoanalysts began to differentiate between the hostile superego and the loving ego ideal. (See Roy Schafer's The Loving and Beloved Superego in Freud's Structural Theory [1960] for an additional discussion of the libidinal components of superego functioning and the importance of the idealized parent in the healthy development of the mature superego. I will discuss Schafer in the final section of this chapter.)

在《论自恋：一篇导论》[On Narcissism: An Introduction]（1914a）中，弗洛伊德讨论了作为原初婴儿期自恋之储存库的 自我理想 [ego ideal] 如何在内部被创造出来。对自我理想的认同[identification]带来价值感、正向的角色示范[role modeling]、抱负[ambitions]与志向[aspirations]。这一观念后来有一个更为重要的展开，即超我[superego]概念。与自我理想不同，超我被灌注了攻击性[aggressive]能量；除其理想性的属性之外，它也是被内化的权威[authority]、恐惧与惩罚的来源。最终，精神分析师们开始区分敌意的超我与慈爱的自我理想。（关于超我功能中的力比多[libidinal]成分，以及被理想化的父母在成熟超我之健康发展中的重要性，另见罗伊·谢弗[Roy Schafer]的《弗洛伊德结构理论中慈爱的与被爱的超我》[The Loving and Beloved Superego in Freud's Structural Theory]（1960）。我将在本章最后一节讨论谢弗。）

In addition to their recognition that idealization plays an important role in the initial internalization and structuralization of the superego and ego ideal, classical analysts recognized that the idealization of external objects continues to be important throughout life. For example, adolescents have a developmentally appropriate need to idealize role models and mentors, who then become the sources of real-life choices and ambitions. But even in adulthood, the use of idealized relationships can function to support and guide people, granting to the external world some of the power and perfection of the archaic anaclitic relation with the parent. In addition to this normal role, idealization can also play a role in psychopathology, most commonly with the projection of superego fantasies, resulting in external objects that are not only aggrandized but charged with aggression, perhaps tormenting and restrictive.

除了认识到理想化在超我与自我理想的初始内化与结构化中扮演重要角色之外，经典分析师们也认识到，对外部对象的理想化在人的一生中始终重要。例如，青少年有一种在发展上恰当的需要，去理想化角色榜样与导师[mentors]，后者于是成为现实生活选择与抱负的来源。但即便在成年期，对理想化关系的运用也能起支持与引导人的作用，把与父母之间那种古老依附性关系的部分力量与完美，赋予外部世界。除了这一正常作用之外，理想化也可能在精神病理学中起作用，最常见的是把超我幻想投射出去，导致外部对象不仅被抬高[aggrandized]，还被灌注了攻击性，或许变得折磨人、限制人。

Joseph Sandler (1987) argued that Freud never clearly distinguished between the superego and ego ideal in his writings. Sandler saw this as a problem, since the superego is essentially a prohibitory structure and ultimately hostile to the ego or self. He believed that there was a need to posit another internal structure, the ideal self, which exists as a representation within the mind. He wrote that the sources of the contents of the ideal self could be categorized as:

约瑟夫·桑德勒[Joseph Sandler]（1987）主张，弗洛伊德在其著作中从未清楚地区分超我与自我理想。桑德勒认为这是个问题，因为超我在本质上是禁止性的结构[prohibitory structure]，并且最终对自我或自体怀有敌意。他认为有必要设定另一种内部结构，即 理想自体 [ideal self]，它作为心灵内部的一种表征[representation]而存在。他写道，理想自体之内容的来源可分类如下：

1. Identification with aspects of loved, admired, or feared objects. 2. Identification with the image of the "good" or "desirable" child as conveyed by the objects. 3. Identification with previous shapes of the individual's own self. By this is meant the construction of ideals based upon the wish to attain "ideal" states previously experienced or in fantasy. (p. 87).

1. 对所受、所敬、所惧之对象诸方面的认同。 2. 对对象所传达的"好的"或"令人向往的"孩子形象的认同。 3. 对个体自身先前诸形态的认同。此处指的是：基于先前体验过或在幻想中体验过的"理想"状态之愿望，而建构出理想。 （p. 87）

Unlike the superego, which results from the internalization of representations loaded with the aggressive fantasies that characterize the dynamics of the Oedipus complex, the ideal self develops in interaction with the idealized parents, both real and fantastic. Sandler noted that these ideal images of self and object exist at all levels of consciousness, and thus can influence multiple aspects of psychological life. He argued that the function of the ideal self is not limited to self-esteem regulation, but, in a broader sense, it also bears on the well-being of the entire organization of self-images that populate the representational world. He wrote:

与超我不同——超我源自那些承载着攻击性幻想（这些幻想构成了俄狄浦斯情结[Oedipus complex]的动力）的表征之内化——理想自体是在与被理想化的父母（既包括真实的父母，也包括幻想中的父母）的互动中发展出来的。桑德勒指出，这些关于自体与对象的理想意象存在于一切意识层面，因而能影响心理生活的多个方面。他主张，理想自体的功能并不局限于自尊调节[self-esteem regulation]；在更宽泛的意义上，它还关乎充斥于表征世界[representational world]的整个自体意象之组织的健康。他写道：

The establishment of an ideal self within the representational world of the child provides him with a potential source of well-being. Some of the libido attached to the objects can now be transferred to the ideal and the child becomes more independent of the love, praise, and encouragement of his objects, attempting to avoid the disappointment and frustration by living up to his ideal self. (1987, p. 88)

在孩子之表征世界内部建立起一个理想自体，为其提供了健康福祉[well-being]的一个潜在来源。先前附着于诸对象的一部分力比多，如今可以转移到这个理想之上，孩子于是变得更不依赖其对象的爱、赞扬与鼓励，并试图通过活出自己的理想自体来避免失望与挫折。 （1987, p. 88）

Unlike the prohibitive and restrictive function of superego representations, the ideal self and ideal objects have a positive impact. They offer the child the opportunity to achieve increasing degrees of self-refinement and even perfection. The projection and internalization of these ideals create the feeling of the world's value and, by association, the self's worth.

与超我表征那种禁止性与限制性的功能不同，理想自体与理想对象具有积极的影响。它们为孩子提供了机会，去达到越来越高程度的自我精炼[self-refinement]乃至完美。这些理想的投射与内化，创造出对世界之价值的感受，并连带创造出对自体之价值的感受。

Heinz Kohut (1971) placed idealization at the center of his model of the self. He argued that the internalization of selfobject experiences is essential to the structurization of the self. Specifically, the functions of the idealized parent imago and the grandiose self are broken down and taken in as self-structure. The essence of any selfobject experience is idealization. Parent representations, self-representations, and the relationship with the twin-other are all idealized. Empathic failure creates crisis in idealization, the sustaining connection in which the individual feels known and valued is broken. Transmuting internalization converts the idealization embedded in the selfobject tie into a self-reflective sense of one's own worth.

海因茨·科胡特[Heinz Kohut]（1971）把理想化置于其自体模型的中心。他主张，自体客体体验[selfobject experience]的内化对自体的结构化至关重要。具体而言，被理想化的父母意象[idealized parent imago]与夸大自体[grandiose self]的功能被分解，并作为自体结构被纳入。任何自体客体体验的本质都是理想化。父母表征[parent representations]、自体表征[self-representations]，以及与孪生他者[twin-other]的关系，都被理想化。共情性失败[empathic failure]在理想化中造成危机；那种使个体感到被了解、被珍视的维系性联结被打破。转变性内化[transmuting internalization]把嵌入自体客体纽带中的理想化，转化为一种自我反身性的[self-reflective]自身价值感。

Of course, other functions are internalized, but they are nothing compared to the experience of oneself as being of value. In normal instances, the capacity to idealize the other is also maintained. Even with the recognition of the other's subjectivity (warts and all), the capacity to value and love the other endures and colors the emotional tone of one's attitude toward the social world. In addition, the sustained idealization of twinship ties matures into an ongoing valuing of friendships and love relationships.

当然，被内化的还有其他功能，但它们与"把自身体验为有价值的"相比都微不足道。在正常情形中，理想化他人的能力同样得以保持。即便认识到他者的主体性（连同其瑕疵[warts and all]），珍视与爱他者的能力依然持存，并为一己对社会世界之态度的情感基调染上色彩。此外，孪生关系[twinship ties]持续的理想化，会成熟为对友谊与爱情关系的一种持续的珍视。

Joseph Lichtenberg (1989) took issue with Kohut's idealization model. He argued that the keys to the infant's experience do not lie in the preservation of idealization, grandiosity, omnipotence, or any version of these reified concepts. Rather, he noted:

约瑟夫·利希滕贝格[Joseph Lichtenberg]（1989）对科胡特的理想化模型提出异议。他认为，理解婴儿经验的关键，不在于理想化、夸大、全能[omnipotence]，或这些被具体化[reified]之概念的任何版本的保全。毋宁说，他指出：

The experience of the baby is best conveyed by visualizing a scene in which a smiling infant's whole body becomes taut and vibrant as his mother smiles back and exclaims, "Oh, what a beautiful smile!" A 10-month-old baby conveys the sense of sharing an intersubjective state and her mother who is being entertained by father making funny faces, looking at each other laughing, almost simultaneously pointing to "the clown," and looking at each other. Or the expansive joy of the child securely riding on his father's back or shoulders or being tossed in the air. (p. 265)

要传达婴儿的体验，最好的办法是设想这样一幕：一个微笑着的婴儿，在母亲回以微笑并惊叹"哦，多美的笑容！"时，整个身体变得紧绷而充满生气[taut and vibrant]。一个 10 个月大的婴儿传达出一种共享主体间状态[intersubjective state]的感受，而她的母亲正被父亲扮鬼脸逗乐，两人相视大笑，几乎同时指向"那个小丑"，又彼此对看。或者，是孩子稳稳地骑在父亲背上或肩上、或被抛向空中时那种扩展性的喜悦[expansive joy]。 （p. 265）

Lichtenberg believed that, rather than abstract notions like idealization and grandiosity, it is these types of intensely affective, intersubjective, and complex relational experiences that are internalized as components of the child's representational world. It is the internalization and generalization of these types of relational model scenes that form the root of later behaviors. He concluded:

利希滕贝格认为，被内化为孩子表征世界之组成部分的，并非理想化与夸大这类抽象概念，而正是这些强度极高的、主体间的、复杂的关系体验。正是这类关系模型场景[relational model scenes]的内化与泛化，构成了后来行为的根源。他总结道：

I regard the mirror, alter ego, and idealizing transferences, and the experiences beginning in infancy and continuing throughout life from which they are transformed, to be nuclear model scenes of the attachment and affiliative motivational system. When the appropriate lived experiences are present in infancy, the self will throughout life be motivated to repeat them, improve on them, and adapt new versions to new challenges and opportunities. When these experiences are inadequate at any time of life, the self will want to seek or restore them. (1989, p. 266)

我认为，镜像移情[mirror transference]、另我移情[alter ego transference]与理想化移情[idealizing transference]，以及那些始于婴儿期并贯穿一生、经其转化而来的体验，都是依恋与亲和动机系统[affiliative motivational system]的核心模型场景[nuclear model scenes]。当适宜的生活体验在婴儿期存在时，自体将终其一生受到激励去重复它们、改进它们，并使新的版本适应新的挑战与机遇。当这些体验在生命的任何时刻不充分时，自体就会想要寻求或恢复它们。 （1989, p. 266）

Lichtenberg emphasized the interactive and intersubjective experiences that lie at the core of idealization. As in the examples given in the preceding quotation, the child and parents are engaged in mutually enhancing interactions that lead to physical and psychological states of mutual appreciation and enjoyment. In fact, it is precisely these types of compelling and complex engagements in which the experience of idealization is stimulated, elaborated, and structuralized. The mother's statement about the child's "beautiful smile" and the child's nonverbal response – "such a beautiful mother" – repeated over and over, in an increasingly amplified and mutually confirming process of communication, lead to a sustained expectation of shared valuation. Idealization is a conceptualization of this process of mutually amplified idealization.

利希滕贝格强调位居理想化核心的那些互动性与主体间性体验。如上一段引文所举的例子，孩子与父母投入到相互增益的互动中，导向彼此欣赏与享受的身体与心理状态。事实上，正是在这类引人入胜、复杂精微的投入之中，理想化的体验被激发、被精致化、被结构化。母亲对孩子"美丽的笑容"的表述，以及孩子非言语的回应——"多么美丽的妈妈"——一遍又一遍地重复，在一种不断增强、相互确认的沟通过程中，导向对共享价值[shared valuation]的持续期待。理想化，就是对这一相互放大的理想化过程的一种概念化。

Others have argued to expand our view of idealization. These psychoanalysts view idealization as a broad, ongoing human need, more central to human psychological and relational life than was thought to be the case by earlier analysts. Let me briefly note the views of Person and Mitchell.

另一些论者主张扩展我们对理想化的看法。这些精神分析师把理想化视为一种广泛而持续的人类需要，它比早期分析师所认为的更加居于人类心理与人际生活的核心。请容我简要介绍珀森[Person]与米切尔[Mitchell]的观点。

Ethel Person (1992) believes that idealization is a means by which people give meaning and value to their lives in an increasingly multicultural, nontraditional social world. She has described the phenomenon of "sequential idealization" (p. 94), which occurs throughout a person's life as various objects are invested with high value. The role of idealization in love, work, and self-experience becomes even more important when traditional values and prescribed social roles no longer hold such a compelling place in life. It is through idealization that we establish highly valued, psychologically meaningful relations to the object world, both internally and externally. In a shifting, decentered, nonauthoritarian world, the establishment and sustaining of ideals and relationships with ideal objects is essential to a healthy life and to optimal self-esteem.

埃塞尔·珀森[Ethel Person]（1992）认为，在一个日益多元文化、非传统的社会世界里，理想化是人们为自己的生活赋予意义与价值的一种手段。她描述了"序列性理想化"[sequential idealization]（p. 94）这一现象：它发生在人的一生之中，随着各种对象被投注以高度价值而更迭。当传统的价值与被规定的社会角色不再在生活中占据那样不可抗拒的位置时，理想化在爱、工作与自体体验中的作用就变得更加重要。正是通过理想化，我们与对象世界（无论内部还是外部）建立起被高度珍视、在心理上有意义的关系。在一个流动的、去中心的、非威权主义的[nonauthoritarian]世界里，建立并维系理想、以及与理想对象的关系，对健康的生活与最佳的自尊而言是不可或缺的。

Additionally, Stephen Mitchell (2002) argued that idealization is an essential human capacity that allows us to experience a powerful and passionate sense of the value of the self and of our loved ones. He disputed the notion that idealization is an undesirable distortion of reality, placing it squarely at the center of the human desire to give meaning and value to life. In his posthumous book Can Love Last? he argued that romance is a form of idealization that enhances intimacy, and rather than unrealistically distancing people from one another, it is romance that creates the most intense and therefore vulnerable potential for human connection. Mitchell refuted the idea that idealization was inevitably linked to archaic forms of self-aggrandizement arising out of infantile self-states of omnipotence. He viewed idealization as a lifelong phenomenon that has its source in the relational matrix:

此外，斯蒂芬·米切尔（2002）主张，理想化是一种根本性的人类能力，它使我们能够体验到自体与我们所爱之人的价值，并且这种体验是强有力而充满激情的。他质疑"理想化是对现实的一种不可取的扭曲"这一说法，径直把它放在人类为生活赋予意义与价值这一愿望的中心。在他身后出版的《爱能持久吗？》[Can Love Last?] 中，他主张浪漫[romance]是理想化的一种形式，它增进亲密；而且，浪漫并非不切实际地把人彼此隔开，恰恰是浪漫创造出人际联结中最强烈、因而也最脆弱的潜能。米切尔驳斥了"理想化不可避免地与源自婴儿期全能自体状态的那种古老的自体抬高[self-aggrandizement]形式相连"这一观念。他把理想化视为一种终身现象，其源头在关系基质[relational matrix]之中：

Grandiosity and idealization function as interactional modes, arising as learned patterns of integrating relationships, and maintained as the vehicle for intimate connections (real and imagined) with others. (p. 200)

夸大与理想化作为互动模式[interactional modes]发挥功能，作为整合关系的习得模式而产生，并被维持为与他人（真实与想象中）建立亲密联结的载体。 （p. 200）

Mitchell believed that analysts, in their emphasis on narcissism, have viewed only half of the puzzle in their discussion of idealization. The other half is relatedness and its incorporation of idealization as a learned mode of connection with others. (Unfortunately for our purposes here, Mitchell's discussion of idealization was brief and presented as part of his consideration of other subjects.)

米切尔认为，分析师们在讨论理想化时，由于过分强调自恋，只看到了这一谜题的一半。另一半则是关联[relatedness]，以及它把理想化纳入为一种与他人建立联结的习得模式。（对本文的目的而言不幸的是，米切尔对理想化的讨论较为简短，且是作为他对其他主题之考察的一部分而提出的。）

Psychoanalysts have traditionally viewed idealization as a regressive or defensive psychological process. The assumption has been that idealization distorts and misrepresents reality, and therefore needs correction. An objective of analytic treatment is the reduction of idealization, as part of the eventual full recognition of true reality. However, analysts like Sandler and Person have argued that idealization is a normal and even desirable process that not only plays an important part not just in childhood, but also in mature forms of love, morality, and self-experience.

精神分析师传统上把理想化视为一种退行性的[regressive]或防御性的心理过程。其假设是：理想化扭曲并误现了现实，因而需要矫正。分析性治疗的一个目标就是减少理想化，作为最终充分认识真实现实的一部分。然而，像桑德勒与珀森这样的分析师主张，理想化是一种正常的、甚至是可取的过程；它不仅在童年期扮演重要角色，在成熟形式的爱、道德与自体体验中同样如此。

I believe that Lichtenberg and Mitchell offer the most significant revisions of the concept. Both these analysts argue that idealization is best understood as a mode of relatedness. Lichtenberg sees idealization as synonymous with the experience of attunement and fittedness that characterize the experience of the parent/infant dyad.

我认为，利希滕贝格与米切尔对这一概念作出了最重要的修订。这两位分析师都主张，理想化最好被理解为一种关联模式[mode of relatedness]。利希滕贝格把理想化视为与调谐[attunement]和适配[fittedness]的体验同义——而后者正是亲子二元关系经验的特征。

The intent of this chapter is to further expand on this new view of idealization. Backed by data from infant research, I will show that idealization plays a part in attachment and in the development of internal representations of self and other. Also discussed are the role of value affects in idealization, and I suggest ways in which idealization is elaborated into adult relationships and self-experience.

本章的意图是进一步展开这一关于理想化的新观点。以婴儿研究[infant research]的数据为支持，我将表明理想化在依恋以及对自体与他人之内部表征的发展中起作用。文中还将讨论价值情感[value affects]在理想化中的作用，并就理想化如何被精致化为成年关系与自体体验提出我的看法。

Idealization: A Developmental/Relational Model（理想化：一个发展性/关系性模型）

Idealization begins in earliest infancy, but unlike Freud, who located its source in the infant's self-aggrandizement, I believe that idealization crystallizes within an intersubjective dialogue in which a shared sense of valuing results in the capacity to develop and sustain connection. There is a process of shared idealization in the normal and healthy mother/infant interaction: mutual pleasure, excitement, and joy at the sight of each other, the thrill of touching and vocalizing – this is the stuff of idealization. But this is no accident; I believe that the need to idealize is one of the driving forces in the motivation for attachment. Thus, idealization becomes psychologically structured; and as the tendency to idealize is extended beyond the early relational context, it becomes a source of motivation to seek out, create, and engage with objects and experiences that are highly valued and gratifying, such as in the production and/or enjoyment of art.

理想化始于最早的婴儿期；但与弗洛伊德把其源头定位于婴儿的自体抬高[self-aggrandizement]不同，我认为理想化结晶于一种主体间对话之中——在其中，共享的价值感带来发展与维系联结的能力。在正常而健康的母婴互动中，存在一种共享理想化的过程：相互的愉悦、兴奋，看到彼此时的欣喜，触碰与发声时的战栗——这些就是理想化的质料。但这并非偶然；我认为，理想化的需要是依恋动机的驱动力之一。于是，理想化在心理上被结构化；而当理想化的倾向延展到早期关系情境之外，它就成为一种动机来源，驱使人去寻求、创造并投入那些被高度珍视且令人满足的对象与体验，例如艺术的生产与/或欣赏。

According to attachment theory, there is a biological need for the infant and the parent to get physically close to each other, thus facilitating essential caretaking behaviors. In this way, closeness increases the probability of survival for the dependent (and for the species in general). The quality of attachment is crucial, not only for physical survival, but also for psychological security and well-being and the general capacity to engage effectively in life and relationships.

根据依恋理论[attachment theory]，婴儿与父母之间存在一种生物学上的需要，要彼此在身体上靠近，从而促成必要的照料行为[caretaking behaviors]。这样一来，亲近就提高了受养者（以及整个物种）存活的概率。依恋的质量至关重要，它不仅关乎身体存活，也关乎心理上的安全与健康，以及有效投入生活与关系的一般能力。

I suggest that shared idealization of parent and infant, each for the other, as well as idealization of the relationship, is important to the strength of the desire for attachment, and to the quality of the experience of the other and of the relationship. The child does not seek out just anyone; he or she is drawn to and desires to get close to a special object that is felt to be ideal: "my beautiful, perfect, irreplaceable mother." And the same goes for the mother's need for the child, who, she feels, is "the best baby, my most beautiful little one."

我认为，父母与婴儿之间彼此对对方的共享理想化，以及对这个关系本身的理想化，对依恋愿望的强度、对体验他者与体验这段关系的质量，都很重要。孩子寻求的并非随便什么人；他/她被吸引着、渴望着去靠近一个被感受为理想的特殊对象："我那美丽的、完美的、无可替代的妈妈。"母亲对孩子的需要同样如此——在她的感觉中，孩子是"最好的宝宝，我最美丽的小家伙"。

In fact, this adds an important dimension to the phenomenon of bonding in which the mother begins to value the newborn. At the core of bonding is the idealization of the child of the mother and vice versa. The pleasurable experience of engrossment with the perfect object increases the parent's attunement to the infant, which results in greater emotional health as well as increased survival probability. The experience of the ideal relationship makes each partner want to protect, pursue, and cultivate the ideal tie. I believe that it is this biologically based drive to idealize that contributes to the motivation to form, maintain, and even intensify attachment. Therefore, in parent/infant interaction, the observer should see behaviors and communications that reflect idealization in action. (See Emde et al. [1987] for a discussion of "positive affect sharing" as the basis for the development of morality in young children.)

事实上，这为"联结[bonding]"这一现象——母亲开始珍视新生儿的过程——增添了一个重要维度。联结的核心，是母亲对孩子的理想化，以及孩子对母亲的理想化，反之亦然。那种全神贯注于完美对象的愉悦体验，增强了父母对婴儿的调谐，从而带来更好的情绪健康以及更高的存活概率。理想关系的体验，使每一方都想保护、追寻并培育这条理想的纽带。我相信，正是这种基于生物学的理想化驱力，促成了形成、维系甚至强化依恋的动机。因此，在亲子互动中，观察者应当看到那些反映出理想化正在发生的行为与沟通。（关于"积极情感共享"[positive affect sharing]作为幼儿道德发展之基础的讨论，见 Emde 等 [1987]。）

Confirmation of this can be found in recent infant research. In The Interpersonal World of the Infant, Daniel Stern (1985) demonstrates that it is the parent's attunement to the infant's changing affect states that contributes most to the development of self-experience. Stern stresses that it is not only just awareness and remediation of negative affects that are important, but also attunement to the sharing of positive affects. He highlights these vitality affects as reflecting the infant's spontaneous and lively response to experience. The parent's mirroring and resonance with these affects play a major part in the child's sense of aliveness and vitality.

这一点可在晚近的婴儿研究中得到印证。在《婴儿的人际世界》[The Interpersonal World of the Infant] 中，丹尼尔·斯特恩[Daniel Stern]（1985）表明，正是父母对婴儿不断变化的情感状态的调谐，对自体体验的发展贡献最大。斯特恩强调，重要的不只是对负面情感的觉察与修复，还包括对积极情感之共享的调谐。他把这些 活力情感 [vitality affects] 凸显出来，视之为婴儿对经验之自发而活跃的反应的体现。父母对这些情感的镜映[mirroring]与共鸣，对孩子"活着、有活力"的感受起了主要作用。

In addition, Robert Emde (1992), in his research, discovered the central role of positive affects in development, and, like Stern, Emde emphasized the developmental importance of positive affect sharing and emotional availability in infancy. (See also Schore [2004, pp. 8–11] for a review of research concerning the crucial role of the sharing and amplification of positive affect states in early brain development.) Building on the ideas of both Emde and Stern regarding positive affect, I suggest that there is an aspect of affect sharing that has not yet been articulated: the dimension of value. These affects reflect the personal significance and value that certain experiences, objects, and people have to the infant. They can be expressed noisily – for example, pleasurable laughter at the unexpected entrance of a loved one – or quietly, such as in the gentle cooing of a mother while rocking her child, or in a woman stroking her lover's body.

此外，罗伯特·埃姆德[Robert Emde]（1992）在其研究中发现了积极情感在发展中的核心作用；与斯特恩一样，埃姆德强调婴儿期积极情感共享与情感可得性[emotional availability]在发展上的重要性。（关于积极情感状态的共享与放大在早期大脑发育中的关键作用，另见 Schore [2004, pp. 8–11] 对相关研究的综述。）在埃姆德与斯特恩关于积极情感的观念基础上，我认为情感共享中还有一个尚未被阐明的面向：价值 的维度。这些情感反映了某些体验、对象与人对于婴儿的个人意义与价值。它们可以被喧闹地表达出来——例如，所爱之人意外出现时那种愉快的笑声——也可以被安静地表达，例如母亲摇着孩子时轻柔的哼唱，或一个女人抚摩爱人身体时的动作。

Most important, these value affects are communicative – they convey how one feels about the other. Affects such as love have internal referents, but they are ineluctably tied to the other. "I love" is only half of the equation, while "I love you" is a value affect. A familiar value affect is what Kohut (1971) described as the gleam in the mother's eye. The best way to categorize this affect is that it communicates the parent's pleasure, joy, and pride at the sight of his or her child.

最重要的是，这些 价值情感 [value affects] 是沟通性的——它们传达了一个人对他者的感受。像爱这样的情感有其内部的指涉物[internal referents]，但它们不可避免地与他者相连。"我爱"只是这个等式的一半，而"我爱你"才是一种价值情感。一种为人熟知的价值情感，就是科胡特（1971）所描述的 母亲眼中的光芒 [the gleam in the mother's eye]。对这一情感最好的归类方式是：它传达出父母看到自己孩子时的愉悦、欣喜与自豪。

Within the relational matrix, these affects mutually communicate the experience of shared value. Admiration of the father, joy at the sight of the mother, pleasure about playing with a child, excitement about the proximity of a lover – all these communicate in a visceral and compelling way a sense of valuing and idealization.

在关系基质之中，这些情感相互传达着共享价值的体验。对父亲的钦佩，看到母亲时的欣喜，与孩子玩耍时的愉悦，对爱人靠近的兴奋——所有这些都以一种发自肺腑而不可抗拒的方式，传达出一种珍视与理想化的感受。

Value affects can be observed in many interchanges between mother and child. Most models of affect view them as endogenously arising, semi-somatic eruptions of feeling states, although there may occasionally be an external stimulus. On the other hand, value affects are usually both elicited and reciprocated. Amplification and elaboration of the affective exchange result in a state of shared idealization for the dyad.

价值情感可以在母婴之间的许多交流中被观察到。多数的情感模型把它们视为内源性地产生[endogenously arising]、半躯体性的[semi-somatic]情感状态之迸发，尽管偶尔也可能有外部刺激。另一方面，价值情感通常既被引发，也被回馈。情感交换的放大与精致化，为二元关系带来一种共享理想化的状态。

In examining the infant research studies of Stern (1985) and Beebe and Lachmann (2002), one finds interesting observational support for the notion that idealization arises and is elaborated in the dynamic interchange between parent and child. These researchers focus on the issue of affective attunement between mother and infant; however, I think it is also important to consider the nature of the affects being shared. In positive interactions, it is obvious that both members of the dyad are resonating with each other's value affects. That is, mother and child are communicating through affect the feeling of valuing each other. There is the gleam in the mother's eye, the smile on her face, the excitement in her tone of voice, all of which say, "I love you. You are special to me. You are the best little baby in the whole wide world." And then there is the child's simultaneous communication that "You are the best, most beautiful mother in the world."

在考察斯特恩（1985）以及比布与拉赫曼[Beebe and Lachmann]（2002）的婴儿研究时，人们会发现一些有趣的观察证据，支持"理想化在亲子之间的动态交流中产生并得以精致化"这一看法。这些研究者关注母婴之间的情感调谐问题；然而，我认为同样重要的是考虑被共享的情感本身具有何种性质。在积极的互动中，显然二元关系的双方都在与彼此的价值情感共鸣。也就是说，母亲与孩子通过情感交流着相互珍视的感受。有母亲眼中的光芒，她脸上的笑容，她语调中的兴奋——这一切都在说："我爱你。你对我很特别。你是这整个广阔世界里最好的小宝宝。"而与此同时，还有孩子在传达："你是世界上最好的、最美丽的妈妈。"

These affects are mutually confirming responses between individuals who value each other. Repeated over time, such interactions increase idealization, which becomes structuralized and sustained in the relationship. As a result, the affective state of the other (for example, their valuing of the subject) becomes incorporated into the complementary value affect. My love for you increases in intensity as I accept your love for me. Each value affect adds to the other and vice versa.

这些情感是彼此珍视的个体之间相互确认的回应。随着时间反复发生，这类互动增强了理想化，而理想化在关系中被结构化并得以维系。其结果是，他者的情感状态（例如，他对主体的珍视）被纳入互补的价值情感之中。随着我接受你对我的爱，我对你的爱在强度上有所增加。每一种价值情感都增益于另一种，反之亦然。

To summarize this point of view, then, the infant is hardwired to seek out the special object and respond to it. The parent, in bonding, experiences the child as ideal and treats the child accordingly, as "my precious darling." The infant reciprocates in increasingly specific and idiosyncratic ways, and, over time, as recognition of the mother increases, the infant returns her joy. Each member of the couple stirs up in the other an exciting, confirming sense of shared idealization: "we are a wonderful couple." Value affects are consistently resonant and increasingly amplified. Thus, the child gradually develops internal representations of the ideal parent who will protect and mirror him or her – the ideal self that is important and worthy of self-care and autonomous efforts at survival. The mirrored idealization results in the child's experience of the self as ideal, thus leading him or her to feel more integrated and loved.

那么，概括这一观点：婴儿天生就被设定为去寻求那个特殊对象并对其作出反应。父母在联结之中把孩子体验为理想的，并据此对待孩子，视之为"我珍贵的宝贝"。婴儿以越来越具体而独特的方式回馈；随着时间推移，随着对母亲的识别[recognition]增强，婴儿把欣喜回赠给她。这一对偶中的每一方都在对方身上激起一种令人兴奋而具确认感的共享理想化："我们是一对了不起的伴侣。"价值情感持续地产生共鸣，并被不断增强。于是，孩子逐渐发展出关于那个会保护他、镜映他的理想父母的内在表征——那个重要、值得自我照料、值得为存活而自主努力的理想自体。被镜映的理想化，使孩子体验到自体是理想的，从而使他/她感到更整合、更被爱。

Over the course of development, idealization is both refined and reconciled with reality. Most important, the ability and willingness of the child to recognize the subjectivity (and real flaws) of the mother, while at the same time maintaining an idealized valuation of her, is an achievement that permits the maintenance of idealization in the face of impingements and failures. Of course, as Kohut showed, this maintenance of idealization can only occur when there is nontraumatic failure and gradual recognition of the mother or parent as a separate human being. This process is an enriching one for the child, provided that the real-world parent can be both known and idealized, allowing idealization to expand from fantasy and archaic wish fulfillment to a valuation that survives reality testing.

在发展过程中，理想化既被提炼，也与现实相调和。最重要的是，孩子既能识别母亲的主体性（及其真实缺陷），同时又保持对她的一种理想化评价——这种能力与意愿是一项成就，它使理想化在遭遇侵扰[impingements]与失败时仍得以维系。当然，正如科胡特所示，这种理想化的维系，只有在失败是非创伤性的、并且逐渐认识到母亲或父母是一个独立的人时，才可能发生。只要现实中的父母既能被认识、也能被理想化，这一过程对孩子就是丰富的，它使理想化从幻想与古老愿望的满足，扩展为一种能够经受现实检验[reality testing]的评价。

In addition, over developmental time, the urge to idealize is expanded and extended beyond the parent/infant dyad to other objects and experiences in the child's life. Winnicott's (1971) model of the transitional object is useful in understanding this process. The transitional object was primarily viewed as a psychological space between the subjective and objective realms. The object, both created and found, functions to facilitate the child's sense of the self as embedded, which, at least regarding the object, interpenetrates fantasy life. But the transitional object is also an object of value; it is the first idealized object other than the parent. It is the gradual recognition of the object's existence outside the omnipotence of the self – and the object's continued idealization by the child – that contribute to the extension of idealization beyond the dyad.

此外，在发展过程中，理想化的冲动被扩大并延展到亲子二元关系之外，指向孩子生活中的其他对象与体验。温尼科特[Winnicott]（1971）关于过渡性对象[transitional object]的模型，对理解这一过程很有用。过渡性对象最初被视为位于主观领域与客观领域之间的一处心理空间。这个既是创造出来、又是被找到的对象，其功能是促进孩子对自体作为"嵌入的[embedded]"的感受，而后者（至少在涉及该对象的层面）与幻想生活相互渗透。但过渡性对象同时也是一个有价值的对象；它是除父母之外的第一个被理想化的对象。正是逐渐认识到对象存在于自体之全能之外——以及孩子对该对象的持续理想化——共同促成了理想化超出二元关系的延展。

Furthermore, the developmental model of idealization has important implications for the view of idealization as a distortion or an unrealistic valuation of reality. As noted earlier, Mitchell (2002) disputed the idea that idealization was inevitably illusory. He argued that contemporary psychoanalysis, grounded as it is in a postmodern worldview, saw the human world as a construction in which meaning and value arise not out of inherent qualities of objects, but as modes of relatedness that crystallize in interaction with important others and are elaborated and expanded across the relational world. In this light, to say that idealization is illusory is perhaps an oxymoron; a negative judgment would therefore be implied about illusion as opposed to "reality."

再者，理想化的发展性模型，对"理想化是对现实的一种扭曲或不切实际的评价"这一看法具有重要含义。如前所述，米切尔（2002）质疑"理想化必然是一种幻觉"这一观念。他主张，当代精神分析既植根于后现代世界观，就把人类世界视为一种建构；在其中，意义与价值并非源自对象的固有品质，而是作为一种关联模式而产生——它们在重要他人的互动中结晶，并在整个关系世界中得到精致化与扩展。就此而言，说理想化是幻觉，或许是一种自相矛盾[oxymoron]；因为这样一来，相对于"现实"，就暗示了对幻觉的一种否定性判断。

However, I would argue that all value judgments are illusory because they are not qualities of reality, but rather statements about the worth of objects or events to human desire and need. On the other hand, paradoxically, idealization – rather than blocking recognition – seems to enhance perception and the cognitive and emotional significance of the object. For example, the gaze of the nursing child is not typically languid or dulled by the closeness of the ideal mother; in fact, in most instances, one is struck by the child's alertness – the way in which he or she appears to search the mother's face, watching intently and appearing to be vividly aware of each expression, each line, and each passing mood on the mother's face. And in an adult's love, either for a child or for a lover, the idealization of the cherished other does not typically lead to lack of awareness; rather, there is an intensified recognition, an alertness to the internal and external state of the object, its mood, health, and gestures. Love is only blind in certain pathological relationships that require the denial of the other's subjectivity. But in normal love relations, love opens the eyes and other senses, reality becomes more vivid, and the true nature of the other (and self) is more apparent and meaningful.

然而，我要主张，一切价值判断都是幻觉性的，因为它们并非现实的品质，而是关于对象或事件对人的欲望与需要而言有何价值的陈述。另一方面，吊诡的是，理想化似乎并非阻断认识，反而增强了知觉，以及对象在认知与情感上的重要性。例如，吃奶的婴儿的凝视通常并非慵懒的、也未被理想母亲的靠近所钝化；事实上，在多数情形中，令人触动的是孩子的警觉——他/她似乎在搜寻母亲的脸，专注地注视，并且显得对母亲脸上每一处表情、每一道纹路、每一种掠过的情绪都鲜明地觉察。而在成年人的爱中（无论是对孩子还是对爱人），对所珍爱之人的理想化通常并不导致觉察的缺失；毋宁说，那里有一种被强化的识别，一种对对象内部与外部状态、对其情绪、健康与姿态的警觉。爱只有在某些要求否认他者主体性的病理性关系中才是盲目的。但在正常的爱的关系中，爱打开了眼睛与其他感官，现实变得更鲜明，他者（以及自体）的真实本性变得更显而易见、更有意味。

Parent and child experience idealized attachment in terms of fantasy and desire (as all later idealizations are also experienced). The most important fantasies are derived from sexual and aggressive wishes. One of the least discussed aspects of Oedipal relating is the idealized nature of these fantasies. As part of their relationship, the child and parent experience wishes for sexual involvement that are elaborated in fantasy, consistent with the highly valued nature of the idealized attachment. Given this, the sexual and aggressive fantasies take on an idealized nature, with the mother's being experienced not simply as a person but as a queen, the father a king, and the child as usurper to the throne (the regal nature of the original oedipal triangle was not simply a dramatic conceit, but a reflection of archaic idealization). Thus, sexuality within the child's internal world is elaborated not only as physical desire, but also as representations of a wonderful, powerful, and exalted nature.

父母与孩子都以幻想与欲望的方式来体验被理想化的依恋（正如后来一切理想化也被如此体验）。最重要的幻想源自性与攻击性的愿望。在俄狄浦斯式关联中，讨论得最少的一个方面，就是这些幻想的理想化性质。作为他们关系的一部分，孩子与父母都体验到对性卷入的愿望，而这些愿望在幻想中被精致化，与被理想化之依恋的高度珍贵性质相一致。有鉴于此，性与攻击性的幻想带上了一种理想化的性质：母亲不只被体验为一个人，而是被体验为女王；父亲是国王；孩子则是王位的僭越者（原初俄狄浦斯三角的"王家"性质，并不只是一个戏剧化的设喻，而是古老理想化的映现）。于是，孩子内部世界中的性，不仅被精致化为身体欲望，也被精致化为某种奇妙、有力而尊贵之性质的诸种表征。

Of course, these fantasy wishes are not realizable within the context of family relationships and must be repressed. The typical resolution of the Oedipal complex involves the internalization of these sexual and aggressive fantasies that are exceedingly idealized. Optimally, under conditions of normal development, the child develops the capacity to sublimate these wishes through activities of a socially acceptable nature, such as in later adult love relations and in artistic activity.

当然，这些幻想性的愿望在家庭关系的语境中无法实现，必须被压抑[repressed]。俄狄浦斯情结的典型解决，涉及对这些被极度理想化的性与攻击性幻想的内化。最理想的情形下，在正常发展的条件下，孩子发展出升华[sublimate]这些愿望的能力，通过社会可接受性质的活动——例如后来的成年爱的关系与艺术活动——来加以实现。

In addition, parent/child relations are not limited to mutual affection and idealization; clearly the adult's capacity to discipline, modulate, contain, and channel the child's aggression and sexuality is an essential part of the parenting role. Roy Schafer (1960) argued the following:

此外，亲子关系并不局限于相互的情感与理想化；显然，成人去规训[discipline]、调节[modulate]、涵容[contain]并引导[channel]孩子的攻击性与性的能力，是养育角色不可或缺的一部分。罗伊·谢弗（1960）有如下主张：

There is a necessity to examine the parent's superego to understand the child's. Much will depend on how right or conflict free the parent feels in his role of moral guide, how much he can genuinely and realistically act "in the divine conviction of doing the right thing . . . Unconflicted gentleness is likely to go hand in hand with unconflicted firmness." (pp. 184–185)

要理解孩子的超我，就必须考察父母的超我。这在很大程度上取决于父母在其道德引导者角色中感到多么正当、多么无冲突，取决于他能在多大程度上真诚而合乎现实地"怀着做正确之事的神圣信念"去行事……无冲突的温柔，很可能与无冲突的坚定携手同行。 （pp. 184–185）

Schafer believed that the optimal parent disciplines and even punishes the child without a loss in idealization. In fact, he argued that the child may experience some punishments as acts of love and protection from a powerful and protective parent. In other words, healthy idealization survives and may even be strengthened by the parent's efforts to instill in the child internal superego values that are important to self-regulation, adult relationships, and socialization. The essential thing is that the parent be able to communicate affection and admiration for the child, despite the need to set limits and even to punish. In this case, the reciprocal idealizing processes may be suspended but quickly restored, allowing for a sense of continuity in idealization and the maintenance of the fundamental integrity of the parent/child bond and sense of self (of child and parent).

谢弗相信，最理想的父母在规训甚至惩罚孩子时，并不会丧失理想化。事实上他主张，孩子可能把某些惩罚体验为来自一位有力而具保护性的父母的爱与保护之举。换言之，健康的理想化能够存活下来，甚至还可能因父母努力向孩子灌输那些对自我调节、成年关系与社会化都很重要的内部超我价值而得到强化。关键在于，尽管需要设定限制甚至施以惩罚，父母仍能对孩子传达情感与欣赏。在这种情况下，相互的理想化过程可能被暂时中断，但很快得以恢复，从而容许理想化具有一种连续感，并维系亲子纽带与自体感（孩子与父母双方的）的根本完整性。

However, in instances where the parent's own superego is impaired or untempered by healthy idealization, the parent/child interaction may be disrupted and the idealizing processes collapse. During acts of discipline, the parent may not only be uncertain of his or her own goodness, but also unable to sustain idealization of the child in the face of frustration and anger, or the parent may be unable to modulate aggression inherent in the moral structures that there is a wish to enforce. In these instances, the bond of idealization is broken and experienced as lost. All that remain are hostility and negative judgment. The child comes to feel shame, guilt, or hatred (or a combination of all three); the result is the classic Oedipal conflict and a severe superego.

然而，在父母自身的超我受损、或未受到健康理想化的调和的情形中，亲子互动可能被破坏，理想化过程随之崩塌。在施行规训时，父母可能不仅对自身之善心存疑虑，也无法在挫折与愤怒面前维系对孩子的理想化；或者，父母可能无力调节那些想要去执行的道德结构本身所固有的攻击性。在这些情形中，理想化的纽带断裂，并被体验为丧失。所剩下的只有敌意与否定性评判。孩子于是感到羞耻、罪疚或憎恨（或三者兼有）；其结果是经典的俄狄浦斯冲突与一个严苛的超我。

I would add that in such cases, there is also a profound rupture in the relational tie with the parent. The child and parent are estranged by acts of discipline, rather than united, and the child's sense of human connection may be broadened to an internal sense of being unfit or unacceptable, due to wishes and needs that have been attacked and repudiated by the parent. Ultimately, the collapse of idealization processes, as they have been both internalized and enacted, leads to clinical syndromes of a narcissistic or neurotic nature.

我要补充的是，在这类情形中，与父母的关系纽带也出现了深刻的断裂。孩子与父母因规训之举而被疏离，而非被联结；由于自己的愿望与需要遭到父母的攻击与拒斥，孩子对人类联结的感受可能被泛化为一种内在的"我不合适、我不可接受"之感。最终，理想化过程既被内化、又被付诸行动，其崩塌会导致自恋性或神经症性的临床综合征[clinical syndromes]。

What is the fate of idealization in these instances where the normal urge for enthrallment and wonder is met with indifference, rejection, or misattunement? I suggest three broad possible outcomes: (1) the suppression of idealization through affective deadening or withdrawal of attachment, (2) intensification of idealization of self or other in an effort to restore connection and heighten security and well-being, or (3) a combination of suppression and intensification in the splitting off of aspects of self and other that are idealized from those parts that are felt to be unwanted, deficient, or damaged (Kohut's [1971] vertical split).

在这些情形中——当那种对迷恋与惊奇[enthrallment and wonder]的正常渴望遭遇冷漠、拒绝或调谐失误[misattunement]时——理想化的命运如何？我提出三种宽泛的可能结果：（1）通过情感麻木[affective deadening]或撤回依恋[withdrawal of attachment]来压制理想化；（2）强化对自体或他人的理想化，以图恢复联结、提升安全感与健康福祉；或（3）在把自体与他者中那些被理想化的方面，与那些被感受为不想要的、有缺陷的或受损的部分分裂开来[splitting off]的过程中，压制与强化并存（科胡特 [1971] 所谓的垂直分裂[vertical split]）。

As an illustration of the clinical implications of these three outcomes, as well as of other vicissitudes of idealization, I offer the following case report. This patient, Dave, suffered the death of his mother in adolescence. As a result, for many years, he lacked the ability and motivation to idealize either himself or others. He experienced all three outcomes of idealization. Prior to and in the early days of treatment, Dave's capacity to idealize was repressed, and his unconscious fantasies of special or powerful parents were inaccessible to his conscious life.

为说明这三种结果的临床含义，以及理想化的其他变迁，我提供以下个案报告。这位病人戴夫[Dave]在青少年期经历了母亲的死亡。其结果是，多年来他既缺乏理想化自己、也缺乏理想化他人的能力与动机。他体验到了理想化的全部三种结果。在治疗之前以及治疗初期，戴夫理想化的能力被压抑，而他关于特殊或强大的父母的潜意识幻想，则无法进入其意识生活。

As treatment progressed, he experienced a renewed capacity to idealize, and engaged in a relationship with a charismatic man. The treatment most productively focused on the unsettling relationship with the aspects of self and other that had been depressed and depleted (associated with isolation and loss), and the self with other that became vitalizing and renewing, a door to a restored life.

随着治疗推进，他体验到理想化能力的复兴，并投入与一位具有卡里斯玛魅力[charismatic]的男性的关系之中。治疗最具成效地聚焦于一种令人不安的关系：一方面是那些被抑郁与耗竭（与隔绝和丧失相关）的自体与他者之方面，另一方面是变得有活力、具更新性的自体与他者，一扇通往复苏之生的门。

Clinical Illustration（临床例证）

Dave was a thirty-two-year-old man who sought analytic treatment due to chronic depression, academic problems, and long-term concerns about his self-worth and ability to engage in satisfying relationships. He was a doctoral student in economics at a major university and was working on his dissertation. He felt locked in a silent struggle with his advisers. His presenting concern was that these men seemed to want to attack and eliminate any expression of his self in his work. He discussed at length how he was asked to cut this or that section of his thesis, parts that he invariably felt expressed his own unique view of the problem. He complained of feeling alone, not taken seriously; he did not seem to measure up. At the same time, he complained about his superiors, whom he felt were blind to or uncaring about his talent and insights. He lived in a frustrating, depleted world, in which his subjective life languished.

戴夫是一名三十二岁的男性，因长期抑郁、学业问题，以及对其自我价值与投入满意关系之能力的长期担忧而寻求分析性治疗。他是某重点大学经济学专业的博士生，正在撰写学位论文。他感到自己陷入与导师们无声的角力之中。他的主诉困扰在于：这些人似乎想要攻击并消灭他在工作中任何自我的表达。他详细谈到自己如何被要求删去论文的某一部分——而凡此种种，他总会觉得那正表达了他对该问题的独特看法。他抱怨自己感到孤独、不被认真对待；他似乎总是不够格。与此同时，他又抱怨自己的上司，觉得他们对他才华与洞见视而不见、毫不在意。他生活在一个令人挫败、枯竭的世界里，他的主体性生活在此中日渐委顿。

Dave had grown up in a middle-class, white family that was good enough for most of his childhood. The primary problem that he remembered was the absence of his father from the family, due (overtly) to work demands. It became clear that the father rarely tried to connect to his son, and even when around the house, he was preoccupied and unavailable. Dave's closest relationship was to his mother, and he frequently recalled the times when he would sit with her in the kitchen while she cooked. She was attentive, and he would be able to discuss with her his daily experiences, concerns, and joys. To the present time, Dave remained a motivated and resourceful cook.

戴夫成长于一个中产白人家庭，这个家庭在他童年的大部分时光里都算"足够好"。他所记得的首要问题，是父亲在家庭中的缺席，表面上是因为工作需要。逐渐清楚的是，父亲很少尝试与儿子建立联结，即便在家中也心不在焉、不可接近。戴夫最亲近的关系是与母亲的；他时常回想那些他坐在厨房里陪母亲做饭的时刻。她很专注地听他说话，他可以与她谈论自己日常的经历、忧虑与喜悦。直到当时，戴夫仍然是一个有动力、有办法的厨师。

When Dave was fourteen, his mother was diagnosed with a cancer that would kill her four years later. The family did not talk about the mother's illness or her impending death. The children took up the slack for the mother, with Dave becoming the family cook. Over time, the family's life began to contract around the mother's cancer, and Dave remembered spending more and more time at home, losing touch with friends, escaping only for short rides on his motorcycle.

戴夫十四岁时，母亲被诊断出癌症，四年后这病夺去了她的生命。家里从不谈论母亲的病，也不谈她即将到来的死亡。孩子们替母亲分担了缺位的事务，戴夫成了家里的厨师。随着时间推移，家庭生活开始围绕着母亲的癌症而收缩；戴夫记得自己待在家里的时间越来越多，与朋友失去联系，唯一的逃离只是骑摩托车短途兜风。

A crucial memory was when, at the age of fifteen, as his mother's illness became apparent, he confronted his father, demanding that he tell him what was happening to her. "He just looked at me as if I was doing something wrong," Dave remembered. "Then he walked away. That was the closest we came to acknowledging what was happening . . . nothing."

一个关键的记忆是：他十五岁时，随着母亲的病情变得明显，他去面对父亲，要求父亲告诉他母亲究竟怎么了。"他只是看着我，好像我做错了什么事，"戴夫回忆道。"然后他走开了。那就是我们最接近承认正在发生什么的一次……什么也没有。"

In our early sessions, Dave and I spent a good deal of time exploring those years, as if it were necessary to give voice to what he had observed and how he had felt. Over time, his memories, restored and more vivid, became a major part of our sessions as he tried to reconstruct what had happened to his mother, his family, and himself.

在早期的会谈中，戴夫和我花了大量时间去探索那些年，仿佛有必要为他所观察到的一切、以及他当时的感受赋予声音。随着时间推移，他的记忆被恢复、变得更为鲜活，成为我们会谈的重要部分——他试图重构究竟发生了什么，发生在母亲、家庭与他自身身上。

The cancer took its time, and Dave's mother died in the summer of his eighteenth year. Soon after the funeral, he left for college far away. He returned home, where his father lived with Dave's two younger sisters, only for short stays, and they never talked about his mother's death again. From that point on, Dave pursued his academic career, eventually attending graduate school and entering a doctoral program at a major school of economics. His life was that of a solitary dreamer, his most positive activities being reading and indulging in big ideas that allowed him to give full rein to his creativity and excitement about life. But, in fact, his life tended to be dreary and lonely.

癌症拖延着，戴夫的母亲在他十八岁那年的夏天去世。葬礼之后不久，他便远赴他乡上大学。他回家探望——父亲与戴夫的两个妹妹住在那儿——但只作短暂停留，他们此后再也没有谈起母亲的死。从那时起，戴夫专攻学业，最终进入研究生院，考入一所著名经济学院的博士项目。他的生活如同一个孤独的梦想家，他最积极的活动是阅读，以及沉溺于那些宏大的观念——这让他得以充分释放自己的创造力与对生活的兴奋。但事实上，他的生活往往沉闷而孤独。

A year into his twice-weekly treatment, Dave began to talk about a charismatic political figure, Solodor, who visited his school. He was excited by this man's vision and ambition and began to talk about devoting his postgraduate work to economic policy development. When Solodor took over a major foundation, Dave, newly graduated, joined his administrative staff.

在每周两次的治疗进行一年后，戴夫开始谈起一位来访过他学校的、具有卡里斯玛魅力的政治人物——索洛多[Solodor]。他被此人的愿景与雄心所激动，开始谈及要把自己研究生阶段的工作投入经济政策的发展。当索洛多接管一家大型基金会时，刚毕业的戴夫加入了他的行政团队。

Dave's relationship with this idealized, bigger-than-life figure energized his life. He felt for the first time acknowledged and empowered. When he at times felt uncertain and doubtful about himself, he would have a supervision with his boss and returned to psychotherapy sessions feeling renewed and confident. During sessions, we would discuss these fluctuations in mood and self-experience. Without Solodor, Dave felt conflicted about his ambitions and doubted whether he measured up. He longed to be taken seriously but doubted that he deserved to be. Solodor seemed to magically restore Dave's sense of self. The attention from and dialogue with this ideal man had a compelling effect, Dave's career flourished, and socially, he began to reach out to people. He married and had a child.

戴夫与这个被理想化的、比生命更宏大的人物之间的关系，为他的生活注入了能量。他第一次感到被承认、被赋权。当他偶尔对自己感到不确定与怀疑时，只要与他的上司进行一次督导谈话，回到心理治疗会谈中时便感到焕然一新、充满信心。在会谈中，我们会讨论这些情绪与自体体验的波动。没有了索洛多，戴夫便对自己的抱负感到冲突，怀疑自己是否够格。他渴望被认真对待，却又怀疑自己配不上被认真对待。索洛多仿佛能神奇地恢复戴夫的自体感。来自这位理想人物的关注与对话具有不可抗拒的效果：戴夫的职业生涯蒸蒸日上，在社交上他也开始主动接触他人。他结了婚，有了一个孩子。

I considered that the idealization of Solodor was a displacement from the transference, and that it functioned partly to defend against Dave's unconscious competitiveness and self-assertion; but, as I will discuss, the benefits for the treatment outweighed any difficulties that the idealization of Solodor may have caused. Dave, for the first time in many years, experienced himself as effective and valued. His mood improved; he engaged in life more vigorously.

我当时认为，对索洛多的理想化是一种从移情[transference]中移置出去的现象，并且它部分地起着防御戴夫潜意识中的竞争性与自我主张[self-assertion]的作用；但正如我将要讨论的，对治疗的收益超过了对索洛多的理想化可能造成的任何困难。戴夫许多年来第一次把自己体验为有效的、有价值的。他的情绪改善了；他更有活力地投入生活。

In sessions, we explored the ways in which Solodor functioned for Dave. I interpreted the fluctuation of Dave's self-state and mood as they were tied to variations in his relationship with Solodor. We explored the sources of Dave's inhibitions in the blocked and withered relationship with his father. He acknowledged that he had suppressed his vitality and excitement as a teenager in the face of his mother's decline and the grim collapse of his family.

在会谈中，我们探索了索洛多对戴夫而言是如何发挥功能的。我对戴夫自体状态与情绪的波动加以诠释[interpreted]，指出它们与他同索洛多关系的变化相关联。我们探索了戴夫种种抑制的根源，即他与父亲之间那种阻塞而枯萎的关系。他承认，作为一个少年，在母亲日渐衰弱、家庭严酷瓦解之际，他压制了自己的活力与兴奋。

Nothing about me was important. Mom was more and more in her room. I would sit with her, and we would talk. My father was working. And did you ever talk with her about how you felt? No . . . we never seemed to consider that. She was failing. You were losing her. Both your parents were collapsing for you. You must have been terrified, but there was no one to speak to about it. I just shut down, I guess. I would go riding, and in the summer go down to the water and watch the sunset.

关于我的一切都不重要。妈妈越来越多地待在她自己的房间里。我会坐在她身边，我们说说话。我父亲在上班。 那你有没有和她谈过你的感受？ 没有……我们似乎从没想过要谈这个。 她在衰弱下去。你在失去她。对你来说，你的父母都在崩塌。你一定吓坏了，却没有人可以谈谈这件事。 我想我只是关闭了自己。我会去骑车，夏天就到水边去，看日落。

The idealization of Solodor represented the renewal of hope for Dave. The engagement with this energetic man empowered him, and Solodor's vision stimulated Dave's vision. His ambition nurtured Dave's desire to succeed.

对索洛多的理想化代表着戴夫希望的更新。与这个充满能量的人的投入赋予他力量，索洛多的愿景激发了戴夫的愿景。他的雄心滋养了戴夫对成功的渴望。

For most of the first years of their relationship, there was dissociation between the old, depressed Dave and the new, vital Dave. However, the relation between these two selves was a rich source of feeling and fantasy to explore in sessions. With Solodor, Dave imagined that the economy of the world could be transformed, and world hunger would end. He spoke of saving the planet from catastrophe (this was, in fact, the actual mission of the foundation), while acknowledging that he was trying to overcome his own losses and helplessness.

在他们关系最初几年的多数时间里，那个旧日的、抑郁的戴夫与那个新的、有活力的戴夫之间存在着解离[dissociation]。然而，这两个自体之间的关系，成了会谈中可供探索的丰富的情感与幻想来源。与索洛多在一起时，戴夫想象世界的经济可以被改造，世界性的饥饿将会终结。他谈到要把这个星球从灾难中拯救出来（事实上这正是该基金会的实际使命），同时承认他也是在试图克服自己的丧失与无助。

But as Solodor became increasingly distant due to his expanding commitments, and thus less available to Dave, Dave would begin to feel small and unimportant; rather than saving the planet, he worried that he would be unable to save his job. Nonetheless, the gradual loss of Solodor did not destroy Dave. He struggled but felt that he could function alone. Over the years, he had developed a reputation and network of professional relationships that provided real support. His sense of self and his social environment were now durable enough to sustain him and to keep him from falling back into depression.

但随着索洛多因不断扩张的公务而日益疏远，因而对戴夫越来越不可得，戴夫开始感到自己渺小、无足轻重；他不再想着拯救地球，而开始担忧自己连工作都保不住。尽管如此，索洛多的逐渐失去并未摧毁戴夫。他挣扎着，但觉得自己可以独自运作。多年来，他已积累起声誉，并建立起一个提供真实支持的专业人际网络。他的自体感与他的社会环境如今已足够坚实，足以支撑他，使他不再坠回抑郁之中。

During Dave's childhood, the absence of his father appeared to have been compensated for by a close, supportive tie to his mother. This worked fine until her illness and death during his adolescent years. Then he was left with nothing: an absent father and a radically devalued mother, dead from cancer. When he came to treatment, his long-term adaptation to this loss had been shaken by the pressures of graduate education. He was forced to engage actively with an older man and to begin to compete. This resulted in powerful conflicts and resurgent depression.

在戴夫的童年期，父亲的缺席似乎由与母亲之间亲近而支持性的纽带所补偿。这一直运作良好，直到他青少年期母亲患病与去世。那时他一无所有：一个缺席的父亲，一个被彻底贬抑的、死于癌症的母亲。当他来接受治疗时，他对此丧失的长期适应已被研究生教育的压力所动摇。他被迫与一位年长的男性主动打交道，并开始竞争。这导致了他身上强烈的冲突与再度兴起的抑郁。

At the beginning of treatment, Dave suffered from the types of distortions in superego structuralization and function that Schafer (1960) noted. These conflicts were primarily stimulated by his relationships with mentors and authority figures. He was self-blaming, felt that he did not measure up (always to a vague but nevertheless severe standard), and would chronically inhibit desires and actions that expressed his ambitions. He had no ability to recognize loving feelings from superiors and seemed incapable of sustaining any level of idealization. As a result, his sense of self and his feelings for his work were depleted, at times becoming the source of tremendous anxiety.

治疗之初，戴夫所苦的正是谢弗（1960）所指出的那类超我结构化与功能的扭曲。这些冲突主要由他与导师及权威人物的关系所激发。他自责，觉得自己不够格（总是相对于某种模糊却严苛的标准而言），并且会长期压抑那些表达其抱负的欲望与行动。他没有能力辨认来自上级的爱的情感，并且似乎无法维系任何程度的理想化。其结果是，他的自体感与他对工作的感受都被耗竭，有时更成为巨大焦虑的来源。

Loewald (1960) noted that the analyst offers him- or herself as a new object to the patient, different from the archaic figures of the transference, and that the patient may – often for the first time – experience love in the form of the analyst's interest and concern. This sense of love (at first stimulating understandable anxiety) eventually becomes the occasion for the development of trust and the activation of both disintegrative and then integrative processes. I would argue that the resulting idealization of the analyst and the treatment reflect the activation of reparative efforts by the patient.

洛瓦尔德（1960）指出，分析师把自身作为一个新的对象提供给病人，这一对象不同于移情中那些古老的人物；而病人可能——往往是第一次——以分析师的兴趣与关切的形式体验到爱。这种爱的感受（起初会激起可以理解的焦虑）最终成为信任之发展、以及先解体性[disintegrative]而后整合性[integrative]过程被启动的契机。我要主张，由此产生的对分析师与对治疗的理想化，反映的正是病人修复性努力[reparative efforts]的启动。

In the case of Dave, it would not have been surprising to see an idealizing transference develop in the treatment. In fact, it did develop, unconsciously, as if to protect and preserve the tie against Dave's own feared destructiveness and Oedipal rage. He maintained a motivated and effective therapeutic relationship with me, even while his most conscious and dynamic idealization flourished in his work with Solodor.

就戴夫的个案而言，在治疗中出现一种理想化移情[idealizing transference]本不令人意外。事实上它确实出现了，是无意识地出现的，仿佛要保护并保全这条纽带，以免于戴夫自己所恐惧的破坏性与俄狄浦斯式愤怒。他与我维持着一段有动力、有成效的治疗关系，与此同时，他最处于意识层面、最具动力的理想化则在他的索洛多工作中蓬勃生长。

Dave's relationship with Solodor satisfied his need for an idealized tie. As Solodor's lieutenant, he felt valued and empowered. In his admiration of Solodor, he felt stimulated and supported by a powerful man. Solodor offered a real, idealized attachment in which dialogue and interchange played a central part. It was the value of their contacts, discussions, and shared adventures that mattered most to Dave. He cherished encouragement from Solodor and hung on every word of praise and admiration that the older man expressed about him. Of course, this engagement was highly charged with fantasy.

戴夫与索洛多的关系满足了他对一条被理想化纽带的需要。作为索洛多的副手，他感到被珍视、被赋权。在对索洛多的钦佩之中，他感到被一个有力的人所激励与支持。索洛多提供了一种真实的、被理想化的依恋，在其中，对话与交流占据核心位置。对戴夫而言，最重要的是他们之间接触、讨论与共同冒险的价值。他珍视来自索洛多的鼓励，并且紧抓这位年长者关于他的每一句赞扬与欣赏之辞。当然，这份投入高度地贯注着幻想。

It was Dave's active engagement in the idealized relationship to Solodor that fueled his renewed ambitions and self-esteem. He gradually came to recognize his mentor's limitations, and our work, over time, began to center on the waning of his idealization, and then on the complete loss of Solodor when the latter took a position in another town. Dave's experience of abandonment by his mentor led to the emergence of memories of his father's chronic unavailability and later rejection. He struggled to maintain his self-experience and a new, more vitalized life. Fantasies of rejection and failure plagued him. He feared being laid off, and as a new director took over the agency, he watched from a distance as this person took charge. Eventually, Dave made an appointment with him to discuss his job, and the new boss "blew off" the appointment. Dave felt crushed, crying with rage as he cursed his own weakness. "Why do I always try to look up to these guys? I don't trust any of them" – by which I assumed he also meant me.

正是戴夫对索洛多这段被理想化关系积极主动的投入，为他更新了的抱负与自尊提供了燃料。他逐渐认识到导师的局限；随着时间推移，我们的工作开始聚焦于他理想化的衰减，随后则是索洛多的彻底失去——后者去另一座城市任职。戴夫被导师抛弃的体验，使关于父亲长期不可得、以及后来被拒斥的记忆浮现出来。他挣扎着要维系自己的自体体验，以及一种新的、更有活力的生活。被拒斥与失败的幻想折磨着他。他害怕被解雇；当一位新主管接管机构时，他远远地看着这个人掌权。最终，戴夫与新主管约了一次会面，想谈谈自己的工作，而这位新上司"放了鸽子"。戴夫感到被击碎，愤怒地哭着诅咒自己的软弱。"为什么我总是试图仰视这些人？我一个都不信任"——我猜想他这话也把我包含在内。

"You feel you can't trust us, but I think that, most painfully, you feel that it is, in fact, your fault, that there is something wrong with you." Dave stopped crying and smiled slightly. "And some people say there is no discipline in therapy."

"你觉得你无法信任我们，但我认为，最令你痛苦的是，你觉得这事实上是你的错，是你身上有什么地方不对。" 戴夫停止了哭泣，微微一笑。"有些人还说治疗里没有规矩呢。"

Dave experienced this interpretation as my reinstatement of the new, mature parental object that understood and valued him. This restored his motivation to idealize me and the treatment, mitigated the primitive torments of his resurgent superego, and helped to restore his capacity for reflection and self-integration. After we discussed the relation between his damaged self and the failure of these idealized men, he spoke of a new man he had met recently; this man had just left a high-powered job and might recommend Dave for his former position.

戴夫把这一诠释体验为我重新确立起那个理解他、珍视他的新的、成熟的父母对象。这恢复了他去理想化我与治疗的动机，减轻了他卷土重来的超我之原始折磨，并帮助他恢复了反思与自我整合的能力。在我们讨论了他受损的自体与这些被理想化之人的失败之间的关系之后，他谈起一位最近认识的新男性；此人刚辞去一份高位工作，或许可以为戴夫推荐他原来的职位。

"You know," Dave said to me, "This guy is a good guy. He's not a narcissistic asshole. He might be someone I can admire and trust."

"你知道，"戴夫对我说，"这人是好人。他不是那种自恋的混蛋。他也许是一个我可以钦佩并信任的人。"

Idealization in Creativity and Aesthetic Experience（创造力与审美体验中的理想化）

An artwork that is loved reflects the ideals of the self and is loved for that very reason. The artwork contains projections of the self-ideals of the artist. The press to employ the imagination to overcome reality through the construction of the ideal also reflects a longing to make ideal a reality. The need to transcend reality through the creation of the ideal is neither a delusion nor a form of pathology. It is the creative realization of the wish to refashion a lesser reality into something more . . . an act of transcendence. (Kainer, 1999, pp. 19, 28)

一件被爱的艺术作品映现着自体的理想，并且正因如此而被爱。艺术作品包含着艺术家自体理想[self-ideals]的投射。那种要运用想象、通过建构理想来克服现实的驱迫，也反映着一种"把理想变成现实"的渴望。通过创造理想来超越现实的需要，既不是妄想，也不是一种病理形式。它是"把一种次等的现实重塑为更多之物"这一愿望的创造性实现……是一种超越[transcendence]之举。 （Kainer, 1999, pp. 19, 28）

The model of idealization that we have been discussing, while optimal, is not always the experience of actual parent/child couples. We know that failures in early parent/child relationships are common, especially in those persons who seek clinical help. What is the fate of idealization in instances where the normal urge for enthrallment and wonder is met with indifference, rejection, or malattunement?

我们一直在讨论的这一理想化模型虽然是最理想的，却并非现实中每对亲子都会有的体验。我们知道，早期亲子关系中的失败很常见，尤其在那些寻求临床帮助的人身上。当那种对迷恋与惊奇的正常渴望遭遇冷漠、拒绝或调谐失误时，理想化的命运又如何？

We know that many artists, living and deceased, experienced significant failure in early relationships. Art and creativity become an attempt to articulate an idealized relationship with a part of reality (subjectively conceived) that functions to express unconscious wishes, as well as to restore self-experience and the powerful archaic experience of self-in-relation. In the next section, we consider how this need to idealize is elaborated through mature forms of aesthetic experience found in art creation and appreciation.

我们知道，许多艺术家——在世的与已故的——都曾在早期关系中经历重大失败。艺术与创造力于是成为这样一种尝试：去清晰地表达与某一部分现实（以主体方式构想的部分）之间的一种理想化关系，这种关系既发挥表达潜意识愿望的功能，也发挥恢复自体体验、恢复那种强烈的"关系中的自体[self-in-relation]"之古老体验的功能。在下一节，我们将考察这种理想化的需要，如何通过艺术创作与艺术欣赏中所见的成熟形式的审美体验而被精致化。

Aesthetic experience involves a phenomenon (object, event, sound, or other perception) or a set of phenomena (a group of objects, a sequence of events, a melody, or complex structure of perceptions) that are felt to possess perfection or ideal form. Aesthetic experience is fundamentally subjective, but also grounded in the objective qualities of the object. As Bernard Bosanquet (1915) stated: "The aesthetic attitude is an attitude in which we imaginatively contemplate an object, being able in that way to live in it, as an embodiment of our feeling" (pp. 29–30). In maturity, aesthetic experience recaptures the state of ideal attunement and mutual valuation that characterized our archaic tie to loved objects. It invariably includes affects such as joy, sadness, wonder, and awe. The quality of self-experience is also part of it; the person feels whole, vitalized, more positive, and closely engaged with the world.

审美体验涉及某一现象（对象、事件、声音或其他知觉），或一组现象（一群对象、一系列事件、一段旋律，或复杂的知觉结构）——它们被感受为拥有完美或理想的形式。审美体验从根本上是主观的，但同时也奠基于对象的客观品质。正如伯纳德·鲍桑葵[Bernard Bosanquet]（1915）所言："审美态度是这样一种态度：我们以想象去观照一个对象，从而能够活在其中，把它当作我们情感的具体体现"（pp. 29–30）。在成熟状态中，审美体验重新捕捉到那种理想调谐与相互珍视的状态——那正是我们与所爱对象之间古老纽带的特征。它总是包含喜悦、悲伤、惊奇与敬畏等情感。自体体验的品质也是其中一部分；人感到完整、有活力、更积极，并且与世界紧密相连。

Idealization is central to aesthetic experience. Freud, who viewed aesthetic experience as the sublimation of sexual desire into cultural objects and pursuits, argued that the desexualized cathexis resulted in an overestimation of the object, an idealization that contributed to the sense of the perfection of the artwork or other object of beauty. Melanie Klein and Hanna Segal felt that the ideal nature of aesthetic experience functioned to resolve the fragmentation of the paranoid-schizoid position, facilitating the psychological integration that characterizes the depressive position.

理想化在审美体验中居于核心。弗洛伊德把审美体验视为将性欲升华为文化对象与文化追求的过程，他主张，去性化的贯注[cathexis]导致了对对象的高估[overestimation]——这种理想化促成了对艺术作品或其他美之对象之完美性的感受。梅兰妮·克莱因[Melanie Klein]与汉娜·西格尔[Hanna Segal]则认为，审美体验的理想性质发挥功能，去解决偏执—分裂位相[paranoid-schizoid position]的碎裂，促成那种作为抑郁位相[depressive position]之特征的心理整合。

From a similar point of view, H. B. Lee (1948) argued that the idealized nature of aesthetic experience protects the individual from destructive internal fantasies, with the sensation of beauty quelling aggression and protecting the ideal other from our hostility. Self psychologists also link idealization and aesthetic experience, as artist and audience seek to recapture archaic states of merger with idealized selfobject experiences. In short, despite differing theoretical models, analysts have agreed that idealization plays an important role in aesthetic experience. This process is manifest in the sense of beauty.

从一个相似的视角出发，H. B. 李[H. B. Lee]（1948）主张，审美体验的理想化性质保护个体免受具破坏性的内部幻想之扰，美的感受平息了攻击性，并保护那理想的他者免受我们的敌意。自体心理学家同样把理想化与审美体验联系起来，因为艺术家与观众都试图重新捕捉与被理想化的自体客体体验相融合的古老状态。简言之，尽管理论模型各异，分析师们都同意理想化在审美体验中扮演重要角色。这一过程显现于美感[the sense of beauty]之中。

As we will see in chapter six, the aesthetic experience of beauty duplicates the actual or longed-for perception of the ideal. The affective states associated with beauty – enthrallment, optimism, and vitality – are similar responses to what is felt in childhood when one is close to the ideal parent, or in adulthood when one is with one's lover. In the appreciation of beauty, the form of the beautiful thing is experienced as expressive of value affects. Its form resonates with the enjoyer's affective readiness and response. The joy and excitement that the enjoyer feels toward the beautiful thing is confirmed and mirrored by the object's beauty. The richer and complex the meanings of the object or piece of artwork, the more stimulating and elaborate the response and the more enduring the aesthetic experience.

正如我们将在第六章看到的，对美的审美体验复制了对理想的实际或渴望已久的知觉[perception]。与美相关联的情感状态——迷恋、乐观与活力——是对童年时靠近理想父母、或成年时与爱人相伴时所感受到之物作出的相似反应。在对美的欣赏中，美之物的形式被体验为价值情感的表达。它的形式与享受者情感上的准备状态与反应产生共鸣。享受者对美之物所感到的欣喜与兴奋，被对象的美所确认、所镜映。对象或艺术品之意义越丰富复杂，反应就越有激发力、越精致，审美体验也越持久。

I am sure we have all felt an immediate rush of aesthetic enjoyment at the first glimpse of a piece of art, only to find it fade rapidly when the depth of meaning fails to unfold beyond a superficial level. The responsiveness that we find in a great work of art involves a feeling of revelation that is renewed with each viewing. Our valuing of it is confirmed and even amplified as we discover hidden beauties and exciting formal possibilities. It is a dialogue with the concretized imagination of the artist in a dialectic with our own sensibility, but it is also a dialogue fueled by the excitement with and hunger for an ideal creation, the sense of life refined and elaborated into its most exquisite and complex formal arrangement.

我确信我们都曾有过这样的经验：乍见一件艺术品时，一阵审美享受即刻涌来，随后却发现，当意义的深度未能展开、只停留在表层时，这种享受便迅速消退。我们在一件伟大艺术作品中所发现的回应性，包含着一种每次观看都会更新的启示感。当我们发现那些隐秘的美与令人兴奋的形式可能性时，我们对它的珍视得到确认，甚至被放大。这是与艺术家的具体化想象之间的一场对话，它与我们自身的感受力构成辩证；但它同时也是一场被"对理想创造的兴奋与渴求"所驱动的对话，是生命感被提炼、被精致化为其最精妙、最复杂之形式安排的过程。

The creation of art is one of the most dynamic and refined forms of sublimation. Through creative activities, the artist attempts to do three things simultaneously: (1) to produce an object that, through its symbolic content, allows for the expression of forbidden fantasies of a sexual and aggressive nature (we are familiar with this process as described in traditional psychoanalytic theory), (2) to express these wishes in an ideal form, which re-creates the original experience of the relationship with the original idealized objects of childhood, and (3) to externalize aspects of self-experience that, also possessing an idealized form, mirror the self's grandiosity, in a manner similar to that experienced in interaction with the parent.

艺术的创造是升华最具动力、最精致的形式之一。通过创造性活动，艺术家试图同时做到三件事：（1）产生一个对象，它通过其象征性内容，让那些具性与攻击性质的被禁忌的幻想得以表达（我们对传统精神分析理论所描述的这一过程已然熟悉）；（2）以理想的形式表达这些愿望，从而重新创造与童年原初被理想化对象之间关系的原初体验；（3）把自体体验的某些方面外化——这些方面同样具有理想化的形式，并以一种类似于与父母互动时所体验到的方式，镜映自体的夸大。

It is the process of idealization that distinguishes artistic creativity from dreamwork and from other forms of sublimation. The creative process is a dialectic in which the artist engages a subjectively produced object and refines it. In other words, subjectivity is externalized and its expression perfected. More specifically, the creative process is an attempt to re-create the early experience of the ideal relationship (as it was or as fantasy), but this time with an externalized part of the self. That part, concretized in the artwork, is refined and perfected as the artist seeks to create or restore a perfect state of resonance with an ideal other – the other being in this case, both object and self simultaneously – a "perfect match."

正是理想化的过程，把艺术创造力与梦的工作[dreamwork]、以及与其他形式的升华区分开来。创造过程是一种辩证：艺术家投入一个由主体所产出的对象，并加以提炼。换言之，主体性被外化，其表达被完善。更具体地说，创造过程是一种尝试：去重新创造理想关系的早期体验（无论是当时实有的，还是幻想中的），但这一次是与自体中被外化出来的那一部分一起。那一部分在艺术作品中具体化，随着艺术家寻求创造或恢复与理想他者之间完美的共鸣状态而被提炼、被完善——在此情形中，这个他者同时既是对象又是自体——一种"完美的匹配[perfect match]"。

Part of the artist's aesthetic experience is value affect resonance, as the beauty/perfection of the creation mirrors the emotional state of the artist, articulating the artist's self-experience in an ideal form. Value augmentation, which we discussed in relation to the parent/infant relationship, also characterizes the artist's engagement with the artwork. In other words, the artist, through skill and vision, refines the artwork, perfects the expression of his or her subjective experience, and the sense of the object's increasing quality results in an enhanced vitality for the artist, who becomes further engrossed in the work and further motivated to perfect it.

艺术家审美体验的一部分是价值情感共鸣[value affect resonance]：创造物之美/完美镜映着艺术家的情绪状态，以一种理想的形式把艺术家的自体体验清晰表达出来。我们在亲子关系中讨论过的价值增益[value augmentation]，同样也是艺术家与艺术作品之投入的特征。换言之，艺术家通过技艺与眼光提炼作品、完善其主体性体验的表达；而对对象品质日益提升的感受，又为艺术家带来增强的活力——他因此更加全神贯注于作品，更有动力去完善它。

Creativity and aesthetic experience involve the idealization of self-experience as mirrored in the perfection of the object. Lee (1948) describes the state of being "love-worthy" as one stands before the beautiful object. We are close to the ideal one, but the other responds, it mirrors our own perfection, and the dialogue that follows results in a reciprocal value affect augmentation. We love – we accept love, and both object and self increase in value. The extent to which the appreciation of the beautiful object evokes actual or fantasized experiences of value resonance will determine the enhancement of self-experience. This accounts for the pronounced sense of inner vitality and goodness that we feel as we enjoy art or beautiful things in general.

创造力与审美体验涉及自体体验的理想化，正如它被镜映在对象的完美之中。李（1948）描述了这样一种状态：当人站在美之对象面前时，成为"值得被爱[love-worthy]"的。我们已接近那个理想者，但他者作出回应，镜映我们自身的完美，随之而来的对话带来一种相互的价值情感增益。我们 爱 ——我们接受爱，于是对象与自体都在价值上增长。对美之对象的欣赏在多大程度上唤起实际的或幻想中的价值共鸣体验，将决定自体体验被增强的程度。这解释了当我们享受艺术或一般意义上美的事物时，所感到的那种显著的内在活力与善好感。

In conclusion, we have seen that aesthetic experience involves the externalization of value affects. Specifically, it is the creation of or encounter with an object whose formal qualities elicit value affect resonance, or, more simply, aesthetic resonance. Aesthetic experience is most pronounced in relation to something that is felt to possess ideal form. By form, I do not mean simply shape or configuration, but rather all the inherent qualities of its being – that which makes it what it is, its essence.

总之，我们已经看到，审美体验涉及价值情感的外化。具体而言，它是创造或遭遇这样一个对象：其形式品质引起价值情感共鸣，或者更简单地说，引起 审美共鸣 [aesthetic resonance]。审美体验在关涉到某种被感受为拥有理想形式之物时最为显著。我所说的形式，并非仅指形状或构型[configuration]，而是指其存在的一切固有品质——那些使它成其为它的东西，它的本质[essence]。

We feel excited by the appearance of this ideal form. We are entranced, in awe, fascinated by its perfection, its balance, its rhythm, its color, shape, and texture. Its meanings are exquisite, complex, and resonant with our most unconscious knowledge. We are eager to engage it, to get close; we have an urge to make it part of us. The earliest, most archaic qualities of enthrallment with the essential other are evoked in aesthetic experience. In the best of cases, it is a feeling of relatedness without comparison, until we recognize its link to the stirring wonder of engagement with the first objects of childhood.

这种理想形式的显现令我们兴奋。我们被人迷住、心怀敬畏，被它的完美、它的平衡、它的节律、它的色彩、形状与质地所吸引。它的意义精妙、复杂，与我们最潜意识的认知产生共鸣。我们迫切地想要投入它、靠近它；我们有一种冲动，要把它变成自己的一部分。与那本质性他者相迷恋的最早、最古老的品质，在审美体验中被唤起。在最理想的情形中，那是一种无可比拟的关联感——直到我们辨认出，它与童年最初那些对象相接时那动人心魄的惊奇之间的联系。
