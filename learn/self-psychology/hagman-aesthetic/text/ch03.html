Chapter 3　The Development of Aesthetic Experience（审美体验的发展）

George Hagman · Chapter 3　The Development of Aesthetic Experience（审美体验的发展） · pp. 27–36 · 51 组英中对照 · 中文默认折叠

In this chapter, we will examine in detail aspects of psychoanalytic developmental theory that support a new understanding of aesthetic experience. It will become clear that taking a developmental approach has significant advantages to a purely philosophical approach or to the classical psychoanalytic perspective that emphasizes individual psychic mechanisms.

在本章中，我们将详细考察精神分析发展理论中那些支持对审美体验[aesthetic experience]作出新理解的方面。我们将看到，采取一种发展的进路[developmental approach]，相较于纯粹的哲学进路，或相较于强调个体心理机制的经典精神分析视角，具有显著的优势。

Looking back at the early development of the aesthetic sense, we can explain several things that have eluded prior theorists. These are some of the remaining unsolved problems: the origin of aesthetic feeling, the psychological basis for the experience of disinterest, the sources of the sense of beauty and of the sublime, and vulnerabilities to ugliness, among others. Because a developmental/relational approach can pinpoint the psychological processes of early human relatedness, it can be a powerful new tool in the understanding of basic problems of human aesthetics and the psychology of art.

回溯审美感[the aesthetic sense]的早期发展，我们能够解释若干历来令先前理论家无从索解的问题。以下是若干仍未解决的核心难题：审美感受[aesthetic feeling]的起源、无利害[disinterest]体验的心理学基础、美感[the sense of beauty]与崇高感[the sense of the sublime]的来源，以及对丑[ugliness]的易感性[vulnerabilities]，等等。由于发展的/关系的[developmental/relational]进路能够精确指认早期人类关联[early human relatedness]的心理过程，它有望成为理解人类美学与艺术心理学之基本问题的有力新工具。

Let us start our discussion by examining one of the primary characteristics of aesthetic experience that is crucial to all modern conceptualizations – that is, disinterestedness. Freud explained this emotional state through the concept of sublimation, the desexualization of drive, and its neutral expression through socially valued behaviors, such as art. One way to conceptualize aesthetic experience that is consistent with contemporary relational and self psychological approaches, while at the same time remaining linked to the classical theory of psychoanalytic aesthetics, is through a reworking of the concept of sublimation. This may seem strange, given that sublimation was one of the cornerstones of drive theory and later of ego psychology, but what I intend to show in this chapter is that the classical approach to sublimation can be usefully updated when the intersubjective context of this important psychic process is fully appreciated.

让我们从考察审美体验的一项首要特征开始讨论，这一特征对一切现代的概念化都至关重要——那就是无利害性[disinterestedness]。弗洛伊德通过升华[sublimation]这一概念来解释这种情绪状态：驱力[drive]的去性化[desexualization]，及其经由艺术等具社会价值的行为所作的中性表达。有一种概念化审美体验的方式，既与当代的关系取向[relational]和自体心理学[self psychological]进路相一致，同时又仍与精神分析美学的经典理论保持联系——那就是对升华概念的重构。这看似奇怪，因为升华曾是驱力理论、后来又是自我心理学[ego psychology]的基石之一；但我在本章想要表明的是：当这一重要心理过程的互为主体[intersubjective]脉络被充分领会之后，关于升华的经典进路可以被有效地更新。

As we have seen, Freud's understanding of sublimation accomplished two objectives: (1) it explained the sources of motivation that underlie normal activities, such as work, play, art, and other cultural pursuits, and (2) it explained why these activities, driven by libidinal energy as they are, do not exhibit obvious sexual characteristics (in fact, the failure of sublimation often results in inhibition due to the flooding of the activity with sexual fantasy and feeling). Thus, sublimation could be viewed as both a defense mechanism and a normal, inevitable vicissitude of libidinal drive.

正如我们已看到的，弗洛伊德对升华的理解达成了两个目标：（1）它解释了工作、游戏、艺术及其他文化追求等正常活动背后的动机来源；（2）它解释了这些活动尽管由力比多能量[libidinal energy]所驱动，为何却不显出明显的性特征（事实上，升华的失败常导致抑制[inhibition]，因为活动被性幻想与性感受所淹没）。因此，升华既可被视为一种防御机制[defense mechanism]，也可被视为力比多驱力的一种正常且不可避免的变迁[vissicitude]。

In fact, one might argue that sublimation as a healthy process might have its own developmental line by which a person develops from a self-centered, id-driven, savage child to a civilized adult. Clearly, much more is involved in this process, but sublimation would play a key part. Regarding aesthetic experience, the familiar sense of pleasant calm and security is easily explained by sublimation. However, sublimation has also been vulnerable to serious criticism.

事实上，人们或许可以主张，作为一种健康过程的升华拥有它自己的发展线[developmental line]，人循此从自我中心的、受本我[id]驱使的野性孩童，发展为文明的成人。这一过程显然牵涉更多因素，但升华会扮演关键角色。就审美体验而言，那种熟悉的愉悦的平静与安全感，用升华便可轻易解释。然而，升华也曾招致严厉的批评。

Hans Loewald developed an important new perspective on the problem of sublimation that is especially significant for the current study of psychoanalytic aesthetics. Loewald argued that the classical model of sublimation did not relate just to the fate of the drives (redirecting their aim and neutralizing them); it also affected the individual's fundamental relationship to the world. Specifically, he felt that sublimation played a key part in bridging the gap between self and world in early childhood and in sustaining the relationship between these domains throughout life. Loewald (1988) wrote:

汉斯·洛瓦尔德[Hans Loewald]就升华问题发展出一种重要的新视角，这对当前的精神分析美学研究尤为关键。洛瓦尔德主张，升华的经典模型并不只关乎驱力的命运（改变其目标并使之中性化），它还影响个体与世界之间的根本关系。具体而言，他认为升华扮演了关键角色：在幼儿期弥合自体[self]与世界[world]之间的裂隙，并在整个人生中维系这两个领域之间的关系。洛瓦尔德（1988）写道：

The polarization that arises in the differentiation of primary narcissism into narcissistic and object libido is counterbalanced, modulated, tempered by sublimation. Relations with external objects change into internal "narcissistic" relations, and these desexualized libidinal bonds are instrumental in molding aims and relations with external objects, so that these themselves are likely to become desexualized. Freud said that the shadow of the object falls on the ego. Equally, the shadow of the altered ego falls on objects, and object relations. Sublimation is a kind of reconciliation of the subject-object dichotomy – atonement for the polarization (the word atone derives from at one) and a narrowing of the gulf between object libido and narcissistic libido, between object world and self. (p. 20)

在原发性自恋[primary narcissism]分化为自恋力比多[narcissistic libido]与对象力比多[object libido]的过程中所产生的极化[polarization]，被升华所平衡、调节与缓和。与外部对象的关系转变为内部的"自恋性"关系，而这些去性化的力比多纽带，对塑造目标以及与外部对象的关系起着关键作用，以致这些目标与关系本身也趋于去性化。弗洛伊德说，对象的阴影落在自我[ego]之上。同样地，被改变了的自我的阴影也落在对象与对象关系之上。升华是主体—客体二分[subject-object dichotomy]的一种和解——为这种极化赎罪（atonement，即 at-one-ment，"成为一体"，词源为 at one），并缩小对象力比多与自恋力比多之间、对象世界与自体之间的鸿沟。 （p. 20）

Thus, it is by means of sublimation that we enter object relations. Drives are no longer just seething, amorphous bodily states, but gradually become linked to experiences of objects that are internalized as structured fantasy that contains elements of both passion and actuality. Loewald (1988) elaborated:

因此，正是凭借升华，我们才进入对象关系[object relations]。驱力不再只是翻涌的、无定形的身体状态，而是逐渐与对象的经验相联结，这些对象被内化[internalized]为结构化的幻想[structured fantasy]，其中同时包含激情与现实的成分。洛瓦尔德（1988）进一步阐述道：

Sublimation then brings together what had become separate. It plays a decisive part in the "mastery of reality" (Hartmann, 1955) – a mastery conceived not as domination but as coming to terms – as it brings external and material reality within the compass of psychic reality, and psychic reality within the sweep of external reality. In its most developed form in creative work, it culminates in celebration. This "manic" element is not a denial, or not only that, but an affirmation of unity as well. (p. 22)

于是，升华把已然分离之物重新聚合起来。它在"现实的掌控"[mastery of reality]（Hartmann, 1955）中扮演决定性角色——这种掌控并非被理解为支配，而是被理解为达成妥帖、与之相安——因为它把外部与物质现实带入心理现实[psychic reality]的范围，又把心理现实纳入外部现实的疆域。在其于创造性工作中的最发达形态中，它最终升华为庆典[celebration]。这种"躁狂式"[manic]要素并非否认，或者说并非仅是否认，它同时也是对统一[unity]的肯定。 （p. 22）

The mastery of reality that comes with sublimation is not simply externally directed adaptation but is also the maturing capacity to permit fantasy and reality to enter dynamic relation. Thus, we allow for our subjectivity to affect the world, but also for the world to affect ourselves. Through this process, we encounter the world "in itself," but we also discover ourselves in the world. And no longer opponents or strangers, we can enter active relations with reality while feeling joy rather than suspicion and dread. To again quote Loewald (1988):

伴随升华而来的现实掌控，并不只是向外定向的适应[adaptation]，它也是一种日趋成熟的能力：允许幻想与现实进入动态的关系。于是，我们既容许自己的主体性[subjectivity]影响世界，也容许世界影响我们自己。经由这一过程，我们遭遇"自在的"[in itself]世界，同时也在世界之中发现我们自己。我们不再是现实的对立者或陌生人，而能够与现实施加主动的关系，并感到喜悦而非疑虑与恐惧。再次引用洛瓦尔德（1988）：

In genuine sublimation this alienating differentiation is being reversed in such a way that a fresh unit is created by an act of uniting. In this reversal – a restoration of unity – there comes into being a differentiated unity (a manifold) that captures separateness in the act of uniting, and unity in the act of separating. (p. 24)

在真正的升华中，这种造成疏离的分化[alienating differentiation]被逆转，其方式是经由一种统合[uniting]的行动，创生出新的单元[a fresh unit]。在这一逆转中——一种统一的恢复——一种分化了的统一体[differentiated unity]（一个杂多体[a manifold]）得以生成，它在统合的行动中把握住分离，也在分离的行动中把握住统一。 （p. 24）

This is another way of conceptualizing the crystallization of the transitional zone of experience that we will soon address in discussing Winnicott's work.

这是另一种概念化方式，用以描述我们即将在讨论温尼科特[Winnicott]的著作时论及的、经验之过渡区[transitional zone of experience]的结晶化[crystallization]。

Loewald's model of sublimation describes a truly relational perspective on what was originally a purely individual, intrapsychic process. It is not a psychic mechanism, but rather a process that emerges and is articulated between people, in which the caretaker, being of an organizationally more advanced state, engages with the child, and by means of interaction and communication, co-creates a dialectic in which a new self-organization is elaborated and refined. Loewald argues that the child's passionate investment in the other, as well as internalization by means of fantasy (conscious and unconscious), permits the channeling of affect and desire into a new self-structure in which psychic reality and external reality are interpreted and enriched. We will again discuss Loewald's model of sublimation later in this book, in considering the themes of beauty and ugliness.

洛瓦尔德的升华模型，对原本纯粹属于个体内部的、心理内[intrapsychic]的过程，给出了一种真正的关系性视角。它并非一种心理机制[psychic mechanism]，而是一个在人与人之间生成并得以表述的过程；在此过程中，处于组织水平更高级状态的养育者[caretaker]与孩子互动，并借由交互与沟通，共同创造一种辩证法[a dialectic]，在其中新的自体组织[self-organization]得以被逐渐构筑和精炼。洛瓦尔德主张，孩子对他者的热切投注，以及借由幻想（意识与无意识的）所进行的内化，使得情感与欲望得以被导入一种新的自体结构[self-structure]；在其中，心理现实与外部现实被彼此诠释、彼此丰盈。我们将在本书后文讨论美与丑[beauty and ugliness]的主题时，再次讨论洛瓦尔德的升华模型。

I will now turn to a detailed discussion of the developmental processes out of which aesthetic experience crystallizes. In doing so, I will rely on the work of Winnicott, who, as previously stated, conceptualized a developmental process quite compatible with the model of early psychic life put forward by Loewald. But for our purposes, there are two advantages to Winnicott's work: (1) he was always grounded in first-hand experience of the mother/child unit, and (2) he emphasized throughout his work the importance of creativity, aesthetic experience, and the cultural realm of human life – which he (unlike most analysts) saw as not external to the self or as a later acquisition, but as one of the core domains of self-experience.

我现在转到对审美体验得以结晶于其中的那些发展过程的详细讨论。为此，我将倚重温尼科特的著作；如前所述，他所概念化的发展过程，与洛瓦尔德提出的早期心理生活模型相当契合。但就我们的目的而言，温尼科特的著作有两项优势：（1）他始终扎根于对母婴单元[mother/child unit]的第一手经验；（2）他在全部著作中都强调创造性[creativity]、审美体验以及人类生活的文化领域的重要性——与大多数分析师不同，他不把文化视为自体之外的东西或后来的获得物，而视之为自体经验的核心领域之一。

The mother's idiom of care and the infant's experience of the handling is one of the first if not the earliest human aesthetic. The uncanny pleasure of being held by a poem, a composition, a painting, or for that matter, any object, rests on these moments when the infant's internal world is partly given form by the mother since he cannot shape them or link them together without her coverage. (Bollas, 1987, p. 32)

母亲的照护惯习[idiom of care]与婴儿被抱持[handling]的经验，是最早的人类审美之一，即便不是最早的那一个。被一首诗、一部乐曲、一幅画，或就此而言任何对象所抱持的那种不可思议的愉悦[uncanny pleasure]，正奠基于这样的时刻：婴儿的内在世界部分地由母亲赋予形式，因为若没有她的涵容[coverage]，婴儿无法塑造它们、也无法把它们联结起来。 （Bollas, 1987, p. 32）

At the heart of aesthetics is the sense of form that includes physical gesture, sensations, arrangements of color, sound, and physical space, and most important for our discussion, of bodily interaction, various modes of communication, and self-expression. Bollas (1987), in a valuable contribution, argued that it was the mother who gives form to the initially formless child, but I will show that it is the intertwined experience of the infant and mother's relating – their shared gestures, rhythms of response and counterresponse, and mutual mirroring – that determines the basis of form, and that, over time, is elaborated into aesthetic experience.

美学的核心在于形式感[the sense of form]，它包括身体姿态、感觉、颜色、声音与物理空间的排布，而对我们讨论最重要的是身体的交互、各种沟通模式以及自我表达[self-expression]。博拉斯[Bollas]（1987）在一项有价值的贡献中主张，是母亲赋予最初无形式的[formless]孩子以形式；但我将表明，真正决定形式之基础的，是婴儿与母亲之关联相互交织的经验——他们共享的姿态、回应与反向回应[response and counterresponse]的节律，以及相互的镜映[mutual mirroring]——而这些经验随时间推移，被逐渐构筑为审美体验。

So we will start with a brief discussion of the issue of form and its connection to infancy. I hope to show how the early emergence of psychological forms – from innate, potential (but still poorly differentiated), organizations of perceptions of self and other – provides the foundation for mature levels of aesthetic experience.

因此，我们将先简要讨论形式问题及其与婴儿期的关联。我希望表明，心理形式的早期涌现——从先天的、潜在的（但仍分化不足的）关于自体与他者的知觉组织——如何为成熟水平的审美体验提供了基础。

Winnicott conducted the most complex and credible theoretical description of aesthetic processes that occur in infancy. He described the normal transition from formlessness to integration that characterizes the child's passage from neonate to person. In fact, the emergence of the self, of the experience of going on being, is a developmental achievement that is always precarious and vulnerable to failure in attunement as well as impingement. The maturing self-experience of the infant must find a psychic bridge by which the disruptions in self-experience can be endured and by means of which the self can be made whole again. The gradual elaboration of form provides continuity and vitality in the face of impingement and failure. Winnicott (1971) wrote:

温尼科特对婴儿期发生的审美过程作了最复杂、也最可信的理论描述。他描述了从无形式到整合[integration]的正常过渡，这正是孩子从新生儿走向"人"[person]的过程。事实上，自体[self]的涌现、持续存在[going on being]这一经验的涌现，是一项始终岌岌可危的发展成就，既容易因神入[attunement]失败而受挫，也容易受到侵扰[impingement]。婴儿逐渐成熟的自体经验，必须找到一座心理的桥梁，借以承受自体经验中的中断，并借此使自体重新变得完整。形式的逐渐构筑，在侵扰与失败面前提供了连续性与活力。温尼科特（1971）写道：

There are long stretches of time in a normal infant's life in which a baby does not mind whether he is many bits or one whole being, or whether he lives in his mother's face or in his own body, provided that from time to time he comes together and feels something. (p. 150)

在正常婴儿的生活中，有很长一段时间，婴儿并不在意自己是许多碎片还是一个完整的存在，也不在意自己是活在母亲的脸庞里还是活在自己的身体里，只要他不时地聚合起来、并感受到些什么就好。 （p. 150）

The infant's experience is initially relatively unintegrated; in other words, it does not have the degree of formal organization that characterizes maturity. This period can be described as preaesthetic because the child's experience of self and world has not yet developed structure or organization, let alone value or the quality of perfection.

婴儿的经验最初是相对未整合的；换言之，它尚不具备成熟所特有的那种形式组织的程度。这一时期可被描述为前审美的[preaesthetic]，因为孩子对自体与世界的经验尚未发展出结构或组织，更不必说价值或完美[perfection]的品质。

The development of the capacity for aesthetic experience depends on the gradual elaboration of the capacity to give form to thought, sensation, and affect. Winnicott talked about the importance of the spontaneous gesture that arises out of the true self, embodying the unique subjectivity of the infant. This spontaneous gesture is responded to and takes on aesthetic form as it is sculpted through interaction with the mother. It is this relationship that provides experiences of form and value. Winnicott (1971) wrote:

审美体验能力的发展，取决于赋予思想、感觉与情感以形式的能力之逐渐构筑。温尼科特谈到源自真我[true self]的自发姿态[spontaneous gesture]的重要性，它体现着婴儿独特的主体性。这一自发姿态得到回应，并在与母亲的互动中被雕琢，从而取得审美的形式。正是这一关系提供了形式与价值的经验。温尼科特（1971）写道：

The infant comes to the breast when excited, and ready to hallucinate something fit to be attacked. At that moment the actual nipple appears, and he can feel it was that nipple that he hallucinated. So, his ideas are enriched by actual details of sight, feel, smell, and next time this material is used in the hallucination. In this way he starts to build a capacity to conjure up what is available. The mother must go on giving the infant this type of experience. (pp. 152–153)

婴儿在兴奋时来到乳房前，并准备好幻觉[hallucinate]出某个适宜被攻击的东西。就在那一刻，真实的乳头出现了，他能感到自己幻觉出的正是那个乳头。于是，他的观念因视觉、触觉、嗅觉的实际细节而变得丰富，而下次这些材料会被用于幻觉之中。以这种方式，他开始建立起一种召唤出[conjure up]可得之物的能力。母亲必须持续地给婴儿提供这类经验。 （pp. 152–153）

The transitional experience becomes structured according to instinctual desire intertwined with what is available. This occurs during experiences of pleasure and satisfaction. The reliable recurrence of these experiences (which are shared with the mother) leads to an increasing elaboration of formal qualities within the potential space between the infant's psyche and the object world. This space becomes the area of play – the matrix of aesthetic experience. Initially, playing involves the "marriage" of the infant's omnipotence of intrapsychic processes to the baby's engagement with the real.

过渡性经验[transitional experience]依照本能欲望[instinctual desire]与可得之物相互交织的方式而结构化。这发生在愉悦与满足的经验之中。这些（与母亲共享的）经验可靠地反复出现，导致婴儿心灵与对象世界之间的潜在空间[potential space]内，形式品质被日益构筑。这一空间成为游戏[play]的领域——审美体验的母体[matrix]。最初，游戏涉及把婴儿心理内过程的无所不能[omnipotence]与婴儿对真实界的投入相"联姻"。

"The thing about playing is always the precariousness of the interplay of the personal psychic reality and the experience of control of actual objects" (Winnicott, 1971, p. 47). Playing can only develop when mother and infant share the experience of security and reliability, so that the latter can experience the "capacity to conjure up what is actually available." "Baby and object are merged in with one another. Baby's view of the object is subjective, and the mother is oriented towards the making actual of what the baby is ready to find" (p. 47).

"游戏的关键始终在于个人心理现实与对实际对象的控制经验之间相互作用的那种岌岌可危"[the precariousness of the interplay]（Winnicott, 1971, p. 47）。只有当母亲与婴儿共享安全与可靠的经验时，游戏才可能发展，如此婴儿才能体验到"召唤出实际可得之物的能力"。"婴儿与对象彼此融合为一。婴儿对对象的看法是主观的，而母亲则朝向使婴儿准备好去发现之物成为现实"（p. 47）。

Although the baby hovers between omnipotence and recognition of the real, when all goes well, this transition is seamless and magical. But it would not occur without the attunement and responsiveness of the mother, whose ministrations engage, mold, and are molded by the baby's spontaneous gestures and desires. "It is in playing and only in playing that the individual child or adult is able to be creative and to use the whole personality, and it is only in being creative that the individual discovers the self" (1971, p. 54).

尽管婴儿在无所不能与对真实界的承认之间徘徊，但当一切顺利时，这一过渡是无缝的、充满魔力的。但若没有母亲的神入与回应性[responsiveness]，它便不会发生；母亲的操持[ministrations]既介入、塑造着婴儿的自发姿态与欲望，也被后者所塑造。"正是在游戏之中、且仅在游戏之中，作为个体的孩子或成人才能够有创造性并运用其整个人格；也只有在具有创造性时，个体才发现自体"（1971, p. 54）。

The development of play begins with the child's relating to objects as though they were omnipotently controlled, a part of psychic reality. The object is altered and manipulated according to fantasy and desire; thus, the object (still not experienced as separate) begins to mirror the self. The baby may at times play alone, but this is because the baby has had no reason yet to question the mother's availability. However, over time, the area of potential space begins to include the other, beginning with the recognition that the other can be gone and will return. According to Winnicott (1971):

游戏的发展始于孩子把对象当作受其无所不能地控制之物、当作心理现实的一部分来与之关联。对象依照幻想与欲望而被改变和操纵；于是，对象（尚未被经验为分离的）开始镜映自体。婴儿有时会独自玩耍，但那是因为婴儿尚无理由去质疑母亲的可得性[availability]。然而，随时间推移，潜在空间的领域开始把他人包含进来，其开端是承认他人可以离开、并将返回。据温尼科特（1971）：

The next stage is being alone in the presence of someone. The child is now playing based on the assumption that the person who loves and who is therefore reliable is available and continues to be available when remembered after being forgotten. The person is felt to reflect what happened in the playing. The child is now getting ready for the next stage, which is to allow and to enjoy the overlap of two play areas. First, surely, it is the mother who plays with the baby, but she is rather careful to fit in with the baby's play activities. Sooner or later, however, she introduces her own playing, and she finds that babies vary according to their capacity to like or dislike the introduction of ideas that are not their own. Thus, the way is paved for a playing together in a relationship. (pp. 47–48)

下一个阶段是在某人面前独处[being alone in the presence of someone]。孩子此时游戏，是基于如下假设：那个爱他、因而可靠的人是可得的，并且在被遗忘之后又被记起时，仍持续可得。这个人被感觉为能映照游戏中所发生之事。孩子现在正为下一个阶段做准备，即允许并享受两个游戏领域的重叠。首先，当然是母亲与婴儿一起玩，但她相当小心地配合婴儿的游戏活动。然而，或早或晚，她会引入她自己的游戏，而她发现，婴儿们因各自喜爱或不喜爱引入非己之观念的能力而有所不同。于是，在一种关系中共同游戏[playing together]的道路就被铺好了。 （pp. 47–48）

At this point, the aesthetic of play begins to become intersubjective (meaning that there is mutual recognition and engagement). The child can accept, enjoy, and cherish the other, recognize the other's subjectivity, and engage in a dialogue that creates form in the shared psychical space, the potential space, between the two subjectivities. This is the source of dance, ritual, and other communal arts. The intense pleasure of play results from the idealized experience of responsiveness and attunement. The two people conjure up an elaborate structure of sound, affect, gesture, and meaning that is vitalizing and that can be repeated over and over, often increasingly elaborated and enriched through dialogue.

在这一点上，游戏的美学开始变为互为主体的[intersubjective]（意即存在相互的承认与投入）。孩子能够接受、享受并珍视他人，承认他人的主体性，并参与一场对话——这场对话在两位主体性之间的共享心理空间、即潜在空间中创造形式。这正是舞蹈、仪式及其他共同体艺术[communal arts]的源头。游戏的强烈愉悦，源自回应性与神入被理想化[idealized]的经验。两人召唤出一个由声音、情感、姿态与意义构成、且能够赋予活力的精巧结构，它可以被一遍又一遍地重复，并常常通过对话而日益构筑与丰盈。

The aesthetic dimension of early experience (what the cultural anthropologist Dissanayake (1992) referred to as protoaesthetic experience) is imbued with the quality of the parent/infant interaction. In their recent summation of years of empirical reporting, Beebe and Lachmann (2002) elaborated on a model of self- and object representations that emphasized their relational nature:

早期经验中的审美维度（文化人类学家迪萨纳亚克[Dissanayake]（1992）称之为前审美经验[protoaesthetic experience]），浸润着亲子互动的品质。比伯[Beebe]与拉赫曼[Lachmann]（2002）在近期对多年实证报告的总结中，阐述了一种自体与对象表征[representations]的模型，强调其关系性的本质：

Representations are persistent, organized classifications of information about an expected interactive sequence. Basic to representation is the capacity to order and recognize patterns, to expect what is predictable and invariant, and to create categories of these invariants. The ability to categorize experiences provides the organizational framework for memory, language, and the symbolic function. These representations are encoded in a non-verbal, implicit mode of information, which may be motoric (procedural), imagistic, acoustic, or visceral. Implicit processing, such as motoric or imagistic schemas, may under certain circumstances be inaccessible to attention or language, but may nevertheless continue to operate and affect how we act and feel. (p. 149)

表征是关于一个预期互动序列的、持续而条理化的信息分类。表征的基础是如下能力：整理并识别模式、预期何者可预测与恒定不变、并对这些不变量[invariants]加以归类。对经验进行分类的能力，为记忆、语言与象征功能提供了组织框架。这些表征以非言语的、内隐的[implicit]信息模态被编码，可能是动作性的（程序性的）、意象性的、听觉的或内脏的。内隐加工，例如动作性意象或意象性图式，在某些情况下可能无法被注意或语言所通达，但仍可能持续运作，并影响我们如何行动与感受。 （p. 149）

These implicit, procedural relational representations have form, sequence, rhythm, tone, and color. That is, they possess an aesthetic quality that is formally complex, diverse, and affectively rich. It is by means of elaboration of these representations that one's inner life, the experience of self and other, takes on a pervasive aesthetic structure. For example, the music in the vocalizations of mother and child, the sharing of warmth, the rhythmic interaction of mutual touching, the attunement of affect in facial interaction (especially lips and eyes) – these experiences singly and in concert become, over time, an ongoing, internal sensibility, which in the course of development is elaborated and extended into virtually every corner of the child's experience of the world. It becomes the archaic wellspring not only of meaning and fantasy, but also of quality, value, and beauty.

这些内隐的、程序性的关系表征拥有形式、序列、节律、音调与色彩。也就是说，它们具有一种形式上复杂多样、情感上丰盈的审美品质。正是通过对这些表征的构筑，一个人的内在生活——对自体与他者的经验——获得了弥散的审美结构。例如，母婴发声中的音乐性、温暖的共享、相互触摸的节律性互动、面部互动（尤其是嘴唇与眼睛）中情感的调谐——这些经验单个地、也协同地，随时间推移成为一种持续的内在感受性[internal sensibility]，并在发展过程中被构筑与延伸，渗入孩子世界经验的几乎每一个角落。它成为古老的源泉[archaic wellspring]，不仅是意义与幻想的源泉，也是品质、价值与美的源泉。

From the self psychological perspective that I am proposing, protoaesthetic experience is the experience of the formal perfection of archaic selfobject experience prior to the development of mature levels of other recognition and transience. In other words, it is an aspect of archaic idealization. Traditionally, idealization is viewed as a quality of our experience of an other. I believe that idealization does not occur only in relation to the object but is also present and elaborated within a process of interaction. The form(s) of the interpersonal transaction is or are invested with aesthetic value (see chapter four).

从我所提出的自体心理学视角来看，前审美经验，是在对他人之承认[other recognition]与无常[transience]的成熟水平发展出来之前，对古老自体客体经验[archaic selfobject experience]之形式完美[formal perfection]的经验。换言之，它是古老理想化[archaic idealization]的一个面向。传统上，理想化被视为我们对他者之经验的一种品质。我相信，理想化并不只发生在与对象的关系中，它也存在于交互过程之中、并在其中被构筑。人际交互[interpersonal transaction]的形式被投注了审美价值（参见第四章）。

An area in which this protoaesthetic experience is most clearly realized is in the sense of fascination and engagement with the mother's face. Psychological researchers Beebe and Lachmann (2002) argued that "facial mirroring" between parent and infant is "one of the foundations of intimacy throughout life" (p. 98). They pointed out that there is an enormous degree of modulation and subtlety in facial interactions, which influence the internal representations of time, space, affect, and arousal. The experience of engagement with the parent's face is a primary source of affectively charged, formal organization in which space, rhythm, sound, color, and responsivity all come together into a powerful, engrossing, and recurring aesthetic experience.

前审美经验得到最清晰实现的一个领域，是对母亲面庞的着迷与投入之感。心理学研究者比伯与拉赫曼（2002）主张，亲子之间的"面部镜映"[facial mirroring]是"贯穿一生的亲密性的基础之一"（p. 98）。他们指出，面部互动中存在极大程度的调节与微妙之处，这些影响着关于时间、空间、情感与唤醒[arousal]的内部表征。与父母面庞相投入的经验，是充溢情感的、形式化的组织之首要来源；在其中，空间、节律、声音、色彩与回应性汇聚为一种有力、引人入胜且反复出现的审美体验。

In his discussion of Natkin's Face Paintings, art critic Peter Fuller (1988) described aesthetic form as an experience of interaction with the ideal, responsive face of the actual and/or fantasized mother:

在讨论纳特金[Natkin]的《面部绘画》[Face Paintings]时，艺术评论家彼得·富勒[Peter Fuller]（1988）把审美形式描述为与实际的和/或幻想的母亲的理想而具回应的面庞相交互动的经验：

Here, one suspects, is the mother's face (and not only he [the subject]) would have liked to have stared: this is the ideal of that which he was denied, or which he perceived only in glimpses, a face suffused with subtle tenderness, apperceived more than perceived, a face whose features are, in one sense, bound, yet in another fluid and mobile, a face that can transform itself infinitely in response to our gaze, which certainly has a skin which separates it from us but which, in the next moment, can engulf and enfold us into itself. (p. 156)

这里，人们猜疑，正是母亲的面庞（而且不只他［主体］一人想要凝视它）：这是他所被剥夺、或只能瞥见之物中的理想，一张充溢着微妙柔情的脸，被统觉[apperceived]多于被知觉，一张其五官在一个意义上是有界的、在另一个意义上却是流动而多变的，一张能够回应我们的凝视而无限地自我转变的脸，它当然有一层使我们与之相隔的皮肤，却又能在下一刻把我们吞没并裹入自身之中。 （p. 156）

I believe that the psychological foundation of the sense of beauty is often the archaic experience of the infant who is joyously caught up in interaction with the responsive mother's face. The combined qualities of both symmetry and unique expressiveness in the mother's face are progressively elaborated over the course of development with other experiences. However, the mother's face is not beauty (though she may indeed be beautiful); rather, it is our sense of the experience's perfection that is beauty. The maternal face is a content or occasion of the sense of beauty that may underlie many (perhaps most) mature experiences. The reciprocal nature of pleasurable, satisfying, and secure interactions is linked to all later experiences of beauty, and the search for and cultivation of beauty becomes a lifelong human motivation. From a self psychological perspective, the sense of beauty can be understood as a selfobject experience that optimally combines the experience of being in the presence of an idealized other and of having one's perfection mirrored.

我相信，美感[the sense of beauty]的心理基础，往往是婴儿在与其具回应的母亲面庞的互动中欢欣投入的古老经验。母亲面庞上对称性与独特表现力兼具的品质，在发展进程中与其他经验一道被逐步构筑。然而，母亲的面庞并非美本身（尽管她可能确实很美）；毋宁说，是我们的经验具有完美性这一感受，才是美。母性面庞是美感的一个内容或契机，它可能潜在于许多（或许大多数）成熟经验之下。愉悦、满足与安全的互动所具有的互惠性[reciprocal nature]，与所有后来的美的经验相联结，而对美的寻求与培育，成为一种贯穿一生的动机。从自体心理学视角来看，美感可以被理解为一种自体客体经验[selfobject experience]，它最理想地结合了身处理想化的他人之旁的经验，与自身的完美性被镜映的经验。

The source of the feeling of beauty lies in the cumulative integration of experience. For example, although the unconscious source of the sense of beauty may lie in memories of the mother's face, it is the way in which new experiences both duplicate and elaborate on these archaic sources that determines what is considered beautiful.

美的感受之源头，在于经验的累积性整合。例如，尽管美感的无意识源头可能在于对母亲面庞的记忆，但决定何者被认为美，取决于新经验以何种方式既复现又构筑这些古老的源头。

However, Fuller (1988) points out that such memories/fantasies of the mother's face are composite, merged representations of the "mouth-hand, labyrinth-skin, unified situational experience" (see Spitz, 1985). Thus, the wellspring of aesthetic experience is not one specific aspect of relationship with the parent, but the whole, complex, and dynamic set of interactions in which aural tone, rhythm, temperature, touch, and gaze become associated with archaic selfobject experience that eventually functions to organize the infant's ongoing experience of self and self-in-relation-to-world.

然而，富勒（1988）指出，这些关于母亲面庞的记忆/幻想，是"口—手、迷宫—皮肤、统一的情境经验"（参见 Spitz, 1985）之复合的、融合的表征。因此，审美体验的源泉并不是与父母关系中某一特定方面，而是整个复杂而动态的互动集合；在其中，听觉音调、节律、温度、触摸与凝视，与古老的自体客体经验相关联，最终发挥组织婴儿对自体、以及处于与世界关系中的自体[self-in-relation-to-world]之持续经验的功能。

Mother's beauty goes beyond her physical appearance. It includes all aspects of mother/child interaction, such as rhythm of breathing, shared warmth, quality of touch, and facial responsiveness. The "good feed" that Winnicott talks about is not just about appetite and holding; it is also an aesthetic experience. The child experiences a confluence of interactions that possess formal qualities, such as temporal sequencing, vocal pitch, visual balance, and vibrancy. In an encounter with the idealized object, the experience of the meeting is intensely affective, profoundly meaningful, and felt to be perfect. The "good feed" becomes the "ideal feed," in which all aspects of the experience are idealized.

母亲的美超越了她的身体外观。它包含母婴互动的全部方面，例如呼吸的节律、共享的温暖、触摸的品质以及面部的回应性。温尼科特所谈的"好的喂哺"[good feed]，并不只关乎食欲与抱持；它也是一种审美体验。孩子经验到多种互动的汇流，它们具有形式品质，例如时间上的序列、声调的高低、视觉的平衡与振动力[vibrancy]。在与理想化对象的相遇中，会面[the meeting]的体验情感强烈、意义深远，并被感受为完美。"好的喂哺"由此成为"理想的喂哺"[ideal feed]，其中经验的一切方面都被理想化。

The protoaesthetic form of the mother/infant interaction becomes the prereflexive template for an individual's formal experience of the world. At the heart of aesthetic experience is the infant's interaction (playing) with the mother's face and body (and her playing back) that symbolizes the primordial processes of multimodal communication, with its location in the earliest relationships. It consists largely of implicit knowledge and prereflective, internalized representations of self-in-relation. This knowledge is extended and elaborated to other aspects of one's lifeworld. The sense of quality of experience becomes a crucial dimension of living. The inchoate and inert reality is invested with feelings of form and value.

母婴互动的前审美形式，成为个体对世界之形式经验的前反思模板[prereflexive template]。审美体验的核心，是婴儿与母亲面庞和身体（以及母亲回玩[playing back]）的互动（游戏），它象征着多模态沟通[multimodal communication]的原初过程，其位置在最早的关系之中。它大体由内隐知识以及处于关系中的自体的前反思的、被内化的表征所构成。这种知识被延伸并构筑到一个人生活世界的其他方面。经验的品质之感，成为生活的一个关键维度。混沌而惰性的现实，被投注以形式与价值的感受。

Thus, aesthetic experience is the residua of the experience and expectation that rhythm, fittedness, colors, and sound possess meaning and a transcendent sense of value, grace, and beauty. These formal aspects of vital attunement, a state of actual or fantasized perfection, become the foundation for aesthetic experience of the social and natural world. Hence, the world becomes infused with subjectivity; these representations are not limited to visual or otherwise descriptive memories, but are in fact complex, coordinated, and generalized cognitive/affective experiential states that are felt to capture idealized qualities of self and/or world.

因此，审美体验是如下经验与期待的残余[residua]：节律、契合[fittedness]、色彩与声音拥有意义，并具有一种超越性的价值感、优雅与美。这些活力调谐[vital attunement]的形式面向——一种实际的或幻想的完美状态——成为对社会世界与自然世界之审美体验的基础。于是，世界被注入主体性；这些表征不限于视觉的或其他描述性的记忆，而实际上是复杂的、协调的、泛化的认知/情感体验状态，被感受为捕捉到了自体与/或世界的理想化品质。

Over time, the young child's sense of attunement and fittedness with the ideal and beautiful becomes riddled with impingement and failure. Breakdowns in empathy and experiences of unresponsiveness and/or distress disrupt the child's aesthetic experience and necessitate strategies to protect and repair the sense of self and world. This process includes the maintenance of a relationship with the beautiful/the ideal, along with a growing recognition of the hard realities of living. In fact, aesthetic experience can become a means to mitigate failure and loss, transcending grim reality through the transformation of the real into the beautiful. In this way, mature aesthetic experience comes to include the recognition and even celebration of the tragic as well as the sublime. It is that aspect of our experience in which the sense of quality, formal value, and beauty combines with the recognition of failure and mortality.

随时间推移，幼儿与理想和美好之物之间神入与契合之感，变得遍布侵扰与失败。共情[empathy]的崩解，以及无回应与/或痛苦的经验，扰乱了孩子的审美体验，并使得保护与修复自体与世界的感受成为必要，这就需要各种策略。这一过程包括：维系与美/理想的关系，同时也日益承认生活的严峻现实。事实上，审美体验可以成为缓解失败与丧失的一种手段，通过把真实转化为美，来超越严酷的现实。以这种方式，成熟的审美体验逐渐把对悲剧性[tragic]与崇高性[sublime]的承认、乃至庆祝包含进来。它正是我们经验中的这样一个面向：品质之感、形式价值与美，与对失败和必死性[mortality]的承认相结合。

There is a mature level of awareness of the tragic nature of life and of one's own humanity, but this awareness is articulated in a form (visual, verbal, linguistic, musical) that is of such a refined quality that tragedy is given beautiful expression. Thus, tragic man transcends his or her vulnerability and mortality through aesthetic experience. It is pleasurable, vitalizing, and accompanied by a feeling of "fittedness" and positive self-experience. But, in addition, the individual encounters the reality of disjunction, transience, and even death. We are aided in living with the recognition of our vulnerability, our tragedies, and our mortality.

存在一种成熟水平的觉察：对生命之悲剧性本质以及对自身人性的觉察；但这种觉察以一种（视觉的、言语的、语言的、音乐的）形式被表述出来，其品质如此精炼，以致悲剧被赋予了美的表达。于是，悲剧性的人[tragic man]通过审美体验超越了自己的脆弱与必死性。这种体验令人愉悦、赋予活力，并伴随一种"契合感"[fittedness]与积极的自体经验。但除此之外，个体还遭遇了断裂[disjunction]、无常与甚至死亡的真实。承认自身的脆弱、我们的悲剧与我们的必死性，有助于我们更好地生活。

In mature aesthetic experience, there is recognition that all things are temporary, limited, and will ultimately fade and be lost. For mature adults, this can be said to be an ever-present aspect of all experience; however, in instances such as art appreciation, aesthetic experience achieves a heightened, even transcendent level. The sense of reality is organized according to processes of idealization amalgamated with the recognition of the other in the world. Otherness includes acceptance of the inevitability of failure of reality to meet the needs of the self in fundamental ways. In addressing the experiences of failure, loss, and death, great works of art often possess beauty even as they express terrible truths.

在成熟的审美体验中，存在着这样一种承认：一切事物都是暂时的、有限的，终将消逝与被失去。对成熟成人而言，这可以说是贯穿一切经验的一个恒常面向；然而，在诸如艺术鉴赏这类情形中，审美体验达到一种被提升的、甚至超越性的水平。现实感[the sense of reality]按照理想化过程与世界中之他者的承认相融合的方式而被组织。他者性[otherness]包含对如下事实的接受：现实在根本上不可避免地无法满足自体的需要。在应对失败、丧失与死亡的经验时，伟大的艺术作品往往在表达可怕真理的同时，仍拥有美。

Another way to put it is that ideal aesthetic resonance between mother and child does not occur under normal conditions. For most of us, all that is needed to develop an adequate aesthetic sense is "good enough mothering," as described by Winnicott (1971). Given this, the child and mother engage in an adequate amount of aesthetically resonant interactions to facilitate the internalization of a good enough formal experience of self and self-in-world. Thus, most of us are not great artists, brilliant critics, or refined aestheticians, but most of us do experience an ongoing and consistent sense of our world as generally pleasing and potentially beautiful. In extreme instances of psychopathology, deprivation, or trauma, the aesthetic experience may remain undeveloped or suffer disruption, leading to acute or chronic perceptions of ugliness (see chapter seven).

换一种说法是：母婴之间理想的审美共鸣[aesthetic resonance]，在正常条件下并不会发生。对我们大多数人而言，发展出足够的审美感所需要的全部，就是温尼科特（1971）所描述的"足够好的母职"[good enough mothering]。有鉴于此，孩子与母亲进行足量的审美共鸣互动，以促进一种足够好的、关于自体与处于世界中之自体的形式经验被内化。因此，我们大多数人并非伟大的艺术家、杰出的批评家或精微的美学家，但我们大多数人确实经验到一种持续而一贯的感受：我们的世界大体上是令人愉悦的、并且潜在地是美的。在精神病理、剥夺或创伤的极端情形中，审美体验可能保持未发展状态或遭受扰乱，导致急性或慢性的丑陋感知（参见第七章）。

But many artists, often quite talented ones, had deprived or traumatic childhoods; how is it that they still developed such a refined aesthetic sense? In these people, the need to idealize, to seek, and to perfect a formal organization of experience, even when unresponded to in childhood, may persist as a potentiality, especially for those with a talent around which intense gratification, feelings of efficacy, and social validation become possible. There are many accounts of deprived or traumatized persons who discover in art or other creative activity an opportunity to repair or compensate for self-deficits and to create a substitute for what was missed in the infantile tie. These acquired aesthetic relationships, while not possessing the transformative power of actual love relationships (many successful artists suffer greatly, despite the success of their work) nonetheless function psychologically in important ways. With sufficient talent and effort, the organizing impact of the experience or activity can be compelling, and at the very least, it helps compensate for self-deficits or vulnerabilities.

但许多艺术家——常常是相当有才华的那些——拥有被剥夺的或创伤性的童年；他们何以仍发展出如此精炼的审美感？在这些人身上，理想化、寻求并完善一种经验之形式组织的需要，即便在童年未获回应，也可能作为一种潜能持续存在——尤其是对那些拥有某种天赋的人，围绕着这种天赋，强烈的满足、效能感[feelings of efficacy]与社会认可成为可能。有许多关于被剥夺者或受创者的记述表明，他们在艺术或其他创造性活动中发现了修复或补偿自体缺陷[self-deficits]、并为婴儿期纽带中所缺失之物创造替代品的机会。这些后天获得的审美关系，虽不具备真实爱的关系所具有的那种转化力量（许多成功的艺术家尽管作品成功，却深受痛苦），但在心理上仍以重要的方式发挥作用。只要具备足够的天赋与努力，该经验或活动的组织性影响可以是强有力的；而至少，它有助于补偿自体缺陷或脆弱性。

Of course, it is impossible to fully understand anything human outside its cultural context. The deprived or traumatized person has a wide range of resources that the culture makes available to assist with living and survival. A major psychological resource is to be found in art, creative opportunities, and the aesthetic attitude in general. These resources exist in all cultures and are part of humanity's aesthetic orientation toward the community and the world. An individual person, in seeking the means to repair, augment, and sustain self-experience and relatedness, may choose some form of aesthetic resource and make use of it psychologically and socially.

当然，脱离文化脉络，就不可能完整地理解任何属人的事物。被剥夺者或受创者拥有文化所提供的广泛资源，用以协助其生活与生存。一项主要的心理资源，见于艺术、创造性的机会以及总体的审美态度[aesthetic attitude]之中。这些资源存在于一切文化之中，是人类朝向共同体与世界的审美取向[aesthetic orientation]的一部分。个体在寻求修复、增益并维系自体经验与关联性[relatedness]的手段时，可能会选择某种形式的审美资源，并在心理上与社会上加以利用。

In fact, it is possible, given the exquisite refinement of many of the arts and the psychological meaning inherent in them, for even the most deprived and damaged person to wrest some benefit. In these cases, the aesthetic activity acts as a scaffold for a fragile, precarious self. Many artists are driven to engage in aesthetic relating and creativity because of the powerful defensive and compensatory function that art serves for them. They become addicted to the search for ideal aesthetic form and may even feel that their very existence is dependent on the successful creative process.

事实上，鉴于许多艺术门类之精妙与其中所固有的心理意义，即便是最被剥夺、最受损伤的人，也有可能争得某些益处。在这些情形中，审美活动为脆弱、岌岌可危的自体充当脚手架[scaffold]。许多艺术家之所以被驱使去从事审美关联与创造，是因为艺术对他们发挥着强有力的防御性与补偿性功能。他们变得沉溺于对理想审美形式的寻求，甚至可能感到自己的存在本身有赖于创造过程的成功。

Aesthetic experience holds us in its embrace as we both create and discover the world. We are sustained psychologically by the formal perfection of the work, object, or experience. This perfection is not superficial. The real is both conjured up and confronted in an ideal form. It is beautiful while it is also terrible. Our fearful imaginings are reconciled with the dreadful truth – they are merged within the perfect image.

当我们在创造世界的同时也发现世界时，审美体验把我们拥入怀中。我们在心理上被作品、对象或经验的形式完美所支撑。这种完美并不肤浅。真实以理想的形式既被召唤出来、也被正面相对。它既美又可怕。我们恐惧的想象与可怕的真相相调和——它们融合于那完美的意象[image]之中。

The fragments of our inner lives, of our self-experience, are gathered up, coordinated, and held together in and by the aesthetic experience. The terrors of unconscious fantasy and desire are made manifest to consciousness and enter communal life. Many of us, when we enter the Sistine Chapel – a crowd of vulnerable beings – find that we become like gods, our humanity both acknowledged and elevated. The most powerful and terrible fantasies of our culture (the creation and fall of man and his final judgment) are expressed through forms of such an ideal nature that we transcend our fragile humanity and mortality, and, strangely, we become hopeful.

我们内在生活、我们自体经验的碎片，在审美体验之中、并借由审美体验被聚拢、协调并维系在一起。无意识幻想与欲望的恐怖被显明于意识，并进入共同体的生活。我们中的许多人，在走进西斯廷教堂[Sistine Chapel]时——一群脆弱的生命——发现自己变得如同神明，我们的人性既被承认、又被提升。我们文化中最有力、最可怕的幻想（人的创造与堕落、以及他的最后审判）经由如此理想本性的形式被表达出来，以致我们超越了自己脆弱的生命与必死性；而奇怪的是，我们由此变得满怀希望。

Given the central role of idealization in aesthetic experience – and, as we will see, its primacy later in the creative process (chapter five) and in the sense of beauty (chapter six), the next chapter will examine the problem of idealization in some depth. To accomplish this, we will have to backtrack a bit, going over some of the same developmental ground from a different perspective. As we shall see, idealization is a fundamental component of the motivation for attachment, becoming elaborated throughout life as a key aspect of certain important types of relationships. Its most exquisite manifestation is found in aesthetic experience and in the creation and appreciation of art.

鉴于理想化在审美体验中的核心作用——而且正如我们将看到的，它在后文讨论创造过程（第五章）与美感（第六章）时的首要地位——下一章将较为深入地考察理想化问题。为此，我们将不得不稍作回溯，从一种不同的视角重新走过部分相同的发展领域。正如我们将看到的，理想化是依恋[attachment]动机的一个基本成分，它在整个生命历程中被逐步构筑，成为某些重要类型关系的一个关键面向。它最精妙的显现，见于审美体验之中，以及艺术的创造与欣赏之中。
