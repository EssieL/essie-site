Aesthetic experience is as important to human life as sex, hunger, aggression, love, and hate. Although we may rarely be conscious of it, aesthetic experience gives form, meaning, and, most importantly, value to everything we are and everything we do. Theoretically, without it, life would be a shapeless, meaningless, and colorless series of sensations, events, and reactions.

审美体验[aesthetic experience]对于人类生活而言，与性、饥饿、攻击性、爱与恨同样重要。尽管我们或许很少意识到它，但审美体验赋予我们所是的一切、我们所做的一切以形式[form]、意义[meaning]，以及最重要的——价值[value]。从理论上说，若没有它，生活将沦为一系列无形、无意义、无色彩的感觉、事件与反应。

Although aesthetic experience achieves its most refined form in art, all that we sense, imagine, and dream is shaped by it, for better or for worse. When I say shaped, I mean that aesthetic experience is not a quality of objects or sensations; it is an emergent phenomenon that arises in the transitional psychological zone in which our creative engagement with the world is lived. We see its most archaic manifestation in the curve of the mother's shoulder during nursing, her heartbeat and breath, the melody of her voice, the balance of her eyes and smile – all embedded in the warmth, nourishment, and security of the mother/infant interaction. In its mature forms, we find aesthetic experience in the works of, among many others, Michelangelo, Titian, Degas, and Picasso. Although the nursing situation and the viewing of Picasso's Guernica are radically different experiences, I will argue in this book that the fundamental nature of the aesthetic experience is the same, having a common source – the human drive to give form and value to the experiences of self and self-in-relation.

尽管审美体验[aesthetic experience]在艺术中达到其最精纯的形式，但我们所感觉、想象与梦想的一切，无论好坏，都被它塑形[shaped]。当我说"塑形"时，我的意思是：审美体验并非对象或感觉的一种属性[quality]；它是一种涌现现象[emergent phenomenon]，在我们与世界进行创造性投入[creative engagement]的那个过渡性心理区域[transitional psychological zone]中产生。我们在哺乳时母亲肩头的曲线、她的心跳与呼吸、她嗓音的旋律、她眼神与微笑的平衡中，看到它最古老[archaic]的显现——这一切都嵌入在母亲/婴儿[mother/infant]互动的温暖、滋养与安全之中。在其成熟形态中，我们在米开朗基罗、提香、德加与毕加索等众多人的作品中，发现审美体验。尽管哺乳情境与观看毕加索的《格尔尼卡》[Guernica]是截然不同的体验，但我在本书中将论证：审美体验的根本性质是相同的，它们有一个共同的源头——即人类要为自体[self]以及关系中的自体[self-in-relation]的体验赋予形式与价值的驱力[drive]。

I will offer a definition of aesthetic experience in the next chapter, but let me say here that I am extending its meaning beyond the limits of its traditional one. During the eighteenth century, a new approach to aesthetics emerged that emphasized individual experience and a specific type of emotional response to aesthetic objects. Most importantly, Western thinkers were concerned with the internal source of the sense of beauty. Later areas of aesthetic experience, such as the sublime, ugliness, and the picturesque, were also studied, but aesthetic experience continued to be approached as a delimited psychological process, mostly involving responses to artwork or special natural phenomena. Although in this book I highlight these types of special aesthetic responses, the heart of my argument lies in the belief that every aspect of our experience has an aesthetic dimension (or perhaps dimensions), and that this dimension is optimally elaborated and refined throughout life. Examples of this are endless: from the way we dress, the cars we drive, the gardens we tend, the hair we comb, the sunsets we admire, the tone of voice we appreciate, the gestures that entrance us. We each have our aesthetic that is directly linked to our sense of well-being. As we will see, the source of this aesthetic dimension is the internalization of the experience of the formal elements of human relationships, to be investigated in depth in chapter three.

我将在下一章给出审美体验[aesthetic experience]的一个定义，但在此请容我说明：我正在将其含义扩展到其传统界限之外。在18世纪，一种新的美学[aesthetics]进路[approach]兴起，它强调个体经验，以及面对审美对象[aesthetic objects]的一种特定类型的情绪反应[emotional response]。最重要的是，西方思想家关注的是美感[sense of beauty]的内在源头。此后，审美体验的其他领域——例如崇高[the sublime]、丑[ugliness]与画意[the picturesque]——也得到了研究，但审美体验始终被当作一个被划定了边界的心理过程[delimited psychological process]来处理，主要涉及对艺术作品或特殊自然现象的反应。尽管我在本书中着重讨论这些特殊类型的审美反应[aesthetic responses]，但我论证的核心在于这样一个信念：我们经验的每一个方面都具有一种审美的维度[dimension]（或许是复数的诸维度），而这一维度在人的一生中得到最佳的化育[elaborated]与精炼[refined]。这样的例子不胜枚举：从我们穿衣的方式、我们驾驶的汽车、我们打理的庭园、我们梳理的头发、我们欣赏的落日、我们珍视的嗓音语调，到令我们着迷的姿态。我们每个人都拥有自己的审美[aesthetic]，它与我们的幸福感[sense of well-being]直接相连。正如我们将看到的，这一审美维度的源头，是对人际关系之形式要素[formal elements]的经验的内化[internalization]——这一点将在第三章深入考察。

The psychology of aesthetic experience has always been of interest to psychoanalysts. Early on, Freud viewed creativity and art as defensively motivated, sublimatory channels for forbidden sexuality (Freud, 1908, 1910, 1925a, 1925b). His was an interpretive approach in which the symbolic forms of artistic expression revealed hidden wishes. This classical view of creativity as a type of dreamwork has been the center of psychoanalytic aesthetics for generations. Later analysts, such as Ernst Kris (1952), viewed art more progressively, with the ego harnessing the resources of the unconscious for the purpose of self-expression. This approach retained Freud's emphasis on symbolism, but now we see regression as existing at the service of the ego rather than the other way around.

审美体验[aesthetic experience]的心理学一直令精神分析师[psychoanalysts]感兴趣。早期，弗洛伊德将创造力[creativity]与艺术视为被防御所驱动的、为被禁忌的性[forbidden sexuality]提供出口的升华性[sublimatory]渠道（Freud, 1908, 1910, 1925a, 1925b）。他的进路是一种解释性进路[interpretive approach]：在其中，艺术表达的象征形式[symbolic forms]揭示了被隐藏的愿望[hidden wishes]。这一把创造力视为一种梦的工作[dreamwork]的古典观点[classical view]，世代以来一直是精神分析美学[psychoanalytic aesthetics]的中心。后来的分析家，如恩斯特·克里斯[Ernst Kris]（1952），则以更为进步的观点看待艺术：自我[ego]为自我表达[self-expression]的目的而驾驭无意识[unconscious]的资源。这一进路保留了弗洛伊德对象征作用[symbolism]的强调，但如今我们认为，退行[regression]是服务于自我[at the service of the ego]而存在，而非反过来。

Eventually, psychoanalysts such as Gilbert Rose (1992) and Jerome Oremland (1997) would elevate creativity to the status of a complex developmental accomplishment – almost a meta-ego function resulting in a higher level of human experience. Consistent with the classical psychoanalytic perspective on the mind as a self-regulating system, most of the more recent analysts have approached the psychology of art and the artist from a primarily intrapsychic viewpoint, only tangentially related to other people (for example, the potential audience). This has been echoed by our culture's myth of the artist as a solitary rebel who defies convention and critical judgment. However, some recent thinkers have argued that the artist is a far more social being than has generally been realized (Dissanayake, 2000; Press, 2002; Rotenberg, 1988).

最终，像吉尔伯特·罗斯[Gilbert Rose]（1992）与杰罗姆·奥雷姆兰[Jerome Oremland]（1997）这样的精神分析师，把创造力提升为一项复杂的发展成就[developmental accomplishment]——几乎是一种元自我功能[meta-ego function]，带来人类经验的更高层级。与把心灵[mind]视为一个自我调节系统[self-regulating system]的古典精神分析视角相一致，多数较晚近的分析家都从一个主要是心理内部[intrapsychic]的立场来看待艺术与艺术家的心理学，只与其他他人（例如潜在的观众[audience]）有间接的关联。我们文化中那个把艺术家视为抗拒常规与批评判断的孤绝反叛者[solitary rebel]的神话[myth]，正与此相呼应。然而，一些晚近的思想者主张，艺术家[artist]是一种远比通常所认识到的更为社会性的存在（Dissanayake, 2000; Press, 2002; Rotenberg, 1988）。

I believe that the major limitation of the traditional psychoanalytic approach has been its focus on a closed-system model of the artist's mind. This has become increasingly obvious, given the recent revolution in psychoanalytic thinking, which views human psychology as more relational and intersubjective than previously thought (Beebe and Lachmann, 2002; Mitchell, 1988).

我认为，传统精神分析进路的重大局限，在于它聚焦于艺术家心灵的一个封闭系统模型[closed-system model]。鉴于精神分析思想晚近发生的革命——它把人类心理学视为比以往所认为的更具关系性[relational]、更具主体间性[intersubjective]（Beebe and Lachmann, 2002; Mitchell, 1988）——这一点已变得日益明显。

This book emphasizes the central role of relatedness and intersubjectivity in the aesthetic experience and the creative process. From this perspective, I develop a model of aesthetic experience that involves the gradual emergence of a sense of idealized form out of the matrix of the mother/infant interaction. I then apply this model to several problems that I consider important to our understanding of aesthetic experience. In this introductory chapter, I will highlight seven of the most important points of this developmental/relational perspective, in the sequence that they will be discussed in the remainder of the book.

本书强调关系性[relatedness]与主体间性[intersubjectivity]在审美体验[aesthetic experience]与创造过程[creative process]中的核心作用。从这一视角出发，我建立了一个审美体验的模型：它涉及一种理想化形式[idealized form]之感从母亲/婴儿互动的基质[matrix]中逐渐涌现。随后我把这一模型应用于若干我认为对我们理解审美体验十分重要的问题。在本导论章中，我将按它们在本书其余部分被讨论的顺序，着重指出这一发展/关系取向[developmental/relational perspective]最重要的七个要点。

The Developmental Matrix of Aesthetic Experience（审美体验的发展基质）

In the next two chapters, I will introduce the problem of aesthetic experience through a close examination of its developmental and relational sources. Since Freud saw art as a compromise formation between conflicting parts of the mental apparatus, the relational and developmental sources of art were not relevant to him. For Freud, art was just one of many manifestations of the defense mechanism of sublimation, and, while taking on the trappings of culture, it was at heart an intrapsychic phenomenon (Freud, 1908). Later, Ernst Kris viewed art as resulting from regression in the service of the ego, whereby earlier forms of mentation and instinctual life would be temporarily allowed access to consciousness in order to permit aesthetic expression for the purpose of mastery (Kris, 1952). However, once again, the source of art lay in the functions of the ego – which made use of early mental life – but these infantile experiences in no way constituted aesthetic experience itself. Ultimately, the notion of elaboration of the products of regression during the later phase of the creative process placed the heart of successful creativity in the mature capacities of the artist (Rose, 1980; Rotenberg, 1988).

在接下来的两章中，我将通过细致考察审美体验的发展性来源与关系性来源，来引入审美体验这一问题。由于弗洛伊德把艺术视为精神装置[mental apparatus]中相互冲突的各个部分之间的一种妥协形成[compromise formation]，艺术的关系性来源与发展性来源对他而言并不相干。对弗洛伊德来说，艺术不过是升华[sublimation]这一防御机制[defense mechanism]的众多显现之一，它虽披上文化的外衣，但骨子里是一种心理内部现象[intrapsychic phenomenon]（Freud, 1908）。后来，恩斯特·克里斯把艺术视为"服务于自我的退行"[regression in the service of the ego]的结果：借此，较早的心理活动形式[mentation]与本能生活[instinctual life]被暂时允许进入意识，以促成以掌控[mastery]为目的的审美表达（Kris, 1952）。然而，艺术的源头再一次落在自我[ego]的功能之上——自我利用了早期心理生活——但这些婴儿期经验[infantile experiences]绝不构成审美体验本身。最终，把创造过程后期阶段中对退行产物的化育[elaboration]这一观念，将成功创造力的核心置于艺术家的成熟能力[capacities]之中（Rose, 1980; Rotenberg, 1988）。

The core of aesthetic experience contains an intensely interactive component in which the mutual engagement of artist with both artwork and audience results in the emergence of an archaic state of self-experience, associated with the early bond with the mother. Mature creativity involves the activation of archaic affective states and cross-modal perception. This is the adult elaboration of the emergent organization of the infant's subjective world as a template for aesthetic experience.

审美体验的核心包含一个高度互动性的成分：在其中，艺术家与作品及观众双方的相互投入[mutual engagement]，带来一种古老的自体体验状态[state of self-experience]的涌现，这种状态与早期同母亲的联结[bond]相关联。成熟的创造力涉及古老的情感状态[archaic affective states]与跨通道知觉[cross-modal perception]的激活。这是成年人对婴儿主观世界[subjective world]之涌现组织的化育，并将其作为一种审美体验的模板[template]。

Early regressive ego states and experiences play a part in creativity, but, additionally, the progressive organizational capacities of the infant – not just regression – underlie aesthetic processes in later life. These organizational capacities emerge and are elaborated within an interpersonal context. Mature forms of aesthetic experience and creativity involve a type of 'rematriation' (Rose, 1992, p. 64), but, in addition, they involve the self-experience-in-relation-to-other that infant research has highlighted.

早期的退行性自我状态与经验在创造力中起一份作用，但除此之外，婴儿的进展性组织能力[progressive organizational capacities]——而不仅仅是退行[regression]——构成了此后人生中审美过程的基础。这些组织能力在人际语境[interpersonal context]中涌现并被化育。成熟形态的审美体验与创造力涉及一种"回归母体"['rematriation']（Rose, 1992, p. 64），但此外，它们还涉及婴儿研究[infant research]所着重指出的那种"与他者相关的自体体验"[self-experience-in-relation-to-other]。

Art is linked to the re-creation of early experiences of relationships with important caregivers and is related to the expression and embodiment of psychological processes of idealization. Thus, the psychological function of art and creativity is powerfully linked to the intensely relational core of infantile experience. Considering recent insights gained in infant research and in the intersubjective nature of human psychological experience, some authors have begun to consider how the fundamental drive for and experience of mutuality and intimacy play a part in aesthetic experience and the creative process.

艺术与对早期同重要照护者[important caregivers]之关系经验的重创[re-creation]相关联，并且与理想化[idealization]心理过程的表达与具身[embodiment]有关。因此，艺术与创造力的心理功能，与婴儿期经验中那个高度关系性的核心有着强有力的关联。鉴于婴儿研究以及人类心理经验之主体间性质所获得的晚近洞见，一些作者已开始思考：对相互性[mutuality]与亲密[intimacy]的根本驱力与体验，如何参与进审美体验与创造过程之中。

Recent developments in infant research have elucidated the complex interpersonal relatedness of the human infant and the affective interplay that characterizes the parent–infant dyad. The internalization of these rhythms and modes becomes the bedrock of the aesthetic forms, values, and experiences that eventually develop among members of a given culture. In fact, one can find the intimate aesthetics of mother and child in the rituals and art forms of larger cultures, which seek to increase connection and mutuality among members through the evocation of developmentally early experiences of attunement.

婴儿研究的晚近进展已阐明人类婴儿复杂的人际关联性，以及刻画亲子二元体[parent–infant dyad]的情感交互[affective interplay]。对这些节律与模式的内化[internalization]，成为最终在某一既定文化的成员之间发展起来的审美形式、价值与经验的基岩[bedrock]。事实上，人们可以在更大规模文化的仪式[rituals]与艺术形式中，找到母亲与孩子之间那种亲密的审美：这些文化试图通过唤起发展上早期的调谐[attunement]经验，来增进成员之间的联结与相互性。

Idealization（理想化）

In chapter four, I focus more closely on the place of idealization in human psychological life and aesthetic experience. Though traditionally viewed as a defense, a displacement, or a protection from one's own or the other's aggressive wishes, idealization is fundamental to our valuation of self, our relationships, and our experience of the world. Its source lies in the early affective connection between infant and caregiver, during which the communication of mutual idealization arises and is elaborated – this, I argue, is a crucial though unacknowledged motive for attachment. Ultimately, it is the extension of idealization beyond the dyad that gives life value, forming the basis of mature levels of self-esteem, love, and moral judgment.

在第四章，我更细致地聚焦于理想化[idealization]在人类心理生活与审美体验中的位置。尽管理想化传统上被视为一种防御、一种移置[displacement]，或一种对自身或他人的攻击性愿望的防护，但它对于我们评价[valuation]自体、我们的关系以及我们对世界的经验而言是根本性的。其源头在于婴儿与照护者之间早期的情感联结：在其中，相互理想化[mutual idealization]的沟通得以产生并被化育——我主张，这是一种至关重要却未被承认的依恋[attachment]动机。最终，正是理想化向二元体[dyad]之外的延伸，赋予生命以价值，构成成熟水平的自尊[self-esteem]、爱[love]与道德判断[moral judgment]的基础。

Chapter four also explores the role of value affects in idealization and suggests how idealization is elaborated into adult relationships and self-experience. Finally, I argue that idealization is the link between the ideal nature of the parent–child bond and mature forms of aesthetic experience. Specifically, it is the idealization of the formal organization of interaction that grants high value to the gestures, shapes, sounds, colors, and rhythms that characterize the interaction of parent and child.

第四章还探讨价值情感[value affects]在理想化中的作用，并提出理想化如何被化育进成人关系与自体体验。最后，我主张，理想化是亲子联结[parent–child bond]的理想性质与成熟形态的审美体验之间的联结环节。具体而言，正是对互动之形式组织的理想化[idealization of the formal organization of interaction]，赋予了那些刻画亲子互动的姿态、形状、声音、色彩与节律以高度的价值。

The Creative Process（创造过程）

Chapter five concerns the psychological processes in creative work. The normal boundaries between self and other are fluid as the artist engages reality. He or she is not introspectively ruminating in his or her own private world; to the contrary, the artist's self-experience is powerfully linked to the world of objects and others who are also, paradoxically, creations of the artist. In fact, I would argue that creativity is fundamentally intersubjective in this sense. Once an action is taken to alter the object in some way, it can be said that subjectivity is externalized, and the artist enters a relationship with an object that is now invested with qualities of the artist's own subjective experience. This is a non-solipsistic model of aesthetics in which the qualities of the external object are essential (as in the maternal–infant dialogue). In fact, the action taken upon the object must include the qualities of the object (its color, plasticity, and so on), as well as the changing relationship between internal and external aspects of subjectivity.

第五章关乎创造性工作[creative work]中的心理过程。当艺术家投入现实[reality]时，自体与他者之间的正常界限是流动的。他或她并非在内省地反刍自己私人的世界；恰恰相反，艺术家的自体体验有力地与对象世界以及他人相连——而这些人悖论性地也是艺术家的创造物。事实上，我要主张，创造力在这个意义上是根本上主体间的[intersubjective]。一旦采取某个行动以某种方式改动对象，就可以说主体性被外化了[externalized]，艺术家进入与一个对象的关系，而这个对象如今被赋予了艺术家自身主观经验的种种性质。这是一种非唯我论[non-solipsistic]的美学模型：在其中，外部对象[external object]的诸性质是本质性的（正如在母婴对话[maternal–infant dialogue]中那样）。事实上，施加于对象之上的行动必须包含对象的诸性质（它的色彩、可塑性[plasticity]等等），也必须包含主体性之内部方面与外部方面之间不断变化的关系。

However, once an action is taken and an aspect of subjectivity is expressed in the work, this externalized subjectivity becomes, to a greater or lesser degree, disjunctive with self-experience (either because of the object or the form of self-expression or because of the rapid unfolding of the artist's self-experience). The artist then acts upon the subjective objects as both internal and external to self-experience. There is a unique dialectic established between internal and external aspects of subjectivity (impacted also by the nature of the medium and dynamics of the art world). Because of this dialectic, further actions are taken, and the artwork develops toward perfection. The artist engages with an 'other,' which was in fact once part of the self but is now external, possessing through concretization in media or language a separate subjectivity. That which has been made external, in other words, has been 'expressed'; it is the objectification of the artist's self-experience.

然而，一旦采取了某个行动、主体性的某个方面在作品中被表达出来，这个被外化的主体性便会在或大或小的程度上与自体体验相脱节[disjunctive]（或因对象、或因自我表达的形式、或因艺术家自体体验的迅速展开）。艺术家随后把那些主观对象[subjective objects]既当作自体体验之内的、也当作其之外的来施加行动。在主体性的内部方面与外部方面之间，建立起了独特的辩证关系[dialectic]（也受到媒介[medium]之性质与艺术界[art world]之动态的影响）。由于这一辩证关系，更多的行动被采取，作品朝完美[perfection]发展。艺术家与之打交道的，是一个"他者"['other']——它事实上曾是自体的一部分，但如今是外部的，并经由在媒介或语言中的具体化[concretization]而拥有一个独立的主体性。换言之，那被外化出来的东西已经被"表达"了；它是艺术家自体体验的客体化[objectification]。

Self-expression in aesthetic experience is not limited to emotion, affect, or even ideas or impressions (although it may contain all of these). The form of self-expression contained in artistic creation is best captured in the idea of being, of conveying in the work aspects of how it feels to be the living person who one is.

审美体验中的自我表达，并不限于情绪、情感，甚至也不限于观念或印象（尽管它可能包含这一切）。艺术创造中所含的自我表达形式，用"存在"[being]这一观念来把握最为贴切——即在作品中传达作为活生生之人、作为自己所是的那个人的种种感受。

However, art is not simply a mirror, a representation of us; it is a new creation that evokes self-experience and embodies the self-in-relation through aesthetic perfection. This allows us to include within the rubric of art the ornate majesty of Chartres Cathedral alongside the cool simplicity of a Rothko painting. As different as these two may be, both can be said to contain within them some perfect expression of human experience.

然而，艺术并非一面简单的镜子、一种对"我们"的再现[representation]；它是一种新的创造，经由审美的完美唤起自体体验，并具身化"关系中的自体"[self-in-relation]。这使我们得以把沙特尔大教堂[Chartres Cathedral]华美的庄严与罗斯科[Rothko]画作冷峻的简约，一并纳入艺术的名目之下。尽管这两者可能天差地别，但可以说二者内部都含有对人类经验的某种完美表达。

The unfolding dialectic between artist and artwork is accompanied by the fluctuation of states of emotional tension and self-experience. The sense of resonance between the external and internal aspects of subjectivity is self-confirming and pleasurable. Dissonance leads to varying levels of self-crisis. Aesthetic resonance is the degree to which internal aspects of subjectivity, and its external aspects – both concretized in the artwork – are conjunctive, one to the other. In other words, aesthetic resonance is the way in which the relationships between the colors and forms of a painting express the organization of the artist's internal world.

艺术家与作品之间展开的辩证关系，伴随着情绪张力状态与自体体验状态的起伏波动。主体性之外部方面与内部方面之间的共鸣感[resonance]，是自体确认的[self-confirming]且令人愉悦的。不协和[dissonance]则导致不同程度的自体危机[self-crisis]。审美共鸣[aesthetic resonance]是指主体性的内部方面与其外部方面——二者都具体化于作品之中——彼此相互契合[conjunctive]的程度。换言之，审美共鸣是绘画的色彩与形式之间的关系据以表达艺术家内部世界之组织的方式。

Fundamental to this idea is that the artwork and the artist's subjectivity form a single intersubjective field, in which inner and outer are irrelevant. As a result of the experience of aesthetic resonance, the artist feels vitalized, more cohesive, directed, and alive. If he or she is successful in developing the work toward greater perfection, this inner state can be quite powerful, and the artist's self-experience is idealized. However, this resonance can also be fragile and easily lost – thus fueling the desire to create.

这一观念的根本之处在于：作品与艺术家的主体性构成一个单一的主体间场域[intersubjective field]——在其中，内与外都不再相干。作为审美共鸣经验的结果，艺术家感到被赋予了活力[vitalized]、更加凝聚[cohesive]、有方向、有生气。如果他或她成功地把作品推向更大的完美，这种内在状态可以相当强大，而艺术家的自体体验则被理想化[idealized]。然而，这种共鸣也可能脆弱且易于丧失——从而助燃了创造的欲望。

Self psychologists use the term selfobject experience to capture this psychological state. It is a psychological experience in which the individual enters a relationship with an object that is felt to have ideal qualities (an idealized selfobject) and/or when one experiences the object as reflecting ideal qualities of oneself (the grandiose self). In most cases, successful creativity involves both an idealized and a grandiose selfobject experience. The artist feels the self to be in the presence of an ideal object that reflects an experience of the ideal self.

自体心理学家[self psychologists]使用"自体客体体验"[selfobject experience]这一术语来把握这一心理状态。它是一种心理体验：在其中，个体进入与一个被认为具有理想性质的对象的关系（理想化自体客体[idealized selfobject]），和/或当一个人把该对象体验为反映着自身理想性质时（夸大自体[grandiose self]）。在多数情况下，成功的创造力既涉及理想化的自体客体体验，也涉及夸大性的自体客体体验。艺术家感到自体处在理想对象在场[presence]之下，而这个对象反映着对理想自体[ideal self]的体验。

However, selfobject experience tends to be precarious or fragile. The individual inevitably experiences a sense of failure, in which the object, for whatever reason, is no longer experienced as ideal or reflective of one's grandiose self. Such a disjunction occurs, for example, when an artist begins work in the morning and finds that the piece under creation is only a confusing jumble, when the night before he or she had left a 'masterpiece.' In such a case, externalized aspects of subjectivity are no longer in conjunction with self-experience, and they are experienced as something other – at times, as something far removed from what is imagined or desired.

然而，自体客体体验往往岌岌可危、脆弱易失。个体不可避免地会经验到一种失败感：在其中，该对象由于无论何种原因，不再被体验为理想的、或反映着一个人的夸大自体。这样的脱节[disjunction]会发生，例如：当一位艺术家清晨开始工作，发现正在创作的作品不过是一团令人困惑的杂乱，而前一夜他或她留下的还是一件"杰作"。在这种情况下，主体性中被外化的各个方面不再与自体体验相契合，它们被体验为某种他者[other]——有时，被体验为某种与所想象或所欲望之物相去甚远的东西。

This situation leads to a state of self-crisis that can result in a permanent rupture of the relation to the artwork – or, optimally, to further efforts to restore the tie through work. It is important to remember that it is not solely the qualities of the object that determine resonance and dissonance, but rather the relation between the internal subjective world and its externalization in the object. From the point of view of a third party, the object may be beautiful, but the artist may nonetheless experience a sense of disappointment or even failure.

这一处境导致一种自体危机状态，它可能造成与作品之关系的永久断裂[rupture]——或者最理想地，导致通过工作来修复这一纽带的进一步努力。重要的是要记住：决定共鸣与不协和的，并不仅仅是对象的诸性质，而是内部主观世界与其在对象中的外化之间的关系。从第三方的观点看，对象或许是美的，但艺术家仍可能经验到一种失望、甚至失败之感。

Successful art involves the artist's creation of an opportunity for selfobject experience. He or she does this by externalizing some aspect of subjectivity by means of an action upon the external world. The artist then enters a dialectical relationship with these externalized aspects of his or her own subjectivity. The intent of this dialectic is to take further action to alter the artwork toward the goal of greater expressive perfection. On the other hand, the artist is affected by the artwork, by his or her inner working model of the work and the emotional state in response to the object being created. Optimally, the artwork not only resonates with inner experience, but also externalizes subjectivity in an increasingly ideal form. The relationship with the ideal artwork that reflects the perfection of the artist lies at the heart of the successful creative process.

成功的艺术包含艺术家为自体客体体验[selfobject experience]创造出一个机会。他或她通过对外部世界施加某个行动、借此把主体性的某个方面外化，来实现这一点。艺术家随后与自身主体性中被外化的这些方面进入一种辩证关系。这一辩证关系的意图是采取进一步的行动来改动作品，以趋近更大表达完美[expressive perfection]的目标。另一方面，艺术家也受到作品的影响，受到他或她对作品的内在运作模型[inner working model]，以及面对正在被创造的对象时的情绪状态的影响。最理想地，作品不仅与内在经验相共鸣，而且以日益理想的形式把主体性外化。与那种反映着艺术家之完美的理想作品的关系，正处在成功的创造过程的核心。

The Sense of Beauty（美感）

Chapter six focuses on beauty. The developmental source of the sense of beauty is the longed-for experience of perfection found in the early mother/infant bond. Beauty is an invariant formal aspect of anything that is experienced as ideal. We all value and seek beauty as an opportunity for selfobject experience. When we are in the presence of something beautiful, we are enlivened; we feel whole and happy. Beauty is a special element in the aesthetic experience in which the investment of reality with subjectivity creates an experience of that reality as both ideal and harmonious with our inner life (see also Hagman, 2001; Lee, 1947, 1948, 1950).

第六章聚焦于美[beauty]。美感[sense of beauty]的发展性源头，是在早期母婴联结中找到的那种被渴求的完美[perfection]经验。美是任何被体验为理想之物的一种不变的形式方面[invariant formal aspect]。我们全都珍视并追寻美，把它当作自体客体体验的一个机会。当我们身处某种美的东西在场之际，我们便焕发生机；我们感到完整而幸福。美是审美体验中一个特殊的要素：在其中，以主体性对现实[reality]进行投注[investment]，创造出一种把该现实体验为既理想、又与我们的内在生活相和谐的经验（另见 Hagman, 2001; Lee, 1947, 1948, 1950）。

This chapter discusses the nature and function of the sense of beauty in psychological life. Noting the reemergence of interest in beauty by philosophers and art critics, I offer a definition of beauty as an aspect of idealization in which (an) object(s), sound(s), or concept(s) is (or are) believed to possess qualities of formal perfection. Eleven points are discussed: (1) the sense of beauty is a transitional psychological process in which the world is invested with subjectivity – specifically, idealization; (2) beauty is a primary characteristic of idealization; (3) the archaic sources of beauty lie in early relational experiences – most important, in the recognition of the mother's face; (4) the sense of beauty is interactive, not disinterested or passive; (5) beauty involves aesthetic and non-aesthetic emotions; (6) the sense of beauty is a normal process, with no necessary relation to psychopathology, although beauty can function in the following processes: (7) it may play an integrative role in self-disorders; (8) it can assume a reparative role when internal objects are at risk or felt to be damaged; (9) it may fulfill a defensive role to protect against conflict or shore up self-deficits; and, in addition, (10) identification with the beautiful object results in the experience in the enjoyer of being 'loveworthy,' thus enhancing well-being and self-esteem; and (11) beauty can protect against the fear of mortality and vulnerability in general. The chapter closes with a discussion of Freud's until-now unexplained reverence for beauty as a primary trait of civilized life.

本章讨论美感[sense of beauty]在心理生活中的性质与功能。注意到哲学家与艺术批评家对美的兴趣再度兴起，我给出美的一个定义：美是理想化[idealization]的一个方面，在其中，（一个或多个）对象、声音或概念被认为拥有形式完美[formal perfection]的性质。共讨论十一点：(1) 美感是一个过渡性心理过程[transitional psychological process]，在其中，世界被投注以主体性——具体地说，即理想化；(2) 美是理想化的首要特征；(3) 美的古老源头在于早期关系经验——最重要的是对母亲面容的识别[recognition]；(4) 美感是互动性的，而非无利害的[disinterested]或被动的；(5) 美涉及审美的与非审美的情绪；(6) 美感是一个正常过程，与精神病理学[psychopathology]没有必然关联，尽管美可以在以下过程中起作用：(7) 它可能在自体障碍[self-disorders]中扮演整合性的角色；(8) 当内部对象[internal objects]处于危险中或被认为已受损时，它可以承担一种修复性的[reparative]角色；(9) 它可能履行一种防御性的角色，以对抗冲突或撑起自体缺陷[self-deficits]；(10) 此外，与美的对象相认同[identification]会在享用者[enjoyer]身上产生一种"值得被爱"['loveworthy']的体验，从而增进幸福感与自尊；以及 (11) 美能够保护我们免于对必死性[mortality]和一般意义上的脆弱性[vulnerability]的恐惧。本章以讨论弗洛伊德对美的尊崇[reverence]作结——他把美视为文明生活[civilized life]的首要特质，这一点迄今未获解释。

Ugliness（丑）

On the other hand, our aesthetic sensibility may also suffer disruption, and it is this traumatic disruption of our aesthetic experience that we call ugliness. In chapter seven, I will discuss how ugliness is the provocation and projection of unconscious fantasies that alter the sense of aesthetic experience in such a way that the formal qualities of the experience – its shape, texture, and color – become what we experience as sources of the most disturbing and repulsive feelings.

另一方面，我们的审美感受力[aesthetic sensibility]也可能遭到扰乱，而正是这种对审美体验的创伤性扰乱，我们称之为丑[ugliness]。在第七章，我将讨论丑如何是无意识幻想的挑动[provocation]与投射[projection]——这些幻想改变了审美经验之感，以至于该经验的形式性质[formal qualities]（它的形状、质地与色彩）变成了我们体验为最令人不安、最令人厌恶之情感的来源。

This chapter reviews the psychoanalytic writings concerning ugliness. It offers a psychoanalytic model that addresses several key points: ugliness as a failure of sublimation, the collapse of idealization, ugliness and interaction, the affective dimension of ugliness, and ugliness as a symptom in psychopathology. Clinical vignettes and reminiscences are used to illustrate various points. I close the chapter with a discussion of how ugliness can provide an opportunity for the psychoanalyst and the artist: he or she confronts ugliness, and, through the creative and/or the psychoanalytic process, brings form and perfection to disintegration and disorder. In this way, ugliness ultimately succumbs to beauty.

本章回顾关于丑的精神分析著作。它提出一个精神分析模型，处理若干要点：丑作为升华的失败[failure of sublimation]、理想化的崩塌[collapse of idealization]、丑与互动、丑的情感维度，以及丑作为精神病理学中的一种症状[symptom]。临床片段[clinical vignettes]与回忆[reminiscences]被用来阐明各个论点。我以讨论丑如何能为精神分析师与艺术家提供一个机会来结束本章：他或她直面丑，并通过创造性过程和/或精神分析过程，为崩解[disintegration]与失序[disorder]带来形式与完美。以这种方式，丑最终臣服于美[succumbs to beauty]。

The Sublime（崇高）

Beauty is the aesthetic of maternal preoccupation. It is the organization of the ideal elements that composed the formal sense of the interaction between mother and infant. Balance, integration, and clear meaning characterize beauty. On the other hand, power, formlessness, obscurity, and immensity play important roles in the paternal aesthetic, the aesthetic of the sublime.

美是母亲式专注[maternal preoccupation]的审美。它是对那些构成母婴互动之形式感的理想要素的组织。平衡[balance]、整合[integration]与清晰的意义[clear meaning]，是美的特征。另一方面，力量[power]、无形式[formlessness]、晦暗[obscurity]与浩瀚[immensity]，则在父亲式的审美[paternal aesthetic]——即崇高[the sublime]的审美——中扮演重要角色。

Chapter eight focuses on the aesthetics of the childhood experience of the father. Several decades ago, Phyllis Greenacre (1953) argued that awe before the sublime was derivative of the child's first encounters with the father's phallus. Its strangeness, size, and power (all inseparable from fantasy) became the prototype of all experiences in which we perceive the sublime and extraordinary. Building on Greenacre's view, I discuss how the infant moves outward from the enclosed and managed aesthetic space of the mother, into an open, dynamic, and uncontrolled engagement with the world as introduced by the father. The father's relative size; the child's fantasies of the father's power, aggressiveness, and perhaps even omnipotence; the perception of immense and obscure reality beyond and outside dyadic experience – all these combine in the first aesthetics of awe and astonishment.

第八章聚焦于童年期对父亲之经验的美学。数十年前，菲利斯·格林纳克[Phyllis Greenacre]（1953）主张，面对崇高时的敬畏[awe]，派生于孩子最初与父亲阳具[phallus]的相遇。它的陌生[strangeness]、尺寸[size]与力量（三者皆与幻想[fantasy]不可分割），成为我们据以知觉崇高与非凡之物的所有经验的原型[prototype]。在格林纳克观点的基础上，我讨论婴儿如何从母亲那个被围合、被管理的审美空间向外移动，进入一种由父亲所引介的、对世界的开放、动态、不受控制的投入。父亲的相对尺寸；孩子关于父亲的力量、攻击性、或许甚至全能[omnipotence]的幻想；对二元体验[dyadic experience]之外、之彼岸那浩瀚而晦暗之现实的知觉——这一切共同化合为最初的敬畏与惊异[awe and astonishment]之美学。

This chapter concludes with a discussion of the functions of the sublime and consequently the reasons why we are drawn to seek out and cultivate such experiences. The sublime functions to bring several different terrors within a containable, ordered universe. It is the powerful discharge of desire and aggression without catastrophe; it can portray the results of discharge without the destruction of self or a loved one. Death, sexuality, aggression, loss of self, vulnerability, and isolation are embraced and overcome, although not by being negated or denied. Paradoxically, the experience of these terrors in the sublime is vitalizing and self-confirming, not disorganizing. There is an emotional state of arousal/tranquility. What is internally threatening is safely put outside the self. There is an externalization of fantasy, desire, and fear.

本章以讨论崇高的功能、以及由此而来的我们为何被吸引去寻求并培育此类经验的理由作结。崇高的功能，是把若干不同的恐惧[terrors]带入一个可容纳的、有秩序的世界之中。它是欲望与攻击性在没有灾难的情况下强有力的释放[discharge]；它能够描绘释放的结果，而不致毁坏自体或所爱之人。死亡、性、攻击性、自体之丧失、脆弱与孤立，都被拥抱并被克服——尽管并非通过否定[negated]或否认[denied]它们。悖论性地说，在崇高中对这些恐惧的经验是赋予活力、自体确认的，而非造成崩解的。存在一种唤起/宁静[arousal/tranquility]的情感状态。内在具有威胁性的东西，被安全地置于自体之外。于是有了幻想、欲望与恐惧的外化。

The sublime is thus the experience of vulnerability from a position of safety. It begins with the father as observed from the arms of the mother and continues with the world surveyed from within his firm grasp. It is the rawness of affect, the passion of fantasy, and the shock of the new, contained and expressed within a formal structure that is experienced as astonishing and ultimately transcendent.

因此，崇高是从一个安全位置出发去经验脆弱性。它始于从母亲臂弯中观察到的父亲，并延续为在他牢固掌握之中审视世界的经验。它是情感的原始粗粝[rawness of affect]、幻想的热烈[passion of fantasy]以及新事物带来的冲击[shock of the new]——它们被容纳、被表达于一个被体验为令人惊异、并终究超越性的[transcendent]形式结构之中。

Festival（节庆）

In the final chapter, I offer an integration of the multiple levels of subjectivity that make up the aesthetic life of mankind. I will show that art and creativity serve different functions when you approach them from the vantage point of one's culture, relationships, and individual subjective life. The social function of art in the preservation and enhancement of group relations within a particular society is quite different from the psychological function of art in the affirmation and enhancement of the self-experience of the individual working artist. Yet a complete psychoanalytic aesthetic theory must acknowledge the relationship between these different domains.

在最后一章，我提供一种对构成人类审美生活的多重主体性层级[multiple levels of subjectivity]的整合。我将表明，当你从一个人的文化、关系与个体主观生活的视点[vantage point]出发去接近艺术与创造力时，它们服务于不同的功能。艺术在某一特定社会内部维系与增进群体关系时的社会功能[social function]，与艺术在确认并增进作为个体的、正在工作的艺术家的自体体验时的心理功能[psychological function]，相当不同。然而，一套完整的精神分析美学理论必须承认这些不同领域之间的关系。

In this chapter, I emphasize that creative acts are intelligible only from within the culture and social milieu out of which they emerge. Culture provides the language of art. Art cannot be conceived of outside of a cultural milieu in which not only the forms and methods of art are promulgated, but the general standards that define quality are developed as well (at the level of metasubjectivity, as will be elaborated further). This is true even of the most personal aspects of art. The artist who climbs up to the privacy of her garret to create finds there the tools, maps, and measuring instruments of an outside culture, and although she might challenge that culture, she cannot escape it. We take our artistic language from culture, which becomes the horizon within which aesthetic experience is possible and intelligible.

在本章中，我强调，创造性行动[creative acts]只有在它们所由之兴起的文化与社会环境[social milieu]之内才是可理解的。文化提供艺术的语言[language of art]。离开一个文化环境，艺术便无法被设想——在这个环境中，不仅艺术的诸形式与方法被颁布[promulgated]，而且界定质量的普遍标准也被发展出来（在元主体性[metasubjectivity]的层级上，这一点将进一步展开）。即便对艺术最私人的方面而言，这也同样成立。那位攀登到自己阁楼[garret]的私密空间中去创作的艺术家，在那里发现的是外部文化的工具、地图与测量器械；尽管她可能挑战那种文化，她却无法逃离它。我们从文化中获取自己的艺术语言，而文化便成为审美体验得以可能、得以被理解的地平线[horizon]。

Artists are embedded in a network of relationships, within which their aesthetics develop and are perfected (at the level of intersubjectivity). Many have noted the frequent occurrence of artistic dyads in which an artist or artists rely on dialogue with a valued other person who functions for them as a muse. It is out of this intersubjective matrix that many revolutionary aesthetic movements are spawned and sustained. These groupings of artists take from the metasubjective horizon of culture those themes, styles, values, and art forms that are best suited to express their special intersubjective vision. Then, through dialogue, these artists together create the unique aesthetic of their generation. It is these cultural and relational contexts that compose the working ground of individual artistic effort.

艺术家嵌置于一个关系网络之中，他们的美学在其中发展并臻于完善（在主体间性[intersubjectivity]的层级上）。许多人已注意到艺术二人组[artistic dyads]的频繁出现：其中一位或数位艺术家依赖与某位被珍视的他者的对话，后者充当他们的缪斯[muse]。正是在这一主体间基质[intersubjective matrix]之中，许多革命性的美学运动被孕育并得以维系。这些艺术家的群体从文化的元主体地平线[metasubjective horizon]中，选取那些最适合表达其特殊主体间视域[intersubjective vision]的主题、风格、价值与艺术形式。随后，通过对话，这些艺术家一同创造出他们那一代人独特的美学。正是这些文化与关系的语境，构成了个体艺术努力的工作场地[working ground]。

In the end, neither culture nor relationships have the adaptability and resourcefulness of the individual artist's creative process (at the level of the subjective). This is why, although art spawns grand cultures and great movements, it is nevertheless the individual struggle of the single artist that gives life to these larger processes. The artist working on his or her own, taking from and acting against the culture and relationships within which she lives, creates something new. It is this domain of aesthetics that is the primary concern of chapter nine.

归根结底，文化也好、关系也好，都不具有个体艺术家创造过程（在主体[subjective]的层级上）那种适应性与机变[resourcefulness]。这就是为什么，尽管艺术孕育出宏大的文化与伟大的运动，赋予这些更大过程以生命的，却仍然是一位位单个艺术家的个体挣扎。独自工作的艺术家——从她生活于其中的文化与关系中取用、又与之对抗——创造出某种新的东西。正是这一美学领域，是第九章的首要关切。

I will emphasize the way in which self-experience (which includes the experience of self-in-relationship) is expressed in the formal structure of the artwork. However, I must be clear that the individual intrapsychic experience of the artist crystallizes out of and is constituted by relational and cultural meanings that are also the artist's motive, form, and subject. In fact, artists may be more profoundly engaged with their environment and social milieu than are many others.

我将强调自体体验[self-experience]（它包含"关系中的自体"[self-in-relationship]的经验）在作品之形式结构[formal structure]中被表达的方式。然而，我必须阐明：艺术家个体的心理内部经验[intrapsychic experience]，是从关系性的与文化性的意义中结晶[crystallizes]出来的，并由这些意义所构成——而这些意义同时既是艺术家的动机[motive]、形式，也是其主题[subject]。事实上，艺术家可能比许多其他人更深刻地投入自己的环境与社会环境。

This book provides an approach to psychoanalytic aesthetics that emphasizes the central role of early childhood experience in the emergence and structuralization of the sense of aesthetic form. It argues that because of developmentally based processes of idealization, the child's formal aesthetic sense takes on a profound and lifelong concern with form and quality. Thus, the creative artist is concerned not just with articulation of subjective states of feeling, but also with the most refined and perfect expression possible of her internal vision.

因此，创造性的艺术家所关切的，不仅是对主观情感状态的表述[articulation]，也是对其内在视像[internal vision]尽可能最精纯、最完美的表达。

The most exquisite embodiment of ideal aesthetic form is beauty, and it is the multiple functions that the sense of beauty fulfills in our psychological lives that motivate us to pursue creation and to get nearer to it. On the other hand, ugliness represents the failure of our aesthetic sensibility, in which fantasy and desire disrupt the formal structure of our experience. Conversely, the sublime offers us a means through the paternal aesthetic to contain fear, and even horror, which we can then integrate into a powerful and astonishing aesthetic form. Finally, I propose that aesthetic experience is elaborated by means of multiple levels of subjectivity: the metasubjective (social), the intersubjective (relational), and the personal (subjective). It is the rich interrelationship between these levels that composes the complexity and profound depth of human aesthetic life.

理想审美形式最精妙的具身化[embodiment]是美；而正是美感[sense of beauty]在我们的心理生活中所履行的多重功能，驱动我们去追求创造、去更靠近它。另一方面，丑[ugliness]代表着我们审美感受力的失败——在其中，幻想与欲望扰乱了经验的形式结构。与之相反，崇高[the sublime]通过父亲式的审美为我们提供一种手段，去容纳恐惧、甚至恐怖[horror]，而后我们便能把它整合进一种强大而令人惊异的美学形式。最后，我提出：审美体验是经由多重主体性层级而被化育的：元主体（社会）[metasubjective (social)]、主体间（关系）[intersubjective (relational)]，以及个人（主体）[personal (subjective)]。正是这些层级之间丰富的相互关系[interrelationship]，构成了人类审美生活的复杂性与深邃。

This is a book for persons interested in the psychoanalytic approach to art and aesthetic experience. I have tried to make clear that, even though I focus mainly on art and artists, many of these ideas are applicable to psychological life in general. We all organize our lives aesthetically, whether or not we are aware of it. Of course, it is in our experience of art that the aesthetic experience is most intensely felt, most clearly illustrated and explicated. It is my hope, then, that the book may have a broader audience and find some application to areas of psychological life beyond art. (In fact, a psychoanalyst colleague who read the chapter on beauty felt that the capacity to experience beauty might constitute a sign of psychological health.)

这是一本为对艺术与审美体验之精神分析进路感兴趣的人而写的书。我已努力阐明：尽管我主要聚焦于艺术与艺术家，但这些观念中有许多适用于一般的心理生活。我们全都在审美地组织着自己的生活，无论我们是否意识到这一点。当然，正是在我们对艺术的经验中，审美体验被最强烈地感受到、最清晰地例示[illustrated]并阐明[explicated]。因此，我的希望是，本书能有更广泛的读者，并在艺术之外的诸心理生活领域找到某些应用。（事实上，一位读过论美那一章的精神分析师同行觉得，体验美的能力或许构成心理健康[psychological health]的一个标志。）

Although this book is not primarily clinical, I have included clinical process to illustrate some of the ideas presented. In this respect, I hope that there may be a way to elaborate on some of my thoughts to inform psychotherapeutic treatment. However, I should also note here that I consistently emphasize a nonpathological orientation toward my topics. I will discuss how aesthetic experience develops and is elaborated, with less attention to pathological variants. For example, it is acknowledged that idealization can be used as a defense and that, at times, this can result in significant internal and social distress. Much has been written about this topic, but it is not my focus in chapter four.

尽管本书主要不是临床性的，我仍纳入了临床过程[clinical process]，以阐明所提出的一些观念。就此而言，我希望或许有办法把我的某些思考加以展开，以为心理治疗提供参考。然而，我也应在此指出，我对诸主题一贯强调一种非病理性的[nonpathological]取向。我将讨论审美体验如何发展、如何被化育，而较少关注病理性的变体[pathological variants]。例如，人们承认理想化可以被用作一种防御，有时这会导致显著的内在痛苦与社会痛苦。关于这一主题已有大量著述，但这并非我在第四章的焦点。

In addition, the sense of beauty can also be misused, and I make a brief comment regarding this in my discussion of anorexia and depression – but this is a brief and very incomplete discussion. I do not want to suggest that psychopathological conflicts, deficits, and distortions are not important in understanding aesthetic experience, but a thorough consideration of such areas will have to be carried out elsewhere. In the present project, I will begin by establishing some agreement about normal processes – an important first step.

此外，美感也可能被误用，我在讨论厌食症[anorexia]与抑郁[depression]时对此作了简短评述——但这是一个简短且非常不完整的讨论。我并不想暗示精神病理性的冲突、缺陷与扭曲在理解审美体验时不重要，但对这些领域的透彻考察必须另处进行。在当前的计划中，我将以就正常过程[normal processes]达成某种共识作为开始——这是重要的第一步。

Art and Self（艺术与自体）

This chapter presents a contemporary self psychological perspective on aesthetic experience, art, and creativity. I argue that aesthetics is as important to human life as sex, hunger, aggression, love, and hate. Although we may rarely be conscious of it, aesthetic experience gives form, meaning, and, most importantly, value to everything we are, all we experience, and everything we do. Theoretically without it, life would be a shapeless, meaningless, and colorless series of sensations, events, and reactions. Aesthetic experience achieves its most refined form in the fine arts. However, we can also see its most archaic manifestation in the curve of the mother's shoulder during nursing, her heartbeat and breath, the melody of her voice, the balance of her eyes and smile–all embedded in the warmth, nourishment, and security of the mother/infant interaction. This chapter reviews recent analytic writings on psychoanalytic aesthetics that emphasize the central role of early childhood relational experiences in the emergence and structuralization of the sense of aesthetic form. I argue that because of developmentally based processes of idealization, the child's aesthetic sense takes on a profound and lifelong concern with form and quality. I extend this model and propose a new definition of creativity and the nature of art, arguing that the creative artist is concerned not just with articulation of subjective states of feeling but also with the most refined and perfect expression possible of his or her internal vision. Although a major application of this model is to art and creativity, it is argued that aesthetic experience is a pervasive human trait that impacts on our entire experience of life, self, and relationships.

本章提出一种关于审美体验、艺术与创造力的当代自体心理学[self psychological]视角。我主张，美学对人类生活而言与性、饥饿、攻击性、爱与恨同样重要。尽管我们或许很少意识到它，但审美体验赋予我们所是的一切、我们所经验的一切、我们所做的一切以形式、意义，以及最重要的——价值。从理论上说，若没有它，生活将沦为一系列无形、无意义、无色彩的感觉、事件与反应。审美体验在美术[fine arts]中达到其最精纯的形式。然而，我们也能在哺乳时母亲肩头的曲线、她的心跳与呼吸、她嗓音的旋律、她眼神与微笑的平衡中，看到它最古老的显现——这一切都嵌入在母亲/婴儿互动的温暖、滋养与安全之中。本章回顾关于精神分析美学的晚近分析性著述，这些著述强调童年早期关系经验在审美形式感[sense of aesthetic form]之涌现与结构化[structuralization]中的核心作用。我主张，由于基于发展的理想化过程，孩子的审美感获得了一种对形式与品质的深刻而终身的关切。我扩展这一模型，并提出一个关于创造力与艺术之性质的新定义，主张创造性的艺术家所关切的，不仅是对主观情感状态的表述，也是对其内在视像[internal vision]尽可能最精纯、最完美的表达。尽管这一模型的一个主要应用领域是艺术与创造力，但本书主张，审美体验是一种普遍的人类特质[a pervasive human trait]，它影响着我们对生活、自体与关系的整个经验。

Creative Analysis（创造性分析）

In this chapter I extend our discussion of the aesthetic foundation of human experience and show how an aesthetic perspective can shed light on the psychoanalytic process (Hagman, 2010, 2015, 2016). I begin with a review of the aesthetic dimension of early childhood experience, the emergence of aesthetic form and value from the child's experience of the relational field, and its elaboration into a mature, fully developed aesthetic sense. I then show how aesthetics can inform our understanding of psychotherapeutic process in terms yet neglected. Finally, I discuss creativity as it is manifest in psychotherapy through the construction of the cotransference (Orange, 1993), an aesthetically organized subjective object, which becomes the focus of analysis and creative change (Winnicott, 1971). The chapter closes with a case example as an illustration of the structure and dynamics of a creative analysis (Hagman, 2015)

在本章中，我扩展我们对人类经验之审美基础的讨论，并表明一种审美视角如何能照亮精神分析过程[psychoanalytic process]（Hagman, 2010, 2015, 2016）。我以对童年早期经验之审美维度的回顾开始，继而考察审美形式与价值如何从孩子对关系场域[relational field]的经验中涌现，以及它如何被化育为一种成熟、充分发展的审美感。随后我表明，美学如何能以其迄今被忽视的措辞，为我们理解心理治疗过程[psychotherapeutic process]提供信息。最后，我讨论创造力如何在心理治疗中，通过"共移情"[cotransference]（Orange, 1993）的建构而显现——那是一个审美地组织起来的主观对象[subjective object]，它成为分析与创造性改变[creative change]的焦点（Winnicott, 1971）。本章以一个案例示例作结，用以例示一场创造性分析[creative analysis]的结构与动力（Hagman, 2015）

The Aesthetics of Music, the Musician and the Creative Process（音乐的美学、音乐家与创造过程）

Music is the embodiment through sound of lived experience. Conscious and unconscious modes of subjectivity are woven together in a tapestry of tone and sound, which is less about the world and more about the symbolic equivalent of human subjectivity itself. Musicians through their interpretation of a composition invest their performance with self-experience, and they come to experience themselves as vibrantly mirrored in the ideal form of the music. In other words, the musical performance is an opportunity for selfobject experience. Musical performance involves a two-phase process. The first is the practice phase, during which the musician seeks to achieve the experience of aesthetic resonance in which self-experience and music are brought into sync through the perfection of the performance. The dynamic role of selfobject failure and restoration in the musician's motivation to perfect the performance is stressed. The second phase is the public performance, during which the musician exhibits his or her ideal creation and experiences the mirroring of the audience. During these phases, there is a creative dialectic between internal and externalized aspects of self-experience. An examination of case material from analytic work with a musician shows how an inhibition in the creative process may be based on the expectation of selfobject failure and how musical creativity can be facilitated through new opportunities for selfobject experience in the transference.

音乐是通过声音对活生生经验[lived experience]的具身化。意识与无意识的主体性模式被编织进一幅音调与声音的织锦[tapestry]之中——它较少关乎世界，而更多关乎人类主体性本身的象征等价物[symbolic equivalent]。音乐家通过对一部作品的演绎，为其演奏注入自体体验，并逐渐把自己体验为在音乐的理想形式中熠熠生辉地被镜映[mirrored]。换言之，音乐演奏是自体客体体验的一个机会。音乐演奏涉及一个两阶段的过程。第一阶段是练习阶段[practice phase]：在其中，音乐家力求获得审美共鸣的体验，即通过演奏的完美使自体体验与音乐同步。自体客体失败[selfobject failure]与修复[restoration]在音乐家完善演奏的动机中所起的动力性作用受到强调。第二阶段是公开演奏[public performance]：在其中，音乐家展示自己理想的创造，并体验到观众的镜映[mirroring of the audience]。在这些阶段中，存在着自体体验之内部方面与外在方面之间的创造性辩证关系。对一位音乐家分析工作的案例材料的考察表明：创造过程中的抑制[inhibition]如何可能建立在对自体客体失败的预期之上，而音乐创造力又如何能通过移情[transference]中新的自体客体体验的机会而得到促进。

The Erotics of Beauty（美的爱欲）

This chapter is a study of Death in Venice by Thomas Mann, a 1911 novella that dramatizes the function of the experience of beauty in the main character's struggle to ward off impending self-disintegration. Interestingly, it was Heinz Kohut who published the first full psychoanalytic interpretation of the work considering the psychology of its author (Kohut, 1957). There Kohut argued that the breakdown of the sublimating function of art resulted in the main character's descent into previously forbidden drive fantasies and disruptive desires. Written in 1948 and published in 1957 after Mann's death, Kohut's analysis understandably reflected his theoretical background at the time. In this paper, I would like to reinterpret Von Aschenbach's erotic infatuation, the dramatic focus of the work, as his effort to maintain self-cohesion through the experience of an archaic, sexualized idealization of the beautiful boy Tadzio. I will argue that Aschenbach's obsession, rather than being a symptom of self-disintegration, is in fact a restorative effort, the boy's beauty offering Aschenbach an experience of perfection, in other words an opportunity for a transcendent selfobject experience.

本章是对托马斯·曼[Thomas Mann]《死于威尼斯》[Death in Venice]的一项研究——这部1911年的中篇小说[novella]，戏剧性地呈现了美的经验的作用：主人公挣扎着抵挡即将来临的自体崩解[self-disintegration]。有趣的是，正是海因茨·科胡特[Heinz Kohut]发表了关于这部作品的第一篇充分考虑其作者心理学的完整精神分析解释（Kohut, 1957）。科胡特在那里主张，艺术之升华功能的崩溃，导致主人公堕入先前被禁忌的驱力幻想[drive fantasies]与破坏性欲望[disruptive desires]。这篇分析写于1948年、在曼去世后的1957年发表，因此它反映了科胡特当时的理论背景，这是可以理解的。在本文中，我想重新解释冯·阿申巴赫[Von Aschenbach]的爱欲迷恋[erotic infatuation]——作品的戏剧焦点——把它解读为他经由对美少年塔齐奥[Tadzio]的一种古老的、性欲化的理想化经验，来维持自体凝聚[self-cohesion]的努力。我将主张，阿申巴赫的执迷[obsession]，与其说是自体崩解的一种症状[symptom]，不如说是一种修复性努力[restorative effort]：男孩的美为阿申巴赫提供了一种完美的经验，换言之，一个通往超越性自体客体体验[transcendent selfobject experience]的机会。

Hitler's Aesthetics（希特勒的美学）

Beauty, the formal aspect of idealization, can embody the finest and most transcendent values in human aesthetic experience, but beauty can also be perverse and in some instances it can serve the needs of evil. This paper will examine the dark side of beauty. We will see how beauty can become grotesque and ultimately be destroyed when it is forced to fulfill the needs of the most terrible of self-disorders. In his book Hitler and the Power of Aesthetics, Frederic Spotts (2003) argued that our image of Hitler as a monster is incomplete. He asserted that historians have failed to appreciate the place of Hitler's aesthetics in our understanding of his motives and aims. Spotts pointed out that the young Hitler pursued a career as an artist for several years, until professional failure and the opportunity for political success offered him the narcissistic gratification through demagogy that he so longed for from the arts. Once in power, it was Hitler's obsession with aesthetic matters that dominated his passion for culture and disguised his hatred and aggression. Monomaniacal, he pursued the destruction of Western culture in his quest to remake and mold the aesthetic landscape according to his own idealized and grandiose self-image.

美，作为理想化的形式方面[formal aspect]，能够具身化人类审美经验中最精微、最超越的价值；但美也可能乖戾反常[perverse]，在某些情形中甚至可以为恶的需要服务。本文考察美的阴暗面[dark side of beauty]。我们将看到：当美被迫去满足最可怕的自体障碍[self-disorders]的需要时，它如何可能变得怪诞[grotesque]，并最终被摧毁。在其著作《希特勒与美学的力量》[Hitler and the Power of Aesthetics]中，弗雷德里克·斯波茨[Frederic Spotts]（2003）主张，我们把希特勒视为怪物的形象是不完整的。他断言，历史学家未能领会希特勒的美学在我们理解其动机与目标时所占据的位置。斯波茨指出，年轻的希特勒曾追求艺术家的生涯达数年之久，直到职业上的失败与政治上成功的机会，通过蛊惑宣传[demagogy]给予了他那种他从艺术中如此渴求的自恋满足[narcissistic gratification]。一旦掌权，正是希特勒对审美事务的执迷支配着他对文化的热情，并掩盖了他的仇恨与攻击性。他偏执单一[monomaniacal]，在他按照自己理想化且夸大的自体意象[idealized and grandiose self-image]去重塑、铸造审美景观的寻求中，追逐着对西方文化的摧毁。
